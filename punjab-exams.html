Which posts are covered on this page?

Five Punjab Police posts — Constable, ASI, SI, Intelligence Assistant and Jail Warder — plus PSSSB, PPSC and PSPCL posts from Patwari and Forest Guard to Clerk and Lab Attendant.

Does this site publish vacancies, dates or admit cards?

No. Recruitment dates change and this portal does not track them. Always read the official notification on the recruiting body's own website before acting on any date.

Is the material affiliated with any commission?

No. House of Aspirants is an independent, unofficial study resource. It is not connected to or endorsed by Punjab Police, PSSSB, PPSC or PSPCL in any way.

Where should I start if my exam is two months away?

Take a full mock test today to find your weakest subject, then study that subject's guide and drill its topics daily while keeping the Daily Quiz habit running.
