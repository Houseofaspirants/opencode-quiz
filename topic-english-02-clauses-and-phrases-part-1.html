02 Clauses And Phrases Part 1: all 25 questions

  1. Which best distinguishes a phrase from a clause?

    1. A clause has a subject and a verb; a phrase does not have both together.
    2. A phrase always expresses a complete thought.
    3. A clause has no verb.
    4. A phrase must contain two clauses.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A clause has a subject and a verb; a phrase does not have both together.

    Explanation: A clause has a subject and a verb; a phrase does not have both together. is correct according to the chapter rule.

  2. Which kind of clause expresses a complete thought by itself?

    1. Dependent clause
    2. Independent clause
    3. Phrase
    4. Adjective
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Independent clause

    Explanation: Independent clause is correct according to the chapter rule.

  3. Which kind of clause cannot express a complete thought by itself?

    1. Independent clause
    2. Main sentence
    3. Dependent clause
    4. Predicate
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Dependent clause

    Explanation: Dependent clause is correct according to the chapter rule.

  4. “In the blue shirt” is an example of what?

    1. Independent clause
    2. Dependent clause
    3. Complete sentence
    4. Phrase
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Phrase

    Explanation: Phrase is correct according to the chapter rule.

  5. “When the bell rang” is what?

    1. Dependent clause
    2. Phrase
    3. Independent clause
    4. Complete sentence
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Dependent clause

    Explanation: Dependent clause is correct according to the chapter rule.

  6. What two elements must a clause contain?

    1. A noun and an article
    2. A subject and a verb
    3. Two subjects
    4. A conjunction and an object
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) A subject and a verb

    Explanation: A subject and a verb is correct according to the chapter rule.

  7. Why is “because the tracks were wet” dependent?

    1. It has no verb.
    2. It is a phrase without a subject.
    3. It does not express a complete thought by itself.
    4. It is a complete command.
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) It does not express a complete thought by itself.

    Explanation: It does not express a complete thought by itself. is correct according to the chapter rule.

  8. Which is an independent clause?

    1. While the guests were arriving
    2. Behind the curtains
    3. Before the guests arrived
    4. The principal announced the results.
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) The principal announced the results.

    Explanation: The principal announced the results. is correct according to the chapter rule.

  9. Which is a phrase?

    1. Behind the curtains
    2. The guests arrived
    3. The principal spoke
    4. When the bell rang
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Behind the curtains

    Explanation: Behind the curtains is correct according to the chapter rule.

  10. A phrase can function as what in a sentence?

    1. A complete clause in every case
    2. One unit of meaning
    3. A finite verb
    4. A full sentence by itself
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) One unit of meaning

    Explanation: One unit of meaning is correct according to the chapter rule.

  11. In “The boy was wearing a blue shirt when I saw him,” which is dependent?

    1. The boy was wearing a blue shirt
    2. The boy
    3. when I saw him
    4. was wearing a blue shirt
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) when I saw him

    Explanation: when I saw him is correct according to the chapter rule.

  12. Which fragment has a subject and verb but is incomplete alone?

    1. Near the station
    2. Under the table
    3. A blue shirt
    4. Since the road was blocked
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Since the road was blocked

    Explanation: Since the road was blocked is correct according to the chapter rule.

  13. Which fragment is an independent clause?

    1. We took another route.
    2. Since the road was blocked
    3. After the meeting ended
    4. Behind the curtains
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) We took another route.

    Explanation: We took another route. is correct according to the chapter rule.

  14. A clause has which feature that a phrase may lack?

    1. A preposition
    2. A subject–verb combination
    3. A modifier
    4. An article
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) A subject–verb combination

    Explanation: A subject–verb combination is correct according to the chapter rule.

  15. “Carrying a basket of fruit” is best classified as what?

    1. Independent clause
    2. Dependent clause
    3. Phrase
    4. Interrogative sentence
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Phrase

    Explanation: Phrase is correct according to the chapter rule.

  16. Which pair contains an independent clause followed by a dependent clause?

    1. In the garden / under the tree
    2. Because it rained / although it was late
    3. The train / arrived
    4. The train arrived late / because the tracks were wet
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) The train arrived late / because the tracks were wet

    Explanation: The train arrived late / because the tracks were wet is correct according to the chapter rule.

  17. Which item cannot stand alone as a complete thought?

    1. While the guests were arriving
    2. The guests arrived.
    3. The staff prepared the hall.
    4. The shop closes at eight.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) While the guests were arriving

    Explanation: While the guests were arriving is correct according to the chapter rule.

  18. The clause “if you practise daily” is what?

    1. Independent clause
    2. Dependent clause
    3. Phrase
    4. Predicate only
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Dependent clause

    Explanation: Dependent clause is correct according to the chapter rule.

  19. What does a phrase not necessarily contain together?

    1. A noun or pronoun
    2. A preposition
    3. Its own subject and finite verb
    4. A modifier
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Its own subject and finite verb

    Explanation: Its own subject and finite verb is correct according to the chapter rule.

  20. Which is not a clause?

    1. The chairs were placed.
    2. The guests arrived.
    3. The programme began.
    4. Near the stage
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Near the stage

    Explanation: Near the stage is correct according to the chapter rule.

  21. Which sentence contains an independent and a dependent clause?

    1. The dog barked whenever the phone rang.
    2. The dog barked loudly.
    3. The phone rang.
    4. Behind the curtain.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) The dog barked whenever the phone rang.

    Explanation: The dog barked whenever the phone rang. is correct according to the chapter rule.

  22. “Before the guests arrived” is incomplete because it is what?

    1. An independent clause
    2. A dependent clause
    3. A complete sentence
    4. A phrase
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) A dependent clause

    Explanation: A dependent clause is correct according to the chapter rule.

  23. Which fragment is a phrase rather than a clause?

    1. The players rested
    2. The shop closes
    3. After the long practice session
    4. The phone rang
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) After the long practice session

    Explanation: After the long practice session is correct according to the chapter rule.

  24. In “The children played in the school garden,” which is the independent clause?

    1. In the school garden
    2. School garden
    3. Played in
    4. The children played in the school garden
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) The children played in the school garden

    Explanation: The children played in the school garden is correct according to the chapter rule.

  25. A dependent clause usually needs what to complete its meaning?

    1. An independent clause
    2. Another phrase only
    3. An article
    4. A second verb without a subject
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) An independent clause

    Explanation: An independent clause is correct according to the chapter rule.
