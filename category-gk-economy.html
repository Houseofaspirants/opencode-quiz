All about Economy

Where this lane sits inside General Knowledge.

Economy reads like a foreign language until you have met the vocabulary twice. Banking terms, inflation and price indices, budget language and the agriculture picture that Punjab's own economy rests on - each question assumes you have simply seen the term before.

That is an easy gap to close. Attempt these sets, keep the definitions short, and let the explanations teach the vocabulary. Two sessions a week are enough to turn the section from a risk into a routine.

This lane sits inside General Knowledge (6 lanes in total), so it stays narrow on purpose: 0 topic sets and 0 questions currently publish under Economy. Every question is timed at 30 seconds, carries four options and explains why the right answer is right.

Run a set untimed first to find the gaps, then repeat it against the clock. The questions you miss twice are your revision list - keep them to one line each and revise the file the night before the paper.
