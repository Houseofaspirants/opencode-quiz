About this cluster

What Science covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Science is a broad but forgiving lane in Punjab papers, drawing simple questions from physics, chemistry and biology together with everyday scientific knowledge and the instruments used to measure things. Most questions test recall of facts you either know or do not, so the efficient approach is breadth rather than depth: cover the common definitions, units, phenomena and discoveries, then repeat them as short sets until recognition is instant. Everyday science questions are especially worth securing because they tend to recur across years with only the wording changed. The lanes below split the subject into its three branches plus the cross-cutting topics examiners like to mix, which lets you revise a branch completely before moving on rather than switching context constantly.
