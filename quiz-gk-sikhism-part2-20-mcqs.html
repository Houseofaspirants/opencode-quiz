How many questions are in the Sikhism (Sikh Dharam) - Part 2 quiz?

20 questions, about 10 min at 30 seconds each. Every question has four options, and the review screen shows the explanation behind each answer.

What counts as a pass?

A set is cleared at 40% - that is 8 of 20 questions. You can retake it immediately, as many times as you like.

Can I pause, bookmark or retake the quiz?

Yes. Answers autosave, any question can be bookmarked or marked for review, and Restart quiz clears the session so you can run the set again from zero.

Do I need an account to start a quiz?

No. You can open any quiz and answer questions immediately. A Google sign-in is optional and is only needed to save scores, bookmarks, streaks and progress across devices.
