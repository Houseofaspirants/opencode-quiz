Preparing for Excise Inspector

Why this paper is on the portal and what to run for it.

General awareness and English carry this paper. Track Punjab policy news daily and revise it as MCQs, not as notes.

Excise Inspector preparation on this portal starts from the subjects it actually tests: General Knowledge, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, Computer, Punjabi, English, Current Affairs. Each subject page below lays out its categories, the quiz sets inside them and the other exams that lean on the same material, so the route from exam to question set takes two clicks.

Practise in the order the marks come: untimed topic sets until the pattern is familiar, then the same sets against the clock (30 seconds a question, 60 for Reasoning and Quantitative Aptitude), then a full mock test at 40% to clear. Wrong answers save themselves to bookmarks, so revision stays inside the tool you practise in.
