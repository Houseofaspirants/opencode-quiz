Question bank

Economic Planning & Five Year Plans - Part 2: all 18 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. The Second Five Year Plan was based on the:

    1. Mahalanobis model
    2. Harrod–Domar model
    3. Chakravarty model
    4. Gunnar Myrdal model
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Mahalanobis model

    Explanation: The Second Plan was based on the Mahalanobis model. The other options are incorrect because Harrod–Domar was used for the First Plan, Chakravarty for the Third, and Myrdal is associated with the Rolling Plan. Heavy industries received priority in the Second Plan.

  2. The Second Five Year Plan gave priority to:

    1. Agriculture
    2. Family planning
    3. Inclusive growth
    4. Heavy industries
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Heavy industries

    Explanation: The Second Plan gave priority to heavy industries. The other options are incorrect because agriculture was the First Plan priority, family planning featured in the Sixth, and inclusive growth was an Eleventh Plan theme. The Industrial Policy of 1956 accepted a socialistic pattern of society as the goal.

  3. Which one of the following steel plant–foreign assistance pairs is NOT correctly matched?

    1. Bhilai — USSR
    2. Durgapur — Britain
    3. Rourkela — Germany
    4. Bokaro — Britain
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Bokaro — Britain

    Explanation: Bokaro Steel Plant was set up with USSR help, so “Bokaro — Britain” is not correctly matched. The other options are incorrect because Bhilai–USSR, Durgapur–Britain and Rourkela–Germany are correct pairings. Bhilai and Rourkela both started production in 1959.

  4. Industrial Policy 1956 accepted which goal?

    1. Socialistic pattern of society
    2. Growth facilitator state
    3. Faster and sustainable growth
    4. New agricultural strategy
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Socialistic pattern of society

    Explanation: Industrial Policy 1956 accepted a socialistic pattern of society as the goal. The other options are incorrect because growth facilitator belongs to the Ninth Plan shift, faster sustainable growth to the Twelfth Plan theme, and new agricultural strategy to the first plan holiday. The Second Plan’s priority was heavy industry.

  5. The Third Five Year Plan was based on the:

    1. Mahalanobis model
    2. Harrod–Domar model
    3. Ashok Rudra–Alan Manne model
    4. Chakravarty model
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Chakravarty model

    Explanation: The Third Plan was based on the Chakravarty model. The other options are incorrect because Mahalanobis was the Second Plan model, Harrod–Domar the First, and Ashok Rudra–Alan Manne the Fourth. The Third Plan failed due to wars and droughts.

  6. Assertion (A): The Third Five Year Plan was a failure. Reason (R): It was affected by wars and droughts.

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

    Explanation: Both A and R are true, and wars and droughts explain why the Third Plan failed. The other options are incorrect because the remaining choices either deny a true statement or break the cause-and-effect link. Despite failure, panchayat elections were started during the Third Plan.

  7. Which development was started during the Third Plan despite its failure?

    1. National Highway system
    2. Panchayat elections
    3. Family planning
    4. PURA
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Panchayat elections

    Explanation: Panchayat elections were started during the Third Plan, although the plan failed. The other options are incorrect because the National Highway system came in the Fifth Plan, family planning in the Sixth, and PURA in the Tenth. The Green Revolution was also introduced in the Third Plan.

  8. Bokaro Steel Plant was set up with the help of:

    1. Britain
    2. Germany
    3. USSR
    4. Japan
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) USSR

    Explanation: Bokaro Steel Plant was set up with USSR help. The other options are incorrect because Britain is linked with Durgapur, Germany with Rourkela, and Japan is not given for Bokaro. Bokaro is associated with the Third Plan.

  9. The Green Revolution was introduced during the:

    1. First Plan
    2. Annual Plans / Plan Holiday (1966–69)
    3. Third Plan
    4. Fourth Plan
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Annual Plans / Plan Holiday (1966–69)

    Explanation: The Green Revolution (the new agricultural strategy based on HYV seeds, fertilisers and irrigation) was introduced in 1966, during the Annual Plans or Plan Holiday (1966–69) that followed the Third Plan. Its gains were consolidated during the Fourth Plan.

  10. The first Plan Holiday, consisting of three annual plans, covered:

    1. 1961–1964
    2. 1964–1966
    3. 1966–1969
    4. 1969–1971
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1966–1969

    Explanation: The first Plan Holiday covered 1966–1969 and consisted of three annual plans. The other options are incorrect because the other periods do not match the first plan holiday. A new agricultural strategy was adopted during this holiday.

  11. Which of the following formed part of the new agricultural strategy during the first Plan Holiday? 1. High-yielding variety seeds 2. Extensive use of fertilisers 3. Irrigation and soil conservation Select the correct answer using the code below.

    1. 1 and 2 only
    2. 2 and 3 only
    3. 1 and 3 only
    4. 1, 2 and 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation: All three—high-yielding seeds, extensive fertiliser use, and irrigation with soil conservation—formed the new agricultural strategy. The other options are incorrect because each partial code leaves out one element of the strategy. This strategy is listed under the 1966–1969 annual-plan period.

  12. The Fourth Five Year Plan was based on the model of:

    1. Ashok Rudra and Alan Manne
    2. Harrod and Domar
    3. P.C. Mahalanobis
    4. Chakravarty
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Ashok Rudra and Alan Manne

    Explanation: The Fourth Plan was based on the Ashok Rudra and Alan Manne model. The other options are incorrect because Harrod–Domar, Mahalanobis and Chakravarty belong respectively to the First, Second and Third Plans. Fourteen major banks were nationalised in the Fourth Plan.

  13. During which plan were 14 major banks nationalised in 1969?

    1. Third Plan
    2. Fourth Plan
    3. Fifth Plan
    4. Sixth Plan
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Fourth Plan

    Explanation: Fourteen major banks were nationalised in 1969 during the Fourth Plan. The other options are incorrect because the Third Plan is linked to the Green Revolution’s introduction, the Fifth to MNP, and the Sixth to NABARD. The Fourth Plan also advanced agriculture through the Green Revolution.

  14. The Fifth Five Year Plan was terminated one year early by the:

    1. Congress government
    2. Janata government
    3. Planning Commission
    4. NITI Aayog
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Janata government

    Explanation: The Janata government terminated the Fifth Plan one year early. The other options are incorrect because the Planning Commission only prepared plans, NITI Aayog replaced it in 2015, and it was the Janata government that ended the Fifth Plan early. The Janata government then introduced the Rolling Plan.

  15. The National Highway system was introduced for the first time during the:

    1. Third Plan
    2. Fourth Plan
    3. Fifth Plan
    4. Sixth Plan
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Fifth Plan

    Explanation: The National Highway system was introduced for the first time in the Fifth Plan. The other options are incorrect because the Third, Fourth and Sixth Plans are associated with other features, not this introduction. The Fifth Plan also began the Minimum Needs Programme in its first year.

  16. Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched?

    1. Minimum Needs Programme — prepared by D.P. Dhar
    2. Minimum Needs Programme — suggested by Gunnar Myrdal
    3. Minimum Needs Programme — recommended by A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
    4. Minimum Needs Programme — prepared by C. Rangarajan
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Minimum Needs Programme — prepared by D.P. Dhar

    Explanation: The Minimum Needs Programme began in the first year of the Fifth Plan and was prepared by D.P. Dhar. The other options are incorrect because Myrdal suggested the Rolling Plan, Kalam recommended PURA, and Rangarajan prepared the Eleventh Plan. The Fifth Plan ran from 1974 to 1979.

  17. The Rolling Plan (1978–1980) was suggested by:

    1. D.P. Dhar
    2. Gunnar Myrdal
    3. M. Visvesvaraya
    4. P.V. Narasimha Rao
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Gunnar Myrdal

    Explanation: The Rolling Plan was suggested by Gunnar Myrdal. The other options are incorrect because D.P. Dhar prepared MNP, Visvesvaraya made the 1934 planning proposal, and Rao was PM during the 1991 LPG reforms. Rolling Plan was the second plan holiday and lasted two years.

  18. What is the defining feature of a Rolling Plan?

    1. It fixes targets for fifteen years
    2. It is evaluated every year and a new plan is made for the next year
    3. It consists only of three annual plans without revision
    4. It is a one-time plan for heavy industries
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) It is evaluated every year and a new plan is made for the next year

    Explanation: A Rolling Plan is evaluated every year and replaced by a new plan for the next year; allocations and targets therefore change. The other options are incorrect because a 15-year horizon refers to the NITI Vision Document, three annual plans to the first holiday, and heavy-industry priority to the Second Plan. The Janata government rejected the Fifth Plan and introduced this plan.
