Question bank

Average MCQ – Part 4: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Find the average of the 6 terms 3, 6, 12, 24, 48 and 96.

    1. 32
    2. 30.5
    3. 63
    4. 31.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 31.5

    Explanation: Their sum is 3 × (2^6 − 1) = 189. Average = 189 ÷ 6 = 31.5.

  2. A training centre has 80 candidates. Successful candidates average 76 marks, unsuccessful candidates average 44 marks, and all candidates average 68. How many candidates were successful?

    1. 20
    2. 60
    3. 50
    4. 64
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 60

    Explanation: Successful fraction = (68 − 44) ÷ (76 − 44) = 3/4. Successful count = 80 × 3/4 = 60.

  3. A group averages 45 kg. 3 members averaging 41 kg leave and 5 averaging 57 kg join. The new average is 48 kg. Find the original number of members.

    1. 22
    2. 24
    3. 20
    4. 26
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 22

    Explanation: For original count n, 45n − 3 × 41 + 5 × 57 = 48(n + 2). Thus 3n = 66 and n = 22.

  4. 9 numbers average 32. The first 4 total 116 and the last 4 total 134. Find the middle number.

    1. 38
    2. 36
    3. 40
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 38

    Explanation: Middle number = 9 × 32 − 116 − 134 = 38.

  5. The average of the first n positive odd integers is 37. Find n.

    1. 36
    2. 38
    3. 74
    4. 37
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 37

    Explanation: The average of the first n positive odd integers is n. Hence n = 37.

  6. A class has 24 pupils averaging 13 years. Their teacher is included, and after 2 years the same 25 people average 16 years. Find the teacher’s present age in years.

    1. 36
    2. 40
    3. 38
    4. 35
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 38

    Explanation: Today’s combined average = 16 − 2 = 14. Teacher’s present age = 25 × 14 − 24 × 13 = 38 years.

  7. Equal amounts of money are spent on tea priced at ₹240 and ₹360 per kg. Find the average purchase price per kg in ₹.

    1. 300
    2. 280
    3. 312
    4. 288
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 288

    Explanation: For equal expenditure, average price = 2 × 240 × 360 ÷ (240 + 360) = ₹288 per kg.

  8. 3 groups contain 10, 15 and 25 workers with average outputs of 24, 28 and 32 units. Find the average output per worker.

    1. 29.2
    2. 28
    3. 30
    4. 28.8
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 29.2

    Explanation: Average = (10 × 24 + 15 × 28 + 25 × 32) ÷ 50 = 29.2 units.

  9. 11 readings average 40. The first 5 average 36 and the last 5 average 43. If the 6th reading is increased by 21, what will be the average of the first 6 readings?

    1. 41
    2. 40
    3. 39
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 41

    Explanation: 6th reading = 440 − 180 − 215 = 45. New first-6 average = (180 + 45 + 21) ÷ 6 = 41.

  10. The average of 5 numbers is 62. 4 have deviations 8, −5, 3 and −10 from 62. Find the 5th number.

    1. 58
    2. 62
    3. 70
    4. 66
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 66

    Explanation: Deviations must sum to 0, so the 5th deviation is 4. 5th number = 62 + 4 = 66.

  11. A team of 25 averages 64 points. 5 players leave, and the remaining average rises to 67. Find the average points of those who left.

    1. 49
    2. 55
    3. 61
    4. 52
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 52

    Explanation: Departing average = 64 − 20 × (67 − 64) ÷ 5 = 52.

  12. A cyclist covers equal distances at 18 and 30 km/h. How many minutes are needed to cover 90 km at the resulting average speed?

    1. 225
    2. 240
    3. 200
    4. 270
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 240

    Explanation: Average speed = 2 × 18 × 30 ÷ 48 = 22.5 km/h. Time = 90 ÷ 22.5 × 60 = 240 minutes.

  13. 30 measurements average 48. entries were recorded as 83 and 26 instead of 38 and 62. Find the corrected average.

    1. 47.7
    2. 48.3
    3. 46.5
    4. 49.2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 47.7

    Explanation: Total correction = 38 + 62 − 83 − 26 = −9. Correct average = 48 − 9 ÷ 30 = 47.7.

  14. Find the average of all 2-digit positive integers divisible by 13.

    1. 45.5
    2. 52
    3. 58.5
    4. 50
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 52

    Explanation: The sequence runs from 13 to 91. Average = (13 + 91) ÷ 2 = 52.

  15. A club has 14 members averaging 40 years. How many members averaging 28 years must join to reduce the average to 36 years?

    1. 6
    2. 7
    3. 8
    4. 14
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 7

    Explanation: Existing excess = 14 × (40 − 36) = 56 years. Each newcomer offsets 36 − 28 = 8 years, so 56 ÷ 8 = 7 join.

  16. 40 players average 60 kg. 4 players averaging 52 kg are replaced, raising the group average to 61.2 kg. The new players’ weights are in ratio 1 : 2 : 2 : 3. Find the heaviest new player’s weight in kg.

    1. 90
    2. 84
    3. 104
    4. 96
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 96

    Explanation: New players total = 4 × 52 + 40 × 1.2 = 256 kg. Heaviest = 256 × 3 ÷ 8 = 96 kg.

  17. A batter averages 38 runs per innings in 15 innings. In his next 2 innings he scores 54 and 73. Find his new average runs per innings.

    1. 42
    2. 41
    3. 40
    4. 43
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 41

    Explanation: Extra above the old base = 54 + 73 − 2 × 38 = 51. New average = 38 + 51 ÷ 17 = 41.

  18. Permanent workers average ₹900 per day and temporary workers ₹600. The combined average is ₹780. What percentage of workers are permanent?

    1. 40
    2. 50
    3. 60
    4. 70
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 60

    Explanation: Permanent fraction = (780 − 600) ÷ (900 − 600) = 3/5. Percentage = 60%.

  19. A bowler concedes 52 runs in 6 overs and 3 legal balls. Find his average runs conceded per over, taking 6 legal balls per over.

    1. 8.25
    2. 8.5
    3. 8
    4. 7.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 8

    Explanation: 6 overs and 3 balls = 6.5 overs, not 6.3. Runs per over = 52 ÷ 6.5 = 8.

  20. 11 friends share a meal. 7 pay ₹180 each, 3 pay ₹240 each, and the organiser pays ₹120 more than the average payment of all 11. Find the organiser’s payment in ₹.

    1. 318
    2. 330
    3. 300
    4. 342
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 330

    Explanation: If the average is x, then 7 × 180 + 3 × 240 + x + 120 = 11x, giving x = 210. Organiser pays 210 + 120 = ₹330.

  21. A candidate averages 64 marks in 3 equally weighted papers. The fourth paper carries double the weight of each earlier paper. What mark in it gives a final weighted average of 70?

    1. 76
    2. 82
    3. 79
    4. 88
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 79

    Explanation: Total weight = 3 + 2 = 5. Required mark = (5 × 70 − 3 × 64) ÷ 2 = 79.

  22. A worker earns ₹700 per day for 6 days. Find his average daily income over the full 7-day week if he earns 0 on the 7th day.

    1. 700
    2. 650
    3. 580
    4. 600
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 600

    Explanation: Weekly income = 6 × 700 = ₹4200. Daily average = 4200 ÷ 7 = ₹600.

  23. A delivery van covers 150 km in 3 hours and 90 km in 2 hours. Its overall average including 1 halt is 40 km/h. Find the halt duration in minutes.

    1. 60
    2. 48
    3. 45
    4. 72
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 60

    Explanation: Total time = 240 ÷ 40 = 6 hours; moving time = 3 + 2 = 5 hours. Halt = (6 − 5) × 60 = 60 minutes.

  24. Is the average of 4 real numbers greater than 25? I: Each of the 3 smallest numbers exceeds 20, and the largest exceeds 40. II: All 4 numbers are positive. Choose the code: 1 = I alone is sufficient; 2 = II alone is sufficient; 3 = both together are needed and sufficient; 4 = both together are insufficient.

    1. 2
    2. 3
    3. 1
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1

    Explanation: I forces the total above 3 × 20 + 40 = 100, hence the mean exceeds 25. II only forces the mean above 0, so I alone is sufficient.

  25. A father and daughter average 34 years, and their ages are in ratio 13 : 4. Find the daughter’s age after 5 years, in years.

    1. 16
    2. 20
    3. 21
    4. 25
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 21

    Explanation: Daughter’s present age = 2 × 34 × 4 ÷ 17 = 16. After 5 years, she will be 21.
