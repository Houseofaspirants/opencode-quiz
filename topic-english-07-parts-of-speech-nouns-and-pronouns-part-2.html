Question bank

07 Parts Of Speech Nouns And Pronouns Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Complete the sentence: The books belong to Meena; please return ___ to ___.

    1. they; her
    2. them; she
    3. them; her
    4. their; hers
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) them; her

    Explanation: Them is the object of return, and her is the object of to. She is a subject pronoun and cannot follow to here.

  2. Complete the sentence: This umbrella belongs to us; it is ___.

    1. our
    2. ours
    3. ourselves
    4. us
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) ours

    Explanation: Ours is an independent possessive pronoun. Our must precede a noun.

  3. Choose the correct replacement for 'two luggages': The passenger carried two luggages.

    1. two luggage
    2. two piece of luggage
    3. two luggage pieces of
    4. two pieces of luggage
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) two pieces of luggage

    Explanation: Luggage is uncountable and can be counted as pieces of luggage. Two luggages incorrectly treats it as an ordinary count noun.

  4. In 'We ourselves arranged the exhibition', what function does 'ourselves' perform?

    1. Possessive reference
    2. Reciprocal reference
    3. Relative reference
    4. Emphatic reference
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Emphatic reference

    Explanation: Ourselves emphasises that we personally arranged the exhibition. It is not reciprocal because it does not express an action exchanged among people.

  5. Complete the sentence: The receipt ___ you requested is attached.

    1. which
    2. who
    3. whom
    4. whose
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) which

    Explanation: Which refers to the inanimate noun receipt. Whom refers to a person and cannot refer to this document.

  6. Choose the sentence in which 'paper' is a countable noun.

    1. The editor rejected three papers.
    2. The parcel was wrapped in paper.
    3. The mill manufactures paper.
    4. The drawer was lined with paper.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) The editor rejected three papers.

    Explanation: Papers here means separate written articles and can be counted. Paper used as wrapping material is a mass noun.

  7. Choose the correct completion: The association represents twenty teachers. This is the ___ annual meeting.

    1. teacher's
    2. teachers'
    3. teachers's
    4. teachers
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) teachers'

    Explanation: A regular plural ending in s takes an apostrophe after s to show possession. Teacher's would indicate one teacher.

  8. The noun 'copper' in 'The cable contains copper' is a:

    1. proper noun
    2. collective noun
    3. material noun
    4. abstract noun
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) material noun

    Explanation: Copper names a substance and is a material noun in this sentence. A collective noun would name a group.

  9. Complete the sentence: The manager asked Ritu and ___ to verify the vouchers.

    1. I
    2. mine
    3. myself
    4. me
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) me

    Explanation: The pronoun is part of the object of asked, so me is required. The presence of Ritu does not make I an object form.

  10. Complete the sentence: Of the two sealed envelopes, you may select either; ___ contains the same instructions.

    1. it
    2. they
    3. them
    4. theirs
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) it

    Explanation: Either refers to one of the two envelopes, so the singular pronoun it follows. They would refer to both envelopes together.

  11. Complete the sentence in formal English: The scholarship should go to ___ ranks first in the final assessment.

    1. whomever
    2. whom
    3. whoever
    4. whose
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) whoever

    Explanation: Whoever is the subject of ranks within the clause that follows to. The preposition governs the entire clause and does not require whomever inside it.

  12. Complete the sentence: The applicant ___ the panel considered unsuitable requested written reasons.

    1. who
    2. whose
    3. whom
    4. whoever
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) whom

    Explanation: Whom is the object of considered: the panel considered the applicant unsuitable. Who would be appropriate if the relative pronoun were the subject of a verb.

  13. Choose the correct completion using a possessive before the gerund: The supervisor objected to ___ leaving before the stock count ended.

    1. we
    2. our
    3. ours
    4. ourselves
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) our

    Explanation: A possessive determiner can mark the understood subject of a gerund, giving our leaving. Ours is an independent possessive and cannot directly modify leaving in this construction.

  14. Complete the sentence: The association invited its male former students, traditionally called ___, to the reunion.

    1. alumnus
    2. alumni
    3. alumna
    4. alumnuses
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) alumni

    Explanation: Alumni is the traditional plural for male former students. Alumnus refers to one male former student.

  15. Complete the sentence: The mechanic injured ___ while repairing the engine.

    1. his
    2. he
    3. himself
    4. him
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) himself

    Explanation: Himself refers the action back to the subject, the mechanic. Him would ordinarily refer to another male person.

  16. Choose the correct completion: Simran owns one clinic and Navdeep owns another. ___ clinics have different registration numbers.

    1. Simran and Navdeep's
    2. Simrans' and Navdeeps'
    3. Simran's and Navdeep
    4. Simran's and Navdeep's
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Simran's and Navdeep's

    Explanation: Separate ownership requires a possessive ending on each owner's name. Simran and Navdeep's would ordinarily indicate joint ownership.

  17. Complete the sentence using the plural scientific treatment of 'data': The data collected from the sensors ___ consistent with the model.

    1. are
    2. is
    3. has been
    4. was
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) are

    Explanation: Under the specified plural scientific treatment, data takes a plural verb, are. Is follows the mass-noun treatment, which the question expressly excludes.

  18. Choose the correct completion: The contractor must give ___ notice before cancelling the agreement; the notice period is exactly two weeks.

    1. two week's
    2. two weeks's
    3. two weeks
    4. two weeks'
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) two weeks'

    Explanation: A plural time expression used possessively takes an apostrophe after its plural s: two weeks' notice. Two week's incorrectly uses a singular possessive after two.

  19. Complete the sentence: Let ___ inspect the damaged bridge before traffic resumes.

    1. they and we
    2. them and we
    3. they and us
    4. them and us
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) them and us

    Explanation: Both pronouns function as objects of let and therefore take the objective forms them and us. Them and we incorrectly retains one subject form.

  20. Complete the sentence: The estate belonged to a duke, and after his death his wife, the ___, managed it.

    1. dukeness
    2. dukeress
    3. duchess
    4. duchessess
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) duchess

    Explanation: Duchess is the conventional feminine counterpart of duke. Dukeness is not a standard English title.

  21. Complete the sentence: The nurse examined the child's ___.

    1. tooths
    2. toothes
    3. tooth's
    4. teeth
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) teeth

    Explanation: Teeth is the irregular plural of tooth. Tooths is not a standard plural.

  22. Choose the correct completion: These spectacles belong to the librarian; they are ___.

    1. her's
    2. hers
    3. hers'
    4. her
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) hers

    Explanation: The possessive pronoun hers takes no apostrophe. Her's incorrectly applies noun possessive punctuation to a pronoun.

  23. Complete the sentence: Both ___ submitted their reports to the governor.

    1. attorneys general
    2. attorney generals
    3. attorneys generals
    4. attorney's general
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) attorneys general

    Explanation: The standard plural of attorney general is attorneys general, with the noun attorney pluralised. General functions as a modifier in this title.

  24. Choose the correct completion: The spokesperson's statement, ___ surprised the reporters, was later withdrawn.

    1. that
    2. which
    3. what
    4. whom
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) which

    Explanation: Which introduces this non-defining relative clause about the statement. That does not introduce a non-defining clause set off by commas in standard written English.

  25. Complete the sentence: The village elders listened patiently to one ___ concerns.

    1. another's
    2. anothers
    3. anothers'
    4. another
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) another's

    Explanation: One another forms its possessive as one another's. Anothers' incorrectly adds a plural ending before the apostrophe.
