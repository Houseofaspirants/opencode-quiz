Modern History gets a lot of questions in Punjab papers, and for Punjab-specific history, the **Anglo-Sikh Wars** are the most asked chapter. Every question is a date, a battle, a treaty or a name, so this is a topic you can master fully with three revisions. I have arranged it in the order the questions come: background, first war, treaties, second war, annexation.

## What this topic covers

### Background: why the wars happened

- **Treaty of Amritsar, 1809:** Maharaja Ranjit Singh and the British (through **Charles Metcalfe**) fixed the **Sutlej** as the boundary. Ranjit Singh stayed north of it; the British protected the cis-Sutlej states.
- **Ranjit Singh died on 27 June 1839.** After him the Lahore Darbar fell into chaos. In six years four rulers came and went: **Kharak Singh, Nau Nihal Singh, Sher Singh** and finally the child **Duleep Singh**, with his mother **Rani Jind Kaur (Jindan)** as regent.
- The **Khalsa army** became the real power. The British moved troops close to the Sutlej. In **December 1845** the Sikh army crossed the Sutlej and war was declared.

### First Anglo-Sikh War (1845-46)

- **Governor-General:** Lord **Hardinge**. **Commander-in-chief:** Sir **Hugh Gough**.
- The Sikh commanders, **Lal Singh** (wazir) and **Tej Singh** (commander), are remembered for betraying their own army.

| Battle | Date | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mudki | 18 December 1845 | British win |
| Ferozeshah | 21-22 December 1845 | British win, very costly |
| Baddowal | 21 January 1846 | Sikh win (Ranjodh Singh Majithia) |
| Aliwal | 28 January 1846 | British win |
| Sobraon | 10 February 1846 | Decisive British win |

At **Sobraon**, **Sardar Sham Singh Attariwala** fought to death. He is the hero of this war in every Punjab textbook, and his name is a common answer.

### The three treaties of 1846

1. **Treaty of Lahore, 9 March 1846:**
   - The **Jalandhar Doab** (between the Beas and the Sutlej) went to the British.
   - A war indemnity of **₹1.5 crore** was imposed. The Darbar could pay only part of it.
   - The Sikh army was cut down, and a British Resident was posted at Lahore.
2. **Treaty of Amritsar, 16 March 1846:** the British sold **Kashmir** to **Gulab Singh** of Jammu for **₹75 lakh**. Don't confuse this with the 1809 Treaty of Amritsar.
3. **Treaty of Bhairowal, 16 December 1846:**
   - Rani Jindan was removed from power and given a pension.
   - A **Council of Regency** under the British Resident, **Henry Lawrence**, ran Punjab until Duleep Singh came of age.
   - From here, Punjab was effectively under British control.

### Second Anglo-Sikh War (1848-49)

- **Governor-General:** Lord **Dalhousie**.
- **Spark:** the revolt of **Diwan Mulraj** at **Multan** in **April 1848**. Two British officers, **Vans Agnew** and **Anderson**, were killed there.
- The revolt spread. **Sher Singh Attariwala** and his father **Chattar Singh Attariwala** led the Sikh side.

| Battle | Date | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ramnagar | 22 November 1848 | Indecisive |
| Chillianwala | 13 January 1849 | Indecisive, heavy British losses |
| Gujrat | 21 February 1849 | Decisive British win ("Battle of the Guns") |

### Annexation

- On **29 March 1849**, Lord **Dalhousie** annexed Punjab.
- **Duleep Singh** was given a pension and sent away, first to **Fatehgarh**, and later to **England**.
- The **Koh-i-Noor** was taken and presented to **Queen Victoria**.
- Punjab was run by a **Board of Administration**: **Henry Lawrence** (president), **John Lawrence** and **Charles Mansel**.

## How it is asked in the exam

- **Match the battle to the year or the war:** "Chillianwala was fought in which war?" (Second).
- **Last battle / decisive battle:** Sobraon (first war), Gujrat (second war).
- **Governor-General:** Hardinge (first), Dalhousie (second and annexation).
- **Treaty details:** which treaty gave away the Jalandhar Doab (Lahore), which one sold Kashmir (Amritsar 1846), which one set up the Council of Regency (Bhairowal).
- **People:** Sham Singh Attariwala (Sobraon), Mulraj (Multan), Rani Jindan, Duleep Singh (last Maharaja).

## Points to remember

- Two **Treaties of Amritsar**: **1809** (Ranjit Singh and Metcalfe, Sutlej boundary) and **1846** (Kashmir sold to Gulab Singh).
- Baddowal was the **only** Sikh victory of the first war.
- **Gujrat** here is the town in Punjab (now in Pakistan), not the Indian state Gujarat. Paper setters use this confusion.
- Duleep Singh was the **last Maharaja** of the Sikh Empire.

## Common mistakes

- Giving **Dalhousie** for the first war. It was **Hardinge**.
- Putting **Chillianwala** in the first war because it sounds like Ferozeshah.
- Mixing up **Bhairowal** (December 1846, regency council) with **Lahore** (March 1846, Jalandhar Doab).
