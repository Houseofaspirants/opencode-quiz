Culture questions look easy but cost a lot of marks, because the options are all real fairs and real dances. The paper only checks whether you can match the right pair. So I study this topic as **pairs**, and I revise the pairs, not paragraphs.

## What this topic covers

### Fairs and where they are held

| Fair | Place | When / about |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hola Mohalla** | Sri Anandpur Sahib | Day after Holi; started by Guru Gobind Singh Ji; martial skills of the Nihangs |
| **Maghi Mela** | Sri Muktsar Sahib | January (Maghi); remembers the **Chali Mukte** (forty liberated ones) |
| **Shaheedi Jor Mela** | Sri Fatehgarh Sahib | December; martyrdom of the younger Sahibzadas |
| **Baisakhi Mela** | Talwandi Sabo (Damdama Sahib), Anandpur Sahib and across Punjab | 13 or 14 April; harvest and the founding of the Khalsa in 1699 |
| **Chhapar Mela** | Chhapar village, Ludhiana | September; in the memory of **Gugga Pir** |
| **Harballabh Sangeet Sammelan** | Devi Talab Mandir, Jalandhar | December; one of India's oldest classical music festivals, since 1875 |
| **Kila Raipur Sports Festival** | Kila Raipur, Ludhiana | February; the "Rural Olympics" |
| **Baba Sheikh Farid Aagman Purb** | Faridkot | September; the arrival of Baba Farid |

### Festivals

- **Lohri:** 13 January, the night before Maghi; bonfire, the story of **Dulla Bhatti**.
- **Baisakhi:** 13 or 14 April; harvest festival and **Khalsa founding day (1699)**.
- **Teeyan:** in Sawan (monsoon); women's festival of swings and Giddha.
- **Bandi Chhor Divas:** celebrated with Diwali; release of **Guru Hargobind Ji** from Gwalior Fort.

### Folk dances

| Performed by men | Performed by women |
| --- | --- |
| **Bhangra** (harvest, with dhol) | **Giddha** (with boliyan and clapping) |
| **Jhumar** (slow, graceful, from the Sandal Bar) | **Sammi** (from the Sandal Bar, now Pakistan) |
| **Luddi** (dance of victory) | **Kikli** (girls in pairs, holding crossed hands) |
| **Malwai Giddha** (men's Giddha of Malwa) | **Jaago** (wedding night, pot of lamps on the head) |

### Folk instruments

- **Dhol** (with Bhangra), **Tumbi** (one-string; made famous by **Lal Chand Yamla Jatt**), **Algoza** (twin flutes), **Chimta**, **Sarangi**, **Dhad** (small hourglass drum of the dhadi singers), **Bugdu** and **Kato**.

### Crafts and dress

- **Phulkari:** embroidery with silk thread; a fully covered piece is called a **Bagh**. Phulkari has a **GI tag**.
- **Punjabi Jutti**, **Paranda** (hair tassel) and the **Pagg** (turban).

### The five Takhts

1. **Sri Akal Takht Sahib**, Amritsar (established by Guru Hargobind Ji in 1606)
2. **Takht Sri Kesgarh Sahib**, Anandpur Sahib (birthplace of the Khalsa)
3. **Takht Sri Damdama Sahib**, Talwandi Sabo, Bathinda
4. **Takht Sri Patna Sahib**, Bihar (birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh Ji)
5. **Takht Sri Hazur Sahib**, Nanded, Maharashtra

Three Takhts are in Punjab; two are outside.

## How it is asked in the exam

- "Maghi Mela is held at?" (Sri Muktsar Sahib.)
- "Which of these is a women's folk dance?" (Giddha / Sammi / Kikli.)
- "Lal Chand Yamla Jatt is associated with?" (Tumbi.)
- "Harballabh music festival is held in?" (Jalandhar.)
- "Which Takht is in Bathinda district?" (Damdama Sahib.)

## Points to remember

- **Jhumar and Sammi** both come from the **Sandal Bar** region (now in Pakistan). Jhumar is men's, Sammi is women's.
- **Malwai Giddha** is danced by **men**, even though Giddha is a women's dance. This is a favourite trap.
- **Dulla Bhatti** is linked with **Lohri**; **Gugga Pir** with **Chhapar Mela**.

## Common mistakes

- Placing Hola Mohalla at Amritsar. It is **Anandpur Sahib**.
- Mixing Maghi (Muktsar) with Jor Mela (Fatehgarh Sahib).
- Counting all five Takhts in Punjab.

## Practise straight after reading

Make flash cards with one side "fair / dance / instrument" and the other side "place / who performs / artist". Shuffle and test yourself tomorrow, after 7 days and after 30 days.
