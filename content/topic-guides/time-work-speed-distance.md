Most doubts in my live sessions are about Maths, and these two chapters come up again and again. Both look formula-heavy, but in the exam you need only a few ideas. Learn the method once, then practise each type until you stop thinking about the method.

## What this topic covers

### Time and Work: the LCM method

Take the **total work = LCM** of the days given. Then each person's work per day is a whole number.

- **A does a job in 10 days, B in 15 days. Together?**
  Total work = LCM(10, 15) = 30 units. A = 3 units/day, B = 2 units/day. Together 5 units/day → 30 ÷ 5 = **6 days**.
- **A and B together take 12 days; A alone takes 20. B alone?**
  Total = LCM(12, 20) = 60. (A + B) = 5/day, A = 3/day, so B = 2/day → 60 ÷ 2 = **30 days**.

### Pipes and cisterns

The same method. A pipe that **empties** does negative work.

- **Pipe A fills a tank in 20 hours, pipe B empties it in 30 hours. Both open?**
  Total = 60. A = +3/hour, B = −2/hour. Net +1/hour → **60 hours**.

### Wages

Wages are shared **in the ratio of work done**, not in the ratio of days. In the first example, A and B share wages 3 : 2.

### Speed, Time and Distance

- **Distance = Speed × Time.**
- **km/h → m/s: × 5/18.** **m/s → km/h: × 18/5.** (72 km/h = 20 m/s.)
- **Average speed for equal distances** at x and y: **2xy/(x + y)**.

### Trains

- Crossing a **pole or a person:** distance = **length of the train**.
- Crossing a **platform or bridge:** distance = **train + platform**.
- **Two trains:** relative speed = **sum** if moving in **opposite** directions, **difference** if in the **same** direction; distance = **sum of both lengths**.

### Boats and streams

- Downstream speed = **u + v**; upstream = **u − v** (u = boat in still water, v = stream).
- **Still water speed = (down + up) / 2**; **stream speed = (down − up) / 2**.

## How it is asked in the exam

### Example 1: train and pole, then platform

*A 200 m train runs at 72 km/h. How long does it take to cross a pole, and a 300 m platform?*

72 km/h = 20 m/s. Pole: 200 ÷ 20 = **10 s**. Platform: (200 + 300) ÷ 20 = **25 s**.

### Example 2: two trains, opposite directions

*Trains of 150 m and 100 m run towards each other at 50 km/h and 40 km/h. How long do they take to cross each other?*

Relative speed = 90 km/h = 25 m/s. Distance = 250 m. Time = **10 s**.

### Example 3: average speed

*A man drives to a town at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. What is his average speed?*

2 × 40 × 60 ÷ 100 = **48 km/h**.

### Example 4: boats

*A boat goes 15 km/h downstream and 9 km/h upstream. Find the speed in still water and of the stream.*

Still water = (15 + 9) ÷ 2 = **12 km/h**. Stream = (15 − 9) ÷ 2 = **3 km/h**.

## Points to remember

- **Average speed is never the simple average** when distances are equal.
- **Always convert units first.** Train lengths are in metres, speeds usually in km/h.
- In time and work, **efficiency and time are inversely proportional**: if A is twice as fast as B, A takes half the time.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting to add the **platform** length.
- Adding speeds for trains moving in the **same** direction.
- Splitting wages by days worked instead of work done.
