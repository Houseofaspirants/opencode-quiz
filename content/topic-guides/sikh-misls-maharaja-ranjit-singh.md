This chapter connects to the Anglo-Sikh Wars: one is the rise of the Sikh state, the other is its fall. I study them together, one after the other, so that the dates form one long timeline in my head instead of two separate lists.

## What this topic covers

### The Misl period (18th century)

- After Banda Singh Bahadur's execution (1716), the Sikhs fought in small bands called **jathas**.
- **Dal Khalsa** was formed in **1748** at Amritsar under **Nawab Kapur Singh**, with **Jassa Singh Ahluwalia** as commander. The jathas were grouped into **twelve Misls**.
- **Sarbat Khalsa:** the general assembly of the Sikhs, held at Amritsar, usually on Baisakhi and Diwali. Its decisions were called **Gurmata**.
- **Rakhi system:** villages paid a Misl for protection, about one-fifth of their produce, twice a year.

### The twelve Misls

| Misl | Remember it by |
| --- | --- |
| **Sukerchakia** | Charat Singh, Mahan Singh, **Ranjit Singh** |
| **Ahluwalia** | **Jassa Singh Ahluwalia**; later Kapurthala state |
| **Ramgarhia** | **Jassa Singh Ramgarhia** |
| **Bhangi** | Held **Lahore and Amritsar** before Ranjit Singh |
| **Kanhaiya** | Jai Singh; **Sada Kaur**, Ranjit Singh's mother-in-law |
| **Phulkian** | Patiala, Nabha, Jind; **Baba Ala Singh** founded Patiala |
| **Nakai** | Ranjit Singh's wife Datar Kaur came from this Misl |
| **Dallewalia** | Tara Singh Gheba |
| **Faizullapuria (Singhpuria)** | Nawab Kapur Singh |
| **Karorsinghia** | Karora Singh |
| **Shaheedan** | Baba Deep Singh |
| **Nishanwalia** | Carried the Dal Khalsa's flag (nishan) |

The **Phulkian Misl** stayed outside the Dal Khalsa's core in the Malwa region, and its states later sought British protection.

### Two Ghallugharas (holocausts)

- **Chhota Ghallughara, 1746:** under Lakhpat Rai, diwan of Lahore.
- **Vadda Ghallughara, 1762:** at **Kup** (near Malerkotla), by **Ahmad Shah Abdali**.

### Maharaja Ranjit Singh (1780-1839)

- **Born:** 13 November 1780, **Gujranwala** (now Pakistan). He lost the sight of one eye to smallpox as a child.
- **Lahore:** captured on **7 July 1799**.
- **Maharaja:** proclaimed on Baisakhi, **12 April 1801**.
- **Amritsar:** taken from the Bhangis in **1805**.
- **Treaty of Amritsar, 1809:** with the British (Charles Metcalfe); the **Sutlej** became the boundary.
- **Koh-i-Noor:** obtained from **Shah Shuja** in **1813**.
- **Multan 1818, Kashmir 1819, Peshawar 1834.**
- **Tripartite Treaty, 1838:** Ranjit Singh, the British and Shah Shuja.
- **Died:** **27 June 1839** at Lahore.

### Army and court

- **Fauj-i-Khas:** the model brigade trained by European officers **Ventura** and **Allard**; also **Court** and **Avitabile**.
- **Hari Singh Nalwa:** his greatest general; governor of Kashmir and Peshawar; died at **Jamrud** in **1837**.
- **Fakir Azizuddin:** foreign minister and adviser.
- **Dhian Singh Dogra:** prime minister (wazir).
- He covered the Harmandir Sahib's upper floors with gilded copper, which gave it the name **Golden Temple**.
- His title: **Sher-e-Punjab** (Lion of Punjab). His coins were called **Nanakshahi**.

## How it is asked in the exam

- "Ranjit Singh belonged to which Misl?" (Sukerchakia.)
- "Who founded the Dal Khalsa?" (Nawab Kapur Singh, 1748.)
- "Patiala state was founded by?" (Baba Ala Singh, Phulkian Misl.)
- "Which European generals trained the Fauj-i-Khas?" (Ventura and Allard.)
- "Hari Singh Nalwa died in which battle?" (Jamrud, 1837.)
- "Lahore was captured in?" (1799.)

## Points to remember

- **1799 Lahore, 1801 Maharaja, 1809 Treaty, 1839 death.** Write these four dates on your wall.
- The **Bhangi Misl** held both Lahore and Amritsar; Ranjit Singh took both from it.
- Vadda Ghallughara (1762) was by **Abdali**, not by a Mughal governor.

## Common mistakes

- Giving **1801** as the year Lahore was captured. Lahore was **1799**; the title was **1801**.
- Making Jassa Singh **Ramgarhia** the Dal Khalsa commander. It was Jassa Singh **Ahluwalia**.
- Confusing the **1809** Treaty of Amritsar (Ranjit Singh) with the **1846** one (Kashmir to Gulab Singh).
