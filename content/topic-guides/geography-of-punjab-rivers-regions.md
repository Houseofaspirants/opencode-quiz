Punjab GK was the part of my preparation with no single good book. I made my own notes from many sources, and geography is the easiest place to start, because it barely changes. Learn it once with a map in front of you, revise it with 1-7-30, and these questions become fixed marks.

## What this topic covers

### Basic facts

- **Area:** 50,362 sq km, about **1.53%** of India's area.
- **Districts:** **23**. Malerkotla became the 23rd district in **2021**.
- **Capital:** **Chandigarh**, a Union Territory and also the capital of Haryana.
- **The name:** Punjab comes from Persian, *panj* (five) + *ab* (water): the land of five rivers. Before 1947 those were the Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas and Sutlej.

### Neighbours

- **West:** Pakistan (international border).
- **North:** Jammu and Kashmir.
- **North-east:** Himachal Pradesh.
- **South and south-east:** Haryana.
- **South-west:** Rajasthan.

Six districts touch the Pakistan border: **Pathankot, Gurdaspur, Amritsar, Tarn Taran, Ferozepur and Fazilka.**

### The three rivers of present-day Punjab

| River | Key points |
| --- | --- |
| **Sutlej** | Rises in Tibet near Lake Rakas Tal. **Bhakra Dam** (Bilaspur, Himachal) and **Nangal Dam** (Rupnagar, Punjab) are on it. Longest river of Punjab. |
| **Beas** | Rises at **Beas Kund** near Rohtang Pass. **Pong Dam** (Himachal) is on it. It flows only in India and joins the Sutlej at **Harike**. |
| **Ravi** | Rises in Himachal. **Ranjit Sagar (Thein) Dam** near Pathankot and the **Shahpur Kandi** project are on it. Forms part of the border with Pakistan. |

The **Ghaggar** is a seasonal river in the south-east (Patiala and Sangrur side).

### The five historic doabs

A doab is the land between two rivers. Paper setters love these names:

| Doab | Between |
| --- | --- |
| **Bist (Jalandhar) Doab** | Beas and Sutlej |
| **Bari Doab** | Beas and Ravi |
| **Rachna Doab** | Ravi and Chenab |
| **Chaj Doab** | Chenab and Jhelum |
| **Sind Sagar Doab** | Jhelum and Indus |

Memory trick: the names are made from the rivers' first letters. **Bist** = BeasSutlej, **Bari** = BeasRavi, **Rachna** = RaviChenab, **Chaj** = ChenabJhelum.

### Majha, Doaba and Malwa

- **Majha:** north-west of the Beas. Amritsar, Tarn Taran, Gurdaspur and Pathankot.
- **Doaba:** between the Beas and Sutlej. Jalandhar, Kapurthala, Hoshiarpur and Shaheed Bhagat Singh Nagar.
- **Malwa:** south of the Sutlej. The largest region, with most of the districts, including Ludhiana, Patiala, Bathinda and Sangrur.

### Wetlands (Ramsar sites)

Punjab's Ramsar sites include **Harike** (the largest wetland of northern India, where the Beas meets the Sutlej), **Kanjli** (Kapurthala), **Ropar**, **Nangal**, **Keshopur-Miani** (Gurdaspur) and the **Beas Conservation Reserve**. Harike was the first, in **1990**.

## How it is asked in the exam

- "Which river does not flow through present-day Punjab?" (Options include Chenab or Jhelum.)
- "Harike wetland is at the confluence of?" (Beas and Sutlej.)
- "Bist Doab lies between?" (Beas and Sutlej.)
- "Which district is newest?" (Malerkotla.)
- "Which state does not share a border with Punjab?" (Options include Uttarakhand or Delhi.)

## Points to remember

- **Bhakra Dam is in Himachal Pradesh**, not Punjab. Nangal Dam is in Punjab.
- **Beas** is the only one of the three that flows entirely in India.
- Bist Doab = Jalandhar Doab, the land Punjab lost to the British in 1846.

## Common mistakes

- Placing Bhakra in Punjab.
- Swapping Majha and Doaba. Remember: **Do-aba = two waters**, so it is the land between two rivers, the Beas and Sutlej.
- Counting 22 districts from an old book. It is **23**.
