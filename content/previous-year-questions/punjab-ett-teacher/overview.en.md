## What is on this page

Every Punjab ETT Teacher paper we have, as released by the Punjab School Education Department and its recruitment bodies. Each row is one year: the question paper, and the official answer key beside it where one was released. Where no key was released, the cell says so.

## Papers in this folder

- 2023: Question papers (Paper 1, Punjabi, 5 Mar / Paper 2, 5 Mar); official answer keys (Paper 1, Final / Paper 1, Provisional / Paper 2, Final / Paper 2, Provisional)

## How to use these papers

1. Solve the first paper without a clock, to see which topics are really asked.
2. Attempt the next one strictly timed, and check it only against the official key.
3. Note every mistake as a concept gap or a speed gap, and revise those topics with our free topic-wise quizzes.
