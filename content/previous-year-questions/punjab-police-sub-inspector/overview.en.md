## What is on this page

One row for every year with a paper in this folder: the question paper and the
official answer key beside it, each with a download. The files are the ones
the conducting body released. Where a key has not been published yet the cell
reads *Not published* - it is never filled in from memory, and a paper is
never held back while it waits for its key.

## About the exam

The papers below are the ones released for this post, so they are the only
honest description of what is actually asked - treat them as the syllabus's
proof rather than as a mock with a score attached. Read one untimed before
you plan your preparation, so that the syllabus you work through is the one
the paper tests. Attempt each one afterwards under a strict clock, and mark
it only against an official key: until a key appears in the table, the paper
is practice, not a score.
