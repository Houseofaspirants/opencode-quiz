## What is on this page

One row for every year this exam has been held: the question paper and the
official answer key beside it, each with a download. The files are the ones
the conducting body released. Where a key has not been published yet the cell
reads *Not published* - it is never filled in from memory, and a paper is
never held back while it waits for its key.

## About the exam

Head Constable is the communication and clerical tier of Punjab Police.
Punjabi, English and general knowledge carry the paper, and computer
awareness is worth real marks. Attempt the first paper untimed, to learn the
shape of the thing; attempt the second strictly timed, to learn what the
clock will do to you on exam day. Separate every mistake into a concept gap
or a speed gap before you move on.
