## What is on this page

Every Punjab Police Constable question paper published here, one row per year,
with the official answer key beside it and a download for each file. The
answer-key cell reads *Not published* until the conducting body releases one -
it is never reconstructed from memory, and a paper appears as soon as it
exists rather than waiting for its key.

## About the exam

Constable is the highest-volume recruitment on the Punjab list, which is why
the papers below are worth more than another mock: they are the only record of
what the board actually asked. Build the base first - Punjab GK and current
affairs - then drill reasoning until speed is automatic. Attempt the first
paper after one full pass of the syllabus, untimed; the second strictly timed.
