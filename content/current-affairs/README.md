# content/current-affairs/

Current affairs articles — one development at a time, explained in Punjabi and
English. Documents in this folder are rendered to static pages by
`scripts/build_content.py` and published at `current-affairs.html` +
`ca-<slug>.html` (Punjabi edition under `/pa/`).

Schemas, fields and rules: see [`content/README.md`](../README.md).
