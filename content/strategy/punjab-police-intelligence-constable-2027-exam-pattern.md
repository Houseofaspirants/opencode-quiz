I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. The Intelligence and Investigation cadre exam is harder than the usual constable paper - more computer, more current affairs and a real maths section.

> The last round was **Punjab Police Advertisement No. 03 of 2021** (published **26 July 2021**, applications till **16 August 2021**) for **Intelligence Assistants** in the Intelligence Cadre and **Constables** in the Investigation Cadre. A **CBT was held on 7 September 2021**, and a later written exam on **14 October 2022** had its key modified after objections. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Punjab Police has been advertising its **district and armed cadre constable** posts almost every year, but the specialised Intelligence and Investigation cadres come **less often**. A 2027 round is **possible but not confirmed**. The good news: the syllabus overlaps heavily with the regular constable exam, so your preparation is not wasted either way.

## Eligibility (Advt 03/2021)

- **Age:** 21 to 28 years as on 1 January 2021, with relaxation for reserved categories.
- **Education:** a graduation-level qualification as set out in the advertisement - check the exact degree list in the next notice.
- **Physical standards:** reported as 5 ft 5 in for men and 5 ft 1 in for women.

## Exam pattern

**CBT of 7 September 2021:** 150 objective questions, conducted online.

**2022 booklet (Series BA):**

| Section | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| General Awareness | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude and Numerical Skills | 30 |
| Punjabi Language | 20 |
| **Total** | **100 questions, 400 marks, 120 minutes** |

- **Marking:** +4 for a correct answer, **-1 for a wrong answer**.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers:** we have the [2021 CBT and 2022 papers with keys](pyq/punjab-police-constable-intelligence-investigation/index.html).
2. **Current affairs daily** - the 2021 paper asked about international alliances and recent schemes.
3. **Computer basics** - languages, terms and shortcuts.
4. **Maths 30 questions** - percentages, ratio, time-work and simple data questions.
5. **Punjabi grammar** - 20 questions that should be near-full marks.
