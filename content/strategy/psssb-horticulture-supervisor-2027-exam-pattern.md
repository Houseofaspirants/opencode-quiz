I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. This is a niche post with only a few seats, so the candidates who win it know their horticulture properly.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 05 of 2022** - **28 Supervisor (Horticulture) posts in PUDA**. The exam was on **30 October 2022** and the result on **22 November 2022**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Urban development authorities keep adding parks, green belts and nurseries, but the cadre is **small**. A 2027 round is **possible but not confirmed**.

## The 2022 recruitment in brief

- **Posts:** 28, in PUDA.
- **Application window:** 18 May to 18 June 2022.
- **Age:** 18-37 as on 1 January 2022.
- **Selection:** written exam, document checks and medical.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## Eligibility (2022 advertisement)

- **Education:** at least **10+2**, and a **certificate in gardening training in Horticulture or Agriculture** from a recognised university or institution.
- **Punjabi:** matriculation with Punjabi as a compulsory or elective subject (or an equivalent Punjabi examination).
- **Cut-off date:** every certificate had to be issued by the last date of applying, **18 June 2022**.
- **Age:** 18 to 37 years as on 1 January 2022 for General candidates, with the usual Punjab relaxations (SC/BC of Punjab up to 42, government employees up to 45, persons with disabilities up to 47).

The post was advertised at a **basic pay of Rs 25,500** (7th CPC pay matrix), plus allowances as the Punjab Government sanctions from time to time.

## Exam pattern (official plan of the written exam)

| Part | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part A - Horticulture and agriculture (subject) | 90 | 90 |
| Part B - GK, current affairs, reasoning and mental ability, English, Punjabi | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

- **Type:** MCQ on an OMR sheet, **2 hours**.
- **Negative marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer; unattempted questions carry no marks.
- **Qualifying:** at least **40%** marks in the written exam to be considered further; ties go to the older candidate.
- **Selection:** written exam, then counselling and document checks.

## Syllabus (official, Supervisor Horticulture)

**Part A - Subject (90 marks)**

- **Horticulture:** importance and scope; fruits and vegetables in nutrition; nursery production; site selection, layout, planting time and methods for orchards; training and pruning of fruit crops; sowing and planting time of vegetables, flowering annuals and flower crops; types of vegetable garden; seed production in vegetables; landscape use of ornamental plants; making and maintaining a lawn; formal and informal gardens; soil, climate, improved varieties, propagation, cultural practices, post-harvest management and plant protection of the important fruit, vegetable, flower and ornamental crops of **Punjab**.
- **Agriculture:** importance and branches; climate, soil and water in crop production; farm tools and implements; crop seasons; seed structure and germination; phases of plant growth; classification and status of the state's important crops; agronomic practices - seed bed, sowing, fertiliser, weed control, harvesting, threshing and marketing; land measurement; intercultural operations; identification of field crops and seeds; depth of seed placement; methods of irrigation; farm records; effect of weather and climate on crops.
- **Plant protection:** plant diseases of different crops; insect pests of field crops and their management.
