I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. This is a niche post with only a few seats, so the candidates who win it know their horticulture properly.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 05 of 2022** - **28 Supervisor (Horticulture) posts in PUDA**. The exam was on **30 October 2022** and the result on **22 November 2022**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Urban development authorities keep adding parks, green belts and nurseries, but the cadre is **small**. A 2027 round is **possible but not confirmed**.

## The 2022 recruitment in brief

- **Posts:** 28, in PUDA.
- **Application window:** 18 May to 18 June 2022.
- **Age:** 18-37 as on 1 January 2022.
- **Selection:** written exam, document checks and medical.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper:** [Horticulture Supervisor 2022 paper](pyq/psssb-horticulture-supervisor/index.html).
2. **Revise horticulture** - propagation, nursery, orchards, lawns, landscaping, plant protection.
3. **Punjab agriculture facts** - major fruits and districts.
4. **GK and reasoning** for the rest of Part B.
