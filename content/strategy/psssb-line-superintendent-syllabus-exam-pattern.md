I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Line Superintendent pays Rs 35,400 for a diploma - one of the best returns on a polytechnic course in PSSSB. The paper is mostly your diploma syllabus.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 08 of 2023** (published 8 September 2023, applications 8 to 29 September 2023, fee by 1 October 2023), with **6 Line Superintendent posts**.

## Latest notification (Advt 08/2023)

| Category | Posts | For women |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | 4 | 1 |
| SC (Mazhbi & Balmiki) | 1 | 1 |
| SC (Ramdasia & others) | 1 | - |
| **Total** | **6** | **2** |

- **Department:** Power, New and Renewable Energy Sources, Punjab.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC, BC and EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen and dependants Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.

## Eligibility

1. A **three-year diploma in Electrical Engineering** from a recognised institution.
2. **Punjabi up to matric**.

**Age:** 18 to 37 years for General; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab; up to 45 for government employees; up to 47 for persons with disabilities of Punjab; up to 40 for widows and divorcees.

## Salary and pay

**Rs 35,400** (7th CPC), as per Advt 08/2023.

## Exam pattern (Advt 08/2023 official plan)

| Part | What | Questions / marks | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Matric-level Punjabi | 50 | **Qualifying - 25 marks needed**, no negative marking |
| B | GK, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English, ICT | 40 | Counts for merit |
| C | Electrical engineering subject | 60 | Counts for merit |

- **Time:** 2 hours 30 minutes. **1/4 mark (0.25) cut** for each wrong answer in Parts B and C.

## Syllabus

**Part B (40):** GK and current affairs 10, Punjab history and culture 5, reasoning and numeracy 10, Punjabi 5, English 5, ICT 5.

**Part C (60) - electrical engineering:**

- **Basics and circuits** - resistance, inductance, capacitance; Kirchhoff's laws and network theorems; magnetic circuits and electromagnetic induction.
- **AC fundamentals** - RMS and average values, series and parallel RLC circuits, resonance, three-phase star and delta, transient response.
- **Measurement** - power and energy (two-wattmeter method), frequency and phase, ammeters, voltmeters, multimeter, megger, AC bridges, CRO, CT and PT, earth-fault detection.
- **Machines** - DC machines, single and three-phase transformers, three-phase induction motors, fractional-kW and single-phase motors, synchronous machines.
- **Generation, transmission and distribution** - power stations, load and demand factors, tariffs, power-factor improvement, faults, switchgear and protection (circuit breakers, HRC fuses, Buchholz relay, Merz-Price), lightning arresters, cables.
- **Estimation and costing, earthing and IE Rules; utilisation** - illumination, heating, welding, drives.
- **Electronics** - diodes, BJT and JFET, simple circuits, industrial and digital electronics.

## How to prepare

1. **Revise your diploma notes for machines and protection** - they carry most of Part C.
2. **Learn the IE Rules on earthing and clearances**; they suit a line supervisor's paper.
3. **Do not ignore the 40 general marks** - in a 6-post merit list, every mark counts.
