I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Excise and Taxation Inspector (ETI) is a well-paid graduate-level PSSSB post, so this page puts the official notification and the official exam plan in one place, so you know exactly what you are preparing for.

> The latest ETI recruitment is PSSSB Advertisement No. 01/2026, published on 6 February 2026, with **197 posts**. It replaced Advertisement 14/2024 (41 posts), which was withdrawn. All facts below come from the 01/2026 notification and the board's plan of written examination for it.

## The recruitment at a glance

- **Posts:** 197 Excise and Taxation Inspector posts, 65 of them reserved for women
- **Department:** Excise and Taxation Department, Punjab
- **Pay:** Rs 35,400 (7th CPC)
- **Applications:** online at sssb.punjab.gov.in from 13 February to 6 March 2026 (fee by the same date)
- **If you applied under Advt. 14/2024:** you had to apply again, but **without fee**, and your age is counted on 1 January 2024

## Eligibility

1. A **bachelor's degree** in any discipline from a recognised university.
2. A **120-hour computer course** (office productivity or desktop publishing) from a government-recognised or ISO 9001 institution, **or** a computer course equivalent to the DOEACC **'O' Level**.
3. **Matriculation with Punjabi** as a subject, or an equivalent Punjabi examination.

You must hold every qualification by the **last date of applying**.

## Age limit (as on 1 January 2026)

- **Minimum:** 18 years
- **General:** up to 37 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 42 years
- **State and central government employees:** up to 45 years (with an NOC at counselling)
- **Persons with disabilities of Punjab:** up to 47 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain other married women:** up to 40 years
- **Ex-servicemen:** as per the ex-servicemen rules

## Exam pattern

One **MCQ exam of 2 hours 30 minutes** on an OMR sheet, in two parts:

| Part | Subjects | Questions / marks | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Punjabi, matriculation standard | 50 / 50 | **Qualifying only - at least 25 marks (50%). No negative marking** |
| **Part B** | GK & current affairs, Punjab history & culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English, ICT | 100 / 100 | **Merit list is made only from Part B. 1/4 mark cut for each wrong answer** |

Part B is checked only if you score at least 25 in Part A.

- **Ties:** the older candidate ranks higher; then the higher percentage in the required qualification; then matric marks.
- After the merit list, candidates are called for **counselling** and document verification.

## Syllabus

**Part A - Punjabi (qualifying):** lives and works of Sri Guru Nanak Dev Ji, Guru Angad Dev Ji, Guru Ramdas Ji, Guru Arjan Dev Ji, Guru Tegh Bahadur Ji and Guru Gobind Singh Ji; antonyms and synonyms; idioms and proverbs; kinds of words; prefixes and suffixes; number and gender; punctuation; correcting words and sentences; Punjabi forms of English words; numbers, months and days in Punjabi; and questions on Punjabi language, Punjab's history and Punjab's culture.

**Part B - weightage (approx., out of 100):**

| Section | Marks | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 25 | Polity, environment, current affairs, science & technology, economy, Indian history with focus on the freedom struggle, sports, cinema & literature, geography |
| Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability | 25 | Reasoning and mental ability (17), basic numerical skills and percentages (4), data analysis - charts, tables, spreadsheets (4) |
| Punjab History & Culture | 17 | Physical features and ancient history, social, religious and economic life, language, literature and arts, Afghan/Mughal period, Bhakti and Sufi movements, Sikh Gurus and saints, Adi Granth, Sikh rulers, freedom movements of Punjab |
| Punjabi | 13 | Correct usage, spelling, prefixes/suffixes, synonyms/antonyms, nouns, pronouns and verbs, gender and number, idioms and proverbs, English-to-Punjabi translation, one-word substitution |
| English | 12 | Basic grammar, subject-verb, adjectives and adverbs, synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, fill in the blanks, sentence correction, idioms, spellings, articles, prepositions, narration, voice |
| ICT | 8 | Computer basics, network and internet, Word, Excel/spreadsheets and PowerPoint |

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers first.** We have the PSSSB Excise Inspector papers of [2021 and 2023, with answer keys](pyq/psssb-excise-taxation-inspector/index.html). They show exactly what PSSSB asks.
2. **Reasoning and GK are half of Part B.** Together they carry 50 marks - make them your strongest areas.
3. **Punjab history counts twice.** It is 17 marks in Part B and also appears in the Punjabi part, so revise the Sikh Gurus and Punjab's history thoroughly.
4. **Don't treat Punjabi lightly.** 25/50 in Part A is a hard floor, and Punjabi is again 13 marks in Part B.
5. **Mind the negative marking.** Each wrong Part B answer costs 1/4 mark - skip blind guesses and practise full timed papers in the last month.
