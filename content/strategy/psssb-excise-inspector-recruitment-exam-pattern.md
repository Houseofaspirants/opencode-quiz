I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Excise and Taxation Inspector (ETI) is a well-paid graduate-level PSSSB post, so this page puts the official notification in one place, so you know exactly what you are preparing for.

> The latest ETI recruitment is PSSSB Advertisement No. 14/2024, published on 6 December 2024. All facts below come from that notification. We will update this page when the next one is published.

## The recruitment at a glance

- **Posts:** 41 Excise and Taxation Inspector posts, 13 of them reserved for women
- **Department:** Office of the Excise and Taxation Commissioner, Punjab
- **Pay:** Rs 35,400 (7th CPC)
- **Applications:** online at sssb.punjab.gov.in from 1 to 21 January 2025 (5 PM); fee by 24 January 2025

## Eligibility

1. A **bachelor's degree** in any discipline from a recognised university.
2. A **120-hour computer course** (office productivity or desktop publishing) from a government-recognised or ISO 9001 institution, **or** a computer course equivalent to the DOEACC **'O' Level**.
3. **Matriculation with Punjabi** as a subject, or an equivalent Punjabi examination.

You must meet the qualification when you apply.

## Age limit (as on 1 January 2025)

- **Minimum:** 18 years
- **General:** up to 37 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 42 years
- **State and central government employees:** up to 45 years (with an NOC at counselling)
- **Persons with disabilities of Punjab:** up to 47 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain other married women:** up to 40 years
- **Ex-servicemen:** as per the ex-servicemen rules

## Fee

| Category | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| General, freedom fighter, sports | Rs 1,000 |
| SC / BC / EWS | Rs 250 |
| Ex-servicemen and dependents | Rs 200 |
| Persons with disabilities | Rs 500 |

The fee is not refunded.

## Selection process

One **objective (MCQ) written exam** in two parts:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Punjabi language, matriculation standard | **Qualifying only - you need at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Based on the educational qualification of the post | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- Failing the Punjabi part rules you out, whatever your Part B score.
- **Ties:** the older candidate ranks higher; then the higher percentage in the required qualification; then matric marks.
- After the merit list, candidates are called for **counselling** and document verification.
- The notification said the detailed syllabus and pattern would be uploaded on the board's website.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers first.** We have the PSSSB Excise Inspector papers of [2021 and 2023, with answer keys](pyq/psssb-excise-taxation-inspector/index.html). They show exactly what PSSSB asks.
2. **Don't treat Punjabi lightly.** Part A is "only" qualifying, but 50% is a hard floor. Practise matric-level grammar, vocabulary and comprehension.
3. **Part B decides everything.** It is set at the level of the post's qualification (graduation). Go through the 2021 and 2023 papers to see which subjects it covered, and build those first.
4. **Computers count twice.** The 120-hour course is required for eligibility, and computer questions come in the paper.
5. **Only 41 posts.** Competition is tight - aim well above the cut-off with timed full papers in the last month.
