I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2, scoring 713 out of 800**. The Head Constable paper is built on almost the same lines as SI, so this guide gives you the full pattern from the official notification along with what works.

> The latest Punjab Police Head Constable (Investigation Cadre) notification we have is Advt. No. 4 of 2021, published on 4 August 2021. Prepare on this pattern, and we will update this page when a new notification is published.

## The 2021 recruitment at a glance

- **Posts:** 811 Head Constables in the Investigation Cadre: 24 for sportspersons and 787 through this recruitment, of which 257 were reserved for women
- **Category-wise:** General 332; SC (Balmiki/Mazhbi Sikh) 82; SC (Ramdasia & others) 81; BC 81; EWS 81; ex-servicemen 105; wards of freedom fighters 8; wards of police personnel 17
- **Applications:** 4 to 25 August 2021
- **Pay:** Rs 25,500 per month (minimum pay, Level 4) during the 3-year probation
- **Fee:** Rs 1,000 for General; Rs 550 for SC, BC and EWS; Rs 400 for ex-servicemen of Punjab

## Eligibility (2021 notification)

- **Education:** graduation from a recognised university
- **Punjabi:** matriculation with Punjabi as a compulsory or elective subject, or an equivalent Punjabi examination
- **Age:** 21 to 28 years as on 1 January 2021. SC and BC candidates of Punjab and serving government employees get up to 5 years of relaxation. Ex-servicemen deduct their service period as per rules.
- **Height:** 5 ft 5 in for men and 5 ft 1 in for women

Note the minimum age: it is **21**, not 18 as for Constable and SI.

## Selection process

1. **Stage I:** Computer Based Test: two papers on the same day, in Punjabi and English (the English version prevails if there is any doubt)
2. **Stage II:** document scrutiny, Physical Measurement Test (PMT) and Physical Screening Test (PST)

The merit is made from the **total marks of Paper 1 and Paper 2** after normalization. PMT and PST are qualifying. There is no waiting list.

Unlike Constable and SI, there is **no separate Punjabi qualifying paper**. Punjabi is a 20-question section inside Paper 1.

## Exam pattern

| Paper | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper 1 | 100 (4 marks each) | 400 | 2 hours |
| Paper 2 | 100 (4 marks each) | 400 | 2 hours |

**Negative marking:** each wrong answer costs **1 mark** (25% of 4).

**Minimum marks (after normalization):** **30% in each paper separately** for General and other categories, **25%** for SC, BC, EWS and ex-servicemen.

**Ties:** if two candidates have the same total, the older candidate ranks higher. Next comes the higher Paper 1 score, then the higher matriculation percentage.

## Official syllabus

**Paper 1**

| Section | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| General Awareness | 50 |
| Quantitative Aptitude & Numerical Skills | 30 |
| Punjabi Language | 20 |

- **General Awareness:**
  - Indian Constitution and its features; Central and State legislature, executive and local government institutions; judicial institutions
  - History and culture of Punjab
  - Science and technology, Indian economy and agriculture, geography of India
  - National and international current affairs, including current legal developments
- **Quantitative Aptitude:**
  - Numbers and their relations, simplification, decimals and fractions
  - Ratio and proportion, percentage, average (mean, mode, median)
  - Profit and loss, simple interest
  - Time and work, speed, time and distance
- **Punjabi:**
  - Sentence completion and structuring, error detection
  - Vocabulary (synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution), comprehension
  - English-to-Punjabi translation, precis, fill in the blanks, idioms and phrases

**Paper 2**

| Section | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Logical & Analytical Reasoning | 50 |
| Digital Literacy & Computer Awareness | 30 |
| English Language | 20 |

- **Reasoning:**
  - Statements and conclusions, number and letter series, sequencing, missing number, pattern completion
  - Order and ranking, direction and distances, relationship problems
  - Puzzles, calendars, coding-decoding
  - Verbal, non-verbal and legal reasoning
- **Digital Literacy:**
  - Computer fundamentals, hardware, email basics
  - MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  - Internet and the web, social media, search engines
  - Mobile phones (basic concepts)
- **English:**
  - Sentence completion and structuring, error detection
  - Vocabulary (synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution), comprehension
  - Punjabi-to-English translation, precis, fill in the blanks

## Physical tests (2021 notification)

| Candidates | Events |
| --- | --- |
| Men (except ex-servicemen 35 and above) | 800 m run in 4:00 min (1 chance); long jump 2.75 m and high jump 0.90 m (3 chances each) |
| Women | 400 m run in 2:00 min (1 chance); long jump 1.80 m and high jump 0.75 m (3 chances each) |
| Ex-servicemen, male, 35 and above | 800 m run/walk in 6:00 min (1 chance) |

You must clear every event for your category; failing any one disqualifies you.

## How to prepare

1. **Respect the negative marking.** With -1 for a wrong answer, a blind guess loses on average. Attempt a question when you can cut it down to two options.
2. **General Awareness and Reasoning decide the merit.** They are 50 + 50 of the 200 questions, which is 400 of the 800 marks. Give Punjab history and culture extra time.
3. **Digital Literacy is 120 marks.** It goes up to Excel and PowerPoint. Most candidates under-prepare it, so it is where you can gain the most.
4. **Practise translation daily.** Punjabi (Paper 1) and English (Paper 2) each carry 80 marks, and both include translation and precis.
5. **Clear 30% in each paper separately.** A strong Paper 1 cannot rescue a weak Paper 2.
6. **Do not ignore the physical test.** The 800 m in 4 minutes is easier than the Constable run, but you still need a few weeks of practice.
