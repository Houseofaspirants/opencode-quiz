I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Chandigarh Police is a Union Territory force, so its recruitment runs separately from Punjab Police - different notice, different portal, different paper.

> The last round was the **Chandigarh Police notice of 20 May 2023** for **700 Constable (Executive) posts**, plus a separate sports-quota list. Selection had a **written test, physical test, document check and medical**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Our archive has papers from **2013, 2018 and 2023** - roughly one big round every few years. On that pattern a 2027 round is **possible but not certain**. Keep your 10+2 marks sheet, category certificate and fitness ready, because Chandigarh windows are short.

## Eligibility (2023 notice)

- **Education:** 10+2 from a recognised board.
- **Age:** reported as 18 to 25 years (as on 20 May 2023), with relaxation for reserved categories.
- **Physical standards and test:** height, chest and running standards apply - read them in the notice.
- **Fee (2023):** reported as Rs 1,000 for general/OBC, Rs 800 for SC and EWS, and nil for ex-servicemen.

## Selection process

| Stage | What |
| --- | --- |
| Written test | Objective MCQ - reported as 100 marks, 1 mark each, 1/4 cut for a wrong answer |
| Physical test | Running and physical measurement |
| Documents and medical | Final checks |

## How to prepare

1. **Solve all three papers:** [Chandigarh Police Constable 2013, 2018 and 2023](pyq/chandigarh-police-constable/index.html).

3. **Reasoning and maths** at 10+2 level.
4. **English and computer basics.**
5. **Start running now** - the physical test is where many written-test toppers fall.
