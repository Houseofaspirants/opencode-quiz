I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. The Librarian paper is unusual: about **three quarters of it is library science**, so a B.Lib student who knows DDC, cataloguing and reference sources well has a real edge over general aspirants.

> The latest round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 13 of 2025** (published 28 November 2025, applications 5 to 27 December 2025), which had **3 Librarian posts** in the Department of Technical Education and Industrial Training at **Rs 29,200 (Level-5)**. Earlier, **School Librarian** posts were filled through **Advt 04/2021** (paper held in 2022), and a Librarian plan was published for **Advt 13/2022**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Librarian posts have come in **2021 (School Librarian), 2022 and 2025**, each time for a different department. Schools, colleges, ITIs and government libraries all need librarians, so a 2027-28 round is possible, but numbers are small. Keep your B.Lib or M.Lib marks and experience certificates ready.

## Eligibility (Advt 13/2025, Librarian)

1. A **B.Lib with one year of experience**, or
2. A **B.A./B.Sc with a library diploma** and the experience the advertisement lists.
3. **Punjabi at matriculation level**.

School Librarian rules are set by the Education Department and can differ; check that advertisement separately.

## Age, fee and pay

- **Age:** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees, 47 for persons with disabilities, 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC/BC/EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.
- **Pay:** Rs 29,200 (Level-5, 7th CPC) for the 2025 posts.

## Exam pattern (official plan, Advt 13/2022)

| Section | Approx. questions |
| --- | --- |
| Logical reasoning and mental ability | 10 |
| GK and current affairs (incl. Punjab history) | 10 |
| English | 5 |
| Punjabi | 5 |
| Library science and ICT in libraries | 90 |
| **Total** | **120** |

- **2 hours**, OMR sheet, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- A **qualifying matric-level Punjabi** paper (50%) also applies to every PSSSB exam since October 2022.

## Library science syllabus

1. **Libraries** - purpose, types, laws of library science, ILA, ALA, IFLA, UNESCO, RRRLF, library movement in India, consortia
2. **Library sections** - acquisition, periodicals, technical, reference, circulation, maintenance
3. **Reference service** - CAS, SDI, sources of information, DELNET, INFLIBNET, NISCAIR
4. **Bibliography** - kinds, national bibliographies (INB, BNB)
5. **Classification** - facet analysis, call number, DDC, Colon Classification, Web Dewey
6. **Cataloguing** - entries, subject headings, chain procedure, MARC, ISBD, CCF, RDA
7. **School library system** and **library users** - information needs and user studies
8. **ICT** - computer basics, internet, office tools, KOHA, SOUL, LIBSYS, barcode, RFID, e-publishing

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper.** We have the [School Librarian 2022 paper (Set C) with the final key](pyq/psssb-school-librarian/index.html).
2. **Classification and cataloguing give the most questions.** Learn DDC main classes and Colon fundamental categories by heart.
3. **Make a one-page list of associations, networks and founders.** These are quick, sure marks.
4. **Don't skip the 30 general questions.** In a 3-post exam, they decide the merit.
