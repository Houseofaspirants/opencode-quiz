I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Clerk IT is the clerk post where a computer degree actually pays off: the general clerk paper is open to every graduate, but the Clerk IT paper is mostly your own subject. This page puts the official rules in one place.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 11 of 2025** (published 18 November 2025, applications 26 November to 26 December 2025). It had 69 Clerk posts and **1 Clerk IT post** (Tourism Department). The written exam was held on **28 February 2026**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Clerk IT has been advertised in **2021 (Advt 18/2021), 2022 (Advt 15/2022) and 2025 (Advt 11/2025)**. The numbers swing a lot - from a big batch to a single post - because each department asks for Clerk IT separately. PSSSB runs a clerk recruitment almost every year, so a Clerk IT line in a 2027 advertisement is a realistic possibility. Prepare now; the core syllabus has not changed.

## Eligibility (Advt 11/2025)

1. A **full-time BCA, B.Sc IT or B.Sc Computer Science** from a recognised university or institution.
2. A **typing test in Punjabi and English at 30 words per minute**, held by the recruiting authority.
3. **Punjabi at matriculation (10th) level**, as per the Punjab Civil Services Rules, 1994.
4. Your degree certificate must be dated before the last date to apply.

## Age, fee and pay

- **Age (as on 1 January 2025):** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees, 47 for persons with disabilities, 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC/BC/EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.
- **Pay:** Rs 19,900 (Level-2, 7th CPC).

## Selection process

| Stage | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi (MCQ) | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Main paper on the board's syllabus | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |
| Typing test | 30 wpm English and Punjabi (Raavi font), on computer | Qualifying only |
| Counselling | Documents and department preference | About three times the posts are called |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Ties:** older candidate first, then higher percentage in the degree, then matric marks.

## What the main paper covers

In the 2022 round (Advt 15/2022) the paper had **120 questions in 2 hours**: **90 from the Clerk IT subject** and **30 from GK, English, Punjabi and reasoning**. The subject syllabus is a full BCA-level course:

- **Computer fundamentals** - networks, internet, email, office tools
- **Operating systems** - process scheduling, deadlocks, memory management, paging, I/O
- **DBMS** - ER model, keys, SQL (DDL, DML, DCL), normalisation up to BCNF and 5NF, transactions
- **Computer organisation** - Boolean algebra, K-maps, flip-flops, multiplexers, memory
- **Data structures** - linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, sorting and searching
- **Programming** - C language, flowcharts and algorithms
- **Networks** - data communication, OSI and TCP/IP models

Since October 2022 every PSSSB exam also carries the **qualifying Punjabi Part A**, so plan for both parts.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers.** We have [Clerk IT papers from 2021, 2022 and 2026 with answer keys](pyq/psssb-clerk-it/index.html) - the 2026 paper shows the current level.
2. **DBMS and operating systems decide the rank.** They give the most questions and reward exact definitions.
3. **Do C output questions by hand.** Pointers, loops and storage classes are standard traps.
4. **Start typing practice early.** Raavi Punjabi at 30 wpm takes weeks; many toppers lose the post here.
5. **Don't skip Part A.** A 50% floor in Punjabi is easy to clear but fatal to miss.
