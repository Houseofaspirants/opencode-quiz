I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Clerk (Legal) is a small but valuable recruitment: three-quarters of the paper is law, so an LLB student who revises the codes properly competes with a much smaller field than the general clerk exam.

> The latest round is **PSSSB Advertisement No. 12 of 2026** (published 18 September 2026, applications 25 September to 19 October 2026). It has **13 Clerk (Legal) posts** at Rs 19,900 across six offices - Excise and Taxation (6), Employment Generation and Training (2), Dairy Development (2), Labour Commissioner (1), Defence Services Welfare (1) and Cultural Affairs (1). The written exam date is still awaited. The previous round was Advt 02/2022, examined on 8 July 2023.

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Advt 12/2026 brought Clerk (Legal) back after more than three years, with **13 posts**. The rules are the same as before: a **law graduate**, matric Punjabi, a qualifying Punjabi Part A (50%), merit from **Part B** with a 1/4 negative, and a qualifying 30 wpm typing test in English and Punjabi (Raavi font). A common merit list is made from typing-pass candidates. Legal-cell demand still arrives irregularly, so treat any 2027 round as a bonus and prepare for the current one. The same law syllabus also prepares you for PCS (Judicial) and Assistant District Attorney posts.

## Exam pattern (Advt 02/2022 official plan)

| Part | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Law subject syllabus (5 units) | 90 | 90 |
| GK, English, Punjabi, logical reasoning and mental ability | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

- **Time:** 2 hours, MCQ on OMR sheet. **Negative marking:** 1/4 mark per wrong answer.
- **Since October 2022:** a **qualifying matric-level Punjabi Part A** (50% needed) comes first; merit is made from the main paper.
- After the written exam: typing test (qualifying) and counselling, as for other clerk posts.

## The five law units

1. **Code of Criminal Procedure** - courts and their powers, arrest and rights of arrested persons, FIR and investigation, charge, trials (sessions, warrant, summons, summary), bail and anticipatory bail, inherent powers of the High Court.
2. **Indian Penal Code** - general explanations, joint liability, punishments, abetment, conspiracy, offences against the State and public servants, murder and culpable homicide, hurt, offences against women, property offences.
3. **Indian Evidence Act** - presumptions, relevancy, admissions and confessions, dying declarations, expert opinion, documentary evidence, burden of proof, estoppel, witnesses.
4. **Code of Civil Procedure** - decree, order and judgment, jurisdiction and its irregular exercise, place of suing, res sub judice and res judicata, and the other civil-procedure topics in the official plan.
5. **Constitutional law** - the Preamble and its objectives onwards; fundamental rights, directive principles and the Union-State structure are the usual core.

### The 2024 change you must not ignore

From **1 July 2024** the **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)**, **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)** and **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)** replaced the IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act. Learn the topics once, but **map every important section to its new number**. Expect questions like "Which section of the BNS defines murder?".

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers.** We have the [Clerk Legal 2023 papers with first and final answer keys](pyq/psssb-clerk-legal/index.html).
2. **Bare acts beat guide books.** Read the definitions sections word by word; options often differ by one word.
3. **Make a section table.** IPC → BNS, CrPC → BNSS, Evidence → BSA for the 60 most-asked sections.
4. **Constitution is shared marks.** It helps in the GK part too.
5. **Keep 30 GK marks safe** with Punjab history, reasoning and grammar.
