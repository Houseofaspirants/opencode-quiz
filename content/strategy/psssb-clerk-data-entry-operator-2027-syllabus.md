I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Clerk cum Data Entry Operator is a general-studies paper with no technical subject, so it is one of the most open PSSSB exams for any graduate. This guide sets out the official plan and what to expect next.

> The last round was **Advertisement No. 03 of 2022**; the written exam was held on **25 June 2023**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Since 2023, PSSSB has put most office vacancies into **general Clerk advertisements** (Advt 05/2024 with 259 posts, Advt 11/2025 and Advt 02/2026). A separate Clerk cum DEO round in 2027 is **uncertain**. That is not bad news: the DEO syllabus is almost the same as the clerk syllabus, so the same preparation works for whichever advertisement comes first.

## Exam pattern (Advt 03/2022 official plan)

| Section | Questions (approx.) |
| --- | --- |
| General knowledge and current affairs (polity, environment, science, economy, Punjab history from the 14th century, Indian freedom struggle, sports, cinema and literature) | 30 |
| Logical reasoning and mental ability (verbal and non-verbal reasoning, percentages, ratio, interest, time and work, data interpretation) | 30 |
| Punjab history and culture | 20 |
| English grammar | 15 |
| Punjabi grammar | 15 |
| ICT (computers, internet, Word, spreadsheet, PowerPoint) | 10 |
| **Total** | **120** |

- **Time:** 2 hours, MCQ on OMR sheet. **Negative marking:** 1/4 mark per wrong answer.
- **Current PSSSB shape:** the 2026 clerk exam had a **qualifying 50-question Punjabi Part A** (25 marks needed, no negative marking) and a **100-question Part B** in 2 hours 30 minutes. Expect that structure in any 2027 round.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper.** We have the [Clerk cum DEO paper of 25 June 2023 with first and final answer keys](pyq/psssb-clerk-cum-data-entry-operator/index.html).
2. **GK and reasoning are half the paper.** Give them half your time.
3. **Punjab history is 20 marks** - Sikh Gurus, Sikh rulers, Bhakti and Sufi movements, Punjab's freedom movements.
4. **Grammar is quick marks** in both English and Punjabi; practise 20 questions a day.
5. **Learn typing anyway.** Clerk posts need a 30 wpm Punjabi and English test.
