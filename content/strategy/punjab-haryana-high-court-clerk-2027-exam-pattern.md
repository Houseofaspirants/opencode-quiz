I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. The High Court Clerk exam is **not like a PSSSB clerk paper**. The objective test only shortlists you; the job is won in a **descriptive English mains**, where essay, precis and letter writing decide the merit. Most candidates realise this too late.

> The last recruitment was **Detailed Employment Notice No. 01/CLK/HC/2023** dated **24 February 2023**, for **157 Clerk posts** on the establishment of the High Court of Punjab and Haryana, Chandigarh. Applications closed on 17 March 2023. **No 2027 notice has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

The High Court recruits Clerks every few years, when vacancies in its own establishment build up. Earlier papers on our site are from **2012, 2017 and 2019**, and the last notice came in **2023**. A new notice in 2027 is **possible**, but the court gives no fixed calendar. The mains needs months of writing practice, so starting early is the only real advantage you can take.

## Eligibility (2023 notice)

1. A **bachelor's degree** in Arts, Commerce, Science or any equivalent degree.
2. **Proficiency in computer operation** - word processing and spreadsheets.
3. **Hindi or Punjabi up to matriculation** standard.
4. **10% of posts** go to B.Com graduates, with preference for Chartered Accountants and M.Com holders.

## Age and fee

- **Age (as on 17 March 2023):** 18 to 30 years. Relaxation: SC/ST/BC of Punjab, Haryana and Chandigarh 3 years; persons with disabilities 10 years; ex-servicemen their service plus 3 years.
- **Fee:** Rs 500 online facilitation charge plus an exam fee of Rs 500 (General) or Rs 250 (SC/ST/BC of Punjab, Haryana and Chandigarh, persons with disabilities, ex-servicemen).

## Selection process

| Stage | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Screening test** | 200 MCQs, 2 hours, computer based | Shortlisting only (about 5x vacancies) |
| **Main written exam** | Descriptive, 3 hours, 300 marks | **Counts in merit** |
| **Typing test** | English, 30 wpm, max 10% mistakes | Qualifying |
| **Viva-voce** | Interview | **50 marks, counts in merit** |

- **Screening test:** GK, numerical ability, English, reasoning, clerical aptitude, general intelligence and general awareness. 1 mark per question, **0.25 cut** for a wrong answer. Marks are **normalised** across shifts.
- **Main exam papers:** English composition **150**, General Knowledge **50**, Precis writing **50**, Hindi or Punjabi **50**.
- To pass the mains you need **40% in aggregate and 33% in each subject**.
- **Final merit = main written exam + viva-voce.**

## How to prepare

1. **Start writing in English now.** Essays, letters, applications and comprehension need weekly practice with corrections.
2. **Practise precis to one-third length.** It carries 50 marks and is the most neglected part of the paper.
3. **Use the old papers.** We have [High Court Clerk papers from 2012, 2017 and 2019](pyq/punjab-haryana-high-court-clerk/index.html) to see the screening test style.
4. **Choose Hindi or Punjabi early** and practise translation and letter writing in it.
5. **Build English typing to 35 wpm**, so 30 wpm under pressure is comfortable.
