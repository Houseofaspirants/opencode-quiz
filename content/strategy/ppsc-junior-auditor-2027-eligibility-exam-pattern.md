I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Junior Auditor is a commerce graduate's exam. The candidates who win it treat it like a B.Com final exam with negative marking, not like a GK test.

> The last round was the **PPSC advertisement of 27 July 2022** for **75 Junior Auditor (Group B) posts** in the **Department of Finance**. The written exam came almost two years later, on **5 May 2024**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Finance department audit posts open up as staff retire or get promoted. The 2022 advertisement took until 2024 to reach the exam stage, so even if a new advertisement comes in 2027, expect a long gap before the paper. A 2027 advertisement is **possible**; prepare the accountancy core now because it doesn't go out of date.

## Eligibility (2022 advertisement)

1. **B.Com or M.Com** with the division conditions set in the advertisement.
2. **Age:** 18 to 37 years as on 1 January 2022, with the usual relaxation for reserved categories.
3. **Punjabi** at matriculation level, as for all Punjab government posts.

## Fee and pay (2022)

- **Fee:** Rs 1,500 for most candidates; Rs 750 for SC/ST of all states and BC of Punjab, as reported.
- **Pay:** Rs 35,400 a month during the 3-year probation.

## Exam pattern (5 May 2024)

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Questions | **120** objective |
| Time | **2 hours** |
| Marks | **480** - 4 per question |
| Negative marking | **1 mark** per wrong answer |

## What the paper asked

- **Financial accounting** - partnership (admission, retirement, dissolution), company accounts, buy-back, internal reconstruction.
- **Cost accounting** - overheads, labour incentive plans such as Halsey and Rowan, standard costing.
- **Company law** basics - for example Section 8 companies.
- **History, GK and reasoning** in the last part.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper with its key:** [Junior Auditor 2024 paper and answer key](pyq/ppsc-junior-auditor/index.html).
2. **Revise B.Com accountancy and costing** chapter-wise with practice problems.
3. **Learn Companies Act 2013 basics.**
4. **Keep 20 minutes for GK and reasoning**, and attempt only what you are sure of.
