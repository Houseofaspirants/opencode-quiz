I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Panchayat Secretary is a village-level post, so the paper rewards people who know rural Punjab and how panchayats actually work.

> The last round in our archive was the **PSSSB recruitment of August 2016** - **800 Panchayat Secretary posts** in the Department of Rural Development and Panchayats. The paper was held in **October 2016**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

There has been **no large Panchayat Secretary round since 2016** in our archive. With panchayat elections and new rural schemes, a fresh round is **possible**, but treat it as uncertain. Prepare in a way that also serves the VDO/Gram Sevak and clerk exams.

## The 2016 recruitment in brief

- **Posts:** 800.
- **Application window:** 8 August to 6 September 2016.
- **Qualification:** 10+2 or graduation, as reported.
- **2016 paper (reported):** 100 marks in four 25-mark sections - language (Punjabi and English), GK, mental ability and maths.
- **Pay (2016):** on the pre-revised pay band of that time; any new advertisement will use the current 7th CPC scale.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper:** [Panchayat Secretary paper of October 2016](pyq/psssb-panchayat-secretary/index.html).
2. **Panchayati Raj** - the 73rd Amendment, Punjab Panchayati Raj Act, gram sabha powers.
3. **Rural development schemes** - MGNREGA, PMAY-G, Swachh Bharat (G).
4. **English and Punjabi grammar** - both were tested in 2016.
5. **Maths and reasoning** at 10+2 level.
