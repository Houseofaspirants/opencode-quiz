I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Technical Assistant is one of the better-paid PSSSB posts at Level-6, but the title confuses people: **different departments use it for completely different jobs**. Read the department line before you prepare.

> The latest round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 12 of 2025** - **2 Technical Assistant posts** (Group-B) in the Directorate of SC Sub-Plan, applications 1 to 23 December 2025. Before that, **Advt 01/2024** (published 9 February 2024) had **6 Technical Assistant and 12 District Technical Coordinator posts** in the Department of Administrative Reforms. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

PSSSB has advertised Technical Assistant posts in **2021, 2024 and 2025**, each time for a different department. That pattern means a 2027 advertisement is quite possible, but you cannot predict which degree it will ask for. If you hold a **master's in Statistics or Economics** or a **B.Tech in IT or CS**, keep an eye on PSSSB notices; posts are few, so competition per post is high.

## Eligibility - the two latest advertisements

| | Advt 12/2025 | Advt 01/2024 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Department | Directorate of SC Sub-Plan | Administrative Reforms |
| Posts | 2 Technical Assistant | 6 Technical Assistant + 12 District Technical Coordinator |
| Degree | Master's in Economics, Mathematics, Agri Economics, Commerce with Statistics, or Statistics - 50% (or below 50% with one year of statistical work) | B.E./B.Tech in IT, ECE or CS with 50%, or a master's in Computer |
| Pay | Rs 35,400 (Level-6) | Rs 35,400 (Level-6) |

Matric-level Punjabi is required for both.

## Age and fee

- **Age:** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees, 47 for persons with disabilities, 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC/BC/EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.

## Selection process

| Stage | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi (MCQ) | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Written exam** | Objective paper | **Merit list** |
| Counselling | Documents | Final step |

- **Marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer (standard PSSSB rule).
- For Advt 12/2025 the board said the **syllabus would be uploaded later**; check its website before the exam.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the 2021 paper.** We have the [Technical Assistant paper of 22 August 2021 with its key](pyq/psssb-technical-assistant/index.html) to see the level.
2. **Treat your degree as 70% of the job.** Statistics candidates: sampling, index numbers, regression, official statistics. IT candidates: networks, DBMS, OS and programming.
3. **Keep GK and Punjabi ticking over.** In a 2-post exam, every general mark matters.
4. **Have your mark sheets ready.** The 50% rule and the experience exception are checked closely.
