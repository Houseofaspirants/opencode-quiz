I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Proof Reader and Copy Holder sound like specialist jobs, but their written test is the same general paper as PSSSB Clerk - with sharp Punjabi and English, you have an edge.

> This page is based on the board's **final plan of written examination for Proof Reader and Copy Holder, Advt No. 04 of 2023**. Our archive does not have the advertisement itself, so eligibility, pay and posts are not given here.

## Latest notification

Proof Reader and Copy Holder were advertised in **PSSSB Advt 04/2023**. No newer advertisement for these posts is in our archive yet.

## Exam pattern (Advt 04/2023 final plan)

| Part | What | Questions / marks | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Matric-level Punjabi | 50 | **Qualifying - 25 marks needed**, no negative marking |
| B | GK, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English, ICT | 100 | **Merit list from Part B only** |

- **Time:** 2 hours 30 minutes, MCQ on an OMR sheet.
- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer; no penalty for a blank.

## Syllabus

**Part A - Punjabi (qualifying):** lives and works of the Sikh Gurus, antonyms and synonyms, idioms, proverbs, parts of speech, prefixes and suffixes, number and gender, punctuation, correcting words and sentences, correct Punjabi forms of English words, numbers, months and days, and questions on Punjabi language, Punjab history and culture.

**Part B - weightage (approx., out of 100):**

| Section | Marks |
| --- | --- |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 25 |
| Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability (reasoning 17, numeracy 4, data analysis 4) | 25 |
| Punjab History & Culture | 17 |
| Punjabi | 13 |
| English | 12 |
| ICT | 8 |

## How to prepare

1. **Spelling and correct usage** are the heart of a proof reader's work - and of the Punjabi and English sections.
2. **Solve PSSSB Clerk papers**; the Part B mix is the same.
3. **Clear Part A comfortably** - 25 out of 50 is the bar, but aim for 40.
