I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Inspector, Cooperative Societies is one of the most popular PPSC graduate posts, and most pages about it online are guesses. This page sticks to what the official PPSC advertisement says.

> The last recruitment was PPSC Advertisement No. 202144, dated 1 December 2021, for 320 posts. A new notification had not been published when this page was written. The facts below come from the 2021 advertisement; the next one may change numbers and dates, so check it when it comes. We will update this page.

- **Post:** Inspector, Cooperative Societies, Department of Cooperation, Punjab

## Eligibility

1. A **bachelor's degree in any stream with at least 60% marks**. This is stricter than most Punjab graduate posts - check your percentage first.
2. A **120-hour computer course** with hands-on PC or IT use, from a government-recognised or ISO 9001 institution. Not needed if a similar course was part of your degree.
3. **Matriculation with Punjabi** as a compulsory or elective subject, or an equivalent Punjabi examination.

You must hold every qualification by the last date of applying.

## Age limit (as on 1 January of the recruitment year)

- **General:** 18 to 37 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 42 years
- **Punjab, other state and central government employees:** up to 45 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain other women:** up to 42 years
- **Persons with disabilities of Punjab:** up to 47 years
- **Ex-servicemen of Punjab:** service period is deducted from age; the result may exceed the limit by up to 3 years

## Exam pattern

One **pen-and-paper exam on an OMR sheet**, 2 hours long:

| Part | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logical reasoning & mental ability | 40 | 160 |
| General knowledge & current affairs | 80 | 320 |
| **Total** | **120** | **480** |

- **+4** for a correct answer, **-1** for a wrong one, 0 for a blank.
- **No interview.** The final selection is on the written exam alone.
- To be appointed you need **45%** marks (**40%** for SC and BC of Punjab).

## Syllabus

**General knowledge & current affairs** of national and international importance:
- Economic issues
- Polity issues
- Environment issues
- Science and technology
- Any other current issues
- History of India, especially the freedom struggle
- History of Punjab from the 14th century onwards

**Logical reasoning & mental ability:**
- Logical reasoning, analytical and mental ability
- Basic numerical skills: numbers, magnitudes, percentages, numerical relations
- Data analysis: charts, tables, spreadsheets and graphic presentation

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper first.** We have the [2022 PPSC Cooperative Inspector paper](pyq/ppsc-cooperative-inspector/index.html). It shows the level and the mix far better than any article.
2. **GK is two-thirds of the marks.** 320 of 480 marks come from GK and current affairs. Read current affairs daily and revise it monthly.
3. **Punjab history from the 14th century** is named in the syllabus - Sufis, Bhakti, the Sikh Gurus, the Misls, Maharaja Ranjit Singh, Anglo-Sikh wars and Punjab's freedom fighters.
4. **Mind the negative marking.** At -1 against +4, a guess is worth it only if you can rule out two options.
5. **Reasoning is the scoring part.** 40 questions of series, coding, directions, percentages and data tables - practise them timed until they take under a minute each.
