I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. This exam is unusual for PPSC: no negative marking and a very mixed syllabus. That changes how you should attempt it.

> The last round was one **competitive exam on 19 June 2022** for **34 Group B posts** - Assistant Director, Assistant Controller of Stores, Store Inspection Officer and Functional Manager - in the **Department of Industries and Commerce**. An interview followed for those who qualified. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

These posts sit in district industries centres and the stores wing. Rounds are **rare** - the 2022 exam was the only one in our archive. A 2027 round is **possible but not likely to be large**. If you are an engineering or science graduate already preparing for PPSC exams, this paper fits in without much extra work.

## Exam pattern (19 June 2022)

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Questions | **120** objective |
| Time | **2 hours** |
| Marks | **300** - 2.5 per question |
| Negative marking | **None** |
| Next stage | Interview |

With no penalty, **attempt every question**. Leave nothing blank.

## What the paper asked

- **Physics and chemistry** at graduation entry level - mechanics, Lewis acids, periodic trends.
- **Maths** - calculus basics and data interpretation from tables.
- **Biology and health** basics.
- **Economy and industry** - PMEGP, KVIC, food processing, MSME schemes, top producing states.
- **General knowledge and current affairs.**

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper** with its key: [Functional Manager 2022 paper and answer key](pyq/ppsc-functional-manager/index.html).
2. **Read the MSME and industry schemes** of the Centre and Punjab.
3. **Revise class 11-12 physics, chemistry and maths** - that level was enough for most science questions.
4. **Prepare for the interview** early - know your district's industries.
