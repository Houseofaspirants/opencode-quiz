I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Forester posts are few, so the competition per seat is tough. If you are preparing for Forest Guard anyway, Forester is a natural add-on.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 07 of 2022** - **204 posts** of Deputy Ranger, Forester and Forest Guard (200 of them Forest Guard). The Forester paper was held on **20 November 2022**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Forester posts open only in **small numbers**, usually inside a Forest Guard drive. A 2027 round is **possible but not confirmed**. Prepare with the Forest Guard syllabus - it covers most of the ground.

## The 2022 recruitment in brief

- **Posts:** 204 in all - 200 Forest Guard, the rest Deputy Ranger and Forester.
- **Starting pay:** Forester Rs 25,500 (Forest Guard Rs 21,700, Deputy Ranger Rs 35,400).
- **Age:** 18-37 as on 1 January 2022, with relaxations.
- **Qualification:** check the exact requirement in the next notice; reports of 2022 differ.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.
