I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Senior Assistant is the Group-B office post most clerks aim for - better pay, the same kind of general paper. This page puts the official advertisement and exam plan side by side.

> The latest round is **PSSSB Advertisement No. 06 of 2026** (published 7 March 2026, applications 7 to 28 March 2026). The board makes **one common merit list** and fills Senior Assistant demands that departments send **up to 30 September 2026**.

## Latest notification (Advt 06/2026)

- **Post:** Senior Assistant, Group-B, in various departments of the Punjab Government.
- **Posts:** decided by department demands received up to 30 September 2026; a category with no demand by then gets no post from this list.
- **Applications:** online at sssb.punjab.gov.in, 7 to 28 March 2026 (fee by the same date).
- **Earlier rounds:** Advt 05/2023 and Advt 05/2025, both with published exam plans.

## Eligibility

1. A **bachelor's degree** from a recognised university or institution.
2. A **120-hour computer course** with hands-on use of a PC in office productivity or desktop publishing, from a government-recognised or ISO 9001 institution, **or** a computer course equivalent to DOEACC **'O' Level**.
3. **Matric with Punjabi** (Rule 17, Punjab Civil Services Rules, 1994).
4. Qualifications must be complete by the **last date to apply**.

## Age and fee

- **Age (as on 1 January 2026):** 18 to 37; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab; up to 45 for state and central government employees (NOC at counselling); up to 47 for persons with disabilities of Punjab; up to 40 for widows and divorcees. Ex-servicemen deduct their service as per the 1982 rules.
- **Fee under Advt 06/2026:** Rs 1,500 for General and other categories; Rs 750 for SC and BC of Punjab; Rs 500 for ex-servicemen, EWS, persons with disabilities and lineal descendants of ex-servicemen of Punjab. The fee is not refunded.

## Exam pattern

The board's plans for **Advt 05/2023** and **Advt 05/2025** are the same:

| Paper | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GK, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English and ICT | 120 | 120 | 2 hours |

- MCQ on an OMR sheet; **1/4 mark (0.25) cut** for each wrong answer; no penalty for a question left blank.
- These plans have **no separate qualifying Punjabi part** - Punjabi is one section of the single paper.
- After the written test: a **qualifying typing test at 30 wpm** in English and Punjabi on computer (Punjabi in Raavi font), then counselling. The common merit list is made from written marks of typing-pass candidates.
- **Ties:** older candidate first, then higher percentage in the required degree, then higher matric marks.

## Syllabus (approx. weightage out of 120)

| Section | Marks | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 30 | Polity, environment, current affairs, science and technology, economy, Indian history with focus on the freedom struggle, sports, cinema and literature, geography |
| Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability | 30 | Reasoning and analytical ability, basic numeracy and percentages, data analysis from charts and tables |
| Punjab History & Culture | 20 | Physical features and ancient history, social and religious life, language and literature, Afghan and Mughal period, Bhakti and Sufi movements, Sikh Gurus, Sikh rulers, freedom movements of Punjab |
| Punjabi | 15 | Correct usage, spelling, prefixes and suffixes, synonyms and antonyms, parts of speech, gender and number, idioms and proverbs, translation, one-word substitution |
| English | 15 | Grammar, synonyms and antonyms, one-word substitution, fill in the blanks, sentence correction, idioms, articles, prepositions, narration, voice |
| ICT | 10 | Computer basics, networks and the internet, office productivity tools |

The 2026 paper's own plan will be put on the board's website; check it when the admit card comes.

## How to prepare

1. **Treat it as a 120-mark general paper.** GK and reasoning are 60 marks between them.
2. **Punjab history is 20 marks** - Sikh Gurus, misls and Maharaja Ranjit Singh come up every time.
3. **Start typing practice now** in Raavi; many candidates lose the post at the typing test.
4. **Use clerk papers** - the syllabus is close to the PSSSB clerk general paper.
