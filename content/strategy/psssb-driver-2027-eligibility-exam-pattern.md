I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Driver is one of the few PSSSB posts where your licence and experience matter as much as your paper. Many experienced drivers lose out only because they treat the written exam lightly. This page sets out the official rules.

> The latest round is **PSSSB Advertisement No. 12 of 2026** (published 18 September 2026, applications 25 September to 19 October 2026). It has **5 Driver posts** in the Rural Development and Panchayat Department at **Rs 21,700 (Level-3)**. Before that, a Driver exam for **Advt 08/2023 and 04/2025** was held on **31 January 2026**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

PSSSB has advertised Driver posts in **2023, 2025 and 2026**. Every department runs official vehicles and drivers retire every year, so the demand keeps coming back in small batches. A 2027 advertisement is **likely**, though the number per department is usually small. Keep your licence and experience certificate valid and in order.

## Eligibility (Advt 12/2026)

1. **Matric** pass.
2. A valid **driving licence for light and heavy vehicles**.
3. At least **three years of driving experience**.
4. Punjabi at matriculation level, as per the Punjab Civil Services Rules, 1994.

## Age, fee and pay

- **Age (as on 1 January 2026):** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC/BC/EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.
- **Pay:** Rs 21,700 to 69,100 (Level-3, 7th CPC).

## Selection process

| Stage | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi (MCQ) | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Objective written paper | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |
| Counselling | Licence, experience and other documents | Final step |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- In the **31 January 2026** Driver paper, Part A had **50 questions** and Part B had **100**.
- **Ties:** older candidate first, then higher qualification marks, then matric marks.
- The board publishes the syllabus and roll numbers on its website.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers.** We have the [Driver paper of 9 July 2023 and the 2026 provisional and final keys](pyq/psssb-driver/index.html).
2. **Learn traffic rules and road signs properly.** Motor Vehicles Act basics, signs, signals and vehicle maintenance are natural questions for this post.
3. **Don't take Part A for granted.** 50% in matric Punjabi is easy only if you revise grammar and spelling.
4. **Add GK, Punjab and basic reasoning** for the rest of Part B.
5. **Get your experience certificate in the right format** before counselling.
