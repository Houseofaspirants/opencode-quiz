I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Fireman is half paper and half fitness. Many strong runners never reach the physical test because they took the written exam lightly - and the reverse happens too.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 01 of 2023** - **1317 posts** (991 Fireman, 326 Driver/Operator). The written exam was on **1 October 2023**, the physical test from **18 to 23 October 2023**, and the physical test result came on **15 November 2023**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Punjab has been adding fire stations and fire tenders, and the 2023 batch was one of the largest PSSSB drives. A fresh round is **possible** as new stations open, but after such a big batch the next one may take time. Keep your fitness up and your documents ready.

## The 2023 recruitment in brief

- **Posts:** 991 Fireman and 326 Driver/Operator.
- **Education (Fireman):** matric, as reported.
- **Age:** 18-37 as on 1 January 2023, with relaxations.
- **Pay:** Rs 19,900 (7th CPC) plus allowances.
- **Selection:** written exam, then physical efficiency and measurement test, then counselling.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper:** [Fireman paper of 1 October 2023](pyq/psssb-fireman/index.html).
2. **Fire safety basics** - classes of fire, extinguishers, first aid.
3. **Punjab GK and reasoning** for Part B.
4. **Train for the physical test** - running and strength - from today.
5. **Don't take Part A Punjabi for granted.**
