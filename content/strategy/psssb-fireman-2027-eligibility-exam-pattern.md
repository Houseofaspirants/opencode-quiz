I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Fireman is half paper and half fitness. Many strong runners never reach the physical test because they took the written exam lightly - and the reverse happens too.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 01 of 2023** - **1317 posts** (991 Fireman, 326 Driver/Operator). The written exam was on **1 October 2023**, the physical test from **18 to 23 October 2023**, and the physical test result came on **15 November 2023**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Punjab has been adding fire stations and fire tenders, and the 2023 batch was one of the largest PSSSB drives. A fresh round is **possible** as new stations open, but after such a big batch the next one may take time. Keep your fitness up and your documents ready.

## The 2023 recruitment in brief

- **Posts:** 991 Fireman and 326 Driver/Operator.
- **Education (Fireman):** matric (minimum); Driver/Operator needs middle pass plus a 5-year-old heavy licence.
- **Age:** 18-37 on the last date of application, with relaxations.
- **Pay:** Rs 19,900 (7th CPC) plus allowances.
- **Selection:** written exam, then physical efficiency and measurement test, then counselling.

## Salary and pay (2023 round)

- **Fireman:** Rs 19,900 a month (7th CPC) - about Rs 2.39 lakh a year. Driver/Operator started at a reported Rs 21,700.
- **Probation:** reported as 3 years; you are confirmed, and further service benefits apply, only after it.
- **Allowances:** DA, HRA, medical and travel allowances are added once probation ends, as for other Punjab recruits.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## Eligibility in detail (official notice, Advt 01/2023)

The posts were in the municipal corporations, councils and nagar panchayats under the Local Government Department (non-cadre Group C). Applications ran from **28 January to 28 February 2023**.

| | Fireman | Driver/Operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Education | **Matric** (minimum) | **Middle (8th)** (minimum) |
| Licence and experience | - | A **heavy-vehicle driving licence held for at least 5 years**, and experience of running repairs to vehicles and fire appliances |
| Punjabi | Matric standard | Matric standard |
| Age | **18 to 37** on the last date of application, relaxation as per government policy | Same |

## Physical standards and fitness test (official, 2023)

**Measurements (both posts)**

- **Height:** at least **5 ft 5 in**.
- **Chest:** **33.5 in** unexpanded, with **1.5 in** expansion.
- **Eyesight:** **6/6 in both eyes without glasses**.
- For **women**, only the height and eyesight standards apply; chest is not measured.

**Physical fitness test (qualifying)**

1. **Run 100 yards carrying 60 kg** of weight (stones) within **one minute**.
2. **Lift the hook ladder to a vertical position** (from the 3rd and 6th rung, as the notice puts it).
3. **Climb a rope or a vertical pipe to 8-10 feet** from the ground.

## Written exam and syllabus (official final plan)

| Part | What | Questions | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Matric-level Punjabi | 50 | **Qualifying - 25 marks needed**, no negative marking |
| B | GK and current affairs (25), reasoning and maths (25), Punjab history and culture (16), Punjabi (13), English (12), ICT (9) | 100 | **Merit from Part B only**; 1/4 mark cut per wrong answer |

- **Time:** 2 hours 30 minutes, MCQ on OMR sheet.
- **GK** includes Punjab history from the 14th century and the Indian freedom struggle; **reasoning** covers coding-decoding, series, directions, percentages, ratio, interest, time and work, time and distance, mensuration and data interpretation.
- There is **no separate fire-science section** in this plan - but basic fire-safety knowledge still helps you in GK and science questions.

3. **Punjab GK and reasoning** for Part B.
