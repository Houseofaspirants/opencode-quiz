I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. This is a subject expert's exam. GK won't save you here - your MSc notes will.

> Our archive has the **2022 Senior Technical Assistant papers** in **Chemistry** and **Mathematics**. The Chemistry paper was split into **Part A and Part B**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Senior Technical Assistant posts are **few and irregular**. A 2027 round is **possible but not confirmed**. If you are already preparing for lecturer or Master Cadre exams in your subject, you're covering most of this paper anyway.

## What the 2022 papers show

- **Separate subject papers** - Chemistry and Mathematics in our archive.
- **Chemistry:** Part A and Part B; inorganic and coordination chemistry at postgraduate level.
- **Qualification:** relevant degree, often master's - confirm in the next notice.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve your subject paper:** [Senior Technical Assistant Chemistry and Maths 2022](pyq/psssb-senior-technical-assistant/index.html).
2. **Revise your MSc syllabus** topic by topic.
3. **Practise numericals** under time.
4. **Don't neglect Part A Punjabi** - it is qualifying.
