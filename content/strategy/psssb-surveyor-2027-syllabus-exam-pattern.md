I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Surveyor is a trade post, and three quarters of the paper is your ITI or diploma syllabus. A candidate who knows levelling and traversing cold will beat a general-studies topper here. This page sets out the official rules.

> The last detailed advertisement we have is **PSSSB Advertisement No. 03 of 2023** (published 29 June 2023, applications 4 to 29 July 2023) for **21 Surveyor posts** - 20 in the Water Resources Department and 1 in the Archives Department. A Surveyor paper was also held on **20 December 2025**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Surveyor posts came in **2022 (Advt 11/2022)** and **2023 (Advt 03/2023)**, and another Surveyor exam was held in December 2025. The main employer is the **Water Resources Department**, which keeps needing field staff for canals and drainage. The numbers are small, so a 2027 round is possible but not guaranteed. Prepare the trade syllabus now; it does not change between rounds.

## Eligibility (Advt 03/2023)

**Water Resources Department:**
1. **Matriculation** from a recognised board.
2. A **two-year ITI certificate of Draftsman**, or higher education in the same line such as a **diploma or degree in Civil or Mechanical Engineering**.

**Archives Department:** a graduate from a recognised university (see the advertisement for the full requirement).

Both: **Punjabi at matriculation level**; the certificate must be dated before the last date to apply.

## Age, fee and pay

- **Age (as on 1 January 2023):** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees, 47 for persons with disabilities, 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC/BC/EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.
- **Pay:** Rs 25,500 (Level-4, 7th CPC).

## Exam pattern (official plan)

| Section | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surveyor subject (Part A of syllabus) | 90 | 90 |
| GK, reasoning, English, Punjabi (Part B of syllabus) | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

- **2 hours**, OMR sheet, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- The 30 general questions split roughly into **GK 10, reasoning and maths 10, English 5, Punjabi 5**.
- Since October 2022 PSSSB also holds a **qualifying matric-level Punjabi paper** (50% needed); merit comes from the main paper.
- **Ties:** older candidate first.

## Surveyor syllabus

1. **Drawing basics** - drawing sheets, lettering, scales and R.F., projections, conventional signs
2. **Chain survey** - ranging, offsets, field book, errors, area of regular and irregular figures
3. **Compass survey** - bearings (W.C.B. and R.B.), included angles, local attraction, declination, closing error
4. **Plane table and theodolite** - radiation, intersection, traversing, consecutive and independent coordinates, balancing a traverse, area from coordinates
5. **Levelling** - instruments, adjustments, level book, reduced levels, curvature and refraction
6. **Tachometry** - constants, horizontal and vertical distances
7. **Contouring** - contour interval, characteristics, methods, interpolation

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper.** We have the [Surveyor question paper of 20 December 2025](pyq/psssb-surveyor/index.html).
2. **Numericals win marks.** Bearing conversions, reduced levels and traverse closing errors are quick marks if you practise them daily.
3. **Use your ITI or diploma notes.** The syllabus follows the ITI Surveyor and Draftsman course closely.
4. **Don't neglect the 30 general questions.** In a small-post exam, 5 extra marks can be 50 ranks.
