I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Master Cadre is a subject exam first and a general exam second. The paper tests what you studied in your degree and B.Ed, so a teacher who truly knows the subject has a real advantage over a pure exam-crammer.

> The last big round was the **4161-post Master Cadre recruitment** by the **Education Recruitment Board, Punjab**, for Mathematics, Science, English, Punjabi, Hindi and Social Science. The Maths and Hindi papers were held on **28 August 2022** and Science on **18 September 2022**; revised answer keys were released. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Punjab fills teacher posts in big rounds rather than every year. After the 2022 round, retirements keep creating vacancies in government schools, so a fresh Master Cadre recruitment in 2027-28 is **possible**. It needs state government approval, so nothing is certain until the board publishes a notice. Keep your PSTET and B.Ed documents ready.

## Eligibility (4161-post recruitment)

1. **Graduation with the subject.** For example, a Maths master needs Mathematics as a subject for all three years of graduation; Punjabi and Hindi need the language as an elective for three years.
2. **Marks:** 45% in graduation for general candidates, with a lower percentage for SC, ST, OBC, BC and physically handicapped candidates.
3. **B.Ed** from a recognised university with that subject as a teaching subject.
4. **Science** (Non-Medical or Medical) and **English** have detailed lists of accepted subject combinations; read them in the notification.
5. **PSTET Paper 2** is generally needed for master-level teaching posts in Punjab; confirm in the next notice.

## Exam pattern (2022 papers)

| Subject | What the 2022 key shows |
| --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 150 questions |
| Punjabi | 150 questions |
| Hindi | 150 questions |
| Science | Sections: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Botany, Zoology (50 each) |
| Social Science | Sections: History, Geography, Economics, Civics (75 each) |

- Several questions were **dropped** in the revised keys, which shows how closely the board reviews objections.
- Expect the paper to be at **graduation level in your subject**, with teaching methods from B.Ed.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the 2022 papers against the revised keys.** We have the [Master Cadre Maths and Science papers and the revised keys for five subjects](pyq/punjab-master-cadre/index.html).
2. **Revise your full graduation syllabus.** Questions go deep into the subject, not just school level.
3. **Keep PSTET Paper 2 valid.** Without it you may not be able to apply.
4. **Practise with a timer.** 150 questions demand quick recall of formulas, rules and facts.
