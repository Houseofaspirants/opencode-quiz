I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Anganwadi Supervisor is a field post with real responsibility - and the paper tests whether you understand child and mother care, not just GK.

> The last exam in our archive was held on **25 September 2022** for the post of **Supervisor** (ICDS). PSSSB's **final key covers 100 questions**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Supervisors oversee clusters of Anganwadi centres, and posts fall vacant through retirement and promotion. A fresh round is **possible**, but the timing depends on the department's requisition.

## The 2022 exam in brief

- **Date:** 25 September 2022.
- **Questions:** 100 (as per the final key).
- **Deleted questions:** marked * - 1 mark to all candidates.
- **Eligibility:** degree-level, with experience conditions in some rounds - confirm in the next notice.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper with its key:** [Supervisor paper of 25 September 2022](pyq/psssb-anganwadi-supervisor/index.html).
2. **ICDS basics** - the six services, nutrition, immunisation, growth monitoring.
3. **Women and child schemes** of the Centre and Punjab.
4. **Child development** - stages, needs, early education.
5. **GK, reasoning and Punjabi** for the rest.
