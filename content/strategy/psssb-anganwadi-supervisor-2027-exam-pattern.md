I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Anganwadi Supervisor is a field post with real responsibility - and the paper tests whether you understand child and mother care, not just GK.

> The last exam in our archive was held on **25 September 2022** for the post of **Supervisor** (ICDS). PSSSB's **final key covers 100 questions**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Supervisors oversee clusters of Anganwadi centres, and posts fall vacant through retirement and promotion. A fresh round is **possible**, but the timing depends on the department's requisition.

## The 2022 exam in brief

- **Date:** 25 September 2022.
- **Questions:** 100 (as per the final key).
- **Deleted questions:** marked * - 1 mark to all candidates.
- **Eligibility:** degree-level, with experience conditions in some rounds - confirm in the next notice.

## Syllabus - topics in the 2022 paper

Our archive does not have the official syllabus for this post. The **2022 paper (25 September 2022, 100 questions)** shows what the board tested, roughly in this order:

| Block | Approx. questions | Topics seen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | 10 | sentence improvement, synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, voice, analogy, idioms, narration |
| Punjabi | 10 | grammar, word meanings and usage |
| Computers (ICT) | 10 | types of computers, browsers, IP address, email (CC/BCC), Word, Excel functions, PowerPoint shortcuts |
| Punjab history and culture | 10 | capital before 1947, Punjabi novels and poets (Waris Shah), Nanakshahi coins, Doaba districts, folk singers, Punjab Kesari, Greek names of rivers, freedom fighters |
| Child development, nutrition and ICDS | 20 | child development stages, emotional and social development, inclusive education, family welfare, Integrated Child Protection Scheme, newborn feeding, protein-energy malnutrition, vitamins, Mission Indradhanush, duties of Anganwadi workers and helpers |
| GK and current affairs | 15 | sports, national film awards, NITI Aayog indices, new laws, international days, Booker Prize, science principles, Constitution |
| Quantitative aptitude | 15 | number system, HCF and LCM, ratio, mensuration |
| Reasoning | 10 | series, analogy, odd one out, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions, symbols, non-verbal figures |

The question counts are our estimate from the paper. Child development and nutrition is the one block only this post asks - give it the most time.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## Eligibility (official, Advt 07/2021)

The last round was **PSSSB Advt. No. 07 of 2021** - **112 posts of Supervisor, for women only**, in the Department of Social Security, Women and Child Development. Its written exam is the one held on **25 September 2022**.

- **Education - either:**
  1. a **graduate** from a recognised university in **Home Science, Nutrition, Child Development, Psychology, Economics, Social Work or Sociology**, **OR**
  2. a graduate in any subject **with 3 years' experience as a Bal Sewika, Gram Sewika or Anganwadi Worker**.
- **Punjabi:** Punjabi up to matriculation standard.
- **Priority:** women retrenched from a Punjab Government department, board or corporation after at least 2 years' work on a women and child welfare scheme get priority and up to 5 years' age relaxation.
- **Age (as on 1 January 2021):** 18 to 37; SC and BC of Punjab up to 42; government employees up to 45; persons with disabilities up to 47; widows and divorcees up to 40.

## Salary and pay

The notice fixed the pay at **Rs 29,200** under the 7th CPC pay matrix (Punjab Government letter of 4 March 2021), plus admissible allowances.

## Selection process (official)

- One **objective (MCQ) written exam**; a common merit list is made from it.
- You need at least **40% (40 marks)** in the written exam to be considered.
- Shortlisted candidates are called for document checks and counselling; ties go to the older candidate.
