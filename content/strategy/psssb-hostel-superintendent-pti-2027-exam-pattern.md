I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Hostel Superintendent cum PTI is a small post that many C.P.Ed holders don't know exists. It sits in the ITI system and asks for a physical education certificate plus experience, so the real competition is limited.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 13 of 2025** (published 28 November 2025, applications 5 to 27 December 2025). It had **68 Hostel Superintendent cum PTI posts** in the Department of Technical Education and Industrial Training, along with Storekeeper, Librarian and other posts. The written exam was held on **28 February 2026** and the final answer key is out. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

This post came in **2022** and again in **2025 (68 posts)**. Because the 2025 round has only just finished its exam, a fresh advertisement in 2027 is **less likely**; a 2028 round, or leftover posts re-advertised, is more realistic. If you are a C.P.Ed or D.P.Ed holder, also watch PTI and Craft Instructor recruitments, which ask for similar skills.

## Eligibility (Advt 13/2025)

1. **Matric in second division** or **10+2**.
2. A **C.P.Ed certificate** (D.P.Ed preferred).
3. **Two years of experience** (see the advertisement for what counts).
4. **Punjabi at matriculation level**.

## Age, fee and pay

- **Age:** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees, 47 for persons with disabilities, 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC/BC/EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.
- **Pay:** Rs 19,900 (Level-2, 7th CPC).

## Selection process

| Stage | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi (MCQ) | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Written exam** | Objective MCQ paper | **Merit list** |
| Counselling | Documents and experience certificate | Final step |

- **Marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Ties:** older candidate first.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve both real papers.** We have the [2022 paper and the 28 February 2026 paper with provisional and final keys](pyq/psssb-hostel-superintendent/index.html). The 2026 paper is the best guide to the current syllabus.
2. **Revise your C.P.Ed course** - rules of games, sports anatomy, first aid, fitness and Olympic and Asian Games facts.
3. **Cover Punjab GK and sports current affairs.** Medal winners, awards and national sports events are common.
4. **Keep your experience certificate in the right format.** It is checked at counselling.
