I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Group D is the PSSSB recruitment with the most applicants, because it needs only matric. That also makes it very competitive, so this page puts both official notifications in one place, so you know exactly what you are preparing for.

## Eligibility

1. **Matriculation with Punjabi** as one of the subjects, from a recognised institution (Punjab State Group D Service Rules, 1963).
2. You must also pass a **matric-level Punjabi test with at least 50%** in the exam itself (see the selection process below).
3. The qualification certificate must be issued before the last date of applying.

## Age limit (Advt. 04/2026, as on 1 January 2026)

- **Minimum:** 18 years
- **General:** up to 37 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 42 years
- **State and central government employees:** up to 45 years (with an NOC at counselling)
- **Persons with disabilities of Punjab:** up to 47 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain other married women:** up to 40 years
- **Ex-servicemen:** as per the Punjab Recruitment of Ex-Servicemen Rules, 1982

## Fee

| Category | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| General, freedom fighter, sports | Rs 1,000 |
| SC / BC / EWS | Rs 250 |
| Ex-servicemen and dependents | Rs 200 |
| Persons with disabilities | Rs 500 |

The fee is not refunded. Reservation is only for residents of Punjab, with a domicile certificate not older than 5 years at counselling.

## Selection process

One **objective (MCQ) written exam** in two parts:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Punjabi language, matriculation (10th) standard | **Qualifying only - you need at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | General paper | **Merit list is made only from Part B. 1 mark per question, 1/4 mark cut for each wrong answer** |

- Failing the Punjabi part rules you out, whatever your Part B score.
- **Ties:** the older candidate ranks higher; then the higher percentage in the required qualification.
- After the merit list, candidates are called for **counselling** and document verification, and are recommended to departments from the common merit list.

## What the 5 April 2026 paper looked like

The written exam under Advt. 08/2025 is the best guide to the syllabus:

- **150 questions** in one booklet.
- **Part A (questions 1-50): Punjabi** - grammar (spelling, synonyms, antonyms, idioms, punctuation), Punjabi months and numbers, dialects of Punjab, and Sikh history and Gurbani (Gurus, Sri Guru Granth Sahib, vaars).
- **Part B (questions 51-150), in English and Punjabi:** general knowledge and Indian geography, environment and ecology, general science, reasoning (directions, blood relations, syllogisms, series, data sufficiency), and an **ICT section at the end (questions 143-150)** - Excel formulas, MS Word and PowerPoint shortcuts, memory, protocols and network topology.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper first.** Download the [2026 Group D paper with the final and provisional answer keys](pyq/psssb-group-d/index.html) and attempt it in one sitting, with negative marking.
2. **Secure the Punjabi part early.** It is matric level, but 50% is a hard floor and it also tests Sikh history and Punjab culture - revise both, not only grammar.
3. **Mind the negative marking.** Each wrong Part B answer costs 1/4 mark, so skip blind guesses. With a huge applicant pool, a few careless marks can cost many ranks.
4. **Do not skip computers.** The last questions are basic ICT - Office shortcuts and formulas are easy marks.
5. **Use other PSSSB papers too.** Clerk, Patwari and Jail Warder papers repeat the same GK, reasoning and Punjabi topics.
