I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Senior Assistant (IT) looks like an office post, but two-thirds of its paper is a computer science exam. A BCA or B.Tech CS candidate starts well ahead here.

> **PSSSB Advertisement No. 05 of 2023** (Group-B, 157 posts in 11 post types) was published on **23 August 2023**; applications ran from **30 August to 27 September 2023**, fee by 29 September 2023. This page uses that advertisement and the board's official plan of written examination for Senior Assistant (IT).

## Latest notification

**Advt 05/2023** had **2 Senior Assistant (IT) posts** in the **Directorate of Tourism** (General 1, SC Mazhbi & Balmiki 1). The same advertisement also had Senior Assistant (12), Senior Assistant (Accounts) (2), Junior Auditor (60) and Personal Assistant (1). No newer Senior Assistant (IT) advertisement is in our archive yet.

## Eligibility (Advt 05/2023)

- **B.E. or B.Tech in IT, Electronics & Communication or Computer Science with at least 50% marks**, or **MCA with at least 50% marks plus 2 years of ICT experience**.
- **Matric with Punjabi.**

## Age and fee (Advt 05/2023)

- **Age (as on 1 January 2023):** 18 to 37 for General; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab; up to 45 for government employees (NOC at counselling); up to 47 for persons with disabilities of Punjab; up to 40 for widows and divorcees. Ex-servicemen deduct their service as per the 1982 rules.
- **Fee:** General, freedom fighters and sportspersons Rs 1,000; SC, BC and EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen and dependants Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500. Not refundable.

## Salary and pay

**Rs 35,400** basic pay (7th CPC Level 6) as per Advt 05/2023.

## Exam pattern (Advt 05/2023 official plan)

| Part | What | Questions / marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | GK and current affairs, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English, ICT | 40 |
| B | Computer science subject | 80 |
| | **Total** | **120** |

- **Time:** 2 hours, MCQ on an OMR sheet.
- **Negative marking:** 1/4 mark (0.25) for each wrong answer; none for a question left blank.

## Syllabus

**Part A (40):** GK and current affairs 10, Punjab history and culture 5, reasoning and numeracy 10, Punjabi 5, English 5, ICT 5.

**Part B (80) - computer science, six units:**

1. **Computer system architecture** - logic gates, Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential circuits, data representation and computer arithmetic, register transfer, basic computer organisation, microprogrammed control, CPU (addressing modes, RISC/CISC), I/O organisation and DMA, memory hierarchy, cache and virtual memory.
2. **Programming languages** - C (data types, arrays, structures, pointers, functions, file handling), OOP concepts, C++ (classes, constructors, overloading, inheritance, templates, exceptions, files).
3. **Database management systems** - architecture and data models, ER diagrams, relational model, relational algebra and calculus, Codd's rules, normalisation, query processing, transactions, concurrency, recovery and security.
4. **Operating systems** - OS structure and system calls, process management and scheduling, interprocess communication, and the other OS topics in the official plan.
5. **Data structures and algorithms** - arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists, trees (BST, AVL, B/B+ trees), graphs, sorting, searching and hashing; complexity and recurrences; divide and conquer, dynamic programming, greedy, backtracking, branch and bound.
6. **Data communication and computer networks** - transmission modes, signals, bandwidth, encoding and modulation, multiplexing, media and errors; topologies, LAN/MAN/WAN; OSI and TCP/IP models, addressing, switching, framing, flow and error control.

## How to prepare

1. **Part B decides merit.** Use a GATE-level or UGC-NET CS book for units 1, 3 and 5.
2. **C and C++ output questions** are quick marks - practise tracing code by hand.
3. **Do not skip Part A.** 40 general marks separate candidates with similar CS scores.
