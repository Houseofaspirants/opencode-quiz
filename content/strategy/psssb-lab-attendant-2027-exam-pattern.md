I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Lab Attendant looks like an easy post, but thousands apply. Basic science and a clean Part B decide who gets through.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 04 of 2023** - Lab Attendant among **95 Group C posts**. The written exam was on **15 February 2025**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Government labs in health, agriculture and education keep needing attendants, so **small batches** come regularly. A 2027 round is **possible**.

## The last round in brief

- **Advertisement:** 04/2023 (95 Group C posts in all).
- **Exam:** 15 February 2025.
- **Starting pay:** Rs 19,900 (7th CPC), as reported.
- **Qualification:** matric or 10+2 (science) - reports differ; confirm in the next notice.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper:** [Lab Attendant paper of 15 February 2025](pyq/psssb-lab-attendant/index.html).
2. **Class 10 science** - lab apparatus, chemicals, safety, basic biology.
3. **Punjab GK and reasoning** for Part B.
4. **Part A Punjabi** - aim well above 50%.
