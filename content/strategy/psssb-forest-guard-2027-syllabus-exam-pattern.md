I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. The Forest Guard paper is a pure general-studies test at Class 12 level, which makes it very similar to the Punjab Police Constable paper. If you are already preparing for police or clerk exams, you are most of the way there.

> PSSSB published the plan and syllabus for **Forest Guard under Advt No. 07 of 2022**. The written exam was held on **13 November 2022**, and revised answer keys came out for all four sets. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

The last big Forest Guard recruitment was in **2022**. Four years on, retirements and new plantation and wildlife work create fresh vacancies in the Forest Department, so a new round in 2027-28 is **possible**. It has not been announced. Because the syllabus is general, preparing for it also covers Constable, Jail Warder and Group-D type exams.

## Exam pattern (official plan, Advt 07/2022)

| Section | Approx. questions |
| --- | --- |
| GK and current affairs | 30 |
| Logical reasoning, mental ability and basic maths | 30 |
| Punjab history and culture | 20 |
| English | 15 |
| Punjabi | 15 |
| ICT (computers) | 10 |
| **Total** | **120** |

- **2 hours**, OMR sheet, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer; unattempted questions get no marks.
- Syllabus level: **up to Class 12**.
- Since October 2022 a **qualifying matric-level Punjabi** paper (50%) also applies to every PSSSB exam.

## Syllabus in brief

1. **GK** - polity, environment, current affairs, science and technology, economy, Punjab history from the 14th century, Indian freedom struggle, sports, cinema and literature
2. **Reasoning and maths** - coding-decoding, analogy, series, directions, relations; percentage, ratio, average, profit and loss, interest, time and work, time and distance, mensuration, data interpretation
3. **Punjab history and culture** - physical features, ancient history, Bhakti and Sufi movements, Sikh Gurus, Adi Granth, Sikh rulers, freedom movements of Punjab
4. **English** - grammar, synonyms, antonyms, idioms, voice and narration, sentence correction
5. **Punjabi** - grammar, spelling, idioms (akhan and muhavare), translation from English
6. **ICT** - computer basics, networks and internet, Word, Excel and PowerPoint

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the 2022 paper and keys.** We have the [Forest Guard 2022 paper with revised keys for sets A to D](pyq/psssb-forest-guard/index.html).
2. **Punjab history and culture is 20 marks.** Few general exams give it so much weight, so it is the best place to pull ahead.
3. **Reasoning and maths are another 30.** Daily timed practice turns these into safe marks.
4. **Keep fit.** Expect physical standards in the next advertisement; check them as soon as it comes.
