I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. My own route was the police exams, but I've watched friends crack the judiciary. The ones who made it treated the bare acts as their textbook from day one.

> The last round was the **Punjab Civil Service (Judicial Branch) Examination-2022** for **159 posts** of Civil Judge (Junior Division)-cum-Judicial Magistrate. The **prelims were held on 22 January 2023**, the **mains from 2 to 4 June 2023**, followed by a viva. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Punjab keeps adding courts and pendency is high, so judicial posts are filled **regularly** rather than once a decade. A new PCS (Judicial) round in or around 2027 is **likely**, but the date depends on the High Court's requisition. Law students in their final year should start now.

## Eligibility (in brief)

- **Law degree (LLB)** that qualifies you to enrol as an advocate.
- **Punjabi** at matriculation level.
- **Age** as in the notification - reports of the 2022 notice differ on the lower limit, so rely on the official text.

## Exam pattern

| Stage | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Prelims** | Objective - 125 questions in 2 hours (2022 exam); screening only |
| **Mains** | Descriptive law and language papers |
| **Viva voce** | Interview |

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real prelims paper:** [PCS (Judicial) 2023 prelims with proposed key](pyq/punjab-pcs-judicial/index.html).
2. **Bare acts first** - Constitution, CPC, CrPC/BNSS, IPC/BNS, Evidence Act/BSA, Contract and Specific Relief.
3. **Punjab local laws** - land and rent laws come up in mains.
4. **Write answers every week** for mains.
5. **Current legal affairs** - landmark judgments of the year.
