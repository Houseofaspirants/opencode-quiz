> Our archive has the **2023 PSSSB paper** for **Restorer and Lab Assistant (Agriculture)**. These posts come in **small numbers** inside mixed Group C advertisements. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Posts like this appear **occasionally and in small numbers**. A 2027 round is **possible but not confirmed**. Watch every PSSSB Group C advertisement - these posts are often tucked inside them.

## What we know

- **Paper:** 2023 (in our archive).
- **Posts:** a handful per round, inside mixed Group C advertisements.
- **Qualification:** differs by post - confirm in the next notice.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper:** [Restorer and Lab Assistant 2023 paper](pyq/psssb-restorer-lab-assistant/index.html).
2. **10+2 science** - chemistry and biology basics, lab safety and apparatus.
3. **Agriculture basics** - soils, fertilisers, seeds.
4. **GK, reasoning and Punjabi.**
