I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. The VDO round of 2022 was one of PSSSB's biggest graduate recruitments, and its paper is a straight general paper with a full 25 marks of maths.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 04 of 2022** for **792 Village Development Organiser (Gram Sewak) posts** in the Department of Rural Development and Panchayats. Applications ran from **15 May to 27 June 2022** (fee by 30 June 2022).

## Latest notification (Advt 04/2022)

- **Posts:** 792, Group-C, Department of Rural Development and Panchayats (33% reserved for women in each category). The category split included 79 posts for EWS, with ex-servicemen, sports, disability and freedom-fighter quotas.
- **Applications:** online at sssb.punjab.gov.in, 15 May to 27 June 2022; fee by 30 June 2022.
- No newer VDO advertisement is in our archive yet. For the related post in the same department, see the [Panchayat Secretary guide](exams/punjab-panchayat-secretary.html).

## Eligibility

1. **Matric with Punjabi** as a subject.
2. **10+2 in second division**.
3. **Graduation in any discipline** from a recognised university.
4. A **one-year diploma course in computer applications** from a recognised institution.

Candidates with a **B.Com or a higher commerce degree** get preference - **5 extra marks**.

## Age limit

**As on 1 January 2022:** 18 to 37 for General; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab; up to 45 for government employees; other relaxations (ex-servicemen, persons with disabilities) as per rules.

## Salary and pay

**Rs 19,900** - minimum pay admissible under the 7th CPC pay matrix, as per Advt 04/2022.

## Exam pattern (Advt 04/2022 official plan)

| Paper | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GK and current affairs, English, Punjabi, mental ability and maths | 100 | 100 | 2 hours |

- MCQ on an OMR sheet; **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer; no penalty for a blank.
- PSSSB papers advertised after October 2022 also have a qualifying matric-level Punjabi Part A, so expect it in any new VDO round.

## Syllabus (approx. weightage out of 100)

| Section | Marks | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GK and current affairs | 25 | Politics, environment, science and technology, economy, Punjab history from the 14th century, Indian freedom struggle, sports, cinema and literature |
| Mental ability | 25 | Coding-decoding, analogy, classification, series, directions, blood relations, mathematical operations, Venn diagrams, cubes and dice, mirror images, puzzles, non-verbal reasoning |
| Mathematical skills | 25 | Number system, LCM and HCF, percentage, ratio, average, ages, profit and loss, interest, time and work, time and distance, mensuration, data interpretation |
| Punjabi | 13 | Spelling, prefixes and suffixes, synonyms and antonyms, parts of speech, gender and number, idioms, translation from English |
| English | 12 | Grammar, synonyms and antonyms, one-word substitution, fill in the blanks, sentence correction, narration, voice, idioms |

## How to prepare

1. **Maths is a full quarter** - unusual for PSSSB. Practise arithmetic daily.
2. **Rural schemes and Panchayati Raj** fit the GK part for a post in this department.
3. **Use the Panchayat Secretary 2016 paper** for the same department's style.
