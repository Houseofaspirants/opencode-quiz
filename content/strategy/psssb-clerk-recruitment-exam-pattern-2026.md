I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. PSSSB Clerk is the most applied-for Punjab government job every year, so this page puts the official notification in one place, so you know exactly what is being tested.

> This page is based on PSSSB Advertisement No. 02 of 2026 (Clerk), published on 4 February 2026. As reported from the board's exam schedule, the **written exam is on 11 October 2026**. Always confirm the date on your admit card and on sssb.punjab.gov.in.

## The recruitment at a glance

- **Post:** Clerk (Group C), for various departments of the Punjab Government
- **Pay:** Level-2, Rs 19,900
- **Applications:** online at sssb.punjab.gov.in from 13 February to 6 March 2026 (now closed)
- **Posts:** the notification does not fix a number. It covers the demand letters departments send to the board up to **30 September 2026**, and the merit list is valid only for those. Reports put the count at about 531.

## Eligibility

1. A **bachelor's degree** in any discipline from a recognised university.
2. A **120-hour computer course** in office productivity or desktop publishing from a government-recognised or ISO 9001 institution, **or** a computer course equivalent to DOEACC **'O' Level**.
3. **Matriculation with Punjabi** as a compulsory or elective subject, or an equivalent Punjabi examination.

You must have every qualification by the last date of applying (6 March 2026).

## Age limit (as on 1 January 2026)

- **Minimum:** 18 years
- **General:** up to 37 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 42 years
- **State and central government employees:** up to 45 years (NOC needed at counselling)
- **Persons with disabilities of Punjab:** up to 47 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain other married women:** up to 40 years
- **Ex-servicemen of Punjab:** service period is deducted from age, and the result may exceed the limit by up to 3 years

## Fee

| Category | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| General, freedom fighter, sports | Rs 1,000 |
| SC / BC / EWS | Rs 250 |
| Ex-servicemen and dependents | Rs 200 |
| Persons with disabilities | Rs 500 |

The fee is not refunded.

## Selection process

**1. Objective (MCQ) written exam in two parts**

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Punjabi language, matriculation standard | **Qualifying only - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Main paper | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, and **1/4 mark is cut for every wrong answer**.
- Failing Part A rules you out, whatever your Part B score.
- The board said the detailed syllabus and pattern would be put on its website - check it there before the exam.

**2. Type test (qualifying)**

- **30 words per minute in English and in Punjabi**, on a computer.
- The Punjabi test is in the Unicode **Raavi** font.
- It is only qualifying, but you must pass it.

**3. Counselling** - document verification, then names are recommended to departments from the common merit list.

**Ties:** the older candidate first; then higher percentage in the required degree; then higher matric marks.

## Last-week plan before the exam

1. **Solve the real papers, timed.** We have the [2021 PSSSB Clerk papers (morning and evening shift) with the answer key](pyq/psssb-clerk/index.html), plus [Clerk IT 2021 and 2022](pyq/psssb-clerk-it/index.html) and [Clerk cum Data Entry Operator 2023](pyq/psssb-clerk-cum-data-entry-operator/index.html).
2. **Don't guess blindly.** With 1/4 negative marking, a guess pays only if you can rule out at least one option.
3. **Secure Part A first.** Revise matric-level Punjabi grammar - you need 50% before Part B even counts.
4. **Start typing practice now.** Practise 15 minutes a day in Raavi (Punjabi) and English. Many people clear the written exam and then lose the job at the type test.
5. **Revise, don't start new topics.** Use the last days for current affairs of the past 6 months, Punjab GK and computer basics.
