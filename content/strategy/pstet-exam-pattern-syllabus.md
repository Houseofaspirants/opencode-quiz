I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. PSTET is the eligibility test every government school teacher in Punjab needs, so this page puts the official PSTET guidelines and syllabus in one place, so you know exactly what you are preparing for.

> All facts below come from the PSTET 2024 notification of SCERT Punjab (public notice dated 10 October 2024, exam held on 1 December 2024). We will update this page when the next PSTET is announced.

## PSTET at a glance

- **Conducted by:** State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT), Punjab
- **Paper 1:** for teachers of classes 1 to 5 (primary stage)
- **Paper 2:** for teachers of classes 6 to 8 (elementary stage)
- **Both papers:** if you want to teach at both levels and meet both qualifications
- **Questions:** 150 MCQs of 1 mark each, two and a half hours, **no negative marking**
- **Language:** the question paper is bilingual
- **Validity:** the qualifying certificate is **valid for life**

PSTET 2024 key dates were: registration 16 October to 4 November 2024, form correction 5 to 8 November, roll numbers on 18 November, and the exam on 1 December 2024.

## Who can appear

1. Any **resident of India**.
2. A person who has the academic and professional qualifications in the **NCTE notification of 23 August 2010**, or who is **pursuing** a teacher education course recognised by the NCTE or RCI listed in it.
3. **No minimum or maximum age limit.**
4. Passing PSTET makes you eligible to apply; it does not give a right to appointment. The recruiting agency checks eligibility at the time of recruitment.

## Fee (PSTET 2024)

| Category | Paper 1 | Paper 2 | Both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General / OBC (Punjab) | Rs 1,000 | Rs 1,000 | Rs 2,000 |
| SC / ST / differently abled (Punjab) | Rs 500 | Rs 500 | Rs 1,000 |
| Ex-servicemen, self (Punjab) | Nil | Nil | Nil |
| Other states (all categories) | Rs 1,000 | Rs 1,000 | Rs 2,000 |

The fee is non-refundable. Dependents of ex-servicemen pay as per their own category.

## Paper 1 pattern (classes 1-5)

| Section | MCQs | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Child Development and Pedagogy | 30 | 30 |
| Language I (Punjabi) | 30 | 30 |
| Language II (English) | 30 | 30 |
| Mathematics | 30 | 30 |
| Environmental Studies | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

Questions are based on the class 1-5 syllabus, but their difficulty can go up to secondary level.

## Paper 2 pattern (classes 6-8)

| Section | MCQs | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Child Development and Pedagogy (compulsory) | 30 | 30 |
| Language I (Punjabi) (compulsory) | 30 | 30 |
| Language II (English) (compulsory) | 30 | 30 |
| Subject paper (one of the options below) | 60 | 60 |
| **Total** | **150** | **150** |

**Subject options in Paper 2:** Mathematics & Science (30 + 30) or Social Studies for those teachers; and since PSTET 2024 also Art & Craft, Physical Education, Home Science, Urdu, Music and Sanskrit - 60 MCQs each. Any other teacher takes either Social Studies or Mathematics & Science.

## Syllabus

**Child Development and Pedagogy (both papers, 30 questions)** - about 70% from child development and inclusive education, 30% from learning and pedagogy:

- Development and learning, heredity and environment, socialisation
- Piaget, Kohlberg and Vygotsky; child-centred and progressive education
- Intelligence and multi-dimensional intelligence, language and thought, gender
- Individual differences, assessment for and of learning, CCE
- Inclusive education: disadvantaged learners, learning difficulties, talented learners
- How children learn and fail, motivation, cognition and emotion

**Language I (Punjabi) and Language II (English)** - unseen passages (prose and poem in Punjabi, two prose passages in English) with comprehension, grammar and verbal ability, plus **language pedagogy (45-50% of the items)**: acquisition, principles of language teaching, the four skills, the role of grammar, remedial teaching.

**Mathematics (Paper 1)** - geometry and shapes, numbers and the four operations, measurement, weight, time, volume, data handling, patterns and money, plus pedagogy (25-30%).

**Environmental Studies (Paper 1)** - family and friends, work and play, animals, plants, food, shelter, water, travel and things we make, plus EVS pedagogy (25-30%).

**Mathematics & Science (Paper 2)** - number system, integers, fractions, algebra, ratio, geometry, symmetry, construction, mensuration, data handling; science: food, materials, the living world, moving things, electricity and magnets, natural phenomena and resources; with pedagogy (25-30% each).

**Social Studies (Paper 2)** - history (earliest societies to India after independence), geography (earth, environment, air, water, resources, agriculture), and social and political life (government, democracy, Constitution, Parliament, judiciary, media, gender), with pedagogy.

For the detailed topic lists, the notification refers to the PSEB and NCERT syllabus and textbooks of classes 1 to 8.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve real PSTET papers.** Start with the [PSTET previous year papers and answer keys](pyq/pstet/index.html) - CDP and pedagogy questions repeat in idea, if not in words.
2. **Pedagogy is half of every language section.** Do not just practise grammar; learn the teaching principles behind it.
3. **Read PSEB and NCERT class 1-8 books.** The content questions come straight from them.
4. **No negative marking** - attempt every question.
5. **Plan both papers** if you hold both qualifications; CDP and the two languages are common to both.
