I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. The Craft Instructor recruitment is one of the biggest PSSSB technical recruitments in years. This page puts everything from the official notification in one place, so ITI and diploma holders know exactly what they are preparing for.

> PSSSB Advertisement No. 03/2026 was published on 8 July 2026. Online applications ran from 10 July to 30 July 2026. All facts below come from that notification.

## The recruitment at a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | Craft Instructor (Group B), Industrial Training Wing, Department of Technical Education & Industrial Training |
| **Total posts** | **681** (218 for women) |
| **Annexure A** (degree / diploma route) | 250 posts (81 for women) |
| **Annexure B** (NTC / NAC route) | 431 posts (137 for women) |
| **Pay** | Rs 35,400 (Level 6) |
| **Applications** | 10 July to 30 July 2026 |
| **Age counted on** | 1 January 2026 |

The posts are spread over many ITI trades. The notification lists them trade by trade, so check your own trade in the PDF below.

## Eligibility

You need **matriculation with Science and Mathematics**, plus one of these:

1. **Degree route (Annexure A):** a degree or B.Voc in the relevant engineering branch, with **1 year of experience**.
2. **Diploma route (Annexure A):** a 3-year diploma in the relevant engineering branch, with **2 years of experience**. You must get the **National Craft Instructor Certificate (NCIC)** within 3 years of appointment.
3. **NTC / NAC route (Annexure B):** NTC or NAC in the relevant trade, **plus NCIC**, with **3 years of experience**.

You also need **Punjabi up to matriculation**, as the notification requires. All qualifications are counted on the last date of applying.

## Age limit (as on 1 January 2026)

- **Minimum:** 18 years
- **General:** up to 37 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 42 years
- **State and central government employees:** up to 45 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain other women:** up to 40 years
- **Ex-servicemen:** as per the Punjab ex-servicemen recruitment rules

## Fee

| Category | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| General and others | Rs 1,500 |
| SC / BC of Punjab | Rs 750 |
| Ex-servicemen, EWS, persons with disabilities of Punjab | Rs 500 |

## Selection process

1. **Written exam:** one objective (MCQ) paper. Each question carries **1 mark** and **1/4 mark is cut for each wrong answer**.
2. **Merit list:** made from the written exam marks.
3. **Ties:** the older candidate ranks higher; then the higher percentage in the required qualification; then the higher percentage in matriculation.
4. **Counselling** and document verification for candidates on the merit list.

The board said the detailed syllabus and the roll numbers will be put on its website, sssb.punjab.gov.in. Until then, prepare from the last instructor paper and the standard PSSSB pattern.

## What the last PSSSB instructor paper looked like

The PSSSB instructor exam held on **22 March 2025** is on this site. It followed the usual PSSSB style, with every question in English and Punjabi:

- **General knowledge and current affairs** - government schemes, World Environment Day themes, NITI Aayog SDG index, global indices.
- **Science and environment** - for example, permitted metal levels in drinking water.
- **Quantitative aptitude and data interpretation** - pie charts and tables.
- **Punjabi grammar** - parts of speech, sentence types and word meanings.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper first.** Download the [22 March 2025 PSSSB instructor paper](pyq/psssb-instructor/index.html) and attempt it in one sitting, with negative marking.
2. **Keep your trade theory fresh.** Craft Instructor exams usually include trade-related questions, so revise your ITI or diploma subjects alongside GK.
3. **Do not leave Punjabi for later.** Matric Punjabi is part of the eligibility and Punjabi grammar comes in the paper.
4. **Mind the negative marking.** Each wrong answer costs 1/4 mark, so skip blind guesses.
5. **Use other PSSSB papers.** Clerk, Excise Inspector and Patwari papers repeat the same GK, reasoning and computer topics.
