I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. District Treasurer sounds like a finance exam, but the 2023 written test was a general paper - the same preparation as Senior Assistant gets you there.

> This page is based on the board's **official plan of written examination for District Treasurer, Advt No. 13 of 2023**. Our archive does not have the advertisement itself, so eligibility, pay and posts are not given here.

## Latest notification

District Treasurer was advertised in **PSSSB Advt 13/2023**, alongside the [Technical Assistant](exams/psssb-technical-assistant.html) posts. No newer District Treasurer advertisement is in our archive yet.

## Exam pattern (Advt 13/2023 official plan)

| Paper | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GK, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English and ICT | 120 | 120 | 2 hours |

- MCQ on an OMR sheet; **1/4 mark (0.25) cut** for each wrong answer; no penalty for a blank.
- No subject part, and no separate qualifying Punjabi part in this plan.

## Syllabus (approx. weightage out of 120)

| Section | Marks |
| --- | --- |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 30 |
| Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability (incl. basic numeracy and data analysis) | 30 |
| Punjab History & Culture | 20 |
| Punjabi | 15 |
| English | 15 |
| ICT | 10 |

The topics inside each section match the [Senior Assistant syllabus](exams/psssb-senior-assistant.html).

## How to prepare

1. **Economy questions sit inside GK** - budget, banking and Punjab's finances are natural for this post.
2. **Numeracy and data tables** are part of the 30 reasoning marks; practise percentages and charts.
3. **Solve Senior Assistant and Clerk papers** - the section mix is almost the same.
