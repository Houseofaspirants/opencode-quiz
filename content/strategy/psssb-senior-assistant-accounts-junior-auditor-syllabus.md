I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. These two posts share one paper, and two-thirds of it is a B.Com accounting exam. If you are a commerce graduate, this is where your degree counts most.

> This page is based on the board's **official plan of written examination for Senior Assistant (Accounts) and Junior Auditor, Advt No. 05 of 2023**. Our archive does not have the advertisement itself, so eligibility, pay and posts are not given here.

## Latest notification

Both posts were advertised in **PSSSB Advt 05/2023**, together with Senior Assistant, Senior Assistant (IT) and Personal Assistant. No newer advertisement for them is in our archive yet. PPSC's Junior Auditor is a different recruitment - see our [PPSC Junior Auditor guide](exams/ppsc-junior-auditor.html).

## Exam pattern (Advt 05/2023 official plan)

| Part | What | Questions / marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | GK and current affairs, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English, ICT | 40 |
| B | Accounting subject | 80 |
| | **Total** | **120** |

- **Time:** 2 hours, MCQ on an OMR sheet.
- **Negative marking:** 1/4 mark (0.25) for each wrong answer; none for a question left blank.

## Syllabus

**Part A (40):** GK and current affairs 10, Punjab history and culture 5, reasoning and numeracy 10, Punjabi 5, English 5, ICT 5.

**Part B (80) - accounting:**

1. **Financial accounting** - concepts and conventions, double entry, journal, subsidiary books, ledger, trial balance, final accounts of sole traders and partnerships, capital vs revenue, depreciation (AS-6), bank reconciliation; partnership admission, retirement, death, dissolution and amalgamation; consignment and joint venture; computerised accounting and **Tally ERP 9**.
2. **Corporate accounting (Companies Act, 2013)** - issue, forfeiture and reissue of shares, redemption of preference shares and debentures, bonus and rights issues, underwriting, company final accounts and managerial remuneration, profit prior to incorporation, holding company statements, amalgamation (Ind AS 14), internal reconstruction and liquidation.
3. **Management accounting** - scope and tools, analysis of financial statements, comparative, common-size and trend statements, **ratio analysis**, fund flow, **cash flow (Ind AS 3)**.
4. **Cost accounting** - cost sheet, material control and pricing, labour cost and incentive plans (Taylor, Halsey, Rowan), overheads, **CVP and break-even**, marginal costing decisions, budgeting (flexible, zero-base, performance), standard costing and variance analysis.

## How to prepare

1. **Cost and management accounting are numerical** - practise ratios, break-even and variances until they take under a minute.
2. **Corporate accounting** questions follow Companies Act and Ind AS wording - read the standards, not only guide books.
3. **Keep 40 general marks safe** with Punjab history, reasoning and grammar.
