I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Jail Warder is one of the most popular 12th-pass jobs in Punjab, and it has a physical test that many people underestimate. This page puts the official notification in one place.

> This page is based on PSSSB Advertisement No. 08 of 2026 (Warder and Matron), published on 28 March 2026. As reported, the **written exam was held on 26 July 2026** and the **result was declared on 30 September 2026**. The PMT/PET schedule will be announced on sssb.punjab.gov.in. If you are preparing for the next round, the same rules are likely to apply.

## Eligibility

- **10+2** or equivalent from a recognised board.
- **Punjabi** as a compulsory or elective subject (or an equivalent Punjabi examination), and matric-level Punjabi under the service rules.
- You must hold the qualification by the last date of applying.

## Age limit (as on 1 January 2026)

**Warder:**
- **General:** 18 to 27 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 32 years
- **Pensioned soldiers:** up to 40; **army reservists:** up to 35
- **Ex-armed forces (3+ years) or 5+ years in police:** up to 30
- **Government employees:** up to 45

**Matron:**
- **General:** 18 to 27 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 32 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain separated women:** up to 35
- **Government employees:** up to 45

## Selection process

1. **Objective (MCQ) written exam in two parts:**
   - **Part A** - matric-level Punjabi, **qualifying only, at least 50%**.
   - **Part B** - the **merit list is made only from Part B**. 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut for each wrong answer**.
2. **PMT and PET** for candidates called from the merit list - both are qualifying.
3. **Counselling** and document verification, then the final result.

**Ties:** older candidate first; then higher percentage in the required qualification; then matric marks.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers.** We have all six shifts of the [2021 PSSSB Jail Warder exam (27, 28 and 29 August)](pyq/psssb-jail-warder/index.html).
2. **Start physical training with the written exam.** The 100 m in 15 seconds is one attempt only - practise starts and sprints three times a week.
3. **The shot put and rope climb need technique.** Get coaching for the shot put glide and build grip strength for the rope.
4. **Secure the Punjabi part.** 50% in Part A is a hard floor.
5. **Watch the negative marking.** At -1/4, skip a question unless you can rule out at least one option.
