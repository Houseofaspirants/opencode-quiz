I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Clerk (Accounts) is the one PSSSB clerk exam where a B.Com student starts ahead: three-quarters of the main paper is your own degree. This page collects what the official exam plans say.

> The latest round is **PSSSB Advertisement No. 12 of 2026** (published 18 September 2026, applications 25 September to 19 October 2026). It has **34 Clerk (Accounts) posts** at Rs 19,900 - 25 in the Employment Generation, Skill Development and Training department and 9 in Excise and Taxation field offices. The written exam date is still awaited. Before that, the last separate round was Advt 19/2021, examined in December 2021 and again in November 2022.

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Advt 12/2026 shows the demand is back: **34 Clerk (Accounts) posts** in one advertisement. Note that eligibility differs by department - the Employment Generation posts ask for a graduate with Statistics, Mathematics or Accounts as a subject, while the Excise and Taxation posts ask for a **commerce degree**. Both need matric Punjabi and a 30 wpm typing test, and merit comes from Part B of the written exam. Whether more posts follow in 2027 depends on new demand, but anyone preparing for Advt 12/2026 is already on the right track.

## Exam pattern (2022 official plan)

| Part | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clerk (Accounts) subject syllabus | 90 | 90 |
| GK, English, Punjabi, logical reasoning and mental ability | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

- **Time:** 2 hours, MCQ on OMR sheet.
- **Negative marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Since October 2022:** every PSSSB exam opens with a **qualifying matric-level Punjabi Part A** (50% needed); merit comes only from the main paper.
- After the written exam: a **qualifying typing test** (30 wpm, English and Punjabi) and counselling.

## Subject syllabus

1. **Financial accounting** - accounting standards (depreciation, inventories, revenue recognition, cash flow, EPS), share capital, bonus and rights shares, buy-back, company final accounts, amalgamation and reconstruction.
2. **Cost accounting** - job, process and activity-based costing, CVP analysis, marginal costing for decisions, budgeting, standard costing and variances.
3. **Taxation** - income tax heads (salary, house property, business, capital gains, other sources), set-off and carry forward, deductions; salient VAT and service tax provisions (revise GST, which replaced them, as well).
4. **Auditing** - company audit, divisible profits and dividends, tax audit, audit of banks, insurance and NGOs.
5. **Financial management** - ratios, fund flow and cash flow, capital budgeting, cost of capital, working capital.
6. **Financial markets and institutions** - money and capital markets, banks and regulators.

## Eligibility (Advt 12/2026)

The degree you need depends on the department you apply to:

| Department | Posts | Degree needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employment Generation, Skill Development and Training | 25 | Graduation with at least **one subject of Statistics, Mathematics or Accounts** |
| Excise and Taxation (field offices) | 9 | A **degree in commerce** |

- **Punjabi:** matric with Punjabi as a subject (Rule 17, Punjab Civil Services Rules, 1994).
- **Typing:** a qualifying test in **Punjabi and English at 30 words per minute**.
- **Cut-off date:** you must hold the qualification by the **last date to apply (19 October 2026)**.

## Age, fee and salary

- **Age (as on 1 January 2026):** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab; 45 for state and central government employees (they need an NOC at counselling); 47 for persons with disabilities of Punjab; 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General and sportspersons Rs 1,000; SC, BC and EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen and dependants Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500. The fee is not refunded.
- **Salary:** **Rs 19,900** at entry (7th CPC, Level-5 in Excise and Taxation), plus allowances as per Punjab rules.

## Exam pattern 2026 (official, Excise and Taxation)

The board has published the plan for the **Excise and Taxation Clerk (Accounts)** paper of Advt 12/2026. It replaces the old 90 + 30 pattern.

| Part | What | Questions / marks | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Matric-level Punjabi | 50 | **Qualifying - 25 marks (50%) needed**, no negative marking |
| B(i) | GK and current affairs (10), Punjab history and culture (5), reasoning and mental ability (10), Punjabi (5), English (5), ICT (5) | 40 | Counts for merit |
| B(ii) | Clerk (Accounts) subject | 60 | Counts for merit |

- **Time:** 2 hours 30 minutes, MCQ on OMR sheet.
- **Negative marking in Part B:** 0.25 mark for every wrong answer; no penalty for a question left blank.
- **Merit:** only candidates who clear Part A are ranked, on their **Part B score (out of 100)**.

## Syllabus 2026 (official, Excise and Taxation)

**Part B(ii) - the subject syllabus (60 marks)**

- **Accounting:** analysis of financial statements - comparative, common-size and trend statements, **ratio analysis**, fund flow and **cash flow (Ind AS 3)**; accounting standards, IFRS convergence and Ind AS; price-level and human-resource accounting; marginal costing and **CVP analysis**; strategic cost management, balanced scorecard, **activity-based**, target, life-cycle, kaizen and back-flush costing; budgetary control, performance and zero-base budgeting; uniform costing, responsibility accounting, transfer pricing, foreign-currency accounting, international taxation.
- **Financial management:** capital budgeting (payback, ARR, **NPV, IRR**, profitability index), cost of capital and WACC, leverage, capital structure, dividend policy, working capital, international finance and FDI.
- **Financial system:** Indian banking, development banks (IFCI, SIDBI), NBFCs, **RBI**, NABARD, **SEBI**; money and capital markets; merchant banking, underwriting, mutual funds, credit rating, venture capital, plastic money.
- **Auditing:** principles and types of audit, auditor independence, Standards on Auditing, **company audit under the Companies Act, 2013**, auditor's report, management, cost, bank and EDP audit.
- **Business laws:** **Contract Act, 1872** (including indemnity, guarantee, bailment, pledge), **Companies Act, 2013**, **Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881**, Competition Act, 2002.
- **Tax laws:** Income Tax Act, 1961 - residential status, the **five heads of income**, set-off and carry forward, deductions, computation for individuals, HUF, firms and companies; tax planning, avoidance and evasion.

The Employment Generation posts are in the same advertisement; check the board's site for their own paper plan.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers.** We have [Clerk Accounts papers from 2021 and 2022 with official answer keys](pyq/psssb-clerk-accounts/index.html).
2. **Revise B.Com numericals fast.** Ratios, depreciation and CVP questions must take under a minute each.
3. **Income tax changes every year.** Read the current slabs and deductions, not your old textbook.
4. **Keep 30 GK marks safe.** Punjab history, basic reasoning and grammar come from the common PSSSB syllabus.
5. **Learn Raavi typing.** The typing test is qualifying, but it decides who actually gets the post.
