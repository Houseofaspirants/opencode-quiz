I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Clerk (Accounts) is the one PSSSB clerk exam where a B.Com student starts ahead: three-quarters of the main paper is your own degree. This page collects what the official exam plans say.

> The last Clerk (Accounts) recruitment was **Advertisement No. 19 of 2021**, examined on 11 December 2021, with a fresh exam on 19 November 2022. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.** Prepare on the pattern below and we will update this page when a new advertisement comes.

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Accounts clerks are needed in every treasury, finance branch and department office, but PSSSB advertises them only when departments send a separate demand. Since 2022 most clerk vacancies have gone into the general clerk advertisements (Advt 05/2024, 11/2025 and 02/2026). A separate Clerk (Accounts) round in 2027 is **possible but not announced**. The good news: the accounts syllabus also covers a large part of other commerce posts such as Junior Auditor and Inspector Audit, so the preparation is never wasted.

## Exam pattern (2022 official plan)

| Part | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clerk (Accounts) subject syllabus | 90 | 90 |
| GK, English, Punjabi, logical reasoning and mental ability | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

- **Time:** 2 hours, MCQ on OMR sheet.
- **Negative marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Since October 2022:** every PSSSB exam opens with a **qualifying matric-level Punjabi Part A** (50% needed); merit comes only from the main paper.
- After the written exam: a **qualifying typing test** (30 wpm, English and Punjabi) and counselling.

## Subject syllabus

1. **Financial accounting** - accounting standards (depreciation, inventories, revenue recognition, cash flow, EPS), share capital, bonus and rights shares, buy-back, company final accounts, amalgamation and reconstruction.
2. **Cost accounting** - job, process and activity-based costing, CVP analysis, marginal costing for decisions, budgeting, standard costing and variances.
3. **Taxation** - income tax heads (salary, house property, business, capital gains, other sources), set-off and carry forward, deductions; salient VAT and service tax provisions (revise GST, which replaced them, as well).
4. **Auditing** - company audit, divisible profits and dividends, tax audit, audit of banks, insurance and NGOs.
5. **Financial management** - ratios, fund flow and cash flow, capital budgeting, cost of capital, working capital.
6. **Financial markets and institutions** - money and capital markets, banks and regulators.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers.** We have [Clerk Accounts papers from 2021 and 2022 with official answer keys](pyq/psssb-clerk-accounts/index.html).
2. **Revise B.Com numericals fast.** Ratios, depreciation and CVP questions must take under a minute each.
3. **Income tax changes every year.** Read the current slabs and deductions, not your old textbook.
4. **Keep 30 GK marks safe.** Punjab history, basic reasoning and grammar come from the common PSSSB syllabus.
5. **Learn Raavi typing.** The typing test is qualifying, but it decides who actually gets the post.
