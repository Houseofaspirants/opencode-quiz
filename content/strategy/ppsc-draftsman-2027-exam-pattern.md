I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Draftsman papers look easy to people who draw every day, yet many lose marks on surveying, RCC and the GK block. Here is what the official 2022 booklet tells us.

> The last round was the **PPSC advertisement of June 2022** for **119 Group B posts - 27 Head Draftsmen and 92 Draftsmen**, as reported. Selection was through one **joint written test**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Draftsmen work in PWD, irrigation, local bodies and other engineering offices. PPSC handles the Group B posts in **occasional rounds**, so a 2027 round is **possible but not confirmed**. Keep an eye on PSSSB too - it recruits Junior Draftsmen separately.

## Exam pattern (2022)

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Questions | **120** objective |
| Time | **2 hours** |
| Marks | **480** - 4 per question |
| Negative marking | **1 mark** per wrong answer |
| Interview | None reported in 2022 |

## What the paper asked

- **Engineering drawing** - projection symbols, layers, sections, conventions.
- **Building construction** - arches, lintels, foundations, concrete mixes.
- **Structures** - composite beams, RCC basics.
- **Surveying** - chain survey, theodolite, principles.
- **AutoCAD** commands and drawing standards.
- **GK, current affairs and reasoning** at the end.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper.** We have the [2022 Draftsman paper, all sets](pyq/ppsc-draftsman/index.html).
2. **Revise your ITI/diploma drawing and construction notes** chapter by chapter.
3. **Practise AutoCAD theory**, not just commands you use daily.
4. **Don't skip the GK block** - with negative marking, sure marks here matter.
