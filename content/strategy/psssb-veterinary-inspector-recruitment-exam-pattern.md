I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Veterinary Inspector is a Level-5 job open to diploma holders in veterinary science, with much less competition than the general posts. This page puts the official notification in one place.

> This page is based on PSSSB Advertisement No. 10 of 2026, published on 24 April 2026. As reported, the **written exam was held on 19 July 2026** and the **result came on 14 August 2026**; document verification follows. If you are preparing for the next round, the same rules are likely to apply.

## The recruitment at a glance: 150 posts

- **Post:** Veterinary Inspector (Group C), Department of Animal Husbandry, Punjab
- **Posts:** 150 (including backlog), 51 of them for women
- **Pay:** Rs 29,200-92,300 (Level-5, 7th CPC)
- **Applications:** 4 to 25 May 2026 (closed)

## Eligibility

1. **10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology** from a recognised board.
2. A **two-year diploma in Veterinary Science and Animal Health Technology** (or equivalent) from a recognised university.
3. Those currently enrolled in this diploma, and diploma holders with 10+2 in Biology/Maths, Physics, Chemistry and English, are also eligible under the notification's proviso.
4. **Matriculation with Punjabi** (or equivalent), under the service rules.

## Age limit (as on 1 January 2026)

- **Minimum:** 18 years
- **General:** up to 37 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 42 years
- **State and central government employees:** up to 45 years (NOC at counselling)
- **Persons with disabilities of Punjab:** up to 47 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain other married women:** up to 40 years
- **Ex-servicemen:** service deducted from age; result may exceed the limit by up to 3 years

## Selection process

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying only - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Main paper | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut for each wrong answer**.
- After the merit list: **counselling** and document verification. Names go to the department as per the posts available at that time.
- **Ties:** older candidate first; then higher percentage in the required qualification; then matric marks.
- Sports-category merit follows the Sports Department notification of 9 March 2026.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers.** We have the [PSSSB Veterinary Inspector papers of 2021 and 2023, with answer keys](pyq/psssb-veterinary-inspector/index.html).
2. **Your diploma subjects carry the paper.** Animal anatomy, diseases, vaccination, breeding and livestock management - revise your diploma notes.
3. **Don't drop the Punjabi part.** 50% in Part A is a hard floor.
4. **Know Punjab's livestock schemes.** Dairy, animal husbandry and state schemes often show up as GK.
5. **Mind the negative marking.** At -1/4, guess only when you can rule out at least one option.
