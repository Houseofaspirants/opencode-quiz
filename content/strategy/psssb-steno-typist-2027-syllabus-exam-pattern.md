I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Steno-Typist is different from every other PSSSB post: the written paper only opens the door, and your shorthand speed decides whether you walk through it. This page sets out the official rules so you train for the right thing.

> The latest round is **PSSSB Advertisement No. 05 of 2026** (published 11 March 2026, applications 12 March to 2 April 2026), filling Steno-Typist demands received by 30 September 2026 from one merit list ranked on the **steno test**. Before that, **Advt 14/2025** had **109 Steno-Typist posts** (Group-C). **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

PSSSB advertised Steno-Typists in **2023 (Advt 07/2023)** and again in **2025 (Advt 14/2025, 109 posts)**. Every department and district office needs stenos, and many posts fall vacant through promotion to Senior Assistant and Personal Assistant. A new advertisement in 2027 or 2028 is realistic. Shorthand takes months to build, so the people who start now are the ones who clear the test when it comes.

## Eligibility (Advt 14/2025)

1. A **bachelor's degree** from a recognised university or institution.
2. **Punjabi stenography** - you must be able to take dictation and transcribe it.
3. A **computer course of at least 120 hours** from a recognised institute (the advertisement lists the accepted courses).
4. **Punjabi at matriculation (10th) level**, as per the Punjab Civil Services Rules, 1994.

## Age, fee and pay

- **Age:** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees, 47 for persons with disabilities, 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC/BC/EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.
- **Pay:** Rs 21,700 to 69,100 (Level-3, 7th CPC).

## Selection process

| Stage | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi (MCQ) | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Objective written paper | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| Steno test | Punjabi dictation at **80 wpm**, transcribed at 15 wpm | The deciding stage |
| Documents | Verification of certificates | Final step |

- **Steno test:** two passages of **200 words each**, dictated at 80 words per minute. You transcribe them at 15 words per minute, and up to **8% mistakes** in total are allowed.
- The board said the Part B syllabus would be uploaded on its website, so check the latest notice before the exam.

## Syllabus (what each test covers)

The board has not published a topic list for the Steno-Typist written test; it said any further syllabus would be put on its website. From the official notices:

- **Punjabi written test (qualifying):** matriculation-standard Punjabi, objective MCQ - grammar, usage, vocabulary and comprehension at class 10 level. You need 50%.
- **Part B written paper (Advt 14/2025, qualifying):** an objective general paper; 50% needed. Prepare the usual PSSSB general syllabus - GK, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English and computers - as in the [Clerk syllabus](exams/psssb-clerk/syllabus.html).
- **Steno skill test (decides merit):** Punjabi shorthand - two passages of 200 words dictated at **80 wpm**, transcribed at **15 wpm**, up to **8% mistakes**. Accuracy in Punjabi spelling and punctuation is the real syllabus here.

## Latest round: Advt 05/2026 (official notice)

PSSSB published **Advertisement No. 05 of 2026** for Steno-Typist on **11 March 2026**; applications ran from **12 March to 2 April 2026**. There is no fixed post count: the board fills the Steno-Typist demands that departments send **by 30 September 2026** from one merit list. Demands received after that date are not filled from this list.

**Eligibility**

1. A **bachelor's degree** from a recognised university or institution.
2. A pass in a **Punjabi stenography test** held by the board.
3. A **120-hour computer course** with hands-on use of a PC in office-productivity or DTP applications, from a government-recognised or **ISO 9001-certified** institution - **or** a course equivalent to **DOEACC (NIELIT) 'O' level**.
4. **Matric with Punjabi** (Rule 17, Punjab Civil Services Rules, 1994).
5. All qualifications must be complete by the **last date to apply**.

**Age, fee and pay**

- **Age (as on 1 January 2026):** 18 to 37; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab; 45 for government employees; 47 for persons with disabilities of Punjab; 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC, BC and EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen and dependants Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500 (not refundable).
- **Pay:** **Rs 21,700** (Level-2 as printed in the notice).

**How selection works in Advt 05/2026**

| Stage | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Written test | Matric-level Punjabi, objective MCQ | **Qualifying - 50% needed** |
| Steno skill test | **Punjabi shorthand at 80 wpm** - two passages of 200 words (400 in all), transcribed at **15 wpm**; up to **8% mistakes** allowed | **Decides the merit** |
| Counselling | Document check, then recommendation to the department | Final step |

- **Merit is made from the steno test itself:** candidates are ranked by **number of mistakes, from 0 up to 32**. Fewer mistakes means a higher rank.
- **Ties:** older candidate first, then higher percentage in the qualifying degree, then higher matric marks.
- The board said any further written-test syllabus or pattern would be put on its website.

In other words, for this round your **shorthand accuracy is your rank**. Clearing the Punjabi test is only the entry ticket.

## How to prepare

1. **Start shorthand today.** Moving from 60 to 80 wpm in Punjabi takes most people three to six months of daily dictation.
2. **Practise transcription on a computer**, in Raavi or the font the board names. Speed in shorthand is wasted if you cannot type it out cleanly in time.
3. **Clear Part A with ease.** Use the [2023 Punjabi qualifying paper with its answer key](pyq/psssb-steno-typist/index.html) to see the level.
4. **Prepare Part B like a clerk paper.** GK, Punjab, English, reasoning and computer basics are the safe bet until the syllabus is out.
5. **Keep your 120-hour computer certificate ready.** A missing certificate is a common reason for rejection.
