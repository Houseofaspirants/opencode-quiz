I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Steno-Typist is different from every other PSSSB post: the written paper only opens the door, and your shorthand speed decides whether you walk through it. This page sets out the official rules so you train for the right thing.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 14 of 2025** (published 28 November 2025, applications 4 to 26 December 2025) for **109 Steno-Typist posts** (Group-C) across departments. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

PSSSB advertised Steno-Typists in **2023 (Advt 07/2023)** and again in **2025 (Advt 14/2025, 109 posts)**. Every department and district office needs stenos, and many posts fall vacant through promotion to Senior Assistant and Personal Assistant. A new advertisement in 2027 or 2028 is realistic. Shorthand takes months to build, so the people who start now are the ones who clear the test when it comes.

## Eligibility (Advt 14/2025)

1. A **bachelor's degree** from a recognised university or institution.
2. **Punjabi stenography** - you must be able to take dictation and transcribe it.
3. A **computer course of at least 120 hours** from a recognised institute (the advertisement lists the accepted courses).
4. **Punjabi at matriculation (10th) level**, as per the Punjab Civil Services Rules, 1994.

## Age, fee and pay

- **Age:** 18 to 37 years; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees, 47 for persons with disabilities, 40 for widows and divorcees.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC/BC/EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.
- **Pay:** Rs 21,700 to 69,100 (Level-3, 7th CPC).

## Selection process

| Stage | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi (MCQ) | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Objective written paper | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| Steno test | Punjabi dictation at **80 wpm**, transcribed at 15 wpm | The deciding stage |
| Documents | Verification of certificates | Final step |

- **Steno test:** two passages of **200 words each**, dictated at 80 words per minute. You transcribe them at 15 words per minute, and up to **8% mistakes** in total are allowed.
- The board said the Part B syllabus would be uploaded on its website, so check the latest notice before the exam.

## How to prepare

1. **Start shorthand today.** Moving from 60 to 80 wpm in Punjabi takes most people three to six months of daily dictation.
2. **Practise transcription on a computer**, in Raavi or the font the board names. Speed in shorthand is wasted if you cannot type it out cleanly in time.
3. **Clear Part A with ease.** Use the [2023 Punjabi qualifying paper with its answer key](pyq/psssb-steno-typist/index.html) to see the level.
4. **Prepare Part B like a clerk paper.** GK, Punjab, English, reasoning and computer basics are the safe bet until the syllabus is out.
5. **Keep your 120-hour computer certificate ready.** A missing certificate is a common reason for rejection.
