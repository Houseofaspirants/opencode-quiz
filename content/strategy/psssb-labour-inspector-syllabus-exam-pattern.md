I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Labour Inspector Grade-I uses exactly the general paper of PSSSB Clerk and Excise Inspector, so one preparation covers all three.

> This page is based on the board's **official plan of written examination for Labour Inspector Grade-I (Group-C), Advt No. 02 of 2024**. Our archive does not have the advertisement itself, so eligibility, pay and posts are not given here.

## Latest notification

Labour Inspector Grade-I was advertised in **PSSSB Advt 02/2024**, which also had the Technician and Technical Officer posts. No newer Labour Inspector advertisement is in our archive yet.

## Exam pattern (Advt 02/2024 official plan)

| Part | What | Questions / marks | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Matric-level Punjabi | 50 | **Qualifying - 25 marks needed**, no negative marking |
| B | GK, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English, ICT | 100 | **Merit list from Part B only** |

- **Time:** 2 hours 30 minutes, MCQ on an OMR sheet.
- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer; no penalty for a blank.

## Syllabus

**Part A - Punjabi (qualifying):** lives and works of the Sikh Gurus, antonyms and synonyms, idioms, proverbs, parts of speech, prefixes and suffixes, number and gender, punctuation, correcting words and sentences, Punjabi forms of English words, numbers, months and days, Punjabi language, Punjab history and culture.

**Part B - weightage (approx., out of 100):**

| Section | Marks |
| --- | --- |
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs | 25 |
| Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability (reasoning 17, numeracy 4, data analysis 4) | 25 |
| Punjab History & Culture | 17 |
| Punjabi | 13 |
| English | 12 |
| ICT | 8 |

## How to prepare

1. **Use the Clerk and Excise Inspector papers** - same Part B structure.
2. **Labour schemes and laws come in GK** - the four labour codes and Punjab's labour welfare schemes are worth a revision.
3. **GK and reasoning are half of Part B**; make them daily habits.
