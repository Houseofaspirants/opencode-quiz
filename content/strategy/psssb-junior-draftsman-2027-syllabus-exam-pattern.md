I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Junior Draftsman is a technical post, and 90 of the 120 questions come from your own trade. Toppers here are usually ITI or diploma students who revised their whole course, not GK specialists. This page gathers the official plan.

> PSSSB published the plan and syllabus for **Junior Draftsman Civil** and **Junior Draftsman (Electrical/Mechanical)** under **Advt No. 09 of 2022**. Papers were held on **4 October 2021** (Advt 12/2021) and again in **2026**, for which the final answer key is out. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Junior Draftsman posts have come in **2021, 2022 and later rounds**, and a 2026 exam has just been completed. Draftsmen are needed by **Water Resources, PWD (B&R), Local Government and power departments**, so vacancies keep coming back. A new advertisement in 2027-28 is possible. Eligibility is usually an ITI Draftsman certificate in the trade or a related diploma; check the exact rule in the next advertisement.

## Exam pattern (official plan)

| Section | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trade subject (Part A of syllabus) | 90 | 90 |
| GK, reasoning, English, Punjabi (Part B of syllabus) | 30 | 30 |
| **Total** | **120** | **120** |

- **2 hours**, OMR sheet, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer; unattempted questions get no marks.
- For **Electrical/Mechanical**, Part A has both subjects and you **attempt any one**.
- A **qualifying matric-level Punjabi** part (50%) applies to every PSSSB exam since October 2022.
- **Ties:** older candidate first.

## Junior Draftsman Civil syllabus

1. Safety, instruments and **B.I.S. drawing codes** (IS 962-1989, SP-46:2003), lettering, dimensioning, scales
2. **Projections** - orthographic, isometric, oblique, perspective
3. **Building materials** - stone, brick, lime, cement, mortar, concrete, timber and substitutes, paints
4. **Building construction** - masonry, foundations, doors, windows, stairs, roofs, damp-proofing
5. **Surveying** basics and **electrical wiring** in buildings
6. **Computer aided drafting** (CAD)
7. **Roads**, **irrigation engineering**, and **estimating and costing**

## Junior Draftsman Electrical/Mechanical syllabus

- **Electrical:** basic concepts and network theorems, AC fundamentals, measuring instruments, signals and systems, electrical machines and FHP motors, power systems, control systems, generation, transmission and distribution, estimation and costing, utilisation of electrical energy, basic electronics.
- **Mechanical:** engineering drawing as per SP-46:2003 - lines, lettering, dimensioning, orthographic and sectional views, projection of solids, development of surfaces, interpenetration curves, isometric projection and free-hand sketching of machine parts.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers.** We have the [Junior Draftsman paper of 2021 with its key, and the 2026 final key](pyq/psssb-junior-draftsman/index.html).
2. **Revise your ITI or diploma course fully.** Materials, construction and estimating give the most questions in Civil.
3. **Learn the IS codes and standard sizes.** Brick size, sheet sizes, scales and symbols are quick, sure marks.
4. **Keep the 30 general questions steady.** Practise GK, reasoning and Punjabi weekly.
