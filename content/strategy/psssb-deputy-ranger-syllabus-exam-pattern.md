I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Deputy Ranger is the senior field post in the Forest Department, and its paper is a science-graduate's exam. Choose your two subjects well and the rest follows.

> PSSSB advertised Deputy Ranger in **Advt 07/2022** (with Forester and Forest Guard, applications 11 May to 1 July 2022) and again in **Advt 04/2023** (5 posts, with Laboratory Assistant), for which the board issued a **revised exam plan**.

## Latest notification

- **Advt 04/2023:** **5 Deputy Ranger posts** (General 3, SC Ramdasia & others 1, BC 1; 1 of them reserved for women) in the **Office of the Principal Chief Conservator of Forests**, sharing one paper with Laboratory Assistant under the board's revised plan. Applications ran from 28 August to 22 September 2023; age as on 1 January 2023.
- **Advt 07/2022:** Deputy Ranger, Forester and Forest Guard - 204 posts in all, almost all Forest Guards. See the [Forester guide](exams/psssb-forester.html) for that round.

## Eligibility (Advt 07/2022)

- A **B.Sc. or B.E. degree** from a recognised university.
- **Matric with Punjabi.**
- **Age:** 18 to 37 years as on 1 January 2022; SC and BC of Punjab up to 42, government employees up to 45, persons with disabilities up to 47, widows and divorcees up to 40.

## Salary and pay

**Rs 35,400** (7th CPC), as per Advt 07/2022 - higher than Forester (Rs 25,500) and Forest Guard (Rs 21,700).

## Exam pattern

**Revised plan, Advt 04/2023 (Deputy Ranger and Laboratory Assistant):**

| Part | What | Questions / marks | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Matric-level Punjabi | 50 | **Qualifying - 25 marks needed**, no negative marking |
| B (I) | GK, reasoning, English, Punjabi | 30 | Counts for merit |
| B (II) | Science - attempt **any 2** of Botany, Zoology, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics | 70 | Counts for merit |

- **Time:** 2 hours 30 minutes. **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer in Part B; merit from Part B only.

**Earlier plan, Advt 07/2022:** 120 MCQs in 2 hours - **90 science** (any 2 of the same five subjects) and **30 general** - with 1/4 negative marking and no Punjabi Part A.

## Syllabus

**General section (30):** GK and current affairs 10 (including geography and Punjab history from the 14th century), reasoning and numeracy 10, English 5, Punjabi 5.

**Science section - choose any two:**

- **Physics:** mechanics of particles and rigid bodies, oscillations and waves, geometrical and wave optics (interference, diffraction, polarisation, lasers), electricity and magnetism, thermal and statistical physics, quantum mechanics, atomic and molecular physics, nuclear and particle physics, solid state physics and electronics.
- **Chemistry:** atomic structure, states of matter, chemical bonding, spectroscopy, equilibrium, redox and electrochemistry, kinetics, surface chemistry and solutions; periodicity, s-, d- and f-block elements, coordination, nuclear and analytical chemistry; organic basics, hydrocarbons and IUPAC naming, halogen and oxygen compounds, polymers, biomolecules and reagents.
- **Mathematics:** algebra, calculus (limits, differentiation, integration, partial derivatives), vectors, trigonometry, coordinate geometry, probability, statics and dynamics.
- **Zoology and Botany:** diversity of the living world, structural organisation, animal and plant physiology, reproduction, cell biology, genetics and evolution, biology and human welfare, biotechnology, ecology and environment.

The level is roughly B.Sc. Pick the two subjects of your own degree.

## How to prepare

1. **Fix your two subjects on day one** and study only those for the 70 science marks.
2. **Forestry GK helps** - Punjab's forest cover, wildlife sanctuaries and Ramsar sites appear in the general section.
3. **Train for the walk test early**; 25 km in 4 hours needs weeks of practice.
