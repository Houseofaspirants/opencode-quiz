I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Dairy is central to Punjab's farm economy, and this post shows up more often than most people expect.

> PSSSB advertised **Dairy Development Inspector (Grade II)** posts in **2021 (25), 2022 (37) and 2023 (21, Advt 08/2023)**. The paper in our archive was held on **20 November 2022**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Three rounds in three years is a **steady pattern**. Another round in the next cycle is **reasonably likely**, though the number of posts will stay modest.

## Recent rounds in brief

- **2021:** 25 posts.
- **2022:** 37 posts; paper on 20 November 2022.
- **2023 (Advt 08/2023):** 21 posts; selection by written exam and documents.
- **Qualification:** graduation-level, with a subject condition in some rounds - check the next notice.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## Eligibility (official, Advt 08/2023)

Dairy Development Inspector Grade-II came in **PSSSB Advt. No. 08 of 2023** (111 posts across nine cadres; **21 DDI posts** in the Dairy Development Department, 7 of them for women).

- **Education:** **B.Tech in Dairy Technology**, OR **B.Sc. Dairying in Dairy Technology or Dairy Husbandry** from a recognised university.
- **Punjabi:** Punjabi up to matric.
- **Age (as on 1 January 2023):** 18 to 37 for General; up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab; up to 45 for state and central government employees (NOC needed at counselling); up to 47 for persons with disabilities of Punjab; up to 40 for widows and divorcees; ex-servicemen as per the 1982 rules.
- **Domicile:** reservation only for residents of Punjab, with a residence certificate not older than 5 years.

The post carries **Rs 29,200** as the initial pay (7th CPC pay matrix, as reported for Advt 08/2023), plus allowances sanctioned by the Punjab Government.

## Syllabus (official, Dairy Development Inspector Grade-II)

The board's plan for this post is an MCQ paper (OMR, 2 hours, 1/4 negative marking) with **90 subject questions (Part A) and 30 general questions**. In the 2025 exam PSSSB also used its standard qualifying matric-level Punjabi paper, and counselling was called **on Part B marks only**.

**Subject syllabus (the 90 questions):**

- **Milk production management and dairy development:** Indian and exotic dairy breeds, breeding and selection, farm practices, care at calving, management of lactating and dry animals, clean milk production, housing and hygiene, common diseases, feeds and nutrition, digestive system of ruminants, mammary system and milk let-down, reproduction, AI and embryo transfer, Indian dairying, five-year plans, Operation Flood, cooperatives and the Anand pattern.
- **Dairy extension education** and **marketing management and international trade.**
- **Physical chemistry and chemistry of milk:** composition of milk of different species, milk proteins, fat and lactose, chemical quality assurance, preparation of reagents.
- **Dairy engineering:** fluid mechanics, heat and mass transfer, thermodynamics, refrigeration and air-conditioning, dairy process engineering, equipment for indigenous products, plant design and layout.
- **Dairy microbiology:** introductory microbiology, good hygiene practices, starter cultures and fermented milks.
- **Dairy technology:** market milk, traditional dairy products, ice-cream and frozen desserts, fat-rich products, cheese, condensed and dried milk, by-products, packaging, judging of dairy products.

**General part (30 marks):** GK and current affairs 10, reasoning and mental ability 10, English 5, Punjabi 5.
