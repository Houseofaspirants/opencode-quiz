I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Dairy is central to Punjab's farm economy, and this post shows up more often than most people expect.

> PSSSB advertised **Dairy Development Inspector (Grade II)** posts in **2021 (25), 2022 (37) and 2023 (21, Advt 08/2023)**. The paper in our archive was held on **20 November 2022**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Three rounds in three years is a **steady pattern**. Another round in the next cycle is **reasonably likely**, though the number of posts will stay modest.

## Recent rounds in brief

- **2021:** 25 posts.
- **2022:** 37 posts; paper on 20 November 2022.
- **2023 (Advt 08/2023):** 21 posts; selection by written exam and documents.
- **Qualification:** graduation-level, with a subject condition in some rounds - check the next notice.

## How PSSSB papers work now

For its recent advertisements PSSSB uses a two-part objective paper:

| Part | What | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Part A** | Matric-level Punjabi | **Qualifying - at least 50%** |
| **Part B** | Post-specific paper plus GK, reasoning and related subjects | **Merit list is made only from Part B** |

- **Part B marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Standard rules:** age 18-37 (up to 42 for SC/BC of Punjab, 45 for government employees); fee Rs 1,000 general, Rs 250 SC/BC/EWS, Rs 200 ex-servicemen, Rs 500 persons with disabilities. Confirm all of this in the next advertisement for this post.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper:** [DDI paper of 20 November 2022](pyq/psssb-dairy-development-inspector/index.html).
2. **Dairy science basics** - milk composition, breeds, feeding, milk products and cooperatives.
3. **Punjab dairy facts** - Milkfed, Verka, schemes.
4. **GK and reasoning** for Part B.
