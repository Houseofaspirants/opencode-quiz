I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Naib Tehsildar is one of the best Group-B posts open to a plain graduate in Punjab: a Level-6 job in the Revenue Department with a clear path to Tehsildar. This guide puts the last official notification in one place and tells you what to expect next.

> The latest round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 01 of 2025**, published on 27 June 2025, with applications from 1 to 21 July 2025. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.** The rules below are the ones most likely to be repeated; we will update this page as soon as a new notification comes.

## The last recruitment at a glance

- **Post:** Naib Tehsildar (Group-B), Department of Revenue, Rehabilitation and Disaster Management, Punjab
- **Recruiting body:** PSSSB in 2025 (PPSC held the 2022 and 2023 exams)
- **Posts:** 13, all backlog - ex-servicemen, sports and persons with disabilities
- **Pay:** Rs 35,400 (Level-6, 7th CPC)
- **Same advertisement:** 3 Audit Officer and 135 Inspector Audit posts in the Co-operation Department

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Nobody can promise a date, but the history is useful. PPSC filled a large batch of 78 posts in the 2022 cycle (re-examined in June 2023), and PSSSB advertised the leftover backlog in 2025. Tehsildar offices run short of Naib Tehsildars every year through promotions and retirements, so a fresh general-category round is a realistic possibility once the department sends a new demand. **The safe plan is to prepare on the 2025 pattern now**, because the syllabus has stayed close to the PSSSB common pattern.

## Eligibility

1. **Graduation** from a recognised university or institution, in any stream.
2. **Punjabi at matriculation (10th) level** or equivalent, under the Punjab Civil Services Rules, 1994.
3. After selection you must clear the training and the departmental examinations specified by the Government.

## Age limit (as on 1 January 2025)

- **Minimum:** 18 years
- **General:** up to 37 years
- **SC and BC of Punjab:** up to 42 years
- **State and central government employees:** up to 45 years (NOC at counselling)
- **Persons with disabilities of Punjab:** up to 47 years
- **Widows, divorcees and certain other married women:** up to 40 years

## Selection process and exam pattern

The 2025 round had **one objective written exam**, followed by counselling and document checks. The merit list is made from the written marks.

| Section | Questions (approx.) |
| --- | --- |
| General knowledge and current affairs (polity, environment, science, economy, Indian history, geography, sports) | 30 |
| Punjab history and culture | 20 |
| Logical reasoning, numerical skills and data analysis | 30 |
| Punjabi grammar | 15 |
| English grammar | 15 |
| ICT (computer basics, internet, Word, Excel, PowerPoint) | 10 |
| **Total** | **120** |

- **Time:** 2 hours. **Marking:** 1 mark per question, **1/4 mark cut** for each wrong answer.
- **Ties:** older candidate first, then higher marks in the qualifying degree, then matric marks.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real papers first.** We have the [Naib Tehsildar papers of 2022 and 2023 with official answer keys](pyq/ppsc-naib-tehsildar/index.html), plus the official syllabus file.
2. **Punjab history carries 20 marks.** Sikh Gurus, Sikh rulers, the Bhakti and Sufi movements and Punjab's freedom struggle come up every time.
3. **Reasoning is 30 marks - a quarter of the paper.** Practise daily, timed.
4. **Don't skip ICT.** Ten easy marks for anyone who has used Word and Excel.
5. **Respect the negative marking.** At -1/4, guess only when you can cut at least two options.
