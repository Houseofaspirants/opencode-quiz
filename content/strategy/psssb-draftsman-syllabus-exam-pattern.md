I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Draftsman is a small recruitment, but its paper is very predictable: two-thirds of it is the ITI Draftsman Civil course.

> **PSSSB Advertisement No. 05 of 2023** (Group-B, 157 posts in 11 post types) was published on **23 August 2023**; applications ran from **30 August to 27 September 2023**, fee by 29 September 2023. This page uses that advertisement and the board's official plan of written examination for Draftsman.

## Latest notification

**Advt 05/2023** had **1 Draftsman post** (SC Mazhbi & Balmiki) in the **Department of Cultural Affairs, Archaeology and Museums, Punjab**. No newer PSSSB Draftsman advertisement is in our archive yet.
For the larger drafting recruitments, see the [PSSSB Junior Draftsman](exams/psssb-junior-draftsman.html) and [PPSC Draftsman](exams/ppsc-draftsman.html) guides.

## Eligibility (Advt 05/2023)

- **Matric with Punjabi** as one of the subjects.
- A **two-year ITI certificate in Draftsman Civil**, or a higher qualification in the same discipline from a recognised university or institute.

## Age and fee (Advt 05/2023)

- **Age (as on 1 January 2023):** 18 to 37 for General; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab; up to 45 for government employees (NOC at counselling); up to 47 for persons with disabilities of Punjab; up to 40 for widows and divorcees. Ex-servicemen deduct their service as per the 1982 rules.
- **Fee:** General, freedom fighters and sportspersons Rs 1,000; SC, BC and EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen and dependants Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500. Not refundable.

## Salary and pay

**Rs 35,400** basic pay (7th CPC) as per Advt 05/2023.

## Exam pattern (Advt 05/2023 official plan)

| Part | What | Questions / marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | GK and current affairs, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English, ICT | 40 |
| B | Draftsman (civil) subject | 80 |
| | **Total** | **120** |

- **Time:** 2 hours, MCQ on an OMR sheet.
- **Negative marking:** 1/4 mark (0.25) for each wrong answer; none for a question left blank.

## Syllabus

**Part A (40):** GK and current affairs 10, Punjab history and culture 5, reasoning and numeracy 10, Punjabi 5, English 5, ICT 5.

**Part B (80) - Draftsman Civil:**

1. **Drawing basics** - BIS and IS 962:1989 code for architectural and building drawings, drawing layout, lines, lettering, dimensioning, scales and RF, orthographic, isometric, oblique and perspective views.
2. **Building materials** - stones, bricks, lime, pozzolana, cement and its tests, sand, clay products, mortar and concrete, timber and its seasoning and preservation; plywood, block board, particle board, FRP and MDF.
3. **Protective materials** - tar, bitumen, asphalt, paints, varnishes, metals and plastics.
4. **Building construction** - sequence of construction, parts of a building, stone and brick masonry and bonds, foundations and their failure, shoring, scaffolding, underpinning, DPC, arches, lintels, RCC reinforcement, doors and windows, roofs, floors, trusses, carpentry joints and stairs.
5. **Surveying** - chain, compass and plane table surveying, levelling, theodolite and total station.
6. **CAD** - introduction to computer aided drafting and its basic commands.
7. **Planning, estimating and services** - principles of planning and orientation, local building bye-laws, quantity estimation, roads and railway gauges, culverts and bridges, irrigation, canals, reservoirs and hydroelectric power, sanitation and sanitary fittings.

## How to prepare

1. **Revise your ITI Draftsman Civil theory books** - Part B follows that course closely.
2. **IS 962 conventions, scales and projections** give quick marks; practise them with diagrams.
3. **Do not skip Part A.** With 40 marks and negative marking, it often decides a one-post merit list.
