I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Building Inspector is a technical post, and the PPSC paper rewards people who know their subject and don't guess. This page is built from the official question booklet of 2022 and the advertisement of that year.

> The last round was the **PPSC advertisement of June 2022** for **157 Building Inspector (Group B) posts** in the Department of Local Government, Punjab. The written test was held in **November 2022**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

Building Inspectors work in Municipal Corporations, Councils and Nagar Panchayats, and cities keep growing. But PPSC fills this post in **large, occasional rounds** - the 2022 round was the big one. A new round depends on the department's next requisition, so treat 2027 as **possible, not certain**. Start the technical part now; it takes the longest.

## The 2022 recruitment in brief

- **Posts:** 157 - reported as 131 for Municipal Councils/Nagar Panchayats and 26 for Municipal Corporations.
- **Initial pay:** Rs 35,400.
- **Qualification:** a technical diploma/degree as set out in the advertisement - read the exact discipline list in the next notification.

## Exam pattern (November 2022)

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Questions | **120** objective (MCQ) |
| Time | **2 hours** |
| Marks | **480** - 4 marks per question |
| Negative marking | **1 mark** cut for each wrong answer |
| Calculator | Not allowed |

With a 1-mark penalty on a 4-mark question, a wild guess costs you more than it usually gains. Attempt a question only when you can cut at least two options.

## What the paper actually asked

- **Architecture and history of buildings** - temple styles, famous structures, the golden ratio.
- **Planning and bye-laws** - ground coverage, FAR, setbacks, fire-safety norms and approval steps.
- **Construction materials** - cement, concrete, bricks, timber.
- **Building services** - air-conditioning systems, energy agencies such as PEDA.
- **Surveying and drawing tools** - total station, AutoCAD commands.
- **Landscaping** terms.
- The last part was **reasoning, maths and current affairs**.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve the real paper first.** We have the [2022 Building Inspector paper, all sets](pyq/ppsc-building-inspector/index.html).
2. **Revise your diploma core** - building materials, construction, planning and surveying.
3. **Read the Punjab building bye-laws** basics - FAR, coverage and setbacks are easy marks.
4. **Practise percentage and profit-loss** for the maths questions.
5. **Follow Punjab and national current affairs** for the last block.
