I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. ETT is the dream post for thousands of D.El.Ed students in Punjab - and because so many apply, a few marks decide everything.

> The last big round was **5994 ETT posts** advertised by the **Education Recruitment Board, Punjab** in **October 2022** (corrigendum dated **1 December 2022**). The written exam was on **5 March 2023** - **Paper 1 (Punjabi)** and **Paper 2**. **No 2027 notification has been released yet.**

## Will there be posts in 2027?

ETT is the **biggest teaching cadre** in Punjab. Teachers retire every year and the state keeps announcing primary-teacher drives, so a new ETT round is **likely** in the coming cycle. The exact year depends on the government. Get your PSTET done now - without it you can't even apply.

## Eligibility (5994 recruitment)

1. **Graduation.**
2. **ETT / D.El.Ed** (two-year elementary teacher training) or an equivalent qualification.
3. **PSTET Paper 1** pass.
4. **Punjabi** at matriculation level.

## Exam pattern (5 March 2023)

| Paper | What | Questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paper 1** | Punjabi language | 100 |
| **Paper 2** | Main subject paper | 100 |

Both final keys gave free marks for a few deleted questions (shown as * in the key), so always check the final key, not the provisional one, when you score yourself.

## How to prepare

1. **Solve both papers with their keys:** [ETT 2023 papers, provisional and final keys](pyq/punjab-ett-teacher/index.html).
2. **Clear PSTET first** - our [PSTET guide](strategy-pstet-exam-pattern-syllabus.html) explains it.
3. **Punjabi grammar and comprehension** daily.
4. **Child development and pedagogy** - common to PSTET and ETT.
5. **Maths, EVS and GK** at primary-teacher level.
