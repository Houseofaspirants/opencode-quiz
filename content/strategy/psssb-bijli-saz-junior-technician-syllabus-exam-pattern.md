I cleared the Punjab Police Sub Inspector exam with **Rank 2 (713/800)**. Bijli Saz-cum-Junior Technician is the PSSSB post for an ITI electrician: 60 of the 100 merit marks come straight from your trade.

> The last round was **PSSSB Advertisement No. 08 of 2023** (published 8 September 2023, applications 8 to 29 September 2023, fee by 1 October 2023). It had **111 posts** across nine post types, including **1 Bijli Saz-cum-Junior Technician** post.

## Latest notification (Advt 08/2023)

- **Posts:** 1 (General), in the **Department of Revenue, Rehabilitation and Disaster Management** (Financial Commissioners' Secretariat, Chandigarh).
- **Same advertisement:** Tabla Instructor, Library Restorer, Ship and Aero Modelling Instructor, Dairy Development Inspector, Line Superintendent, Driver and others.
- **Fee:** General Rs 1,000; SC, BC and EWS Rs 250; ex-servicemen and dependants Rs 200; persons with disabilities Rs 500.

## Eligibility

1. **Matriculation**.
2. **ITI diploma in the Electric trade**.
3. **Punjabi up to matric**.

**Age:** 18 to 37 years for General; up to 42 for SC and BC of Punjab; up to 45 for government employees; up to 47 for persons with disabilities of Punjab; up to 40 for widows and divorcees.

## Salary and pay

**Rs 19,900** (7th CPC), as per Advt 08/2023.

## Exam pattern (Advt 08/2023 official plan)

| Part | What | Questions / marks | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Matric-level Punjabi | 50 | **Qualifying - 25 marks needed**, no negative marking |
| B | GK, Punjab history and culture, reasoning, Punjabi, English, ICT | 40 | Counts for merit |
| C | Electrical trade subject | 60 | Counts for merit |

- **Time:** 2 hours 30 minutes. **1/4 mark (0.25) cut** for each wrong answer in Parts B and C.

## Syllabus

**Part B (40):** GK and current affairs 10, Punjab history and culture 5, reasoning and numeracy 10, Punjabi 5, English 5, ICT 5.

**Part C (60) - electrical trade:**

- Safety rules, fire extinguishers, first aid; BIS standards and trade tools, marking, drilling and sheet-metal tools.
- Fundamentals of electricity, conductors and insulators, joints and soldering, underground cables.
- Ohm's and Kirchhoff's laws, series and parallel circuits, Wheatstone bridge, magnetism and electromagnetism, inductance and capacitance, AC fundamentals (RMS, power factor, impedance).
- Cells and batteries, electrolysis, electroplating, solar cells.
- Domestic and industrial wiring, MCB/ELCB/MCCB, load estimation and testing; earthing; illumination and lamps.
- Measuring instruments (PMMC, moving iron, energy meters), range extension and calibration.
- Domestic appliances, transformers (single and three phase, CT and PT, transformer oil), DC generators and motors, speed control and windings, three-phase induction motors.

## How to prepare

1. **Your ITI theory is Part C** - revise your ITI electrician syllabus unit by unit.
2. **Numericals on Ohm's law, power and transformers** are quick, sure marks.
3. **Part B is only 40** but decides ties between trade-strong candidates.
