## What changed this week

The old habit was rereading. The new one is writing: one page per subject,
written from memory on Saturday and checked against the notes on Sunday.

## The slot that survived

7:30 pm, two weekdays and one weekend morning. Fixed slot, no negotiation,
phone in another room.

## What I stopped doing

- Highlighting entire pages.
- Starting a new subject before the last one was tested.
- Counting hours instead of questions attempted.
