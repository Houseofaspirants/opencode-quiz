To achieve anything in life, two things matter most: **discipline** and **consistency.** For clearing a government exam they matter even more, because this is not a one-day job; it is a long process.

## What discipline means

Whatever you study each day, study it properly and well. Not sitting with a book open for an hour and learning nothing. Sitting and studying are two different things.

## What consistency means

Whatever you did one day, repeat it the next. If you studied 9 hours today, study 9 hours tomorrow, and 9 hours the day after. Keep this going until you reach your goal.

## This is a long process

Clearing a government exam takes time. Even if you follow everything properly, it can take **6 months to a year.** Motivation is not enough to last that long; you need discipline and consistency. Without them, it does not happen.

## What discipline and consistency looked like in my routine

- At my study table at 10 am every day.
- 1.5-hour shifts with fixed breaks, 8-10 hours daily.
- Gym every evening, 8 hours of sleep every night.
- Planning the next day every night from 12 to 1 am.
- 1-7-30 revision for every topic.

None of these works if you do it for one day. They work when they happen every day for months. Even after I failed to clear the paper the first time, I did not give up my preparation, and the next time my marks went from 613 to 713.
