Many aspirants ask me what happens after selection. This is what my training routine at the Punjab Police Academy, Phillaur looked like when I spoke on the podcast. Our batch was appointed the year before and the training period was one year.

## Two programmes

The day is divided into an **outdoor programme** and an **indoor programme**, from early morning to evening.

## A typical day

| Time (approx.) | What happens |
|---|---|
| Early morning | PT period in track suit |
| After PT | 30-minute break to bathe, change into uniform and tie the turban |
| Morning | Weapons lecture: handling and knowledge of different weapons |
| Mid-morning | 1.5-hour break for food and rest |
| Late morning to afternoon | Indoor classes |
| Afternoon | 2-hour break |
| Late afternoon | Parade lecture and parade practice |
| Evening | Games period: any sport you like |

## What is taught indoors

There are almost 17 subjects. Some of them:

- Investigation
- BNS and BNSS, the new criminal laws
- Forensic medicine and science
- Security

## Extra skills

Two or three times a week there are also swimming lectures, and we are taught horse riding. These are skills most of us never had a chance to learn before.

## What I learned from it

Getting the uniform needs hard work, and wearing it properly needs the same hard work. What I value most is the fixed schedule. When you follow a proper routine for a year, many good habits come by themselves. The academy gives you a platform to improve yourself physically and mentally.
