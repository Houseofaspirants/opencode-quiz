Current affairs are very important in every Punjab government exam. In my experience, you cannot clear any government exam without preparing current affairs well. But many students stay confused about how many months of current affairs to read.

## The short answer

- **Minimum:** **1 year** of current affairs before your exam date. This is a must for every Punjab government exam.
- **If you have time:** **1.5 years** before your exam date.

For example, if your exam is in August, read everything from August of last year to now. If you have time, go back 6 more months.

## Why current affairs matter so much

In every Punjab government exam, you will see **10 to 15 questions** from current affairs. That is enough marks to make a big difference on the merit list.

The best part is that the current affairs syllabus is fixed: a set period (1 or 1.5 years) and all the material in one place. That makes it a subject where you can score full marks.

## How to cover this period

1 year means 12 monthly magazines, and 1.5 years means 18. I never read current affairs daily, only from monthly magazines. My blog [Not Daily, Monthly Magazines](blog-current-affairs-monthly-magazine-method) explains this method in detail.

## Things to remember

- Count back from your exam date, not from today.
- Finish 1 year first, then add the 6 months before it if time is left.
- Revision matters more than reading. Before the exam, revise all the magazines at least 4-5 times.
