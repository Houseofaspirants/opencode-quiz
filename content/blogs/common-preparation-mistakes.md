These are the mistakes I have seen many students make, and one of them I made myself. With each mistake is what to do instead.

| # | Mistake | Do this instead |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skipping previous year papers | Solve PYQs in the last 3 months. I made this mistake myself: 613 marks, cut-off 623. |
| 2 | Daily current affairs from YouTube and websites | Monthly magazine, finished in one day, then revised 4-5 times |
| 3 | Sitting with a book open but not studying | Whatever you study, study it with discipline and properly |
| 4 | 10 hours in week one, quitting in week three | Start with 2-3 shifts and add slowly; studying every day matters most |
| 5 | Cutting sleep to study more | Sleep 8 hours daily; take study hours from the day's shifts |
| 6 | Sitting down to study after parathas or a full meal | Eat light food that does not make you sluggish |
| 7 | Studying but never revising | The 1-7-30 rule: next day, after a week, after 30 days |
| 8 | Too few mock tests, or taking them in an AC room | At least 100 mocks, in exam conditions |
| 9 | Taking negative people's words to heart | Meet them less; answer with your result |

Each fix is explained in detail in my separate blogs. All the links are on my [Punjab Police SI Preparation Strategy](blog-punjab-police-si-preparation-strategy) page.
