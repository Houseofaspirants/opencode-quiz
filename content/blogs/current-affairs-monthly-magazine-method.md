Many students read current affairs every day from YouTube videos or websites. My advice is simple: **never read current affairs daily.** The method I used during my preparation was monthly magazines.

## Why not daily current affairs

- **It wastes time.** The time spent finding and watching videos every day could go into another subject.
- **The material stays scattered.** Some in a video, some on a website. At revision time, nothing is in one place.
- **There is no revision.** What you watched once in a video does not stay in memory until the exam.

## The monthly magazine method

1. **Pick one reliable monthly magazine,** such as the House of Aspirants monthly magazine. [Read the monthly magazine](magazine)
2. **Finish it in one day as soon as it comes out.** Study for 8-10 hours straight and cover the whole month's magazine. One day is enough for one month of current affairs.
3. **Keep everything in one place.** 12 magazines for 1 year, and your whole current affairs syllabus is in your hands.
4. **Revise 4-5 times before the exam.** Revise and practise all the magazines at least 4-5 times. The more you revise, the lower the chance of forgetting in the exam.

## Why this method is better

Current affairs take just one day a month; the rest of the days are free for other subjects. All the material is in one place, so revision is easy. And this is the way to full marks in the 10-15 current affairs questions.
