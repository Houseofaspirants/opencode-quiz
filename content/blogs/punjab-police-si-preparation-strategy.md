In my first Sub Inspector exam, I scored 613 and the cut-off was 623. The next time, I scored 713. On this page I am putting everything I did during my preparation in one place. Each part has its own detailed blog.

| Sleep | 8 hours every day, no compromise | [Why I Never Cut My 8 Hours](blog-sleep-during-exam-preparation) |
| Exercise | Gym or ground every evening | [Why I Never Skipped the Gym](blog-gym-during-exam-preparation) |

## If you remember only 3 things

1. **Always solve previous year papers.** They added 100 marks for me.
2. **Revision and mock tests matter as much as reading.**
3. **Study every day, for months.** This is a 6-month to 1-year process.
