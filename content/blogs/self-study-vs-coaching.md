On the Daily Post Punjabi podcast I was asked a question I get every day: self-study, offline coaching or online classes, which is better? Here is my honest answer, including what I spent myself.

## What I did

After graduation I prepared for UPSC. Nobody guided me; I searched online for the best academy in Chandigarh and joined it. I stayed about a year and spent at least 4 to 5 lakh rupees on fees, rent and living. Not every family can manage that, and it should not be the price of a government job.

## It depends on the student

**Self-study works if you:**
- understand a standard book on your own,
- can make your own notes,
- can follow a timetable without someone checking on you.

**You need a teacher (online or offline) if you:**
- read a chapter and still cannot explain it,
- have a weak base, like I did in school,
- lose direction without someone telling you what to read next.

I said it on the podcast in plain words: some students are sharp and can self-study; students who were weak like me need someone who explains from the book.

## Offline vs online

| | Offline coaching | Online / self-study |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | High: fees + rent + food | Low or free |
| Routine | Fixed by the institute | You must fix it yourself |
| Doubts | Face to face | Chat, live sessions, groups |
| Distractions | Fewer in class, more in a new city | Phone and home |
| Best for | Students who can afford it and need strict discipline | Most students, if they stay focused |

My view today: offline is the best experience, but it is expensive. For most students, online is enough. The internet now has more sources than any institute had ten years ago, and you can study from home with focus.

## How to make self-study work

1. Fix one set of books or notes per subject and stop collecting more.
2. Study one chapter, then do its MCQs the same day.
3. Keep fixed study hours, like a class timetable ([my 1.5-hour shift method](blog-study-in-90-minute-shifts)).
4. Solve previous year papers from the start.
5. Join one live doubt session or study group so you are not alone.
