If one thing mattered most in my preparation, it was **mock tests.** However much you study, if you have not practised giving the paper, what you studied does not fully work on exam day.

## What a mock test is

A mock test is an exact copy of the final paper:

- How many questions the paper has in total
- How many questions come from each subject
- How much time the whole paper gets
- And the conditions in which you will sit the paper

## How many mocks, and when

Attempt **at least 100 mock tests** before the final exam, and the more the better. Start once your preparation is complete, 1, 2 or 3 months before the exam.

## Recreate the exam conditions

Many students miss this. Give your mock tests in the same conditions as the final exam.

- If the exam is in summer and the centre will have no AC, give your mocks without AC too.
- If the exam is in winter and there will be no heater, give your mocks without a heater too.
- Set a timer for the full paper time, and do not get up in between.

This gets your brain and body ready to sit the paper in those conditions. On exam day, heat or cold will not break your focus.
