Many students have the same problem: they studied a lot, but forgot it in the exam. The fix is **revision.** The more you revise, the longer things stay in memory, and the more you remember on exam day. During my preparation, I used the **1-7-30 rule** for this.

## What the 1-7-30 rule is

1. **Today:** Study a new topic properly.
2. **Next day (1):** Revise the same topic for 10, 15, 20 minutes or even half an hour, depending on how big it is.
3. **After a week (7):** Revise the same topic again.
4. **After 30 days (30):** Revise the same topic once more.

So every topic is studied once and revised three times.

## An example

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 December | Study the topic |
| 2 December | First revision (10-30 minutes) |
| 8 December | Second revision |
| 31 December | Third revision |

## Why it works

What you study today starts to fade within a few days. Each revision makes it fresh again, and it stays longer than before. By the 30-day revision, the topic is so solid that the chance of forgetting it in the exam drops a lot.

## How to fit it into your routine

- Start your first shift each day by revising the previous day's topic.
- During your night planning for the next day, check which topics are due for their 7-day or 30-day revision.
- Keep a small notebook or calendar of which topic you studied when, so no revision is missed.
