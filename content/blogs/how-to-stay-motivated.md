Preparing for a government exam is a 6-month to 1-year process. In that time, demotivation comes for everyone; it came for me too. These are the 6 things that kept me going.

1. **Let the frustration out at the gym.** Gym or ground every evening. The day's frustration came out there, and my mind got fresh.
2. **Motivational Punjabi songs.** At the gym, only songs by artists like Hardeep Grewal and Ranjit Bawa. They reminded me why I was doing all this.
3. **Distance from negative people.** Meeting relatives and negative people less, going to fewer functions.
4. **One mantra.** "Yudh karo, phal ki chinta mat karo" (fight the battle; don't worry about the result). My only job was to study, not to worry about the result.
5. **Fitness and physical appearance.** Seeing myself fit in the mirror was my best source of motivation.
6. **Rely on consistency, not motivation.** Motivation comes and goes. Even on days you do not feel like it, you sit at your study table at 10.

## When failure comes

The first time, I missed by 10 marks. What could be more demotivating? But I did not give up my preparation, and the next time I scored 713. Failure is part of success.
