Many students ask me this. Someone has got a constable post or another lower post, and now wants to prepare for a higher one. But they cannot manage time alongside the job, and do not know how to study.

I am working as a Sub Inspector right now, and alongside it I am preparing for PCS and UPSC and doing other work too. So I am telling you this from my own experience.

## You have to make the time yourself

With a job, study time does not come on its own; you have to make it. In my view, the two best slots are:

1. **Morning, before going to work:** at least 2 hours.
2. **Night, after coming back from work:** at least 2 hours.

## 4 hours a day is enough

2 in the morning and 2 at night makes 4 hours a day. If this happens every day, it is plenty. 2 hours is the minimum; if you have the capacity, you can do more. Here too, consistency is what matters: 4 hours every day all week is far better than 10 hours one day and zero for the next three.

## Free time during the job

Whenever you get free time during your job, you can study on your phone. This time is good for small things, like revising current affairs or doing the 1-7-30 revision of the previous day's topic.

## Small things that work

- Decide at night what you will study the next morning, so no morning time goes into deciding.
- Keep the morning slot for new topics, because your mind is fresh then.
- Do not cut your sleep. Both the job and studying need a fresh mind.
