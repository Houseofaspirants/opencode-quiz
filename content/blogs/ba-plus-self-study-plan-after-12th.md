Almost every week someone messages me: "Sir, I have just passed 12th. How can I become a Sub Inspector like you?" This is the plan I give them.

## Why BA plus self-study works

Posts like Punjab Police SI, PCS and many PSSSB jobs need graduation. Most students finish the degree first and then start preparing, which wastes two or three years. If you take arts subjects in BA, a large part of your college syllabus (history, polity, economy, geography) is also your exam syllabus. Prepare both together and you are exam-ready the year your degree ends.

## The plan

**Before you start (summer after 12th)**
- Download the syllabus and two previous papers of your target exam.
- Join BA in a college, regular or open mode (check that the university is recognised).
- Decide: self-study or a course. If you cannot self-study, join an online or offline institute; see [Self Study vs Coaching](blog-self-study-vs-coaching).

**Year 1: Foundation**
- Read basic books for history, polity, geography and economy.
- Start Punjabi grammar and English basics; they are scoring in Punjab exams.
- 30–45 minutes of reasoning and maths practice daily.
- Learn computer basics; most Punjab exams have a computer section.

**Year 2: Standard books and MCQs**
- Move to standard books for each subject.
- After every chapter, attempt its MCQs the same day.
- Start reading monthly current affairs (my method is in [The Monthly Magazine Method](blog-current-affairs-monthly-magazine-method)).
- Make short notes for revision.

**Year 3: Exam mode**
- Solve previous year papers subject-wise, then full papers with a timer.
- Take mock tests regularly ([why I recommend 100 mocks](blog-importance-of-mock-tests)).
- Revise current affairs for the last 12 months.
- Apply for exams that match your goal as notifications come out.

## A sample weekday

- College hours or self-study of college subjects
- 2 hours: one exam subject chapter + its MCQs
- 1 hour: reasoning or maths
- 30 minutes: current affairs or Punjabi grammar

## Two warnings

- **Do not wait for the degree to finish.** Most of my own delay came from starting serious preparation late.
- **Check eligibility in the notification.** Age limits, degree requirements and physical standards differ by post and year. Do not depend on what friends say.

All the subject notes and chapter MCQs you need for Years 1 and 2 are on House of Aspirants in Punjabi and English.
