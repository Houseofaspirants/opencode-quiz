Many students think going to the gym or the ground is a waste of time. I think the opposite. I studied 8-10 hours a day, but every evening I took a 2-3 hour break for the gym or the ground. For me, it was part of studying.

## My evening routine

As soon as my afternoon shifts ended, I went to the gym. I trained for 1 to 1.5 hours, was home by 7, bathed and ate, and was back at my study table at 8. My two night shifts went well because of this break.

## How physical activity helped me

- **It let the frustration out.** Preparation has many bad days, when things do not stick or mock test scores drop. Whatever frustration I had, I left it at the gym.
- **It kept my mind fresh and relaxed.** After sitting in a chair all day, moving my body brought a different kind of energy.
- **It protected me from demotivation.** Even on days when I did not feel like studying, the mood changed after the gym.

## Motivational Punjabi songs at the gym

I had one habit. During that hour or so at the gym, I listened only to motivational Punjabi songs, like those of Hardeep Grewal and Ranjit Bawa. These songs reminded me why I was doing all this. They kept me motivated, and the day's tiredness went away.

Make your own gym playlist of songs that fire you up.

## A double benefit for police recruitment

Punjab Police recruitment has a physical test after the written exam. If you go to the ground every day during your preparation, you will not have to start your physical preparation from zero after the written exam.

## How to start

- Fix one time in the day, like my 6 pm.
- If not the gym, the ground, a run or a brisk walk is enough.
- At the gym, use your phone only for music, not social media.
- Fix your study time after the gym too, so the break does not stretch.
