The last 3 months before the exam matter most. For me, these were the 3 months that turned 613 into 713. In this period, focus on paper practice and revision instead of new reading.

## 1. Previous year papers (PYQs)

In the last 3 months, I solved previous year papers of Constable, SI and Head Constable. For every question I did not know, I searched Google. If the topic was new, I studied the whole topic and made notes. In this period, new topics should come only this way, through PYQs.

## 2. Mock tests

Once your preparation is complete, 1-3 months before the exam, take at least 100 mock tests. Take them in the same conditions as the exam: same time, same pattern, and if the centre has no AC or heater, give them without AC or heater.

## 3. Revising current affairs

Revise all the monthly magazines from the last 1-1.5 years at least 4-5 times before the exam. This locks in the 10-15 current affairs questions.

## 4. Do not break your routine

Keep 8 hours of sleep, evening exercise and light food going in the last months too. Cutting sleep close to the exam means even what you studied will not stay.

## Last 3 months checklist

- ✅ Solved previous year papers of Constable, SI and Head Constable
- ✅ Studied the topic of every wrong question and made notes
- ✅ 100 mock tests in exam conditions
- ✅ Revised all monthly magazines 4-5 times
- ✅ Sleep, exercise and diet kept going
