[Discipline and Consistency](blog-discipline-and-consistency) explains why showing up every day matters. This blog is about the how: a small technique that made a habit automatic for me.

## How the gym became automatic

Today, if I do not go to the gym, I feel something is incomplete in my day. It was not always like that. I picked up one idea from Atomic Habits and applied it:

1. **Talk about it positively, again and again.** I kept telling myself and others that the gym is good and that I cannot do without it.
2. **On days I did not feel like it, I still went and sat there for 10 minutes.** No workout promise, just being in that environment.
3. **The environment did the rest.** After a few days of this, I always ended up working out. Slowly I fell in love with it.

## The same rule for studying

**Step 1: Fix the place.**
One table, one chair, used only for studying. No eating or phone scrolling there.

**Step 2: Fix the time.**
Same start time every day. Mine was the morning shift (my full timetable is in [How Many Hours I Studied Daily](blog-how-many-hours-i-studied-daily)).

**Step 3: The 10-minute rule.**
When you don't feel like studying, don't fight it. Tell yourself: I will only sit for 10 minutes, open the chapter and read one page. Most days, those 10 minutes become an hour. On the bad days, 10 minutes still keeps the chain alive.

**Step 4: Say it positively.**
Stop saying "I have to study". Say "I study at 9 every morning". It sounds small, but it changes how the habit feels.

**Step 5: Make the first action tiny.**
Keep the book open on the right page and the pen on top before you sleep. In the morning, starting takes zero effort.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting too big.** Ten hours on day one, burnout by day four. Start with what you can do on your worst day.
- **Changing the place every day.** The habit belongs to a place; keep it fixed.
- **Waiting for motivation.** Motivation comes after you start, not before.
- **Missing twice.** One missed day is normal. Two in a row is the start of a new habit, the wrong one.

## Try it for 21 days

Write the date on a calendar every day you do your 10 minutes. Do not judge the hours, only the chain. After three weeks, you will notice that sitting down to study no longer needs a fight.

Daily chapter MCQs on House of Aspirants are a good fixed task for your 10 minutes.
