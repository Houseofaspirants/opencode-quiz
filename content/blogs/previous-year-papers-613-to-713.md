If you do not study the previous year questions of the exam you are preparing for, clearing it becomes very hard. Let me explain this with my own example.

## First attempt: missed by 10 marks

In my first Sub Inspector exam, I scored **613**. The general cut-off was **623**. I missed the paper by just 10 marks.

## The last 3 months: only previous year papers

For the next year's exam, I spent the last 3 months on just one thing: solving previous year papers of Constable, Sub Inspector and Head Constable. My method was:

1. **Solve the paper.** The questions I knew were fine.
2. **Search Google for every question I did not know.**
3. **If the topic was new to me, study the whole topic from scratch.** Search it on Google and make notes on it.

This way, one question covered a whole new topic, and many other questions from that topic got prepared along with it.

## The result: 713 marks

In my next SI exam, I scored **713**, which is **100 marks more** than before. Those 100 marks came from previous year papers.

| When | My marks | Result |
|---|---|---|
| First attempt | 613 | 10 below the cut-off (623) |
| Next year (last 3 months on previous papers only) | 713 | 100 marks more |
