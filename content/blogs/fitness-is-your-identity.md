While preparing, all our focus is on one thing: a government job. That is right. But I want to add one more thing: **fitness is your identity.**

## Fitness stays with you after the job

Tomorrow, all of you will be in good jobs, with a government job in hand. But even on that day, your fitness and physical appearance matter. The job is your post, but your body is your identity, and it goes with you everywhere.

## Physical appearance: the best source of motivation

When you see yourself fit in the mirror, a different kind of confidence comes from inside. For me, physical appearance was the best source of motivation. It kept me motivated during preparation, and it still does.

## Study and fitness can go together

Many people think: get the job first, see about fitness later. I studied 8-10 hours a day and still did not give up my diet or the gym. That same fitness later helped me a lot in the Punjab Police physical. Fitness did not take time away from studying; it gave energy to my studying.

## Start today

- Give exercise a fixed slot in your daily timetable, just like studying.
- Pay attention to food, because a big part of fitness comes from diet.
- Build fitness not for one exam, but for your whole life.
