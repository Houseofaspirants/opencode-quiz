Have you noticed that if you sit down to study after parathas, your eyes get heavy within half an hour? Or if you eat a full meal and sit down, the book is in front of you but nothing goes in? That is not your fault. It is the food. The same happened to me until I changed my diet.

## As the food, so the brain

My belief was that your brain works the way your food is. In my experience, our everyday food is full of carbs and fat, and it brings sluggishness. So my first step was to change my food so that I would not feel sluggish.

## What my diet looked like

- **Very little roti:** barely half a roti in a whole day.
- **Oats**
- **Shakes**
- **Boiled eggs**
- **Chicken breast**

This food was light but kept me full. I felt energetic all day, did not feel sluggish and did not get sleepy while studying. A 1.5-hour sitting went by without getting bored.

## If you are vegetarian

Instead of eggs and chicken, you can look at foods like paneer, dal, curd or soya. The only point is that the food should be light and should not make you sluggish.

## Small things that work

- Do not eat a full stomach right before sitting down to study.
- Cut down on heavy foods like parathas on study days.
- Notice which foods make you sleepy. Every body is different.

*Note: This is my own experience. I am not a doctor or dietitian. If you have any health problem, talk to a doctor before changing your diet.*
