In Punjab, when a student says "I took arts", people quietly assume he is weak. Commerce is called average, and medical or non-medical is for the "sharp" ones. I want to break this, because it sends many students in the wrong direction.

## The irony

Look at the general studies part of the biggest civil services exams, from IAS to Punjab PCS. A large part of it is history, polity, geography and economy: arts subjects. The same is true for the general knowledge sections of Punjab Police and many PSSSB papers. The stream society calls "weak" is the stream that prepares you best for the top general government jobs.

I myself took non-medical after class 10, mainly to prove teachers wrong. It worked out, but I had to learn history, polity and economy from scratch later.

## How to choose

**Choose arts if:**
- Your goal is a general government job: PCS, Punjab Police SI/constable, clerk-level PSSSB posts, teaching (with the right subjects), or UPSC.
- You like reading, history, current events and writing answers.

**Choose commerce if:**
- You want posts like accountant, clerk (accounts), junior auditor, banking, or careers like CA alongside exams.
- Check each post's notification: some accounts posts ask for a commerce degree.

**Choose science (medical / non-medical) if:**
- You want technical government posts: junior engineer, lab posts, health department, veterinary, forest or agriculture-related posts that ask for a specific degree.
- Or you genuinely love science and want to keep engineering or medicine open.

## Three rules before you decide

1. **Check eligibility, not opinions.** Most general posts ask for graduation in any stream. Technical posts ask for a specific degree. Read the qualification line in the notification of the post you want.
2. **Your marks do not decide your stream; your goal does.** Do not let anyone push you into arts because of low marks, or out of it because "arts is for weak students".
3. **Whatever you choose, start general knowledge early.** Even 30 minutes a day of Punjab GK and current affairs from class 11 puts you years ahead.

## If you have already chosen

Science and commerce students are not behind. Graduation in any stream is enough for most general exams. Just add history, polity, geography and economy to your daily reading. The chapter-wise notes on House of Aspirants are built for exactly that.
