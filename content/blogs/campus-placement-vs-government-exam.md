In the last semester of graduation at LPU, campus placements were starting. At LPU you have to attend special placement classes, and packages there usually start at several lakh a year. I walked up to my teachers and said: I will not sit for placement. I want to go into civil services.

## The reaction

My teachers did not take it lightly. They called my home and told my parents, "We are getting your son a job with a good package, and he says he does not want it." Then I had to convince my family. My father said, "You are grown up now. We trust you; take your decision." My mother worried more. Later she would say, "At least take some small job, a clerk job." But I took my name off the placement list.

## Why I did it

From my last semester I was clear: I wanted a job at a certain level, in civil services or police, where I could serve till retirement. A private job I did not want would have taken my best hours and left my preparation for the night.

I will be honest: it was not a short road. I graduated in 2018 and got my job about six years later. If you choose this path, you must be ready for that.

## How to decide for yourself

Ask yourself these five questions before you say no to a job:

1. **Is my goal one specific exam and post, or just "a government job"?** A vague goal does not survive years of pressure.
2. **Do I know the syllabus and have I attempted a previous paper?** If you have not, you are deciding blind.
3. **Can my family support me for one to three years?** Talk about money openly.
4. **What is my deadline and Plan B?** Decide in advance when you will take a job if it does not work out (read [Why Every Aspirant Needs a Plan B](blog-plan-b-for-government-job-aspirants)).
5. **Can I prepare with the job instead?** Many people do. My blog [Study With a Job](blog-study-with-a-job) shows how.

## If you say no, use the time properly

Leaving a job is not the brave part. Using those hours every day for years is. Fix a routine on day one, attempt previous papers early, and join a study circle so the freedom does not turn into wasted time.
