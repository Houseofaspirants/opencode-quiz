When you start your study journey, you will face many failures along the way. Accept this from the start: there is no example of anyone succeeding without failing. Every person you call successful has failed at some point.

## Failure is part of success

**Failures are part of success.** You move towards success by failing. So never fear failure, and never run from it.

## My own failure

In my first Sub Inspector exam, I scored 613 and the cut-off was 623. I missed by just 10 marks. But I did not give up my preparation, and the next time I scored 713. If I had quit out of fear after that failure, those 100 marks would never have come. The full story is in my blog [From 613 to 713](blog-previous-year-papers-613-to-713).

## My mantra: fight the battle, don't worry about the result

While studying, I always repeated one line: **"Yudh karo, phal ki chinta mat karo"** (fight the battle; don't worry about the result).

My only job was to fight, which meant to study. Whether I got the job or not, I did not worry about it. Worry only troubles you and pulls your focus away from studying. If you truly want the job, drop the worry and just do your work.

## What to do after a failure

- Accept it. It is part of the journey, not the end.
- See where you lost marks and what to do differently next time. For me, it was previous year papers.
- Keep preparing. One exam does not decide your future.
- Stop worrying about the result and focus on the work.

You will reach success through failure.
