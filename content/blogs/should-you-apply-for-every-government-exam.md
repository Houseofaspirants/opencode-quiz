Before the SI exam, I gave one attempt of UPSC and one of PCS. Apart from these I did not fill any other form, no clerk, no constable, no head constable. On the podcast I explained why, and many people did not like it. Here is the full reasoning.

## "Just give it once and see"

My own family said: you don't have to take the job, just appear once and see where you stand. Many students fill every form for the same reason. They believe it builds practice and self-confidence.

My answer was simple: if I am not going to do that job, why should I spoil someone else's seat?

## What happens when you clear and don't join

Imagine a post with a few hundred seats. Many of the people who clear it are already selected somewhere else, as SI, clerk or in excise. They do not join. In recruitments where unfilled seats are not passed down to the next candidates, those seats can stay empty or go to waste.

Now think about the person who was just below the merit line. For him it may have been the last attempt before his age limit, or the job his family badly needed. He missed it, and the seat he needed went to someone who never wanted it.

## The practice argument

Practice matters; I wrote a whole blog on [why mock tests matter most](blog-importance-of-mock-tests). But you do not need a real seat for practice:

- Online mock tests and test series give you real exam conditions.
- Previous year papers with a timer give you the real level.
- Full-length tests at home on a Sunday morning copy the exam-day routine.

## So which forms should you fill?

1. **Your main target posts.** The ones you are preparing for.
2. **Honest fallbacks.** Posts you would really join and stay in if your main target does not come.
3. **Not the rest.** If you already know you will not join, skip the form and take a mock instead.

This is my personal view, not a rule. Some students genuinely need any job first, and that is completely fair. Just be honest with yourself about whether you would join.

## A small thing that adds up

If thousands of aspirants thought this way, more seats would reach people who need them. I can't change the system, but I can decide not to take a seat I don't want.

Free mock tests, chapter MCQs and previous papers are on House of Aspirants for exactly this kind of practice.
