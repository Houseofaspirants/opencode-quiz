When preparation is in full swing, many students let their bodies go: sitting in a chair all day, heavy food and no exercise. For me it went the other way. While studying 8-10 hours a day, I reached my best fitness. Two things made it happen: diet and daily physical activity.

## First: I changed my diet

My belief was simple: the output is only as good as the input. In my experience, our everyday home food is full of carbs and fat, and it makes the body sluggish. So the first thing I changed was my food.

My diet had very little roti, barely half a roti in a whole day. The rest was oats, shakes, boiled eggs and chicken breast. For the full diet, read my blog [What to Eat So You Don't Feel Sleepy While Studying](blog-diet-to-avoid-sleepiness-while-studying).

## Then: gym or ground every day

Every evening, my 2-3 hour break was for the gym or the ground. It was a fixed part of my timetable, however much studying was left.

## Two benefits of the diet

- **Physical results.** I was exercising anyway, but the main reason I reached my best fitness was the diet.
- **Long study sittings.** In a 1.5-hour sitting I did not get bored or sleepy. I felt energetic all day.

## The benefit in the Punjab Police physical

When the Punjab Police physical test came after the written exam, this fitness helped a lot. I did not have to start from zero, because my body was already ready.

## Where to start

1. First notice which foods make you sluggish, and cut down on them.
2. Fix one time in the day for exercise.
3. Do not leave fitness for after the exam. In police recruitment, it is your second paper.
