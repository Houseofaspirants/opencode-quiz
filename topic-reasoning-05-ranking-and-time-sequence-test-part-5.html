Question bank

Ranking and Time Sequence Test MCQ – Part 5: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A runner has the same rank, 26, from both ends of a finishing list. How many runners finished?

    1. 52
    2. 53
    3. 50
    4. 51
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 51

    Explanation: There are 25 runners on each side and the runner herself: 25 + 1 + 25 = 51.

  2. A and B occupy the 13th place from the left and 16th from the right in a 54-person row. How many people lie outside the segment from A to B, excluding neither endpoint from that segment?

    1. 26
    2. 25
    3. 27
    4. 28
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 27

    Explanation: Outside people are 12 before A and 15 after B: 12 + 15 = 27.

  3. In a row of 41, A is left 6, B left 17 and C left 30. First A swaps with B, then A with C. Which person is now exactly midway between the other two named persons?

    1. B
    2. No named person
    3. A
    4. C
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) No named person

    Explanation: The final places are B = 6, C = 17 and A = 30. The midpoint of 6 and 30 is 18, so no named person is exactly midway.

  4. 6 people have distinct heights. Exactly 2 are taller than J. K is shorter than J but taller than L and M; N is taller than J. What is K’s rank from the shortest end?

    1. 3
    2. 2
    3. 4
    4. 5
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 3

    Explanation: J is third tallest; K, L and M are the three below J, with K tallest among them. Thus K is third shortest.

  5. A journey starts at 11:50 am and ends at 2:25 pm the same day. How many minutes of the journey occur after 12 noon?

    1. 135
    2. 165
    3. 145
    4. 155
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 145

    Explanation: From noon to 2:25 pm is 2 hours 25 minutes = 145 minutes. The 10 minutes before noon are excluded.

  6. P and Q are left 12 and 28. P moves 5 places right while Q moves 4 places left, without crossing each other. How many people are between them now?

    1. 8
    2. 5
    3. 6
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 6

    Explanation: New positions are 17 and 24. Between = 24 - 17 - 1 = 6.

  7. A row has 64 applicants. J is 27th from the front. Applicants at original ranks 1 through 8 and 49 through 64 leave. Then the remaining row is reversed. What is J’s new rank from the front?

    1. 22
    2. 21
    3. 19
    4. 23
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 22

    Explanation: Remaining original ranks are 9 through 48, totalling 40. J is 27 - 8 = 19th before reversal, then 40 - 19 + 1 = 22nd.

  8. At a school assembly, girl A is 11th from the left and girl B is 29th from the left. A and B are respectively 4th and 12th among girls from that end. How many boys stand strictly between them?

    1. 9
    2. 12
    3. 11
    4. 10
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 10

    Explanation: There are 29 - 11 - 1 = 17 people between them, including 12 - 4 - 1 = 7 girls. Boys = 17 - 7 = 10.

  9. A Moga recruitment form is submitted on 8 June. Verification occurs on the third day after submission, and approval 2 days after verification. On which date is approval given?

    1. 13 June
    2. 11 June
    3. 14 June
    4. 12 June
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 13 June

    Explanation: Verification is 8 + 3 = 11 June; approval is 11 + 2 = 13 June.

  10. A clinic appointment is shifted 25 minutes later. M arrives 8 minutes before the revised time, at 11:47. What was the original appointment time?

    1. 11:14
    2. 11:22
    3. 11:30
    4. 11:39
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 11:30

    Explanation: Revised time = 11:47 + 8 = 11:55. Original time = 11:55 - 25 = 11:30.

  11. Two aligned rows of 15 face each other. Row I faces north and Row II south. P is 4th from his own left in Row I; Q is opposite P. A new queue takes all of Row I first, then all of Row II, each row in the order of its members’ own left to right. What is Q’s new front rank?

    1. 27
    2. 26
    3. 18
    4. 19
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 27

    Explanation: Opposite facing makes Q 15 - 4 + 1 = 12th from his own left. The first row contributes 15 people, so Q’s new rank is 15 + 12 = 27.

  12. A clerk records positions for a 50-person row. Which pair (left rank, right rank) is inconsistent?

    1. (26, 24)
    2. (18, 33)
    3. (39, 12)
    4. (7, 44)
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) (26, 24)

    Explanation: Valid opposite-end ranks add to 51. Only 26 + 24 = 50 fails this rule.

  13. An office clock always shows 09:18 at actual 09:30. How many minutes must be added to any reading to obtain actual time?

    1. 8
    2. 10
    3. 18
    4. 12
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 12

    Explanation: The clock is 09:30 - 09:18 = 12 minutes slow, so add 12 minutes.

  14. Consider 70 applicants: A is 18th from the left and B is 20th from the right. If all people strictly between A and B leave, how many remain?

    1. 39
    2. 38
    3. 36
    4. 37
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 38

    Explanation: B is left 51, so 51 - 18 - 1 = 32 leave. Remaining = 70 - 32 = 38.

  15. At actual 09:20 a clock is 12 minutes fast. It then loses 8 minutes every 4 true hours at a constant rate. When it shows 16:47 later that day, what is its error?

    1. 3 minutes slow
    2. 3 minutes fast
    3. 15 minutes slow
    4. 15 minutes fast
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 3 minutes slow

    Explanation: The display advances from 09:32 to 16:47, or 435 minutes. True elapsed = 435 × 60/58 = 450 minutes; the clock loses 15 minutes, changing its error from +12 to -3 minutes.

  16. Parcels A, B, C and D weigh 10, 20, 30 and 40 kg in some order. A is heavier than B; C is heavier than D; B is lighter than D; A and C together weigh 60 kg. Which parcel is heaviest?

    1. A
    2. C
    3. D
    4. B
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) C

    Explanation: A and C must weigh 20 and 40 kg. B < D fixes B = 10 and D = 30, so C > D forces C = 40 kg.

  17. A moves forward in a queue from 24th to 18th. How many other people change their ranks because of this single move?

    1. 8
    2. 6
    3. 5
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 6

    Explanation: The 6 people originally at ranks 18 through 23 each move back one place.

  18. A machine completes 3 runs of 42 minutes each. Between consecutive runs it cools for 9 minutes. If the first run starts at 20:18, when does the third finish?

    1. 22:24
    2. 22:33
    3. 22:42
    4. 22:51
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 22:42

    Explanation: 3 runs and 2 cooling gaps take 3 × 42 + 2 × 9 = 144 minutes. 20:18 + 2:24 = 22:42.

  19. A, B, C and D occupy left positions 4, 14, 23 and 34. A swaps with B, B with C, and C with D. Which pair ends with exactly 8 people between them?

    1. A and C
    2. C and D
    3. B and C
    4. A and B
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) A and B

    Explanation: Final places are A = 14, B = 23, C = 34 and D = 4. Only A-B have positional gap 9, hence 8 between.

  20. A departure clock is 9 minutes fast and shows 21:54. An arrival clock is 7 minutes slow and shows 23:08 on the same date. What is the true journey duration in minutes?

    1. 83
    2. 58
    3. 74
    4. 90
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 90

    Explanation: Actual departure is 21:45 and actual arrival is 23:15. Their difference is 90 minutes.

  21. Ranks follow competition ranking: tied candidates share a rank, and the next rank skips the occupied places. 6 candidates scored above Leena and 2 others tied with her. What rank does Leena receive?

    1. 9
    2. 8
    3. 7
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 7

    Explanation: A competition rank is one more than the number strictly above: 6 + 1 = 7. Tied candidates share rank 7.

  22. A row of 36 is split into two groups: original odd positions form one row and original even positions another, preserving order. P was originally 26th. What is P’s rank from the back in the even-position row?

    1. 5
    2. 7
    3. 8
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 6

    Explanation: The even row has 18 people; P is 26/2 = 13th from its front. Back rank = 18 - 13 + 1 = 6.

  23. A is 11th from the left and B is 9th from the right. The row has more than 20 but fewer than 27 people. A lies left of B, with an odd number between them. How many totals satisfy all conditions?

    1. 4
    2. 3
    3. 5
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 3

    Explanation: The gap is N - 11 - 9 = N - 20. For N = 21 through 26 it is odd only at N = 21, 23 and 25.

  24. Two friends agree to meet at 17:00. S comes 12 minutes early and waits 20 minutes before leaving. T comes 11 minutes late. How long after S leaves does T arrive?

    1. 8
    2. 3
    3. 23
    4. 11
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 3

    Explanation: S arrives at 16:48 and leaves at 17:08. T arrives at 17:11, which is 3 minutes later.

  25. Two rows each have 12 seats numbered from the same fixed left. X is 5th from the right in Row II. Row II is reversed end-to-end first. They then merge in pairs as I1, I2, II1, II2, I3, I4, II3, II4, and continue in that pattern. What is X’s final front rank?

    1. 11
    2. 10
    3. 12
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 11

    Explanation: X starts at II8 and becomes II5 after reversal. 2 complete four-person blocks take 8 places; I5, I6 and II5 take the next 3, so X is 11th.
