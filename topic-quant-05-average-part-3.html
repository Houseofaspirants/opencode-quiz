Question bank

Average MCQ – Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. The average of 6 numbers is 84. Their deviations from 80 total how much?

    1. 4
    2. 80
    3. 504
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 24

    Explanation: Total deviation = 6 × (84 − 80) = 24.

  2. A group averages 36 years. When a 60-year-old joins, its average rises by 2 years. How many members were there originally?

    1. 12
    2. 11
    3. 10
    4. 13
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 11

    Explanation: Original count = (60 − 36) ÷ 2 − 1 = 11.

  3. 2 groups average 48 and 78 marks, and together average 60. The lower-scoring group has 7 more members than the other. How many members are in the higher-scoring group?

    1. 21
    2. 35
    3. 7
    4. 14
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 14

    Explanation: Counts are in ratio (78 − 60) : (60 − 48) = 3 : 2. 1 part is 7, so the higher-scoring group has 14 members.

  4. 3 schools send equal numbers of candidates. The combined averages for schools A and B, B and C, and C and A are 58, 64 and 67 marks respectively. Find the average for all candidates.

    1. 62
    2. 64
    3. 65
    4. 63
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 63

    Explanation: Each school is counted twice across the 3 equal-sized pairs. Overall average = (58 + 64 + 67) ÷ 3 = 63.

  5. Find the average of all multiples of 9 from 100 to 200.

    1. 150
    2. 149
    3. 153
    4. 157.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 153

    Explanation: The first and last terms are 108 and 198. Average = (108 + 198) ÷ 2 = 153.

  6. 5 years ago, 4 family members averaged 28 years. Twins were born 3 years ago. 1 original member died 2 years ago aged 58. Nobody else joined or left. Find the present average age of the 5 surviving members in years.

    1. 15.6
    2. 16
    3. 16.4
    4. 17.2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 15.6

    Explanation: Present equivalent total of the original 4 = 4 × (28 + 5) = 132. Remove 58 + 2 and add 3 + 3: average = (132 − 60 + 6) ÷ 5 = 15.6 years.

  7. A courier rides for 2 hours at 36 km/h and then for 3 hours at 54 km/h. Find the average speed in km/h.

    1. 45
    2. 48
    3. 43.2
    4. 46.8
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 46.8

    Explanation: Average speed = (2 × 36 + 3 × 54) ÷ 5 = 46.8 km/h.

  8. A total of 1,764 forms were processed over several days, averaging 84 forms per day. How many days were used?

    1. 20
    2. 21
    3. 22
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 21

    Explanation: Number of days = 1764 ÷ 84 = 21.

  9. 30 students average 52 kg. 3 students averaging 48 kg leave and 2 students join. The remaining 29 students average 54 kg. Find the total weight of the 2 newcomers in kg.

    1. 144
    2. 150
    3. 156
    4. 162
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 150

    Explanation: Newcomers total = 29 × 54 − (30 × 52 − 3 × 48) = 150 kg.

  10. Each of 15 observations is increased by 12%. If their old average was 75, find the new average.

    1. 84
    2. 87
    3. 81
    4. 83
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 84

    Explanation: Average also rises by 12%: 75 × 1.12 = 84.

  11. The average of 14 numbers is 63. Removing the greatest number reduces the average by 3. Find the removed number.

    1. 105
    2. 102
    3. 99
    4. 60
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 102

    Explanation: Removed number = 63 + 13 × 3 = 102.

  12. The first 6 of 10 readings average 42 and the last 6 average 47. All 10 average 44. Find the average of the 2 overlapping readings.

    1. 47
    2. 44
    3. 49
    4. 94
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 47

    Explanation: Overlap total = 6 × 42 + 6 × 47 − 10 × 44 = 94. Overlap average = 94 ÷ 2 = 47.

  13. An average of 25 values is reported as 64 after 1 value is omitted from the total but the divisor remains 25. If the omitted value is 75, find the correct average.

    1. 66
    2. 68
    3. 65
    4. 67
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 67

    Explanation: Correct total = 25 × 64 + 75. Correct average = 64 + 75 ÷ 25 = 67.

  14. 7 consecutive integers average 104. Find the average of the smallest and greatest integers.

    1. 104
    2. 103
    3. 105
    4. 107
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 104

    Explanation: For equally spaced integers, the endpoint average equals the whole-set average: 104.

  15. A batter’s average over 24 innings is 42 runs per innings. Excluding 1 innings of 88 runs and another innings, the average of the remaining 22 is 39. Find the other excluded score.

    1. 64
    2. 60
    3. 62
    4. 66
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 62

    Explanation: Other score = 24 × 42 − 22 × 39 − 88 = 62.

  16. A train’s running speed is 72 km/h, but including halts its average speed is 60 km/h. How many minutes does it halt per hour of total journey time?

    1. 10
    2. 12
    3. 8
    4. 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 10

    Explanation: In 1 hour, 60 km needs 60 ÷ 72 hours of running. Halt = (1 − 60 ÷ 72) × 60 = 10 minutes.

  17. The average of x, 2x + 3 and 3x − 3 is 26. Find x.

    1. 12
    2. 26
    3. 13
    4. 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 13

    Explanation: Their total is 6x, so 6x ÷ 3 = 26 gives x = 13.

  18. A team of 22 averages 48 points. 3 new members score 60, 66 and 78 points. Find the new team average.

    1. 51
    2. 49.6
    3. 50.4
    4. 52
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 50.4

    Explanation: Newcomers contribute 12 + 18 + 30 = 60 above the base. New average = 48 + 60 ÷ 25 = 50.4.

  19. A bowler concedes 117 runs at an average of 6.5 runs per over. How many legal balls has he bowled, taking 6 legal balls per over?

    1. 18
    2. 117
    3. 120
    4. 108
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 108

    Explanation: Overs = 117 ÷ 6.5 = 18. Legal balls = 18 × 6 = 108.

  20. A family of 5 averages 30 years. A member aged 26 marries, and the spouse joins. After 2 years, all 6 average 32 years. Find the spouse’s age when joining, in years.

    1. 28
    2. 32
    3. 30
    4. 26
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 30

    Explanation: Total at joining = 6 × (32 − 2) = 180. Spouse’s age = 180 − 5 × 30 = 30 years.

  21. A grocer buys 12 kg of rice at ₹48 per kg and 18 kg at ₹58 per kg. Find the average purchase price per kg in ₹.

    1. 53
    2. 54
    3. 52
    4. 55
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 54

    Explanation: Average price = 48 + 18 × 10 ÷ 30 = ₹54 per kg.

  22. 2 equally large groups have average scores 55 and 71. Find their combined average score.

    1. 63
    2. 62
    3. 64
    4. 65
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 63

    Explanation: Equal group sizes give average = (55 + 71) ÷ 2 = 63.

  23. A test has 50 questions carrying 2 marks each, with no penalty for wrong answers. If a candidate’s average mark per question is 1.48, how many questions were answered correctly?

    1. 36
    2. 38
    3. 37
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 37

    Explanation: Total marks = 50 × 1.48 = 74. Correct answers = 74 ÷ 2 = 37.

  24. Find the 7th of 13 numbers. I: All 13 average 46 and the first 7 average 48. II: The last 7 average 45. Choose the code: 1 = I alone is sufficient; 2 = II alone is sufficient; 3 = both together are needed and sufficient; 4 = both together are insufficient.

    1. 1
    2. 3
    3. 2
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 3

    Explanation: Neither statement alone isolates the 7th number. Together, it is 7 × 48 + 7 × 45 − 13 × 46 = 53, so both are needed and sufficient.

  25. A shop averages 140 customers per day over a 30-day month. The first 12 days average 125 customers. Find the daily average over the remaining days.

    1. 145
    2. 155
    3. 150
    4. 160
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 150

    Explanation: Remaining average = 140 + 12 × (140 − 125) ÷ 18 = 150.
