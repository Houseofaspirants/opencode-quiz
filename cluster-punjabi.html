About this cluster

What Punjabi covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Punjabi carries real weight in Punjab state-level papers and is often the section that decides a ranking, because candidates who treat it casually tend to lose easy marks. The questions come from grammar, vocabulary, idioms, comprehension and a working knowledge of the literature associated with the language. Because the rules are finite, the subject rewards the same checklist approach used for grammar in any language: settle the rules first, then build vocabulary through repeated exposure rather than one-off memorisation. Idioms and fixed phrases are a recurring question type and are worth revising as pairs with their meanings. The lanes below move from grammar to vocabulary and then to reading and literature, so each step relies only on what has already been practised.

What does Punjabi cover?

Punjabi covers Punjabi Grammar, Vocabulary and Word Meaning, Idioms and Phrases, Literature Basics and the rest. The lane sits under Punjabi. Every area listed above links back to the page that hosts it.

How should I study Punjabi?

Revise one area at a time and test it before moving on - the topics above are ordered so each rests on the one before it. Once they are familiar, switch to timed practice: sets run at 30 seconds a question, 60 for Reasoning and Quantitative Aptitude and clear at 40% accuracy.
