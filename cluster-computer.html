About this cluster

What Computer covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Computer is among the most scoring lanes available to a Punjab exam candidate, because the questions are concrete, repeat often and rarely require interpretation. Marks come from hardware and software classification, operating system basics, common applications, internet and networking terms, security vocabulary and the abbreviations that fill the section. Almost every question has a single unmistakable answer once the terminology is familiar, which makes this an ideal area for daily short sets rather than long study sessions. Abbreviations and device classification are the two highest-frequency patterns and are worth drilling until recall is instant. The lanes below run from the physical machine outward to software, applications and the internet, so each topic rests on the vocabulary introduced in the one before it.
