Question bank

Average MCQ – Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. 6 parcels weigh 48, 52, 47, 55, 51 and 53 kg. What is their average weight in kg?

    1. 50
    2. 51
    3. 52
    4. 306
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 51

    Explanation: Using 51 as a base, deviations total −3 + 1 − 4 + 4 + 0 + 2 = 0, so the average is 51 kg.

  2. The average weight of 24 trainees is 62 kg. 6 trainees with average weight 72 kg join them. Find the new average weight in kg.

    1. 67
    2. 64
    3. 65
    4. 63
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 64

    Explanation: The extra weight above 62 is 6 × 10 = 60 kg, spread over 30 trainees. New average = 62 + 60 ÷ 30 = 64 kg.

  3. A group of 28 players has average weight 54 kg. 2 players weighing 46 kg and 52 kg leave, and 3 new players join. If the new average is 56 kg, what is the average weight of the 3 new players in kg?

    1. 68
    2. 66
    3. 72
    4. 70
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 70

    Explanation: New players total = 29 × 56 − 28 × 54 + 46 + 52 = 210 kg. Their average = 210 ÷ 3 = 70 kg.

  4. A batch has 18 boys averaging 64 marks and 12 girls averaging 74 marks. What is the average mark of the batch?

    1. 68
    2. 69
    3. 70
    4. 66
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 68

    Explanation: Average = 64 + 12 × (74 − 64) ÷ 30 = 68.

  5. The average of 9 consecutive odd integers is 57. Find the greatest integer.

    1. 61
    2. 66
    3. 73
    4. 65
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 65

    Explanation: The middle integer is 57. The greatest is 4 steps of 2 above it: 57 + 8 = 65.

  6. 6 years ago, a family of 4 had average age 21 years. 2 children were born afterwards; nobody else joined or left. Today the 6 members average 19 years, and the children differ in age by 2 years. Find the younger child’s age in years.

    1. 2
    2. 3
    3. 4
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2

    Explanation: The children total 6 × 19 − 4 × (21 + 6) = 6 years. Younger age = (6 − 2) ÷ 2 = 2 years.

  7. Harleen averages 68 marks in 5 tests. What average must she score in the next 3 tests to average 74 over all 8 tests?

    1. 84
    2. 80
    3. 82
    4. 86
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 84

    Explanation: Required total = 8 × 74 − 5 × 68 = 252. Required average = 252 ÷ 3 = 84.

  8. The average of 11 readings is 37. If 1 reading is removed, the average of the remaining 10 is 35. What reading was removed?

    1. 55
    2. 37
    3. 57
    4. 72
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 57

    Explanation: Removed reading = 11 × 37 − 10 × 35 = 57.

  9. The average of 15 numbers is 48. The first 8 average 46 and the last 8 average 51. Find the 8th number.

    1. 48
    2. 56
    3. 51
    4. 104
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 56

    Explanation: The 8th number is counted twice in the 2 groups. It equals 8 × 46 + 8 × 51 − 15 × 48 = 56.

  10. A set of observations has average 28. Each observation is multiplied by 3 and then reduced by 5. What is the new average?

    1. 69
    2. 84
    3. 79
    4. 89
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 79

    Explanation: The average undergoes the same change: 28 × 3 − 5 = 79.

  11. 20 workers average 31 years of age. A worker aged 50 years leaves. Find the average age of the remaining workers in years.

    1. 29
    2. 30.5
    3. 32
    4. 30
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 30

    Explanation: The departing worker is 19 years above average. Average falls by 19 ÷ 19 = 1, giving 30 years.

  12. Technicians average ₹840 per day and helpers average ₹540 per day. Their combined average is ₹660. If there are 18 helpers, how many technicians are there?

    1. 18
    2. 27
    3. 30
    4. 12
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 12

    Explanation: Technicians : helpers = (660 − 540) : (840 − 660) = 2 : 3. Technicians = 18 × 2 ÷ 3 = 12.

  13. A batter scores 92 in his 16th innings, raising his average runs per innings by 4. Find his new average.

    1. 28
    2. 36
    3. 32
    4. 30
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 32

    Explanation: New average = 92 − 15 × 4 = 32.

  14. What is the average of the squares of the first 10 positive integers?

    1. 35
    2. 55
    3. 38.5
    4. 42.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 38.5

    Explanation: Average of the first n squares is (n + 1)(2n + 1) ÷ 6. For n = 10, it is 11 × 21 ÷ 6 = 38.5.

  15. The average of 40 entries was calculated as 52, but 79 was entered as 39. Find the correct average.

    1. 53
    2. 51
    3. 54
    4. 52.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 53

    Explanation: The total is short by 79 − 39 = 40. Correct average = 52 + 40 ÷ 40 = 53.

  16. A bus travels 120 km at 60 km/h and returns over the same route at 40 km/h. It also halts for 1 hour. Find its average speed for the entire trip, including the halt, in km/h.

    1. 40
    2. 48
    3. 50
    4. 45
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 40

    Explanation: Total time = 120 ÷ 60 + 120 ÷ 40 + 1 = 6 hours. Average speed = 240 ÷ 6 = 40 km/h.

  17. The pairwise averages of A and B, B and C, and C and A are 34, 39 and 41 respectively. Find B.

    1. 36
    2. 32
    3. 46
    4. 28
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 32

    Explanation: B = 34 + 39 − 41 = 32.

  18. 40 candidates average 71 marks. The top 10 average 86 marks. What is the average of the remaining candidates?

    1. 68
    2. 69
    3. 66
    4. 56
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 66

    Explanation: The top 10 have 10 × 15 = 150 marks above the overall base. Remaining average = 71 − 150 ÷ 30 = 66.

  19. In a packing unit, 3 cartons weigh 12 kg each, 4 weigh 15 kg each and 3 weigh 18 kg each. What is the average carton weight in kg?

    1. 15
    2. 14
    3. 16
    4. 45
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 15

    Explanation: The equal counts at 12 and 18 balance around 15; the other cartons also weigh 15. Average = 15 kg.

  20. Replacing a 42 kg student with a 66 kg student raises a group’s average weight by 0.8 kg. Later, a 54 kg student is replaced by a 39 kg student in the same group. By how many kg does the average fall in the second replacement?

    1. 0.4
    2. 0.5
    3. 0.6
    4. 0.8
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 0.5

    Explanation: Group size = (66 − 42) ÷ 0.8 = 30. Second fall = (54 − 39) ÷ 30 = 0.5 kg.

  21. The average age of 32 students is 14 years 6 months. Including their teacher raises it by 6 months. Find the teacher’s age in years.

    1. 30.5
    2. 30
    3. 31
    4. 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 31

    Explanation: Teacher’s age = 14.5 + 33 × 0.5 = 31 years.

  22. A bowler concedes 252 runs and takes 14 wickets. What is his bowling average in runs per wicket?

    1. 16
    2. 18
    3. 20
    4. 14
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 18

    Explanation: Bowling average = runs conceded ÷ wickets = 252 ÷ 14 = 18.

  23. An examination gives 40% weight to a written score of 75 and 60% to an interview score. If the final weighted score is 81, find the interview score.

    1. 85
    2. 84
    3. 87
    4. 90
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 85

    Explanation: Interview score = (81 − 0.4 × 75) ÷ 0.6 = 85.

  24. 5 distinct positive integers average 18. If the smallest is 4, what is the greatest possible value of the largest?

    1. 74
    2. 70
    3. 64
    4. 68
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 68

    Explanation: Minimise the other 4 as 4, 5, 6 and 7. Largest = 5 × 18 − (4 + 5 + 6 + 7) = 68.

  25. A family spends an average of ₹18,000 per month for 4 months and ₹21,000 per month for the next 8 months. Its annual savings are ₹48,000. Find its average monthly income in ₹.

    1. 23500
    2. 25000
    3. 20000
    4. 24000
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 24000

    Explanation: Monthly expenditure = (4 × 18000 + 8 × 21000) ÷ 12 = 20000. Add monthly savings 48000 ÷ 12 = 4000 to get ₹24000.

Why Average MCQ – Part 1 is worth the hours

Where the marks actually come from.
