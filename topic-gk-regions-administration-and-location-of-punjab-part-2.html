  1. The Lahore–Wagah branch line is:

    1. 15 km
    2. 20 km
    3. 25 km
    4. 30 km
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 25 km

    Explanation: The Lahore–Wagah branch line is 25 km long. It has 6 stations. It is associated with the Wagah–Attari border route.

  2. The Lahore–Wagah branch line has:

    1. 4 stations
    2. 5 stations
    3. 6 stations
    4. 7 stations
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 6 stations

    Explanation: The branch line has 6 stations. Its length is 25 km. These figures are stated in the notes.

    1. 1971
    2. 1976
    3. 1981
    4. 1986
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1976

    Explanation: Samjhauta Express started in 1976. The notes state that it is currently suspended. The suspension is given as since 2019.

  4. Samjhauta Express has been suspended since:

    1. 2017
    2. 2018
    3. 2019
    4. 2020
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 2019

    Explanation: The notes state that Samjhauta Express has been suspended since 2019. It started in 1976. This answer follows the source note exactly.

  5. Punjab’s latitude extends from:

    3. 28°30′ N to 31°32′ N

    Answer: (A) 29°30′ N to 32°32′ N

    Explanation: Punjab extends from 29°30′ N to 32°32′ N latitude. Its longitude range is 73°55′ E to 76°50′ E. The figures are given in the location section.

    Explanation: Punjab extends from 73°55′ E to 76°50′ E longitude. Its latitude range is 29°30′ N to 32°32′ N. Both ranges are stated in the notes.

  7. Punjab’s total area is:

    1. 40,362 sq km
    2. 45,362 sq km
    3. 50,362 sq km
    4. 55,362 sq km
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 50,362 sq km

    Explanation: Punjab has an area of 50,362 sq km, or 19,445 sq miles. This is about 1.53% of India’s area. The figure is from the source notes.

  8. Punjab accounts for about what percentage of India’s area?

    1. 1.13%
    2. 1.53%
    3. 1.83%
    4. 2.03%
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1.53%

    Explanation: Punjab accounts for about 1.53% of India’s area. Its total area is 50,362 sq km. Both facts are given together in the notes.

  9. Punjab is the ___ largest state by area:

    1. 17th
    2. 18th
    3. 19th
    4. 20th
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 19th

    Explanation: Punjab is ranked 19th largest state by area in the notes. Its total area is 50,362 sq km. This is an area ranking, not a population ranking.

  10. Punjab is described as:

    1. Circular
    2. Rectangular
    3. Triangular
    4. Oval
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Triangular

    Explanation: Punjab is described as triangular. Pathankot is the top, Fazilka the bottom-left and Patiala the bottom-right. These locations illustrate the stated shape.

  11. Punjab’s north–south length is:

    1. 235 km
    2. 300 km
    3. 335 km
    4. 365 km
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 335 km

    Explanation: The north–south length is 335 km. The east–west length is 300 km. These are the dimensions stated in the notes.

  12. Punjab’s east–west length is:

    1. 250 km
    2. 275 km
    3. 300 km
    4. 325 km
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 300 km

    Explanation: The east–west length is 300 km. The north–south length is 335 km. Both measurements are given in the chapter.

  13. Punjab’s approximate rural–urban area distribution is:

    1. 86% and 14%
    2. 90% and 10%
    3. 94% and 6%
    4. 96% and 4%
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 96% and 4%

    Explanation: The notes give about 96% rural and 4% urban area. This is an area distribution. It should not be interpreted as population distribution.

  14. Forests cover approximately:

    1. 2%
    2. 4%
    3. 6%
    4. 8%
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 4%

    Explanation: Forests cover about 4% of Punjab’s area. Hoshiarpur has the most forest area and Sangrur the least. The figure is approximate as stated.

  15. The most forest area is in:

    1. Hoshiarpur
    2. Sangrur
    3. Bathinda
    4. Fazilka
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Hoshiarpur

    Explanation: Hoshiarpur has the most forest area among the districts mentioned. Sangrur has the least. Punjab’s overall forest cover is about 4%.

  16. The least forest area is in:

    1. Hoshiarpur
    2. Sangrur
    3. Rupnagar
    4. Pathankot
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Sangrur

    Explanation: Sangrur has the least forest area according to the notes. Hoshiarpur has the most. Overall forest cover is about 4% of Punjab’s area.

    1. 11
    2. 12
    3. 13
    4. 14
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 13

    Explanation: Punjab has 13 Municipal Corporations. The notes also list 98 Municipal Councils and 55 Town Panchayats. These are urban local bodies.

  18. Punjab has how many Gram Panchayats?

    1. 11,241
    2. 12,241
    3. 13,241
    4. 14,241
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 13,241

    Explanation: The notes list 13,241 Gram Panchayats. They are rural local bodies. The same table lists 153 Block Panchayat Samitis and 22 Zila Parishads.

  19. Punjab has how many Municipal Councils?

    1. 78
    2. 88
    3. 98
    4. 108
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 98

    Explanation: Punjab has 98 Municipal Councils. They are urban local bodies. The table also lists 13 Municipal Corporations and 55 Town Panchayats.

  20. How many Punjab districts border Himachal Pradesh according to the notes?

    1. 3
    2. 4
    3. 5
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5

    Explanation: Five Punjab districts border Himachal Pradesh: Pathankot, Hoshiarpur, Rupnagar, SBS Nagar and SAS Nagar. The notes list these districts under the north and north-east boundary. Pathankot also borders Jammu and Kashmir.

  21. How many Punjab districts border Haryana according to the notes?

    1. 4
    2. 5
    3. 6
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 6

    Explanation: Six districts border Haryana: Muktsar, Bathinda, Mansa, Sangrur, Patiala and Mohali. Haryana lies to the south and south-east of Punjab. This list is distinct from the Rajasthan border list.

  22. How many Punjab districts border Rajasthan according to the notes?

    1. 1
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2

    Explanation: Two Punjab districts border Rajasthan: Muktsar and Fazilka. Rajasthan lies to the south-west of Punjab. Fazilka also has the longest Pakistan-border segment in the notes.

  23. Which district is listed as bordering Jammu and Kashmir?

    1. Pathankot
    2. Gurdaspur
    3. Amritsar
    4. Fazilka
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Pathankot

    Explanation: Pathankot is the Punjab district listed as bordering Jammu and Kashmir. It is also one of the six districts on the Pakistan border. The notes place this boundary to the north.

  24. Which set of local-body figures is correctly matched?

    1. Town Panchayats-55; Block Panchayat Samitis-153; Zila Parishads-22; Cantonment Boards-3
    2. Town Panchayats-153; Block Panchayat Samitis-55; Zila Parishads-3; Cantonment Boards-22
    3. Town Panchayats-22; Block Panchayat Samitis-3; Zila Parishads-55; Cantonment Boards-153
    4. Town Panchayats-3; Block Panchayat Samitis-22; Zila Parishads-153; Cantonment Boards-55
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Town Panchayats-55; Block Panchayat Samitis-153; Zila Parishads-22; Cantonment Boards-3

    Explanation: The correct figures are 55 Town Panchayats, 153 Block Panchayat Samitis, 22 Zila Parishads and 3 Cantonment Boards. These numbers come from the local-governance table. The other combinations interchange the figures.
