Question bank

Average MCQ – Part 7: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. The average of 2, 6, 18 and 54 is 20. Find the average of 6, 18, 54 and 162.

    1. 40
    2. 60
    3. 54
    4. 80
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 60

    Explanation: Every term has tripled, so the mean triples: 20 × 3 = 60.

  2. 2 departments have 30 and 20 employees. Their average monthly salaries differ by ₹5,000, with the larger department paid more. The combined average is ₹28,000. Find the smaller department’s average in ₹.

    1. 26000
    2. 23000
    3. 25000
    4. 30000
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 25000

    Explanation: The combined mean is 30 × 5000 ÷ 50 = ₹3000 above the lower mean. Lower mean = 28000 − 3000 = ₹25000.

  3. 40 parcels average 68 kg. 3 parcels averaging 54 kg are removed. Parcels averaging 78 kg are then added, making the final average 70 kg. How many parcels were added?

    1. 3
    2. 5
    3. 6
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 4

    Explanation: After removal the total is 40 × 68 − 3 × 54 = 2558 kg. The deficit from 37 × 70 is 32 kg; each added parcel offsets 8 kg, so 4 are added.

  4. The average cost of 15 chairs is ₹840. 10 chairs cost ₹780 each. Find the average cost of the other 5 chairs in ₹.

    1. 960
    2. 900
    3. 1020
    4. 920
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 960

    Explanation: The first 10 fall short by 10 × 60 = ₹600. Other chairs average = 840 + 600 ÷ 5 = ₹960.

  5. 2 blocks each contain 3 consecutive even integers. The first block ends immediately before the second begins. If the second block averages 32, find the first block’s average.

    1. 28
    2. 26
    3. 30
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 26

    Explanation: Moving back 1 block shifts every term by 3 × 2 = 6. First-block average = 32 − 6 = 26.

  6. A candidate attempts all 80 questions. Each correct answer earns 2 marks and each wrong answer loses 0.5 mark. His average mark per question is 1.375. How many answers are correct?

    1. 55
    2. 64
    3. 65
    4. 60
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 60

    Explanation: Total score = 80 × 1.375 = 110. Starting from all wrong gives −40; each correct adds 2.5, so correct count = (110 + 40) ÷ 2.5 = 60.

  7. A committee averages 42 years. 2 people averaging 54 years join and the average rises by 1 year. Find the original committee size.

    1. 24
    2. 20
    3. 22
    4. 26
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 22

    Explanation: 2 newcomers bring 2 × (54 − 42) = 24 extra years. Final count is 24 ÷ 1 = 24, so original count = 22.

  8. A group’s average is 54 marks. 2/3 of its members average 60. Find the average of the remaining members.

    1. 48
    2. 36
    3. 45
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 42

    Explanation: Take 3 equal parts: remaining mean = 3 × 54 − 2 × 60 = 42.

  9. The first 4 of 8 numbers average 31 and the last 4 average 39. The middle 4 average 36. Find the average of the first 2 and last 2 numbers together.

    1. 34
    2. 35
    3. 32
    4. 38
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 34

    Explanation: All 8 total 4 × 31 + 4 × 39 = 280. Outer 4 average = (280 − 4 × 36) ÷ 4 = 34.

  10. Every price in a list is first increased by ₹15 and then reduced by 20%. The original average price was ₹85. Find the new average in ₹.

    1. 83
    2. 80
    3. 68
    4. 88
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 80

    Explanation: Apply the same operations to the mean: (85 + 15) × 0.8 = ₹80.

  11. A 16-member club averages 35 years. The oldest member, aged 50, and the youngest, aged 20, leave. Find the new average age in years.

    1. 35
    2. 34
    3. 36
    4. 33
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 35

    Explanation: The departing pair also averages 35. Removing a group with the same average leaves the average at 35 years.

  12. For 50 values, values 65 and 75 are omitted, 26 is entered as 62, and the resulting total is divided by 48 to give 42. Find the correct average.

    1. 43.12
    2. 41.6
    3. 42.4
    4. 44
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 42.4

    Explanation: Correct total = 48 × 42 + 65 + 75 − 62 + 26 = 2120. Correct average = 2120 ÷ 50 = 42.4.

  13. The average age of 12 children is 9 years. Including a coach raises it to 11 years. What was the coach’s age 3 years ago, in years?

    1. 32
    2. 35
    3. 29
    4. 38
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 32

    Explanation: Coach’s present age = 13 × 11 − 12 × 9 = 35. 3 years ago it was 32 years.

  14. Find the average of the integers from 1 to 30 that are not divisible by 3.

    1. 15.5
    2. 16
    3. 15
    4. 14.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 15

    Explanation: Remove the 10 multiples of 3: remaining sum = 465 − 3 × 55 = 300. Average = 300 ÷ 20 = 15.

  15. A batter averages 48 runs per innings in a series of 8 innings. His first 5 innings average 42. Find the average of his last 3 innings.

    1. 56
    2. 60
    3. 54
    4. 58
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 58

    Explanation: Last-3 average = (8 × 48 − 5 × 42) ÷ 3 = 58.

  16. Replacing 1 observation by 84 raises a set’s average by 1.2. Replacing the same original observation by 48 would lower the original average by 0.6. Find the number of observations.

    1. 30
    2. 24
    3. 20
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 20

    Explanation: The 2 replacement totals differ by 84 − 48 = 36, while their means differ by 1.2 + 0.6 = 1.8. Count = 36 ÷ 1.8 = 20.

  17. 3 equal-sized groups average 36, 42 and 54 marks. If the third group doubles in size while retaining its average, find the new combined average.

    1. 46.5
    2. 44
    3. 48
    4. 45
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 46.5

    Explanation: Group weights become 1 : 1 : 2. New average = (36 + 42 + 2 × 54) ÷ 4 = 46.5.

  18. A candidate needs an average of 60 over 6 papers. After 5 papers, the average is 57. If the last paper has maximum 70 marks, by how many marks will the candidate fall short even with full marks?

    1. 3
    2. 10
    3. 5
    4. 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5

    Explanation: Required total = 360; maximum achievable total = 5 × 57 + 70 = 355. Shortfall = 5 marks.

  19. A grouped distribution has intervals 0–10, 10–20 and 20–30 with frequencies 2, 4 and 2. Using class midpoints, find the mean.

    1. 10
    2. 20
    3. 12.5
    4. 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 15

    Explanation: Midpoints are 5, 15 and 25. Mean = (2 × 5 + 4 × 15 + 2 × 25) ÷ 8 = 15.

  20. In a group, 6 people are each 8 kg above the average weight, and 12 are each 3 kg below it. All remaining people are each 2 kg below it. Find the total group size.

    1. 18
    2. 30
    3. 22
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 24

    Explanation: Excess = 6 × 8 = 48 kg; known deficit = 12 × 3 = 36 kg. Remaining count = (48 − 36) ÷ 2 = 6, so total = 24.

  21. A retailer buys 8 kg of almonds at ₹700 per kg and 12 kg at ₹800 per kg. At what price per kg should the lot be sold to earn ₹40 profit per kg on average?

    1. 790
    2. 800
    3. 810
    4. 820
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 800

    Explanation: Average cost = 700 + 12 × 100 ÷ 20 = ₹760. Required price = 760 + 40 = ₹800 per kg.

  22. If 5a + 5b = 430, find the average of a and b.

    1. 86
    2. 43
    3. 21.5
    4. 215
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 43

    Explanation: a + b = 430 ÷ 5 = 86. Average = 86 ÷ 2 = 43.

  23. A traveller spends equal times moving at 48 and 72 km/h. A second traveller covers equal distances at those speeds. By how many km/h is the first traveller’s average speed greater?

    1. 2.4
    2. 3
    3. 4.8
    4. 1.2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2.4

    Explanation: Equal-time average = 60; equal-distance average = 2 × 48 × 72 ÷ 120 = 57.6. Difference = 2.4 km/h.

  24. A list has correct average 24 and includes a 0. A clerk omits the 0, enters 84 as 48 and divides by the remaining count, obtaining 23.5. How many values are in the original list?

    1. 24
    2. 26
    3. 25
    4. 28
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 25

    Explanation: For original count n, 24n − 36 = 23.5(n − 1). Thus 0.5n = 12.5, giving n = 25.

  25. A hostel’s monthly cost is ₹12,000 fixed plus ₹900 per resident. Find the average cost per resident when 40 residents stay, in ₹.

    1. 900
    2. 1200
    3. 1300
    4. 1100
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1200

    Explanation: Per-resident cost = 900 + 12000 ÷ 40 = ₹1200.
