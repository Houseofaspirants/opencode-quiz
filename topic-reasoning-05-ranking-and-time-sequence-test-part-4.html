Question bank

Ranking and Time Sequence Test MCQ – Part 4: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. In a row, J is 9th from the left. K stands 7 places to J’s right. How many people are strictly between J and K?

    1. 8
    2. 7
    3. 5
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 6

    Explanation: A separation of 7 positions leaves 7 - 1 = 6 people strictly between.

  2. In a row, A and B are 10 places apart. They swap. Which quantity necessarily stays unchanged?

    1. A’s left rank
    2. Sum of their left ranks
    3. B’s right rank
    4. Number of people left of A
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Sum of their left ranks

    Explanation: The ranks a and b become b and a, so their sum remains a + b.

  3. P is 9th from the left and Q is 12th from the right. P lies left of Q. The number between them is an even number from 2 to 8 inclusive. How many total row strengths are possible?

    1. 6
    2. 4
    3. 5
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 4

    Explanation: For gap k, total = 9 + 12 + k = 21 + k. Values k = 2, 4, 6, 8 give 4 totals.

  4. In descending height, R is above S and T; S is above U; T is above V. No other comparisons are known. Which pair’s relative order cannot be determined?

    1. S and T
    2. S and U
    3. R and V
    4. R and U
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) S and T

    Explanation: R > S > U and R > T > V are fixed. Nothing compares S with T.

  5. A Bathinda help-desk task needs 75 minutes of work and one 15-minute break. It must finish at 12:20. What is the latest starting time?

    1. 10:35
    2. 11:05
    3. 10:50
    4. 11:20
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 10:50

    Explanation: Required span = 75 + 15 = 90 minutes. Subtract 90 minutes from 12:20 to get 10:50.

  6. A row contains 24 boys and 21 girls. Mannat, a girl, is 10th girl from the left and has 13 boys to her right. What is her overall rank from the left?

    1. 20
    2. 19
    3. 22
    4. 21
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 21

    Explanation: Boys to her left = 24 - 13 = 11. Add 9 girls ahead and Mannat herself: 11 + 9 + 1 = 21.

  7. A row has 56 people, with P at left rank 24. Remove the first 5 people and every person originally at ranks 30 through 36. Then insert 3 people immediately before P and 2 at the right end. Find P’s new right rank.

    1. 27
    2. 29
    3. 26
    4. 28
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 28

    Explanation: New total = 56 - 5 - 7 + 5 = 49; P’s left rank = 24 - 5 + 3 = 22. Right rank = 49 - 22 + 1 = 28.

  8. 4 stages are Registration, Screening, Interview and Verification. Screening is after Registration but before Interview. Verification is after Registration but before Screening. Which stage is third?

    1. Screening
    2. Verification
    3. Registration
    4. Interview
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Screening

    Explanation: The order is Registration, Verification, Screening, Interview. Screening is third.

  9. There are 18 people ahead of Pari and 27 behind her. What is Pari’s rank from the back?

    1. 29
    2. 46
    3. 28
    4. 27
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 28

    Explanation: Back rank counts the 27 behind plus Pari: 27 + 1 = 28.

  10. A stands at left rank 12 and B at left rank 31. How many people must be added strictly between them so that their positional gap becomes 24?

    1. 7
    2. 6
    3. 5
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5

    Explanation: Their current positional gap is 31 - 12 = 19. Each insertion increases it by 1, so 24 - 19 = 5 are needed.

  11. Row I has 20 seats and Row II has 18 seats, facing each other. From a fixed page-left, Row II seat 1 is opposite Row I seat 3. A is 7th from the right in Row I. What is the rank from the fixed page-right in Row II of the person opposite A?

    1. 5
    2. 8
    3. 6
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 7

    Explanation: A is at Row I left 14, opposite Row II left 12. Row II right rank = 18 - 12 + 1 = 7.

  12. A teacher plans to reach a school 20 minutes before assembly. She is delayed by 28 minutes relative to her plan and arrives at 08:18. When was assembly scheduled?

    1. 08:10
    2. 08:02
    3. 08:38
    4. 08:26
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 08:10

    Explanation: Actual arrival is 28 - 20 = 8 minutes after assembly time. Scheduled assembly = 08:18 - 8 = 08:10.

  13. Among A, B, C and D, A is older than exactly 2 people; B is the youngest; C is older than A. Who is second youngest?

    1. D
    2. B
    3. C
    4. A
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) D

    Explanation: The descending age order is C > A > D > B. D is second youngest.

  14. For a candidate, top rank exceeds bottom rank by 15 in a list of 68. Find the top rank.

    1. 41
    2. 43
    3. 42
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 42

    Explanation: Top + bottom = 69 and top - bottom = 15. Top = (69 + 15)/2 = 42.

  15. At actual 18:00, a clock is 20 minutes fast. It loses 5 minutes per true hour. At what actual time does it first become correct?

    1. 23:00
    2. 22:00
    3. 00:00
    4. 21:00
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 22:00

    Explanation: Time to remove the 20-minute lead = 20/5 = 4 true hours. Add 4 hours to 18:00 to get 22:00.

  16. A merit list has 80 names. Removing some names below R changes R’s bottom rank from 31 to 22 but leaves the top rank unchanged. How many names were removed?

    1. 8
    2. 11
    3. 9
    4. 10
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9

    Explanation: Only names below R were removed, so the bottom-rank reduction equals their count: 31 - 22 = 9.

  17. The 1st of a 30-day month is Sunday. What weekday is the 1st of the next month?

    1. Monday
    2. Tuesday
    3. Wednesday
    4. Thursday
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Tuesday

    Explanation: The next 1st is 30 days later; 30 mod 7 = 2. Sunday + 2 days = Tuesday.

  18. A girl is 12th girl from the left and 8th girl from the right. There are 23 boys in the row. Find the total number of students.

    1. 41
    2. 44
    3. 43
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 42

    Explanation: Girls = 12 + 8 - 1 = 19. Total students = 19 + 23 = 42.

  19. In a 43-person row, X is left 8, Y is left 22, and Z is right 6. X swaps with Y, then Y swaps with Z. What is the sum of X’s and Y’s final right ranks?

    1. 30
    2. 26
    3. 28
    4. 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 28

    Explanation: X finishes at left 22, right 22. Y finishes at Z’s original place, right 6; sum = 22 + 6 = 28.

  20. Two tests last 55 and 70 minutes with a 25-minute gap. The second test ends at 13:40. When did the first test start?

    1. 11:10
    2. 11:00
    3. 11:20
    4. 10:50
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 11:10

    Explanation: Total span = 55 + 25 + 70 = 150 minutes. 13:40 - 2 hours 30 minutes = 11:10.

  21. R is 14th from the right in a fixed row. She moves 5 places towards the left. What is her new rank from the right?

    1. 20
    2. 19
    3. 18
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 19

    Explanation: Moving left increases the right rank: 14 + 5 = 19.

  22. A hall admits visitors from actual 10:15 through 10:40, both included. A watch is always 6 minutes fast. Which reading on that watch is outside the admission window?

    1. 10:46
    2. 10:21
    3. 10:34
    4. 10:47
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 10:47

    Explanation: On the fast watch, the allowed interval is 10:21 through 10:46. Thus 10:47 is outside it.

  23. A is 18th from the left and B is 14th from the right. They are distinct, but no order or gap is specified. Which statement is correct?

    1. There is no finite maximum total
    2. Total must be odd
    3. Maximum total is 31
    4. Minimum total is 31
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) There is no finite maximum total

    Explanation: Increasing the total can place B arbitrarily far to A’s right while preserving both ranks. Thus no finite maximum follows.

  24. A dispenser releases a token at 11:07 and every 16 minutes thereafter. What is the time of its 9th release, counting 11:07 as the first?

    1. 13:23
    2. 13:31
    3. 13:47
    4. 13:15
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 13:15

    Explanation: The 9th release follows 8 intervals: 8 × 16 = 128 minutes. 11:07 + 128 minutes = 13:15.

  25. A is left 9 and B is left 36. A person must sit exactly midway between them in an occupied seat, with everyone retaining their positions. Which conclusion is valid?

    1. The person is at 23
    2. No single seat is exactly midway
    3. The person is at 24
    4. The person is at 22
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) No single seat is exactly midway

    Explanation: The midpoint is (9 + 36)/2 = 22.5. It lies between seats 22 and 23, not at a single seat.
