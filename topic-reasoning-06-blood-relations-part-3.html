Blood Relations MCQ – Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Pointing to a woman, a boy, Rohan, says, “Her father is my father’s father, and she is not my mother.” What is she to Rohan?

    1. Sister
    2. Paternal grandmother
    3. Maternal aunt
    4. Paternal aunt
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Paternal aunt

    Explanation: She is a daughter of Rohan’s paternal grandfather, hence his father’s sister.

  2. Kavya is the daughter of Ramesh’s daughter. Ramesh is a man. Which pair correctly names the relationship in both directions?

    1. Kavya: granddaughter; Ramesh: maternal grandfather
    2. Kavya: granddaughter; Ramesh: paternal grandfather
    3. Kavya: niece; Ramesh: maternal uncle
    4. Kavya: daughter; Ramesh: father
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Kavya: granddaughter; Ramesh: maternal grandfather

    Explanation: The middle person is Ramesh’s daughter, so Kavya is his granddaughter through her mother.

  3. Code: X + Y means X is Y’s brother; X - Y means X is Y’s mother; X * Y means X is Y’s husband. In A * B - C + D, how many people are certainly male?

    1. 1
    2. 4
    3. 2
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 2

    Explanation: A is male as husband and C as brother; B is female and D’s gender is unknown.

  4. A verbal tree places Dhruv (male) and Gita (female) as parents of sons Hemant and Ishaan. Hemant’s wife Juhi has their daughter Kiara. Which person is Kiara’s father’s brother?

    1. Dhruv
    2. Ishaan
    3. Hemant
    4. Juhi
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Ishaan

    Explanation: Kiara’s father is Hemant; Hemant’s brother is Ishaan.

  5. Tanya’s daughter is married to Manish. Manish’s mother is Sudha. Relative to Sudha, what is Tanya’s daughter?

    1. Daughter
    2. Mother-in-law
    3. Daughter-in-law
    4. Sister-in-law
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Daughter-in-law

    Explanation: She is the wife of Sudha’s son Manish, so she is Sudha’s daughter-in-law.

  6. Code: X @ Y means X is Y’s daughter; X # Y means X is Y’s brother; X $ Y means X is Y’s mother. N is male. Which expression makes N the maternal grandfather of M’s brother P?

    1. N # R $ M # P
    2. P # M @ R # N
    3. P @ M # R @ N
    4. P # M @ R @ N
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) P # M @ R @ N

    Explanation: P is M’s brother; M is R’s daughter and R is N’s daughter, making N the father of their mother R.

  7. Seven relatives A–G form three generations. A is B’s husband and C is their son. E is F’s mother. D is F’s maternal aunt and C’s wife. G is C’s daughter. E and D are sisters; E’s husband is outside this group. C and D are G’s parents. Who is G’s maternal aunt?

    1. E
    2. B
    3. F
    4. D
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) E

    Explanation: G’s mother is C’s wife D; D’s sister E is G’s maternal aunt.

  8. A and B are siblings of unspecified gender. A is parent of a boy C. B is parent of a girl D. Which relationship is certain?

    1. A is D’s paternal aunt
    2. B is C’s maternal uncle
    3. C is D’s brother
    4. C and D are cousins
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) C and D are cousins

    Explanation: Their parents A and B are siblings, so C and D are cousins regardless of the parents’ genders.

  9. A man, Yusuf, points to a girl: “Her mother and I have the same mother, but her mother is not my wife.” Through which person is Yusuf related to the girl?

    1. His wife, the girl’s aunt
    2. His mother, the girl’s mother
    3. His daughter, the girl’s sister
    4. His sister, the girl’s mother
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) His sister, the girl’s mother

    Explanation: Yusuf and the girl’s mother share a mother; since Yusuf is male, she is his sister.

  10. There are 4 boys and 3 girls in one sibling group. Each child has the same parents. For any one girl, what is the ratio of her brothers to her sisters?

    1. 4:3
    2. 1:1
    3. 2:1
    4. 3:2
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 2:1

    Explanation: A girl has 4 brothers and 2 sisters, excluding herself; 4:2 simplifies to 2:1.

  11. Let X # Y mean X is Y’s father, X @ Y mean X is Y’s sister, and X % Y mean X is Y’s son. In D % C @ B # A, D is A’s _____.

    1. Brother
    2. Father’s sister’s son
    3. Mother’s brother’s son
    4. Father’s brother
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Father’s sister’s son

    Explanation: A’s father is B; B’s sister is C; C’s son is D.

  12. Question: Is A B’s father? I. A is male and B is A’s child. II. B is A’s daughter. Which judgement is correct?

    1. I alone suffices; II alone does not
    2. Both are necessary
    3. Either alone suffices
    4. II alone suffices; I alone does not
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) I alone suffices; II alone does not

    Explanation: I gives a male parent, hence father; II fixes only B’s gender, leaving A’s gender unknown.

  13. A man, Bhuvan, is father of Charu. Charu’s husband Devesh has a sister Ekta. Ekta’s son is Feroz. Which of these links is established between Charu and Feroz?

    1. Charu is Feroz’s grandmother
    2. Charu is Feroz’s mother’s brother’s wife
    3. Charu is Feroz’s father’s sister
    4. Charu is Feroz’s mother’s sister
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Charu is Feroz’s mother’s brother’s wife

    Explanation: Feroz’s mother is Ekta, Ekta’s brother is Devesh, and his wife is Charu.

  14. X + Y means X is Y’s son, X - Y means X is Y’s mother, X * Y means X is Y’s brother. Which expression is inconsistent if each person has only one mother and all letters denote distinct people?

    1. A - B + C - B
    2. A - B * C
    3. A * B + C
    4. A + B - C
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A - B + C - B

    Explanation: A - B and C - B make distinct A and C both B’s mother, violating the condition.

  15. A woman, Pavneet, says of a boy in a school photograph: “His father is my husband’s only brother.” Which description gives the boy’s relationship to Pavneet?

    1. Her own son
    2. Husband’s sister’s son
    3. Husband’s brother’s son
    4. Her sister’s son
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Husband’s brother’s son

    Explanation: The boy’s father is Pavneet’s husband’s brother, so the boy is her nephew through that marriage.

  16. The verbal tree has married parents A (male) and B (female), their sons C and D, C’s wife E, D’s wife F, and daughters G of C–E and H of D–F. Which statement is false?

    1. B is H’s paternal grandmother
    2. E is G’s mother
    3. C and D are brothers
    4. G and H are sisters
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) G and H are sisters

    Explanation: G and H have different sibling fathers, so they are cousins, not sisters.

  17. Six people A–F form exactly two married couples and three generations. A is B’s husband. E is C’s sister and A’s daughter. F is D’s daughter and B’s granddaughter. C, a man, is F’s father. Every child’s parents are married. Which member is simultaneously a son, a husband and a father within this family?

    1. E
    2. D
    3. C
    4. A
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) C

    Explanation: E’s brother C is A–B’s son; F’s parents C and D form the second couple, so C is also husband and father.

  18. Rekha’s brother Sanjay is married to Poonam. Poonam’s sister Usha has a daughter, Vidhi. What is Vidhi’s relationship to Sanjay?

    1. Brother’s daughter
    2. Wife’s sister’s daughter
    3. Wife’s brother’s daughter
    4. Sister’s daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Wife’s sister’s daughter

    Explanation: Sanjay → wife Poonam → sister Usha → daughter Vidhi is the precise route.

  19. Start at a boy A. Go to his mother B, then B’s father C, then C’s son D, then D’s daughter E. What is E’s generation relative to A?

    1. One generation above
    2. Two generations above
    3. The same generation
    4. One generation below
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) The same generation

    Explanation: Two parent steps up and two child steps down cancel: +1 +1 -1 -1 = 0.

  20. A girl has twice as many brothers as sisters. Her brother has the same number of brothers and sisters. How many children share these parents?

    1. 6
    2. 7
    3. 5
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 7

    Explanation: With b boys and g girls, b = 2(g - 1) and b - 1 = g; hence g = 3, b = 4, total 7.

  21. In this code X : Y means X is Y’s mother and X ; Y means X is Y’s father. Read A : B ; C : D. Which ordered pair lists D’s mother and maternal grandfather?

    1. A, B
    2. B, A
    3. C, A
    4. C, B
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) C, B

    Explanation: C is D’s mother; C’s father B is D’s maternal grandfather.

  22. Code: X @ Y means X is Y’s husband; X + Y means X is Y’s father; X - Y means X is Y’s sister. Which expression proves T’s wife is the sister of R’s father?

    1. T @ U - V + R
    2. T @ U + V - R
    3. T - U @ V + R
    4. R + V - U @ T
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) T @ U - V + R

    Explanation: T’s wife is U; U is V’s sister and V is R’s father, giving T @ U - V + R.

  23. I. M is N’s wife. II. N is P’s father. Claim: M is certainly P’s biological mother. No rule about remarriage or children of earlier marriages is supplied. Which assessment is logically sound?

    1. The claim follows from I alone
    2. The claim needs an additional parenthood fact
    3. M must be P’s sister
    4. The claim follows from II alone
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) The claim needs an additional parenthood fact

    Explanation: A father’s current wife need not be his child’s biological mother; explicit maternity is required here.

  24. Nirmal’s father and Sohan’s mother are brother and sister. Both Nirmal and Sohan are men. Which statement describes Nirmal from Sohan’s side?

    1. Mother’s brother’s son
    2. Father’s sister’s son
    3. Mother’s sister’s son
    4. Father’s brother’s son
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Mother’s brother’s son

    Explanation: Sohan’s mother’s brother is Nirmal’s father; Nirmal is therefore Sohan’s maternal-uncle’s son.

  25. A man, Ashok, introduces a woman: “Her only son’s wife is my wife’s only sister.” Ashok’s wife and her sister share the same parents. What is the woman’s son to Ashok?

    1. Sister’s husband
    2. Wife’s brother
    3. Wife’s sister’s husband
    4. Father-in-law
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Wife’s sister’s husband

    Explanation: The woman’s son is married to Ashok’s wife’s sister, making the two men co-brothers-in-law.
