About this cluster

What Sports covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Sports is a dependable scoring lane in the general awareness section, because the questions are factual, limited in number and slow to change. Exams ask about trophies and cups, the venues and organisers of major events, terminology, notable players and the awards associated with achievement in sport. Much of the material is stable across years, so one careful revision followed by repeated testing works better than continuous reading. The recurring trap is pairing errors - associating a trophy with the wrong sport or an award with the wrong discipline - which is why the lanes below group each item with the category it belongs to rather than listing them separately. Secure the trophies and the event calendar first, since those account for the largest share of questions.
