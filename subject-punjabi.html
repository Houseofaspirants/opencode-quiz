About this subject

All about Punjabi

What this page holds and how to work through it.

Punjabi is the section most candidates treat as a formality and then lose marks in anyway. The paper is rarely hard - grammar rules, muhavare, akhan, vocabulary and a short run through literature - it is simply unprepared, which shows up as a minute spent per question on something that should take ten seconds.

Practise it the way you would practise reasoning: small, frequent and self-correcting. Attempt a set, read the explanation behind every wrong answer, and keep the idiom and muhavara notes to one line each. Within a fortnight the same questions stop being reading and start being recognition.

Punjabi on House of Aspirants publishes as separate, self-contained sets rather than one endless paper. Each set runs 30 seconds per question with four options and a written explanation, so a wrong answer teaches the next attempt instead of only scoring it.

Work it in three moves: open the daily quiz (20 questions) to find the lane that is costing you marks, run a topic set when you want depth, and keep a full mock test for the weekend. A set is cleared at 40% accuracy, and your subject-wise accuracy is tracked on the progress page.

A wrong answer lands in your bookmarks together with its explanation, so revision becomes a second pass over the same set instead of a separate chapter of notes.

These are the papers Punjabi shows up in most: Punjab Police ASI, Punjab Police Jail Warder, Senior Assistant. Written guides on the portal cover the same ground: Punjab Police Exam Preparation: Constable, ASI & SI Strategy.
