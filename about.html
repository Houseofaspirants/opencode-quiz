House of Aspirants

“Practice Daily. Crack Punjab Police.” — a distraction-free quiz portal built for serious aspirants of Punjab Police, PSSSB, Punjab Government Exams and other competitive examinations.

Quick answer

House of Aspirants is a free, distraction-free MCQ portal for Punjab Police, PSSSB and Punjab Government exam aspirants: seven subjects, a daily quiz, mock tests, study guides and offline use.

Mission

Why this portal exists

Exam aspirants need one thing above all: consistent, high-quality practice. This portal removes every barrier between you and your next quiz — no fees, no popups, a 2-second Google sign-in to save your progress, works offline, loads fast on a 3G connection and opens like an app on your phone.

Every question is written and reviewed by the House of Aspirants team, with explanations and references so you learn the why, not just the answer.

New here? Start with the free study guides, then build your first mock test.

Who writes the questions and guides?

The House of Aspirants editorial team writes and reviews them, checking each explanation against the exam syllabus and keeping answers self-contained so the reasoning is visible.

Is House of Aspirants affiliated with any recruiting body?

No. This is an independent, unofficial study portal. Nothing here is connected to or endorsed by Punjab Police, PSSSB, PPSC, PSPCL or any government department.

How do I report a wrong answer?

Send the question number and topic through the contact form or on Telegram. Corrections are reviewed before they are published, so mistakes do not stay in the bank.

How is the portal paid for?

It is not monetised. There is no subscription, no paid tier and no advertising network — which is why every guide and quiz stays free for everyone.
