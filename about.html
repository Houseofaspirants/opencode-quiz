Quick answer

House of Aspirants is a free exam learning platform for Punjab Police, PSSSB and PPSC aspirants, run by Punjab Police SI Rank 2 Gurpreet Singh. Study material, expected MCQs, mock tests, previous year papers and monthly current affairs, in Punjabi and English, with nothing behind a paywall.

The story

From aspirant to Rank 2

The SI exam has two scoring papers of 100 questions each, plus a qualifying Punjabi paper. The method that produced 713/800 was simple to say and hard to keep: accuracy over attempts, the static syllabus finished first, and daily revision of what was already read.

Every part of that preparation (the pattern, the full attempt sheet, the marks that leaked, the schedule) is published in the Rank 2 Blueprint so you can check it rather than take it on trust.

Who writes the questions and guides?

The House of Aspirants editorial team writes and reviews them, checking each explanation against the exam syllabus and keeping answers self-contained so the reasoning is visible.

Is House of Aspirants affiliated with any recruiting body?

No. This is an independent, unofficial study platform. Nothing here is connected to or endorsed by Punjab Police, PSSSB, PPSC, PSPCL or any government department.

How do I report a wrong answer?

Send the question number and topic through the contact form or on Telegram. Corrections are reviewed before they are published, so mistakes do not stay in the bank.

How is the platform paid for?

It is supported by a few clearly labelled Google ads. There is no subscription and no paid tier, so every guide and quiz stays free for everyone.
