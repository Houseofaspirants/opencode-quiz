Birthplace of Guru Teg Bahadur Ji - Part 2: all 20 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Where was Guru Teg Bahadur Ji born?

    1. Amritsar
    2. Anandpur Sahib
    3. Patna
    4. Nanded
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Amritsar

    Explanation: The revision box gives Amritsar as Guru Teg Bahadur Ji’s birthplace. Anandpur Sahib is the city he founded, Patna is Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s birthplace, and Nanded is associated with the latter’s Jyoti Jot. The notes also give Guru Teg Bahadur Ji’s birth date as 1 April 1621.

  2. Which group is described as the democratic institution of the Five Beloved Ones?

    1. Masands
    2. Panj Pyare
    3. Chali Mukte
    4. Hill chiefs
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Panj Pyare

    Explanation: The notes describe the Panj Pyare as the democratic institution of the Five Beloved Ones. The Masand system was abolished, Chali Mukte refers to the forty Sikhs associated with Khidrana, and hill chiefs appear in the battle accounts. The Panj Pyare are discussed under the creation of the Khalsa.

  3. Who is identified as Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s eldest son?

    1. Sahibzada Fateh Singh
    2. Sahibzada Jujhar Singh
    3. Sahibzada Zorawar Singh
    4. Sahibzada Ajit Singh
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Sahibzada Ajit Singh

    Explanation: The sons row explicitly identifies Sahibzada Ajit Singh as the eldest. Sahibzada Fateh Singh is marked as the youngest, while Sahibzada Jujhar Singh and Sahibzada Zorawar Singh are the other two sons listed. The battle table associates Sahibzada Ajit Singh and Sahibzada Jujhar Singh with martyrdom at Chamkaur.

  4. Who took Guru Teg Bahadur Ji’s body and cremated it at his own house?

    1. Makhan Shah Lubana
    2. Bhai Jaita Ji
    3. Lakhi Shah Lubana
    4. Bhai Mani Singh
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Lakhi Shah Lubana

    Explanation: The source assigns the cremation of Guru Teg Bahadur Ji’s body to Lakhi Shah Lubana. Makhan Shah Lubana recognised Guru Ji at Baba Bakala, Bhai Jaita Ji carried the sacred head, and Bhai Mani Singh is associated with the scriptural work described in the chapter. Gurdwara Rakab Ganj Sahib now stands at the cremation site.

  5. In which language is Var Sri Bhagauti Ji Ki (Chandi di Var) identified in the notes?

    1. Punjabi
    2. Persian
    3. Hindi
    4. Sanskrit
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Punjabi

    Explanation: The compositions list explicitly identifies Var Sri Bhagauti Ji Ki, also called Chandi di Var, as being in Punjabi. Persian, Hindi and Sanskrit do not match the language specified for this work in the source. It appears among the key compositions associated with Guru Gobind Singh Ji.

  6. Which expression is identified as the Sikh national anthem in the notes?

    1. Bole So Nihal, Sat Sri Akal
    2. Deh Shiva Bar Mohe Ehai
    3. Var Sri Bhagauti Ji Ki
    4. Khalse di Mahima
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Deh Shiva Bar Mohe Ehai

    Explanation: The source identifies Deh Shiva Bar Mohe Ehai as the Sikh national anthem. Bole So Nihal, Sat Sri Akal is given as the jaikara, while the remaining two options are titles in the compositions list. Var Sri Bhagauti Ji Ki is also called Chandi di Var in the chapter.

  7. How many years of Guruship are recorded for Guru Gobind Singh Ji?

    1. 11 years
    2. 33 years
    3. 40 years
    4. 50 years
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 33 years

    Explanation: The revision box records Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s Guruship as thirty-three years. Eleven years belongs to Guru Teg Bahadur Ji’s Guruship, and the other two durations are not assigned to Guru Gobind Singh Ji in the source. His Guruship is shown as extending from 1675 to 1708.

  8. Through which pair was the Zafarnama sent?

    1. Bhai Mati Das Ji and Bhai Dayala Ji
    2. Bhai Jaita Ji and Bhai Mani Singh
    3. Makhan Shah Lubana and Lakhi Shah Lubana
    4. Bhai Daya Singh and Bhai Dharam Singh
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Bhai Daya Singh and Bhai Dharam Singh

    Explanation: The notes state that the Zafarnama was sent through Bhai Daya Singh and Bhai Dharam Singh. The other pairs contain people associated with different events, including martyrdom, care of the sacred head and body, recognition, or scriptural work. The source places the writing of the letter after the Battle of Chamkaur Sahib.

  9. Consider the following statements about Guru Teg Bahadur Ji: 1. His father was Guru Hargobind Ji. 2. His mother was Mata Nanki Ji. Which of the above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Both 1 and 2

    Explanation: Statement 1 is correct because the revision box names Guru Hargobind Ji as his father. Statement 2 is correct because Mata Nanki Ji is named as his mother. Both statements are therefore correct, and Mata Gujri Ji is listed separately as his wife.

  10. Consider the following statements about Guru Gobind Singh Ji: 1. His mother was Mata Gujri Ji. 2. His father was Guru Hargobind Ji. Which of the above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1 only

    Explanation: Statement 1 is correct because Mata Gujri Ji is named as Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s mother. Statement 2 is incorrect because his father was Guru Teg Bahadur Ji. Only statement 1 is correct, and the source identifies Guru Gobind Singh Ji as the tenth Sikh Guru.

  11. Consider the following statements about Khidrana: 1. The battle is also identified as Muktsar in the notes. 2. The opposing side is listed as Mughal forces. Which of the above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Neither 1 nor 2
    4. Both 1 and 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Both 1 and 2

    Explanation: Statement 1 is correct because the battle table labels the entry Khidrana (Muktsar). Statement 2 is correct because the opposing forces are identified as Mughal forces. Both statements are correct, and the same entry records the sacrifice of the Chali Mukte.

  12. Consider the following statements about Hola Mohalla: 1. The notes date its beginning to 1701. 2. It is held on the day before Holi. Which of the above is/are correct?

    1. 2 only
    2. 1 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1 only

    Explanation: Statement 1 is correct because the notes give 1701 as the year Hola Mohalla began. Statement 2 is incorrect because it is held the day after Holi, not the day before. The chapter describes it as a festival of martial skills.

  13. Which battle–result pair is NOT correctly matched according to the notes?

    1. Nirmohgarh — victory
    2. Anandpur (First) — victory
    3. Anandpur (Second) — victory
    4. Khidrana — victory over the Mughals
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Anandpur (Second) — victory

    Explanation: The second Battle of Anandpur is recorded as ending with the evacuation of Anandpur, so the third pair is incorrect. Nirmohgarh and the first Battle of Anandpur are both recorded as victories. At Khidrana, the Sikhs defeated the Mughals, so the fourth pair is correct. The source also records the sacrifice of forty Sikhs at Khidrana.

  14. Which statement is NOT correct according to the account of Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s visit to Talwandi Sabo?

    1. The visit took place before the Battle of Khidrana.
    2. Chaudhary Dalla invited Guru Ji.
    3. Chaudhary Dalla was a Brar Sikh.
    4. Talwandi Sabo is now known as Damdama Sahib.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) The visit took place before the Battle of Khidrana.

    Explanation: The first statement is incorrect because the notes place the visit after the Battle of Khidrana. The invitation came from Chaudhary Dalla, so the second statement is correct. He is described as a Brar Sikh, making the third statement correct, and Talwandi Sabo is identified as Damdama Sahib, making the fourth correct. The source connects this place with the preparation of the final version of Sri Guru Granth Sahib.

  15. Match List I with List II according to the notes: List I (Battle) A. Bhangani B. Nirmohgarh C. Anandpur (Second) List II (Opposing side) 1. Hill chiefs and Mughals 2. Hill chiefs and Wazir Khan 3. Raja Fateh Shah of Garhwal and hill chiefs Select the correct code.

    1. A-1, B-3, C-2
    2. A-3, B-2, C-1
    3. A-2, B-1, C-3
    4. A-3, B-1, C-2
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) A-3, B-1, C-2

    Explanation: At Bhangani, the opposing side is Raja Fateh Shah of Garhwal and hill chiefs, giving A-3. At Nirmohgarh, the source lists hill chiefs and Mughals, giving B-1. At the second Battle of Anandpur, it lists hill chiefs and Wazir Khan, giving C-2. The table distinguishes these opponents from Alif Khan at Nadaun.

  16. Match the events with their places: List I A. Creation of the Khalsa B. Martyrdom of Guru Teg Bahadur Ji C. Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s Jyoti Jot List II 1. Nanded 2. Anandpur Sahib 3. Chandni Chowk, Delhi Select the correct code.

    1. A-2, B-3, C-1
    2. A-3, B-2, C-1
    3. A-2, B-1, C-3
    4. A-1, B-3, C-2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A-2, B-3, C-1

    Explanation: The Khalsa was created at Anandpur Sahib, giving A-2. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji’s martyrdom took place at Chandni Chowk in Delhi, giving B-3. Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s Jyoti Jot is associated with Nanded, giving C-1. The source also identifies Anandpur Sahib by the name Chakk Nanaki.

  17. Arrange the following from earliest to latest according to the notes: A. Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s Jyoti Jot B. Battle of Khidrana C. Birth of Guru Teg Bahadur Ji

    1. A-B-C
    2. B-C-A
    3. C-A-B
    4. C-B-A
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) C-B-A

    Explanation: Guru Teg Bahadur Ji’s birth is dated 1621, so C comes first. The Battle of Khidrana is dated 1705, placing B next. Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s Jyoti Jot is dated 1708, placing A last. The battle table also identifies Khidrana as Muktsar.

  18. Assertion (A): The notes name Bhai Mati Das Ji and Bhai Dayala Ji among the followers martyred with Guru Teg Bahadur Ji. Reason (R): The execution was ordered by Bahadur Shah. Select the correct answer.

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) A is true but R is false

    Explanation: Assertion A is true because the martyrdom account names Bhai Mati Das Ji and Bhai Dayala Ji. Reason R is false because the source attributes the execution order to Aurangzeb. Bahadur Shah is instead associated with Guru Gobind Singh Ji’s assistance in the Battle of Jajau and their subsequent meeting.

  19. Assertion (A): The notes identify Bhai Mani Singh as the scribe in the preparation of the final version of Sri Guru Granth Sahib. Reason (R): Talwandi Sabo is also called Guru Ki Kashi. Select the correct answer.

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A

    Explanation: Assertion A is true because the source names Bhai Mani Singh in connection with the writing of the final version. Reason R is also true because Talwandi Sabo is identified as Guru Ki Kashi. The place’s alternative name does not explain who performed the writing, so R is not the correct explanation of A. The source also calls Talwandi Sabo Damdama Sahib.

  20. Assertion (A): The Khalsa’s founding day is celebrated as Vaisakhi. Reason (R): Khande di Pahul was not given on the day the Khalsa was established. Select the correct answer.

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) A is true but R is false

    Explanation: Assertion A is true because the notes identify Vaisakhi as the celebration of the Khalsa’s founding day. Reason R is false because the source explicitly states that Khande di Pahul was given on the founding day. Therefore, the correct combination is a true assertion and a false reason. The source describes Khande di Pahul as Amrit.
