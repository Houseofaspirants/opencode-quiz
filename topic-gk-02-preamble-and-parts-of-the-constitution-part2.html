Question bank

Preamble and Parts of the Constitution - Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Which Constitutional Amendment Act amended the Preamble?

    1. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
    2. 44th Amendment Act, 1978
    3. 86th Amendment Act, 2002
    4. 7th Amendment Act, 1956
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 42nd Amendment Act, 1976

    Explanation: The Preamble was amended by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976.

  2. Which three words were added to the Preamble by the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976?

    1. Secular, Democratic and Unity
    2. Socialist, Republic and Integrity
    3. Socialist, Secular and Integrity
    4. Sovereign, Socialist and Secular
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Socialist, Secular and Integrity

    Explanation: The 42nd Amendment added Socialist, Secular and Integrity.

  3. Which of the following words was part of the ORIGINAL Preamble?

    1. Democratic
    2. Socialist
    3. Secular
    4. Integrity
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Democratic

    Explanation: Socialist, Secular and Integrity were added in 1976; 'Democratic' was in the original Preamble.

  4. Consider the following statements: 1. The Preamble was amended by the 42nd Amendment in 1976. 2. The word 'Sovereign' was added by the 42nd Amendment. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1 only

    Explanation: Only statement 1 is correct. 'Sovereign' was in the original Preamble; the 42nd Amendment added Socialist, Secular and Integrity.

  5. The procedure for amending the Constitution is defined in:

    1. Rules framed by the President
    2. The Constitution itself
    3. Supreme Court judgements
    4. An Act of Parliament
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) The Constitution itself

    Explanation: Amendment is the procedure for making changes to the Constitution, and this procedure is defined in the Constitution itself (Part XX, Article 368).

  6. Which Part of the Constitution deals with Fundamental Rights?

    1. Part III
    2. Part IV A
    3. Part IV
    4. Part II
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Part III

    Explanation: Part III (Articles 12–35) deals with Fundamental Rights.

  7. Directive Principles of State Policy are contained in which Part of the Constitution?

    1. Part IV A
    2. Part III
    3. Part V
    4. Part IV
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Part IV

    Explanation: Part IV (Articles 36–51) contains the Directive Principles of State Policy.

  8. Fundamental Duties are contained in which Part and Article?

    1. Part V, Article 52
    2. Part IV, Article 51
    3. Part IV A, Article 51A
    4. Part III, Article 35
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Part IV A, Article 51A

    Explanation: Part IV A (Article 51A) deals with Fundamental Duties.

  9. Citizenship is dealt with in which Part and Articles of the Constitution?

    1. Part I, Articles 1–4
    2. Part II, Articles 5–11
    3. Part II, Articles 12–18
    4. Part III, Articles 12–35
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Part II, Articles 5–11

    Explanation: Part II (Articles 5–11) deals with Citizenship.

  10. Articles 52–151 of the Constitution fall under which Part?

    1. Part VI – The States
    2. Part VIII – Union Territories
    3. Part XI – Union-State Relations
    4. Part V – The Union
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Part V – The Union

    Explanation: Part V (Articles 52–151) deals with The Union.

  11. Part VI of the Constitution, covering Articles 152–237, deals with:

    1. The Panchayats
    2. Union Territories
    3. The Union
    4. The States
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) The States

    Explanation: Part VI (Articles 152–237) deals with The States.

  12. Which Part of the Constitution was repealed by the 7th Amendment Act, 1956?

    1. Part VIII
    2. Part VII
    3. Part IX
    4. Part XXII
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Part VII

    Explanation: Part VII was repealed by the 7th Amendment Act, 1956.

  13. Part IX of the Constitution deals with:

    1. The Panchayats
    2. The Municipalities
    3. Union Territories
    4. Co-operative Societies
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) The Panchayats

    Explanation: Part IX (Articles 243–243O) deals with the Panchayats.

  14. Co-operative Societies are dealt with in which Part of the Constitution?

    1. Part XIV A
    2. Part X
    3. Part IX A
    4. Part IX B
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Part IX B

    Explanation: Part IX B (Articles 243ZH–243ZT) deals with Co-operative Societies.

  15. The Municipalities are covered under which Articles of the Constitution?

    1. 244–244A
    2. 243P–243ZG
    3. 243ZH–243ZT
    4. 243–243O
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 243P–243ZG

    Explanation: Part IX A (Articles 243P–243ZG) deals with the Municipalities.

  16. Relations between the Union and the States are dealt with in:

    1. Part XIII (Articles 301–307)
    2. Part XII (Articles 264–300A)
    3. Part XI (Articles 245–263)
    4. Part X (Articles 244–244A)
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Part XI (Articles 245–263)

    Explanation: Part XI (Articles 245–263) deals with relations between the Union and the States.

  17. Trade, Commerce and Intercourse within the territory of India is dealt with in:

    1. Part XII
    2. Part XIV
    3. Part XI
    4. Part XIII
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Part XIII

    Explanation: Part XIII (Articles 301–307) deals with Trade, Commerce and Intercourse within India.

  18. Tribunals are dealt with in which Part of the Constitution?

    1. Part XV (Articles 324–329A)
    2. Part XIV A (Articles 323A–323B)
    3. Part XIV (Articles 308–323)
    4. Part IX B
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Part XIV A (Articles 323A–323B)

    Explanation: Part XIV A (Articles 323A–323B) deals with Tribunals.

  19. Elections are dealt with in which Part of the Constitution?

    1. Part XVI
    2. Part XIV
    3. Part XVII
    4. Part XV
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Part XV

    Explanation: Part XV (Articles 324–329A) deals with Elections.

  20. Official Languages are dealt with in which Articles of the Constitution?

    1. 352–360
    2. 361–367
    3. 343–351
    4. 330–342
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 343–351

    Explanation: Part XVII (Articles 343–351) deals with Official Languages.

  21. Emergency Provisions are contained in which Part of the Constitution?

    1. Part XIX
    2. Part XV
    3. Part XVIII
    4. Part XX
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Part XVIII

    Explanation: Part XVIII (Articles 352–360) contains the Emergency Provisions.

  22. Which Article of the Constitution deals with the amendment of the Constitution?

    1. Article 368
    2. Article 370
    3. Article 352
    4. Article 360
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 368

    Explanation: Part XX (Article 368) deals with the amendment of the Constitution.

  23. Part XXII (Articles 393–395) of the Constitution deals with:

    1. Temporary, Transitional and Special Provisions
    2. Miscellaneous
    3. Short title, Commencement, Authoritative Text in Hindi and Repeals
    4. Official Languages
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Short title, Commencement, Authoritative Text in Hindi and Repeals

    Explanation: Part XXII (Articles 393–395) deals with Short title, Commencement, Authoritative Text in Hindi and Repeals.

  24. Match the Parts with their subjects: A. Part VIII – 1. Scheduled and Tribal Areas B. Part X – 2. Union Territories C. Part XVI – 3. Special provisions relating to certain classes

    1. A-2, B-1, C-3
    2. A-2, B-3, C-1
    3. A-3, B-1, C-2
    4. A-1, B-2, C-3
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A-2, B-1, C-3

    Explanation: Part VIII – Union Territories (239–242); Part X – Scheduled and Tribal Areas (244–244A); Part XVI – Special provisions relating to certain classes (330–342).

  25. Finance, Property, Contracts and Suits are dealt with in which Articles?

    1. 301–307
    2. 264–300A
    3. 245–263
    4. 308–323
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 264–300A

    Explanation: Part XII (Articles 264–300A) deals with Finance, Property, Contracts and Suits.
