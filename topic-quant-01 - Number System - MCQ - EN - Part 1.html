01 Number System MCQ EN Part 1: all 25 questions

  1. Which of the following is an irrational number?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '√18'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '√81'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '0.625'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '−13/4'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) {'label': 'A', 'text': '√18'}

    Explanation: √18 = 3√2, which is irrational; the other three values are rational.

  2. If the four-digit number 52x4 is divisible by 12, find the digit x.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '1'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '7'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '4'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '8'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) {'label': 'C', 'text': '4'}

    Explanation: Divisibility by 4 requires x to be even. The digit sum 11 + x must also be divisible by 3, so x = 4.

  3. How many positive factors of 7,560 are divisible by 12?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '32'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '24'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '48'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '16'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) {'label': 'B', 'text': '24'}

    Explanation: 7,560 = 2³ × 3³ × 5 × 7. For divisibility by 12, the factor must contain at least 2² × 3; the count is (3 − 2 + 1) × (3 − 1 + 1) × 2 × 2 = 24.

  4. Using each of the digits 0, 2, 6 and 9 exactly once, what is the smallest four-digit even number?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '2069'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '2690'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '2906'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '2096'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) {'label': 'D', 'text': '2096'}

    Explanation: Start with 2 and then 0 to minimise the number. Of the remaining digits, 6 must be last to make it even, giving 2096.

  5. What is the unit digit of 3^47?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '7'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '3'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '9'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '1'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) {'label': 'A', 'text': '7'}

    Explanation: The unit digits repeat as 3, 9, 7, 1. Since 47 leaves remainder 3 on division by 4, the unit digit is 7.

  6. What is the least positive integer by which 540 must be multiplied to obtain a perfect square?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '5'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '15'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '10'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '30'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) {'label': 'B', 'text': '15'}

    Explanation: 540 = 2² × 3³ × 5. Multiply by 3 × 5 = 15 to make every prime exponent even.

  7. Find the remainder when 7^103 is divided by 13.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '7'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '5'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '12'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '6'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) {'label': 'D', 'text': '6'}

    Explanation: 7³ leaves remainder 5 and 7^6 leaves remainder −1 modulo 13. Since 103 = 6 × 17 + 1, the remainder is −7 + 13 = 6.

  8. A number divided by 23 gives quotient 17 and remainder 9. Find the number.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '391'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '382'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '400'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '409'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) {'label': 'C', 'text': '400'}

    Explanation: Dividend = 23 × 17 + 9 = 400.

  9. Find the sum of all odd integers from 31 to 79, including both endpoints.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '1375'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '1320'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '1430'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '2750'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) {'label': 'A', 'text': '1375'}

    Explanation: There are (79 − 31) ÷ 2 + 1 = 25 terms. Sum = 25 × (31 + 79) ÷ 2 = 1375.

  10. What is the smallest four-digit number that leaves remainder 5 when divided by each of 12, 18 and 30?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '1005'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '1080'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '1265'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '1085'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) {'label': 'D', 'text': '1085'}

    Explanation: LCM of 12, 18 and 30 is 180. The least multiple of 180 at least 995 is 1080, so the required number is 1080 + 5 = 1085.

  11. What is the greatest positive integer that divides n² − 1 for every odd integer n?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '4'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '8'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '16'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '24'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) {'label': 'B', 'text': '8'}

    Explanation: n² − 1 = (n − 1)(n + 1), a product of consecutive even integers, so it is divisible by 8. Taking n = 3 gives 8, so no larger integer always divides it.

  12. Find the sum of all positive factors of 150.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '360'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '186'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '372'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '300'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) {'label': 'C', 'text': '372'}

    Explanation: 150 = 2 × 3 × 5². Sum of factors = (1 + 2)(1 + 3)(1 + 5 + 25) = 3 × 4 × 31 = 372.

  13. A two-digit number exceeds its reverse by 36. The sum of the squares of its digits is 80. Find the original number.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '84'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '48'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '62'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '73'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) {'label': 'A', 'text': '84'}

    Explanation: For digits x and y, x − y = 36 ÷ 9 = 4. Then (x + y)² = 2(x² + y²) − (x − y)² = 160 − 16 = 144, so x + y = 12 and the digits are 8 and 4.

  14. What is the unit digit of 2^35 + 3^26 + 7^19?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '2'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '0'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '8'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '6'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) {'label': 'B', 'text': '0'}

    Explanation: The three unit digits are 8, 9 and 3 respectively. Their sum is 20, so the required unit digit is 0.

  15. An arithmetic progression begins with 11, 18, 25 and 32. Find its 20th term.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '151'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '140'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '133'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '144'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) {'label': 'D', 'text': '144'}

    Explanation: The common difference is 7. The 20th term is 11 + (20 − 1) × 7 = 144.

  16. If 7x32y is a five-digit number divisible by 72, what is the greatest possible value of x + y?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '6'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '14'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '15'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '18'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) {'label': 'C', 'text': '15'}

    Explanation: Divisibility by 8 gives y = 0 or 8. The digit sum 12 + x + y must be divisible by 9: the pairs (x, y) are (6, 0) and (7, 8), so the greatest sum is 15.

  17. What is the least non-negative integer that must be subtracted from 950 to obtain a perfect square?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '11'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '50'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '49'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '89'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) {'label': 'B', 'text': '50'}

    Explanation: 30² = 900 and 31² = 961. The nearest perfect square below 950 is 900, so subtract 950 − 900 = 50.

  18. How many trailing zeros are there in 125!?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '31'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '25'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '30'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '32'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) {'label': 'A', 'text': '31'}

    Explanation: Count factors of 5: 125 ÷ 5 = 25, 125 ÷ 25 = 5 and 125 ÷ 125 = 1. Total trailing zeros = 25 + 5 + 1 = 31.

  19. Evaluate 1003² − 997².

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '36'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '6000'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '12000'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '24000'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) {'label': 'C', 'text': '12000'}

    Explanation: Use a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b): 6 × 2000 = 12000.

  20. A number divided by a positive integer gives quotient 18 and remainder 7. When the divisor is increased by 5, the quotient becomes 15 and the remainder stays 7. Find the number.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '450'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '547'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '382'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '457'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) {'label': 'D', 'text': '457'}

    Explanation: Let the original divisor be d. Then 18d + 7 = 15(d + 5) + 7, so 3d = 75 and d = 25; the number is 18 × 25 + 7 = 457.

  21. How many positive factors does 81 have?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '5'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '4'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '6'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '9'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) {'label': 'A', 'text': '5'}

    Explanation: 81 = 3^4, so the number of positive factors is 4 + 1 = 5.

  22. A positive integer N leaves remainder 4 when divided by 9. What remainder does N² + 3N + 2 leave when divided by 9?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '0'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '4'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '3'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '6'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) {'label': 'C', 'text': '3'}

    Explanation: Replace N by its remainder 4: 4² + 3 × 4 + 2 = 30. Dividing 30 by 9 leaves remainder 3.

  23. What is the greatest five-digit number exactly divisible by 88?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '99912'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '99968'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '99944'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '99880'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) {'label': 'B', 'text': '99968'}

    Explanation: 99,999 = 88 × 1136 + 31. Subtract the remainder: 99,999 − 31 = 99,968.

  24. Among four-digit numbers of the form 7x2y divisible by 5, 9 and 11, find the difference between the greatest and the smallest. Here x and y are digits.

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '450'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '990'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '545'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '495'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) {'label': 'D', 'text': '495'}

    Explanation: Divisibility by 11 forces x + y = 9, which also makes the digit sum 18. Divisibility by 5 gives y = 0 or 5, producing 7920 and 7425; their difference is 495.

  25. Harjit writes three consecutive odd positive integers whose sum is 195. What is the largest of them?

    1. {'label': 'A', 'text': '67'}
    2. {'label': 'B', 'text': '65'}
    3. {'label': 'C', 'text': '66'}
    4. {'label': 'D', 'text': '69'}
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) {'label': 'A', 'text': '67'}

    Explanation: The middle integer is the average, 195 ÷ 3 = 65. The largest is 65 + 2 = 67.
