Question bank

Ranking and Time Sequence Test MCQ – Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A queue has 39 people. A volunteer marks the 15th person from each end. What is the sum of the two marked persons’ ranks from the front?

    1. 39
    2. 41
    3. 38
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 40

    Explanation: The front ranks are 15 and 39 - 15 + 1 = 25; their sum is 40.

  2. Nimrat and Om exchange positions in a fixed row. Nimrat’s left rank changes from 13 to 27. By how much does Om’s right rank increase?

    1. 14
    2. 12
    3. 13
    4. 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 14

    Explanation: Om moves from left 27 to left 13, so his right rank increases by 27 - 13 = 14.

  3. A is 14th from the left and B is 16th from the right. There are 2 people between them. How many different total strengths are possible?

    1. 4
    2. 2
    3. 1
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2

    Explanation: B can occupy left rank 11 or 17, giving valid totals 26 and 32. Hence 2 totals are possible.

  4. In a row of 48, Harjit occupies the left central seat. Lavish is 9th from the right. How many people sit strictly between them?

    1. 14
    2. 17
    3. 15
    4. 16
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 15

    Explanation: Harjit is left 24 and Lavish is left 40. Between = 40 - 24 - 1 = 15.

  5. K weighs more than both L and M. N weighs less than M. Which comparison is definitely established?

    1. K is heavier than N
    2. L is heavier than M
    3. L is lighter than N
    4. N is heavier than L
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) K is heavier than N

    Explanation: K > M and M > N give K > N. L’s relation to M and N is not fixed.

  6. Of 60 candidates, Hina is 19th. Only the top 35 qualify. What is Hina’s rank from the bottom among qualifiers?

    1. 17
    2. 18
    3. 19
    4. 16
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 17

    Explanation: Use the selected total, 35: bottom rank = 35 - 19 + 1 = 17.

  7. Rows A and B contain 16 and 19 people respectively. Both are counted from the same left side. They merge by taking A1, B1, A2, B2 and so on; after A ends, remaining B members follow. What is the final front rank of the person originally 3rd from the right in B?

    1. 35
    2. 32
    3. 34
    4. 33
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 33

    Explanation: The person is B17. The first 16 pairs occupy 32 places, so B17 occupies place 33.

  8. A film begins at 19:42. Its first half lasts 68 minutes, the interval lasts 17 minutes, and the second half lasts 74 minutes. When does it end?

    1. 22:31
    2. 22:21
    3. 22:41
    4. 22:11
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 22:21

    Explanation: Total duration = 68 + 17 + 74 = 159 minutes, or 2 hours 39 minutes. Add this to 19:42 to get 22:21.

  9. An exam is 17 days after a Monday orientation. On which weekday is the exam?

    1. Saturday
    2. Friday
    3. Thursday
    4. Wednesday
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Thursday

    Explanation: 17 mod 7 = 3; Monday + 3 days = Thursday.

  10. Aman and Bala reach the same appointment 32 minutes apart. Aman is 13 minutes early and Bala is late. How late is Bala?

    1. 45
    2. 19
    3. 13
    4. 21
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 19

    Explanation: Arrival gap = early amount + late amount. Bala’s delay = 32 - 13 = 19 minutes.

  11. Four cards P, Q, R, S occupy places 4, 11, 20, 29 from the left. Swap P-Q, then R-S, then Q-S, then P-R. Which card finishes at place 4?

    1. Q
    2. R
    3. P
    4. S
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) S

    Explanation: After P-Q, place 4 holds Q. R-S does not affect it; Q-S puts S there, and P-R leaves it unchanged.

  12. A mixed queue has 17 boys. A girl is 15th from the front, with 6 girls ahead of her. How many boys stand behind her?

    1. 10
    2. 8
    3. 9
    4. 11
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9

    Explanation: Boys ahead = 14 - 6 = 8. Boys behind = 17 - 8 = 9.

  13. A digital clock is fixed at 7 minutes fast. At actual 23:56 on Monday, what does it display?

    1. Tuesday 00:03
    2. Monday 00:03
    3. Tuesday 00:07
    4. Monday 23:49
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Tuesday 00:03

    Explanation: Adding 7 minutes crosses midnight: Monday 23:56 becomes Tuesday 00:03.

  14. A book’s rank from the left is three times its rank from the right. There are 47 books on the shelf. What is its left rank?

    1. 12
    2. 35
    3. 24
    4. 36
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 36

    Explanation: L + R = 48 and L = 3R. Hence R = 12 and L = 36.

  15. A clock gains 6 minutes during every 2 hours shown by that clock. It is correct at actual 08:00. When it shows 12:00, what is the actual time?

    1. 11:48
    2. 12:24
    3. 12:12
    4. 11:36
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 11:48

    Explanation: Each 120 displayed minutes represents 114 true minutes. For 240 displayed minutes, true elapsed = 228 minutes, so actual time is 11:48.

  16. 5 students have distinct marks. J scored above K and L; M scored below K but above L; N scored below L. Which statement must be false?

    1. M is third
    2. J scored highest
    3. N scored above K
    4. L is fourth
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) N scored above K

    Explanation: The forced order is J > K > M > L > N. N cannot be above K.

  17. A security shift runs from 23:25 to 01:10 the next day. How many minutes does it last?

    1. 115
    2. 125
    3. 105
    4. 95
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 105

    Explanation: 35 minutes to midnight plus 70 minutes after midnight gives 105 minutes.

  18. In a 57-person queue, 10 people precede A and 16 follow B. C is one-third of the positional distance from A towards B. How many people stand between A and C?

    1. 11
    2. 9
    3. 10
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 9

    Explanation: A is 11th and B is 41st, a separation of 30 positions. C is at 11 + 30/3 = 21, leaving 21 - 11 - 1 = 9 between A and C.

  19. X is 23rd from the left, Y is 8th from the right, and exactly 9 people separate them. Which is the complete set of possible totals?

    1. 20 only
    2. 40 only
    3. 21 and 39
    4. 20 and 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 40 only

    Explanation: Y’s left rank could algebraically be 13 or 33, giving totals 20 or 40. Total 20 is invalid because X itself is 23rd; only 40 works.

  20. A and B swap places in a row of 37. Afterwards A is 12th from the left and B is 9th from the right. What was A’s original left rank?

    1. 27
    2. 28
    3. 29
    4. 25
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 29

    Explanation: A’s original place is B’s final place: 37 - 9 + 1 = 29th from the left.

  21. In a queue of 40, Veer moves from 15th to 21st from the front. Which direction and how many places did he move?

    1. 7 forward
    2. 5 backward
    3. 6 forward
    4. 6 backward
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 6 backward

    Explanation: A larger front rank means moving backward: 21 - 15 = 6 places.

  22. A bus is scheduled for 15:20. It arrives 8 minutes late and leaves 6 minutes after arrival. P reaches the stop 5 minutes before the scheduled time. How long does P wait before departure?

    1. 19
    2. 14
    3. 21
    4. 13
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 19

    Explanation: P arrives at 15:15 and the bus leaves at 15:34. Waiting time = 19 minutes.

  23. A list of 90 candidates has no ties. Every candidate at an original rank divisible by 3 withdraws. S was originally 47th. What is S’s new rank from the bottom?

    1. 28
    2. 29
    3. 30
    4. 31
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 29

    Explanation: 30 withdraw, leaving 60. Above S, 15 of the first 46 withdraw, so S becomes 32nd; bottom rank = 60 - 32 + 1 = 29.

  24. A shuttle leaves every 18 minutes from 07:10 onward. A passenger arrives at 08:03. How many minutes must she wait for the next departure?

    1. 7
    2. 11
    3. 3
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1

    Explanation: Departures are 07:10, 07:28, 07:46 and 08:04. The next one is 1 minute after 08:03.

  25. Two equal rows are aligned from a fixed left side. In Row I, A is left 7 and right 18. B sits opposite A in Row II; C sits 5 seats to B’s fixed right. The rows merge as I1, II1, I2, II2 and so on. What is C’s rank from the back?

    1. 23
    2. 26
    3. 25
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 25

    Explanation: Each row has 7 + 18 - 1 = 24 people. C is II12, hence merged front rank 24; back rank = 48 - 24 + 1 = 25.
