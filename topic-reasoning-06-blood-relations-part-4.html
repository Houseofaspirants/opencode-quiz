Question bank

Blood Relations MCQ – Part 4: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A woman U is the sister of V. V is W’s parent, but V’s gender is not given. Which detail is still needed to name U specifically as W’s paternal aunt or maternal aunt?

    1. Whether W has a brother
    2. Whether V is W’s father or mother
    3. Whether W is male or female
    4. Whether U is older than W
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Whether V is W’s father or mother

    Explanation: The paternal or maternal side depends on V’s role, not W’s gender or age.

  2. At a Patiala ceremony, a girl, Avni, introduces a man: “He is my grandmother’s son-in-law and my mother’s husband.” Which relation is explicitly established?

    1. Her mother’s husband
    2. Her mother’s brother
    3. Her own husband
    4. Her paternal grandfather
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Her mother’s husband

    Explanation: The second statement directly identifies him as Avni’s mother’s husband.

  3. Code: X + Y means X is Y’s daughter; X - Y means X is Y’s father; X @ Y means X is Y’s wife. Which insertion makes A the paternal grandmother of D in A __ B - C - D?

    1. No available symbol
    2. +
    3. -
    4. @
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) @

    Explanation: B is C’s father and C is D’s father; A must be B’s wife, so insert @.

  4. Jasbir’s father’s elder brother is married to Kulwant. Which Punjabi relationship correctly identifies Kulwant, a woman, for Jasbir?

    1. Father’s sister
    2. Father’s elder brother’s wife
    3. Father’s younger brother’s wife
    4. Mother’s brother’s wife
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Father’s elder brother’s wife

    Explanation: The uncle is older than Jasbir’s father, so his wife is the elder paternal aunt by marriage.

  5. Seven members A–G include two couples and three generations. A is B’s husband. Their only children are son C and daughter D. F is A’s granddaughter and C’s daughter. E is F’s mother. G is D’s son. Which two members form the second couple? Each child’s named parents are married to each other.

    1. D and E
    2. C and D
    3. C and E
    4. F and G
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) C and E

    Explanation: F’s parents are C and E; under the stated married-parent rule they are the second couple.

  6. A woman, Devika, is married to Raman. Raman’s sister Neelam is married to Harsh. Which of these is Devika’s sister-in-law through her husband?

    1. Raman
    2. Devika
    3. Neelam
    4. Harsh
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Neelam

    Explanation: Her husband Raman’s sister Neelam is Devika’s sister-in-law.

  7. X % Y means X is Y’s mother; X $ Y means X is Y’s brother; X & Y means X is Y’s daughter. In A & B % C $ D, what is the minimum number of females established?

    1. 1
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2

    Explanation: A is female as daughter and B as mother; C is male and D is unspecified.

  8. A couple has two sons and one daughter. Both sons have one daughter each; the daughter has two sons. All children in the younger generation are distinct. How many grandchildren does the couple have?

    1. 5
    2. 3
    3. 7
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 4

    Explanation: Grandchildren are 1 + 1 + 2 = 4; the couple’s own children are not counted again.

  9. Read this verbal tree: Sohan (male) and Tara (female) are parents of daughters Uma and Vani. Uma’s son is Wasi; Vani’s daughter is Zara. The fathers of Wasi and Zara are not shown. What is Uma to Zara?

    1. Mother
    2. Paternal aunt
    3. Maternal aunt
    4. Maternal grandmother
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Maternal aunt

    Explanation: Zara’s mother is Vani, whose sister is Uma.

  10. For the question “Is X Y’s uncle through Y’s father?”, examine I. X is brother of Z. II. Z is Y’s parent. Which judgement is valid?

    1. Even together, they are insufficient
    2. II alone is sufficient
    3. Together they are sufficient
    4. I alone is sufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Even together, they are insufficient

    Explanation: Together they make X a parent’s brother, but Z may be Y’s mother or father.

  11. A woman, Rachna, points to a boy: “His maternal grandmother is my mother, and his mother is not me.” Which relationship joins Rachna to the boy?

    1. Paternal aunt
    2. Maternal aunt
    3. Paternal grandmother
    4. Mother
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Maternal aunt

    Explanation: The boy’s mother is another daughter of Rachna’s mother, hence Rachna’s sister.

  12. Use X / Y for X is Y’s father, X + Y for X is Y’s son, X * Y for X is Y’s sister. Which expression establishes A as B’s brother, even though A is not first?

    1. B * A + C
    2. C / B + A
    3. C * A / B
    4. B / A + C
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) B * A + C

    Explanation: B * A makes B A’s sister, and A + C proves A is male; therefore A is B’s brother.

  13. A man, Pranav, has a daughter Ria. Pranav’s mother’s sister is Sushma; Sushma’s son is Keshav. What is Sushma to Ria’s father?

    1. Mother-in-law
    2. Paternal aunt
    3. Maternal grandmother
    4. Maternal aunt
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Maternal aunt

    Explanation: Ria’s father is Pranav; Sushma is Pranav’s mother’s sister.

  14. Eight people form a family. P is husband of Q; R and S are their daughters. R’s husband T is father of son U. S’s husband V is father of daughter W. R and S are U’s and W’s mothers respectively. Which named man is related to U as his mother’s sister’s husband?

    1. P
    2. U
    3. T
    4. V
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) V

    Explanation: U’s mother R has sister S; S’s husband V is U’s maternal aunt’s husband.

  15. X > Y means X is Y’s father; X < Y means X is Y’s daughter. In A < B > C < D, all four are distinct. Which pair must be C’s parents?

    1. A and B
    2. A and D
    3. B and D
    4. B and C
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) B and D

    Explanation: B > C makes B C’s father, and C < D makes D C’s other parent.

  16. Ritu’s husband and Pawan’s wife are brother and sister. Pawan and Ritu are not siblings. Which description of Pawan from Ritu’s side is certain?

    1. Husband’s sister’s husband
    2. Father’s sister’s husband
    3. Wife’s brother
    4. Husband’s brother
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Husband’s sister’s husband

    Explanation: Pawan’s wife is Ritu’s husband’s sister; Pawan is that sister’s husband.

  17. M is N’s father. N is O’s brother. O is P’s parent. Which pair consists of two people whose genders remain unspecified?

    1. M and N
    2. M and O
    3. O and P
    4. N and P
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) O and P

    Explanation: Father fixes M as male and brother fixes N as male; neither O nor P receives a gender-specific role.

  18. A written tree has married couple A (male)–B (female), daughter C, son D, C’s husband E, and C–E’s sons F and G. Remove both marriage links but retain all parent–child links. Which path still connects E to A?

    1. E → F → C → A
    2. E → G → D → A
    3. E → B → A
    4. E → D → A
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) E → F → C → A

    Explanation: E and C remain connected through their son F, and C is A’s daughter.

  19. A family contains two sisters, each with one son. The sisters’ mother is also present. Count the mother–son pairs among these five people.

    1. 2
    2. 4
    3. 3
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2

    Explanation: Each sister forms one mother–son pair with her son; the eldest woman’s children here are daughters.

  20. X # Y means X is Y’s father; X $ Y means X is Y’s sister; X @ Y means X is Y’s mother. In A # B __ C @ D, which symbol guarantees that A is D’s maternal grandfather?

    1. No available symbol
    2. #
    3. @
    4. $
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) $

    Explanation: B $ C makes B and C siblings with father A; C is D’s mother.

  21. A woman, Simar, introduces a child: “I am the daughter of his father’s elder brother.” The child is a boy. Simar is his _____.

    1. Father’s sister
    2. Sister’s daughter
    3. Father’s elder brother’s daughter
    4. Mother’s sister’s daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Father’s elder brother’s daughter

    Explanation: Her father is the boy’s paternal uncle, older than the boy’s father.

  22. Given: A is B’s mother; B is C’s father; D is C’s sister. Conclusion I: A is D’s paternal grandmother. Conclusion II: D is A’s granddaughter through a son. Which conclusion follows?

    1. Only I
    2. Only II
    3. Neither I nor II
    4. Both I and II
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Both I and II

    Explanation: D shares father B with C; B’s mother A is D’s paternal grandmother, so the reverse relation is granddaughter.

  23. X + Y means X is Y’s father, X * Y means X is Y’s wife, and X - Y means X is Y’s daughter. For P - Q * R + S, what is P’s relationship to S? Q and R are the parents of both children.

    1. Paternal aunt
    2. Sister
    3. Mother
    4. Daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Sister

    Explanation: P is Q’s daughter and S is R’s child; the shared-parent condition makes P S’s sister.

  24. Jaya and Kiran are sisters. Jaya is married to Om and Kiran to Rakesh. Om’s sister is Lata. Which woman is Rakesh’s wife’s sister?

    1. Cannot be determined
    2. Kiran
    3. Lata
    4. Jaya
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Jaya

    Explanation: Rakesh’s wife is Kiran; her sister is Jaya, while Lata belongs to Om’s sibling branch.

  25. A man, Param, is brother of Nisha’s mother. Nisha’s daughter Ovi has a brother, Punit. What is Nisha’s relationship to Param?

    1. Brother’s daughter
    2. Sister’s daughter
    3. Sister
    4. Daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Sister’s daughter

    Explanation: Param’s sister is Nisha’s mother, so Nisha is Param’s niece through his sister; her children do not change that.
