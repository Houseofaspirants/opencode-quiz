Question bank

Problems on Numbers MCQ – Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. 7 times a number exceeds the number by 174. Find the number.

    1. 30
    2. 29
    3. 25
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 29

    Explanation: 6x = 174, so x = 29.

  2. The difference between 5/6 and 3/8 of a number is 77. Find 7/12 of the number.

    1. 98
    2. 112
    3. 84
    4. 168
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 98

    Explanation: (5/6 − 3/8)x = 11x/24 = 77, so x = 168 and 7x/12 = 98.

  3. A library in Patiala lends 1/4 of its books on Monday and 40% of the remainder on Tuesday. After receiving 18 returned books, it has 126 books. How many books did it have initially?

    1. 180
    2. 240
    3. 280
    4. 360
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 240

    Explanation: Books before returns = 126 − 18 = 108; initial stock = 108 ÷ (3/4 × 60/100) = 240.

  4. A number is divided by 3, increased by 14, and then multiplied by 5 to give 180. What is the original number?

    1. 22
    2. 94
    3. 66
    4. 108
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 66

    Explanation: Reverse the operations: (180 ÷ 5 − 14) × 3 = 66.

  5. The sum of two numbers is 148 and their difference is 26. Find the larger number.

    1. 61
    2. 96
    3. 74
    4. 87
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 87

    Explanation: Larger number = (148 + 26) ÷ 2 = 87.

  6. Two positive numbers are in the ratio 5 : 8. After adding 7 to the smaller and subtracting 7 from the larger, their ratio becomes 4 : 5. Find their original sum.

    1. 104
    2. 130
    3. 91
    4. 117
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 117

    Explanation: 5(5k + 7) = 4(8k − 7), so 7k = 63 and the sum = 13 × 9 = 117.

  7. The pairwise sums of three numbers are 47, 59 and 68. Find the middle number when the three numbers are arranged in increasing order.

    1. 31
    2. 40
    3. 28
    4. 19
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 28

    Explanation: Total = (47 + 59 + 68) ÷ 2 = 87; the numbers are 19, 28 and 40, so the middle number is 28.

  8. When the larger of two positive integers is divided by the smaller, the quotient is 4 and the remainder is 13. Their sum is 208. Find their difference.

    1. 130
    2. 156
    3. 143
    4. 117
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 130

    Explanation: If the smaller is s, 5s + 13 = 208 gives s = 39; difference = 3 × 39 + 13 = 130.

  9. The sum of five consecutive odd integers is 345. Find the smallest integer.

    1. 65
    2. 67
    3. 69
    4. 63
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 65

    Explanation: Middle integer = 345 ÷ 5 = 69; smallest = 69 − 4 = 65.

  10. The product of two consecutive positive integers is 5256. Find the larger integer.

    1. 71
    2. 72
    3. 73
    4. 74
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 73

    Explanation: 5256 = 72 × 73, so the larger integer is 73.

  11. For positive numbers a and b, a + b = 34 and a² + b² = 610. Find |a − b|.

    1. 16
    2. 4
    3. 24
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 8

    Explanation: (a − b)² = 2(a² + b²) − (a + b)² = 1220 − 1156 = 64; hence |a − b| = 8.

  12. A positive number greater than 1 and its reciprocal have a sum of 17/4. Find the number.

    1. 8
    2. 4
    3. 1/4
    4. 17/4
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 4

    Explanation: x + 1/x = 17/4 gives (4x − 1)(x − 4) = 0; since x > 1, x = 4.

  13. A two-digit number has digit sum 7 and digit product 12. Its tens digit is smaller than its units digit. Find the number.

    1. 25
    2. 34
    3. 43
    4. 52
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 34

    Explanation: The digits with sum 7 and product 12 are 3 and 4; the smaller digit comes first, giving 34.

  14. A two-digit number exceeds its reverse by 27, and the product of its digits is 28. Find the original number.

    1. 73
    2. 47
    3. 74
    4. 84
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 74

    Explanation: Digit difference = 27 ÷ 9 = 3; digits with product 28 and difference 3 are 7 and 4, giving 74.

  15. A three-digit number has digit sum 15. Its hundreds digit is twice its units digit, and its tens digit is 3 more than its units digit. Find the number.

    1. 663
    2. 363
    3. 633
    4. 636
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 663

    Explanation: Let the units digit be u: 2u + (u + 3) + u = 15 gives u = 3; the number is 663.

  16. A fraction becomes 3/4 when 2 is added to its numerator and 1 to its denominator. It becomes 1/2 when 2 is subtracted from its numerator and 1 from its denominator. Find the original fraction.

    1. 3/5
    2. 9/13
    3. 7/11
    4. 5/9
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 7/11

    Explanation: 4n − 3d = −5 and 2n − d = 3 give n = 7 and d = 11; the fraction is 7/11.

  17. Harpreet multiplies a number by 7 instead of dividing it by 7. His answer exceeds the correct answer by 288. Find the number.

    1. 48
    2. 36
    3. 49
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 42

    Explanation: Error = (7 − 1/7)x = 48x/7 = 288, so x = 42.

  18. A number is equally distant from 43 and 119. Find the number.

    1. 76
    2. 81
    3. 83
    4. 79
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 81

    Explanation: The number is the midpoint: (43 + 119) ÷ 2 = 81.

  19. 3/7 of a number is 32 less than 5/9 of the same number. Find the number.

    1. 252
    2. 126
    3. 504
    4. 189
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 252

    Explanation: (5/9 − 3/7)x = 8x/63 = 32, so x = 32 × 63/8 = 252.

  20. After 20% of the fuel in a tank is used, 60 litres are added. Then 25% of the fuel now in the tank is drawn out, leaving 195 litres. Find the initial quantity in litres.

    1. 300
    2. 200
    3. 325
    4. 250
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 250

    Explanation: Before the last withdrawal, fuel = 195 ÷ 0.75 = 260 litres; initial fuel = (260 − 60) ÷ 0.80 = 250 litres.

  21. The sum of two numbers is 126. The larger exceeds twice the smaller by 12. Find their difference.

    1. 62
    2. 44
    3. 38
    4. 50
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 50

    Explanation: 3s + 12 = 126 gives s = 38; larger = 88, so difference = 50.

  22. If 3/5 of one number equals 4/7 of another, what is the ratio of the first number to the second?

    1. 12 : 35
    2. 20 : 21
    3. 21 : 20
    4. 35 : 12
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 20 : 21

    Explanation: 3a/5 = 4b/7 gives 21a = 20b; hence a : b = 20 : 21.

  23. The average of eight consecutive integers is 46.5. Find the largest integer.

    1. 50
    2. 51
    3. 54
    4. 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 50

    Explanation: Largest integer = 46.5 + 7/2 = 50.

  24. Two consecutive positive even integers have product 1848. Find their sum.

    1. 86
    2. 84
    3. 88
    4. 90
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 86

    Explanation: 1848 = 42 × 44; sum = 42 + 44 = 86.

  25. A two-digit number is 7 times its digit sum. If its units digit is 4, find the number.

    1. 48
    2. 74
    3. 84
    4. 94
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 84

    Explanation: 10t + 4 = 7(t + 4), so 3t = 24 and the number is 84.
