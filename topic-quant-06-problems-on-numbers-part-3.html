Question bank

Problems on Numbers MCQ – Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A number exceeds 28 by the same amount that twice the number falls short of 110. Find the number.

    1. 69
    2. 55
    3. 46
    4. 41
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 46

    Explanation: x − 28 = 110 − 2x, so 3x = 138 and x = 46.

  2. Rajan reads 3/8 of a book on the first day and 1/3 of the whole book on the second day. If 70 pages remain, how many pages did he read on the first day?

    1. 100
    2. 120
    3. 80
    4. 90
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 90

    Explanation: Remaining fraction = 1 − 3/8 − 1/3 = 7/24; total pages = 240, so first-day pages = 90.

  3. The squares of a two-digit number and its reverse differ by 4752. Their digit sum is 12, and the original number is larger. Find the original number.

    1. 48
    2. 75
    3. 84
    4. 93
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 84

    Explanation: Number + reverse = 11 × 12 = 132; difference = 4752 ÷ 132 = 36, so original = (132 + 36) ÷ 2 = 84.

  4. After 9 is subtracted from a number, 3/5 of the result is taken and then 8 is added. The final result is 41. Find the number.

    1. 55
    2. 64
    3. 46
    4. 73
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 64

    Explanation: Reverse the steps: (41 − 8) × 5/3 + 9 = 64.

  5. The average of seven consecutive even integers is 64. What is the fourth integer?

    1. 66
    2. 60
    3. 64
    4. 62
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 64

    Explanation: The fourth of seven equally spaced integers is the middle term, so it equals the average, 64.

  6. Three positive numbers are in the ratio 1 : 3 : 4. The sum of their squares exceeds their pairwise-product sum by 343. Find the largest number.

    1. 28
    2. 35
    3. 49
    4. 21
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 28

    Explanation: For k, 3k, 4k, the difference = (26 − 19)k² = 343; k = 7, so largest = 28.

  7. The sum of three numbers is 138. The first plus twice the second is 112, and the third exceeds the first by 22. Find the first number.

    1. 36
    2. 44
    3. 62
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 40

    Explanation: Third − second = 26, so first − second = 4; then 3 × second + 4 = 112 gives second = 36 and first = 40.

  8. Positive integers a and b satisfy a − b = 4 and ab = 77. Find a³ − b³.

    1. 1052
    2. 988
    3. 924
    4. 324
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 988

    Explanation: a³ − b³ = (a − b)[(a − b)² + 3ab] = 4(16 + 231) = 988.

  9. The sum and difference of two positive numbers are 96 and 32. What fraction of the larger is the smaller?

    1. 1/2
    2. 1/3
    3. 2/3
    4. 3/4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1/2

    Explanation: Smaller/larger = (96 − 32)/(96 + 32) = 64/128 = 1/2.

  10. The squares of three consecutive positive even integers total 1736. Find their average.

    1. 22
    2. 24
    3. 28
    4. 26
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 24

    Explanation: For middle term m, 3m² + 8 = 1736 gives m² = 576; average = m = 24.

  11. Adding 4 to a fraction's numerator and 3 to its denominator makes it 3/4. Subtracting 3 from the numerator and 5 from the denominator makes it 2/3. Find the original denominator minus numerator.

    1. 8
    2. 4
    3. 6
    4. 11
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 6

    Explanation: 4n − 3d = −7 and 3n − 2d = −1 give n = 11, d = 17; d − n = 6.

  12. The sum of two nonzero numbers is 27 and the sum of their reciprocals is 3/20. Find their product.

    1. 240
    2. 180
    3. 160
    4. 120
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 180

    Explanation: 1/a + 1/b = (a + b)/ab, so product = 27 ÷ (3/20) = 180.

  13. A two-digit number has tens digit 6 and is divisible by 17. Find its units digit.

    1. 4
    2. 6
    3. 8
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 8

    Explanation: The multiple of 17 between 60 and 69 is 17 × 4 = 68, so the units digit is 8.

  14. The larger of two positive integers is 5 times the smaller plus 7. Their product is 1078. Find their sum.

    1. 91
    2. 77
    3. 98
    4. 84
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 91

    Explanation: s(5s + 7) = 1078 is satisfied by s = 14 and larger = 77; sum = 91.

  15. A number is increased by its half. After subtracting 18, the result is divided by 9 to give 7. What is 2/3 of the original number?

    1. 36
    2. 54
    3. 42
    4. 27
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 36

    Explanation: Original = (7 × 9 + 18) ÷ (3/2) = 54; its 2/3 = 36.

  16. A clerk should multiply a number by 3/4 and add 21. Instead, he multiplies it by 4/3 and subtracts 21. Both methods give the same result. Find the number.

    1. 84
    2. 56
    3. 36
    4. 72
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 72

    Explanation: (4/3 − 3/4)x = 42 gives 7x/12 = 42; x = 72.

  17. A positive integer n is sought. Statement I: n² = 169. Statement II: n is an odd factor of 39 greater than 3. Which assessment is correct?

    1. Statement II alone is sufficient
    2. Both statements are required
    3. Either statement alone is sufficient
    4. Statement I alone is sufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Statement I alone is sufficient

    Explanation: Statement I gives n = 13 uniquely; Statement II allows 13 and 39.

  18. The difference between 9 times and 4 times a number is 235. What is 3 times the number?

    1. 188
    2. 94
    3. 141
    4. 47
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 141

    Explanation: 5x = 235 gives x = 47; hence 3x = 141.

  19. 3/4 of a number equals 7/8 of 288. Find the sum of the digits of the number.

    1. 18
    2. 12
    3. 15
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 12

    Explanation: 3x/4 = 7/8 × 288 = 252 gives x = 336; digit sum = 3 + 3 + 6 = 12.

  20. Four positive numbers total 180. Adding 5 to the first, subtracting 5 from the second, doubling the third and halving the fourth all give the same result. Find the second number.

    1. 20
    2. 35
    3. 80
    4. 45
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 45

    Explanation: With common result k, the sum = (k − 5) + (k + 5) + k/2 + 2k = 9k/2 = 180; k = 40 and the second number is 45.

  21. Two positive numbers are in the ratio 7 : 11. Subtracting the smaller from 3 times the larger gives 208. Find the smaller number.

    1. 56
    2. 88
    3. 77
    4. 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 56

    Explanation: (33 − 7)k = 208 gives k = 8; smaller = 7 × 8 = 56.

  22. The sum of four consecutive integers is 230. What is the sum of the next four consecutive integers?

    1. 234
    2. 250
    3. 238
    4. 246
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 246

    Explanation: Each corresponding integer increases by 4, so the new sum = 230 + 4 × 4 = 246.

  23. After 35% of a number is removed, 156 remains. Find the number.

    1. 240
    2. 260
    3. 210
    4. 280
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 240

    Explanation: 65% of x = 156, so x = 156 × 100/65 = 240.

  24. If x + y = 26 and x − y = 10, find (x² + y²)/2.

    1. 388
    2. 144
    3. 194
    4. 288
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 194

    Explanation: (x² + y²)/2 = [(x + y)² + (x − y)²]/4 = (676 + 100)/4 = 194.

  25. A two-digit number has digit sum 10. Adding 36 reverses its digits. Find the product of the original number and its tens digit.

    1. 259
    2. 111
    3. 37
    4. 74
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 111

    Explanation: Units − tens = 36 ÷ 9 = 4; with digit sum 10, tens = 3 and number = 37; product = 111.
