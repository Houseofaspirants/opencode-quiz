About this cluster

What Static GK covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Static GK is the backlog of facts that stay true year after year - symbols, capitals, currencies, notable firsts, institutions and records - and it is the lane where preparation returns the most marks per hour spent. Almost every paper includes a slice of it, the material does not expire, and the questions rarely allow partial credit, so full coverage matters more than deep understanding. Because the pool is finite and stable, the most efficient method is a single well-organised compilation that you test yourself on repeatedly until recall is instant. The lanes below group the material by type rather than by source, so each list can be cleared in one sitting. Treat anything that could change as something to confirm against an official source.

What does Static GK cover?

Static GK covers National Symbols, Capitals and Currencies, Firsts in India, Important Institutions and the rest. The lane sits under General Knowledge, inside Others. Every area listed above links back to the page that hosts it.

How should I study Static GK?

Revise one area at a time and test it before moving on - the topics above are ordered so each rests on the one before it. Once they are familiar, switch to timed practice: sets run at 30 seconds a question, 60 for Reasoning and Quantitative Aptitude and clear at 40% accuracy.
