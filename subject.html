What exactly is a topic?

A topic is one sub-section of the syllabus — rivers of India, or number series — with its own folder of MCQs. You can filter the list above if you already know what you want.

Which topic should I start with?

Start with the topic you are least confident about, not the easiest one. Attempt a short set untimed, read every explanation, then come back to the same topic after two days.

Do I need an account to practise?

No. Open any topic and start answering immediately. Google sign-in is optional and only saves your scores, bookmarks and streaks so they follow you across devices.

Where do I see how I am improving?

Each attempt is recorded, and the progress page turns it into subject-wise accuracy, streaks and badges. Bookmark anything you get wrong so your weakest facts stay in front of you.
