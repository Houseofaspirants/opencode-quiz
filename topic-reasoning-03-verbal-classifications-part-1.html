Question bank

Verbal Classifications MCQ – Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A sports shop groups equipment by whether the game uses a racket. Which game must go on a different shelf?

    1. Billiards
    2. Table tennis
    3. Badminton
    4. Squash
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Billiards

    Explanation: Billiards uses a cue; the other three use rackets or bats of the racket-sport family.

  2. Compare consecutive alphabet-position gaps. Which group breaks the equal-gap rule within the group?

    1. EHL
    2. CFI
    3. JMP
    4. ORU
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) EHL

    Explanation: EHL has gaps 3 and 4; each other group has gaps 3 and 3.

  3. Harleen reverses the digits of each number, then adds 1. Three results are perfect squares. Which original number fails this two-step test?

    1. 882
    2. 422
    3. 871
    4. 861
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 871

    Explanation: 861 reverses to 168, then 168 + 1 = 169 = 13²; similarly 422 gives 225 = 15² and 882 gives 289 = 17². But 871 gives 178 + 1 = 179, not a square.

  4. Three devices measure a quantity; one primarily regulates it. Identify that device.

    1. Thermostat
    2. Hygrometer
    3. Speedometer
    4. Anemometer
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Thermostat

    Explanation: A thermostat regulates temperature. The others measure humidity, wind speed and speed respectively.

  5. A common rule combines the first number, its successor and its digit sum to produce the second number. Which pair is inconsistent?

    1. 11 : 134
    2. 17 : 314
    3. 8 : 80
    4. 14 : 219
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 14 : 219

    Explanation: Use n(n + 1) + digit sum of n. For 14, 14 × 15 + 1 + 4 = 215, not 219; the other results are 80, 134 and 314.

  6. Which item cannot join a list restricted to percussion instruments?

    1. Mridangam
    2. Tambourine
    3. Dholak
    4. Shehnai
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Shehnai

    Explanation: A shehnai produces sound using blown air. The other three are percussion instruments, sounded by striking or shaking.

  7. With A = 1 to Z = 26, three groups have the sum of their outer positions equal to the product of their inner positions. Which group fails?

    1. HDFP
    2. FCFL
    3. JDEM
    4. CDFU
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) JDEM

    Explanation: JDEM gives 10 + 13 = 23 but 4 × 5 = 20. The other comparisons are 3 + 21 = 4 × 6, 6 + 12 = 3 × 6 and 8 + 16 = 4 × 6.

  8. Read each pair from left to right as raw material to product. Which pair has the reverse relationship?

    1. Latex : Rubber
    2. Pulp : Paper
    3. Fabric : Cotton
    4. Clay : Pot
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Fabric : Cotton

    Explanation: Cotton is the raw material for fabric, so Fabric : Cotton reverses the direction. The other pairs run from material to product.

  9. A register admits only multiples of 12. Which of these entries was admitted incorrectly?

    1. 732
    2. 1068
    3. 1248
    4. 918
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 918

    Explanation: A multiple of 12 must be divisible by both 3 and 4. Although 918 has digit sum 18, its last two digits, 18, are not divisible by 4.

  10. A biology display separates endocrine glands from other glands. Which label belongs outside the endocrine group?

    1. Adrenal gland
    2. Salivary gland
    3. Pituitary gland
    4. Thyroid gland
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Salivary gland

    Explanation: Salivary glands release saliva through ducts. The other three are endocrine glands that release hormones into blood.

  11. One group contains a different number of English vowels. Pick it.

    1. RIOT
    2. BEAK
    3. MOUS
    4. CLAP
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) CLAP

    Explanation: CLAP has one vowel, A; each other group has two vowels.

  12. Classify each row by its devices’ principal useful output energy. In which row do the devices NOT all share one output category?

    1. LED lamp, fluorescent tube, incandescent bulb
    2. Electric fan, mixer, electric generator
    3. Loudspeaker, electric bell, headphones
    4. Toaster, electric iron, electric kettle
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Electric fan, mixer, electric generator

    Explanation: The fan and mixer give mechanical motion, but the generator gives electrical energy. Each other row has a common principal output: heat, light or sound.

  13. Jaspreet checks whether subtracting the first number from the second gives 27. Which pair fails?

    1. 19 : 46
    2. 46 : 74
    3. 68 : 95
    4. 35 : 62
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 46 : 74

    Explanation: 74 − 46 = 28. The differences in the other three pairs are all 27.

  14. Only one of these numbers is composite. Identify it without testing divisors larger than 11.

    1. 121
    2. 107
    3. 109
    4. 103
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 121

    Explanation: 121 = 11 × 11 is composite. For the others, no prime divisor up to their square root divides exactly.

  15. When each group is reversed, three remain unchanged. Which one changes?

    1. RABAR
    2. KLMKL
    3. NCPCN
    4. TUVUT
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) KLMKL

    Explanation: RABAR, TUVUT and NCPCN read identically in reverse. KLMKL reverses to LKMLK.

  16. At a Ludhiana science fair, Rohit groups four substances as non-metallic elements. Which inclusion is incorrect?

    1. Phosphorus
    2. Iodine
    3. Bromine
    4. Gallium
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Gallium

    Explanation: Gallium is a metal. Bromine, iodine and phosphorus are non-metals.

  17. Three pairs show a creator and the work that person composes. Which pair instead names someone who revises another person's work?

    1. Composer : Symphony
    2. Poet : Sonnet
    3. Editor : Manuscript
    4. Playwright : Play
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Editor : Manuscript

    Explanation: An editor revises a manuscript rather than necessarily composing it. A composer, poet and playwright create the respective works.

  18. For an emergency kit, Aman needs devices that store electrical energy chemically. Which item should be separated?

    1. Solar cell
    2. Lithium-ion battery
    3. Nickel-metal hydride battery
    4. Lead-acid battery
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Solar cell

    Explanation: A solar cell converts light into electricity rather than storing energy chemically. The other three are chemical storage batteries.

  19. For each four-letter group, subtract the second position from the first and the fourth from the third. Which group gives unequal results?

    1. SOVR
    2. JFRO
    3. UQYU
    4. MIPL
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) JFRO

    Explanation: JF gives 10 − 6 = 4, but RO gives 18 − 15 = 3. The two differences match within every other group.

  20. Inspect whether the middle digit equals the sum of the two outer digits. Select the number that breaks this condition.

    1. 473
    2. 584
    3. 594
    4. 682
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 584

    Explanation: For 584, 5 + 4 = 9, which is not the middle digit 8. In each other number, the outer digits add to the middle digit.

  21. Kavya is arranging a Jaipur library by fiction and non-fiction. Which category belongs apart from the other three?

    1. Novel
    2. Fairy tale
    3. Fable
    4. Biography
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Biography

    Explanation: A biography presents a real person's life. The other three categories are forms of imaginative narrative.

  22. A cube-based calculation links the first number to the second in three pairs. Find the pair that does not follow their common rule.

    1. 8 : 518
    2. 6 : 222
    3. 4 : 68
    4. 9 : 738
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 8 : 518

    Explanation: Use n³ + n. For 8 the result is 512 + 8 = 520, not 518; all other pairs satisfy the rule.

  23. In a workshop, three operations remove material from a workpiece. Which operation joins material instead?

    1. Drilling
    2. Milling
    3. Welding
    4. Grinding
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Welding

    Explanation: Welding joins pieces. Drilling, milling and grinding remove material to form holes, surfaces or shapes.

  24. A six-letter code should repeat its first three-letter block after rotating that block one place to the left. Which code is faulty?

    1. BKRRBK
    2. DJMJMD
    3. HNVNVH
    4. FQTQTF
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) BKRRBK

    Explanation: Moving the first letter of BKR to the end gives KRB, so the full code should be BKRKRB. The other codes append the correctly rotated block.

  25. Count positive factors: which number has more than three when each of the others has exactly three?

    1. 361
    2. 169
    3. 225
    4. 289
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 225

    Explanation: A square of a prime has exactly three factors. 169 = 13², 289 = 17² and 361 = 19²; 225 = 3² × 5² has nine.
