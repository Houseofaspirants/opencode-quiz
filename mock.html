Quick answer

A mock test is a full-length, timed paper you build yourself — choose the scope, set the time limit, attempt it under exam conditions, then review every answer with its explanation.

How is a mock test different from an MCQ set?

An MCQ set is a short, topic-focused collection of questions. A mock test is a full-length, timed paper drawn from several subjects under exam conditions, which is the closest rehearsal to the real examination.

Can I set my own time limit?

Yes. You choose 15, 30, 60, 100 or 120 minutes and 25 to 200 questions, so the same builder works for a quick section drill or a full-length 100 or 120 minute paper.

What happens when the timer runs out?

Answers lock automatically at zero. Whatever you attempted is graded, and anything you left untouched is marked unattempted — exactly the behaviour of an online exam.

How do I review my mock test?

The result page shows your score, time taken, subject-wise breakdown and weak areas, then walks through every question with the correct answer and its explanation.
