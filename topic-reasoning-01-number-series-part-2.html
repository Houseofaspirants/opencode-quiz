Question bank

Number Series MCQ – Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Select the next entry: 10, 21, 41, 83, 165, ?.

    1. 330
    2. 331
    3. 329
    4. 332
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 331

    Explanation: Alternate ×2 + 1 and ×2 − 1. The next step is 165 × 2 + 1 = 331.

  2. Jasleen records 43, 46, 52, 62, 77, ? in a number puzzle. What should she record next?

    1. 102
    2. 99
    3. 97
    4. 98
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 98

    Explanation: The gaps are 3, 6, 10, 15, followed by 21; their increases are 3, 4, 5, 6. Hence 77 + 21 = 98.

  3. Which number continues 5, −15, 45, −135, ? correctly?

    1. -405
    2. -270
    3. 405
    4. 270
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 405

    Explanation: Each term is multiplied by −3. Thus −135 × (−3) = 405.

  4. The blank in 20, 200, 23, 196, 28, 187, 35, 171, ?, 146 belongs to which value?

    1. 48
    2. 46
    3. 44
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 46

    Explanation: Odd-position gaps are consecutive primes 3, 5, 7, 11, giving 35 + 11 = 46. Even positions subtract 2², 3², 4², 5².

  5. For 30, 68, 130, 222, 350, calculate the term two places after 350.

    1. 730
    2. 738
    3. 520
    4. 720
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 738

    Explanation: The rule is n³ + n for n = 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. Two places after base 7 is base 9, giving 9³ + 9 = 738.

  6. After the first three terms, each term equals the sum of the previous three: 3, 6, 8, 17, 31, ? . Complete the sequence.

    1. 54
    2. 48
    3. 62
    4. 56
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 56

    Explanation: Use the latest three terms: 8 + 17 + 31 = 56.

  7. A single replacement repairs 90, 91, 83, 110, 47, 171. Which printed term must be replaced?

    1. 110
    2. 171
    3. 47
    4. 83
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 47

    Explanation: The changes are +1³, −2³, +3³, −4³, +5³. The correct chain has 110 − 64 = 46, then 46 + 125 = 171, so 47 is wrong.

  8. Which of the following completes 3, 7, 18, 45, 106, ? when the additions are successive squares?

    1. 237
    2. 228
    3. 248
    4. 236
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 237

    Explanation: Double the preceding term and add 1², 2², 3², 4², then 5². Thus 106 × 2 + 25 = 237.

  9. A temperature pattern is 6.5, 4.75, 3, 1.25, ? . Determine the next reading.

    1. -1.25
    2. -0.5
    3. 0.5
    4. -0.25
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) -0.5

    Explanation: The decrease is 1.75 each time. Therefore 1.25 − 1.75 = −0.5.

  10. In the full prime list starting with 2, Balraj selects primes at positions 2, 4, 7, 11, 16, ... . Which selected prime comes next?

    1. 79
    2. 83
    3. 71
    4. 73
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 79

    Explanation: The position gaps are 2, 3, 4, 5, so the next position is 16 + 6 = 22. The 22nd prime is 79.

  11. Follow the changing ratios in 64, 96, 160, 280, 504, 924, ? . Determine the next term.

    1. 1584
    2. 1716
    3. 1848
    4. 1386
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1716

    Explanation: The ratios are 3/2, 5/3, 7/4, 9/5, 11/6: numerators rise by 2 and denominators by 1. Next use 13/7, giving 924 × 13 ÷ 7 = 1716.

  12. The numbers 8000, 5832, 4096, 2744, ? are cubes. Select the missing cube.

    1. 1728
    2. 1000
    3. 1331
    4. 2197
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1728

    Explanation: The cube bases are 20, 18, 16, 14, then 12. Hence 12³ = 1728.

  13. In 17, 104, 26, 97, 35, 90, 44, 83, ... , what is the sum of the next two terms?

    1. 122
    2. 136
    3. 127
    4. 129
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 129

    Explanation: Odd positions rise by 9, giving 53; even positions fall by 7, giving 76. Their sum is 53 + 76 = 129.

  14. Find the continuation after 164, 81, 38.5, 16.25, 4.125.

    1. 2.0625
    2. -2.0625
    3. -1.9375
    4. -2.9375
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) -2.9375

    Explanation: Halve and subtract 1, 2, 3, 4, then 5. The next term is 4.125 ÷ 2 − 5 = −2.9375.

  15. What number follows 70, 74, 81, 92, 110, 139 if each new difference is the sum of the previous two differences?

    1. 197
    2. 186
    3. 178
    4. 168
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 186

    Explanation: The gaps are 4, 7, 11, 18, 29. The next gap is 18 + 29 = 47, so 139 + 47 = 186.

  16. Inspect 48, 80, 120, 169, 224, 288. Which term fails the rule based on successive odd bases?

    1. 169
    2. 120
    3. 224
    4. 288
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 169

    Explanation: The terms should be 7² − 1, 9² − 1, 11² − 1, 13² − 1, 15² − 1, 17² − 1. Thus 169 should be 168.

  17. A number game shows 11, 22, 66, 132, 396, 792, ? . Supply the next output.

    1. 1584
    2. 1188
    3. 2382
    4. 2376
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 2376

    Explanation: The multipliers alternate 2 and 3. After ×2 gives 792, use ×3: 792 × 3 = 2376.

  18. Naman forms 2, 7, 16, 39, 94, ? using the same two-term recurrence. Determine the missing term.

    1. 188
    2. 211
    3. 227
    4. 230
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 227

    Explanation: Each new term is twice the latest term plus the one before it: 2 × 39 + 16 = 94. Next, 2 × 94 + 39 = 227.

  19. Complete both gaps in 15, 19, 23, 32, 41, ?, ? by choosing the ordered pair.

    1. (57, 73)
    2. (50, 66)
    3. (57, 82)
    4. (66, 91)
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) (57, 73)

    Explanation: Each square gap occurs twice: 2², 2², 3², 3², 4², 4². Thus the next terms are 41 + 16 = 57 and 57 + 16 = 73.

  20. Which entry belongs at position 8 in 3, 256, 9, 128, 27, 64, 81, ? when odd and even positions are treated separately?

    1. 48
    2. 16
    3. 32
    4. 243
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 32

    Explanation: Odd positions triple, while even positions halve: 256, 128, 64, 32. Position 8 therefore contains 32.

  21. Continue 5, 6, 16, 57, 244, ? by extending both the multiplier and the added square.

    1. 1230
    2. 1240
    3. 1245
    4. 1225
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1245

    Explanation: Apply ×1 + 1², ×2 + 2², ×3 + 3², ×4 + 4². Next is 244 × 5 + 5² = 1245.

  22. The list 35, 77, 143, 221, 323, ? uses products of neighbouring primes. Choose the next product.

    1. 493
    2. 391
    3. 419
    4. 437
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 437

    Explanation: The products are 5×7, 7×11, 11×13, 13×17, 17×19. Next is 19 × 23 = 437.

  23. A calculation chain is 15120, 2520, 504, 126, 42, ? . Find its final quotient.

    1. 7
    2. 14
    3. 21
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 21

    Explanation: Divide successively by 6, 5, 4, 3, then 2. Therefore 42 ÷ 2 = 21.

  24. Which term completes 85, 113, 145, 181, ? if each entry adds two consecutive squares?

    1. 221
    2. 202
    3. 219
    4. 225
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 221

    Explanation: The entries are 6² + 7², 7² + 8², 8² + 9², 9² + 10². Next is 10² + 11² = 221.

  25. In the sequence 12, 15, 20, 29, 46, 79, ?, find the next term by examining differences twice.

    1. 142
    2. 144
    3. 143
    4. 145
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 144

    Explanation: First differences are 3, 5, 9, 17, 33; second differences are 2, 4, 8, 16. The next first difference is 33 + 32 = 65, giving 79 + 65 = 144.
