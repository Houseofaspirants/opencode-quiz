Question bank

Simplification MCQ – Part 5: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Evaluate: 45 + 270 ÷ 15 × 2 − 64 ÷ 8.

    1. 89
    2. 65
    3. 46
    4. 73
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 73

    Explanation: Perform the divisions first: 45 + 18 × 2 − 8 = 73.

  2. Evaluate: 4³ ÷ 2^4 + 3².

    1. 25
    2. 12
    3. 13
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 13

    Explanation: Write 4³ as 2^6: 2^(6 − 4) + 9 = 4 + 9 = 13.

  3. Evaluate: 1/(2 − 1/(3 − 1/(2 + 1/2))).

    1. 13/21
    2. 8/13
    3. 21/13
    4. 5/8
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 13/21

    Explanation: From inside out, the values are 5/2, 13/5 and 21/13; the final reciprocal is 13/21.

  4. Evaluate: 900 ÷ [5 × {14 − (8 − 3)}] + 16.

    1. 66
    2. 36
    3. 116
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 36

    Explanation: The grouped divisor is 5 × 9 = 45; hence 900 ÷ 45 + 16 = 36.

  5. In a school, 5/12 of the students travel by bus. Of the others, 3/7 walk and the remaining 160 cycle. Find the total number of students.

    1. 280
    2. 360
    3. 560
    4. 480
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 480

    Explanation: The cycling fraction is (7/12)(4/7) = 1/3; therefore total students = 160 × 3 = 480.

  6. Find 2/(3 × 5) + 2/(5 × 7) + 2/(7 × 9) + 2/(9 × 11) + 2/(11 × 13).

    1. 2/13
    2. 10/39
    3. 5/39
    4. 1/3
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 10/39

    Explanation: Each term is 1/k − 1/(k + 2); cancellation leaves 1/3 − 1/13 = 10/39.

  7. Evaluate: |23 − 41| − |−9| + 4.

    1. 13
    2. 31
    3. -23
    4. 5
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 13

    Explanation: The magnitudes are 18 and 9; hence 18 − 9 + 4 = 13.

  8. Find the value in grams of 3.5 kg − 850 g + 0.65 kg.

    1. 3200
    2. 2450
    3. 3300
    4. 5000
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 3300

    Explanation: Express all quantities in grams: 3500 − 850 + 650 = 3300 g.

  9. If a = √13 + √12 and b = √13 − √12, find (a − b)²/(ab).

    1. 52
    2. 48
    3. 25
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 48

    Explanation: Here ab = 1 and a − b = 2√12; hence the value is 4 × 12 = 48.

  10. Evaluate: (0.96 − 0.36) ÷ (0.25 × 0.08).

    1. 12
    2. 300
    3. 30
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 30

    Explanation: The numerator is 0.60 and the denominator is 0.02; the quotient is 30.

  11. Evaluate: 734 × 11.

    1. 8074
    2. 8134
    3. 8004
    4. 7340
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 8074

    Explanation: Multiply by 10 and add the original number: 7340 + 734 = 8074.

  12. A hall has some benches. Seating 5 students per bench leaves 4 benches empty; seating 4 per bench leaves 8 students standing. How many students are there?

    1. 112
    2. 128
    3. 140
    4. 120
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 120

    Explanation: If b is the bench count, 5(b − 4) = 4b + 8, giving b = 28. Hence students = 4 × 28 + 8 = 120.

  13. If x ≥ 0 and √(x + √(x + 20)) = 6, find x.

    1. 29
    2. 7
    3. 36
    4. 44
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 29

    Explanation: Squaring gives x + √(x + 20) = 36. Put t = √(x + 20); then t² + t = 56 = 7 × 8, so t = 7 and x = 29.

  14. If a △ b = 3a − b and a □ b = a + 2b, find (7 △ 5) □ (4 △ 3).

    1. 50
    2. 32
    3. 34
    4. 25
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 34

    Explanation: The inner results are 16 and 9; apply □ to get 16 + 2 × 9 = 34.

  15. Evaluate: √(81 × 121) ÷ 11.

    1. 11
    2. 9
    3. 99
    4. 81
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 9

    Explanation: The root is 9 × 11; cancel 11 to obtain 9.

  16. If a + b + c = 15 and ab + bc + ca = 61, find a² + b² + c².

    1. 347
    2. 164
    3. 225
    4. 103
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 103

    Explanation: Square the sum and subtract twice the pairwise products: 15² − 2 × 61 = 103.

  17. Which option is closest to (799.2 + 1201.8) ÷ 24.9?

    1. 90
    2. 100
    3. 70
    4. 80
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 80

    Explanation: Use 2000 ÷ 25 = 80; the actual quotient is about 80.36, nearest to 80.

  18. Find the value of ? in 0.45 × ? + 18.6 = 72.6.

    1. 120
    2. 162
    3. 12
    4. 108
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 120

    Explanation: Subtract 18.6 to get 54; then ? = 54 ÷ 0.45 = 120.

  19. Find the value of 125% of 248.

    1. 298
    2. 310
    3. 496
    4. 279
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 310

    Explanation: 125% = 5/4; thus 248 ÷ 4 × 5 = 310.

  20. For t ≠ 0 and t ≠ 4, simplify [(t² − 16)/(t² − 4t)] × t − 4.

    1. t − 4
    2. t²
    3. t
    4. t + 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) t

    Explanation: Factor to obtain [(t − 4)(t + 4)/(t(t − 4))] × t − 4 = t.

  21. Evaluate: (3/5 + 7/10) ÷ (2 + 3/5).

    1. 1/5
    2. 1/2
    3. 2
    4. 13/10
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1/2

    Explanation: The sum is 13/10 and the divisor is 13/5; their quotient is 1/2.

  22. Evaluate: 203 × 207.

    1. 42021
    2. 42000
    3. 42821
    4. 41421
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 42021

    Explanation: Use (205 − 2)(205 + 2) = 205² − 4 = 42021.

  23. Evaluate: (11/14 − 2/7) × (3 + 3/5).

    1. 18/5
    2. 9/10
    3. 9/5
    4. 7/5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9/5

    Explanation: The difference is 1/2; multiplying by 18/5 gives 9/5.

  24. If z + 1/z = 5, find z³ + 1/z³ − z − 1/z.

    1. 110
    2. 115
    3. 120
    4. 105
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 105

    Explanation: The cubic sum is 5³ − 3 × 5 = 110; subtract the given sum 5 to get 105.

  25. Which equality is correct?

    1. 68 × 25 = 1700
    2. 36² = 1266
    3. 999 × 7 = 6999
    4. 125 × 24 = 2500
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 68 × 25 = 1700

    Explanation: Use 25 = 100/4: 68 × 25 = 1700. The other products are 1296, 3000 and 6993 respectively.
