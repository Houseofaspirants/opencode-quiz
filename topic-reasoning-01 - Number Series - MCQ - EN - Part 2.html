  4. The blank in 20, 200, 23, 196, 28, 187, 35, 171, ?, 146 belongs to which value?

  5. For 30, 68, 130, 222, 350, calculate the term two places after 350.

  6. After the first three terms, each term equals the sum of the previous three: 3, 6, 8, 17, 31, ? . Complete the sequence.

  7. A single replacement repairs 90, 91, 83, 110, 47, 171. Which printed term must be replaced?

  8. Which of the following completes 3, 7, 18, 45, 106, ? when the additions are successive squares?

    Answer: (A) {'label': 'A', 'text': '237'}

  9. A temperature pattern is 6.5, 4.75, 3, 1.25, ? . Determine the next reading.
