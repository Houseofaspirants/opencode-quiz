Question bank

Making of the Indian Constitution - Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. When did the Constituent Assembly adopt the National Flag?

    1. 15 August 1947
    2. 24 January 1950
    3. 26 November 1949
    4. 22 July 1947
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 22 July 1947

    Explanation: The Constituent Assembly adopted the National Flag on 22 July 1947.

  2. When did the Constituent Assembly adopt the National Anthem?

    1. 26 January 1950
    2. 26 November 1949
    3. 22 July 1947
    4. 24 January 1950
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 24 January 1950

    Explanation: The National Anthem was adopted on 24 January 1950.

  3. Whom did the Constituent Assembly elect as the first President of India?

    1. Jawaharlal Nehru
    2. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    3. C. Rajagopalachari
    4. Dr. S. Radhakrishnan
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Dr. Rajendra Prasad

    Explanation: The Constituent Assembly elected Dr. Rajendra Prasad as the first President of India.

  4. How many sessions did the Constituent Assembly hold to frame the Constitution?

    1. Nine
    2. Twelve
    3. Seven
    4. Eleven
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Eleven

    Explanation: A total of eleven sessions were held to frame the Constitution.

  5. Which animal was adopted as the symbol (seal) of the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Lion
    2. Bull
    3. Elephant
    4. Horse
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Elephant

    Explanation: The Elephant was adopted as the symbol (seal) of the Constituent Assembly.

  6. How much time did it take to prepare the Constitution of India?

    1. 2 years, 11 months and 18 days
    2. 2 years, 6 months and 11 days
    3. 3 years, 2 months and 10 days
    4. 1 year, 11 months and 18 days
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2 years, 11 months and 18 days

    Explanation: The Constitution took 2 years, 11 months and 18 days to prepare.

  7. Approximately how many amendments were made to the draft Constitution before it was finalised?

    1. About 500
    2. About 7,500
    3. About 100
    4. About 2,000
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) About 2,000

    Explanation: About 2,000 amendments were made to the draft before it was finalised.

  8. When was the final draft of the Constitution introduced in the Constituent Assembly?

    1. 26 November 1949
    2. 21 February 1948
    3. 4 November 1948
    4. 29 August 1947
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 4 November 1948

    Explanation: The final draft was introduced in the Assembly on 4 November 1948.

  9. Arrange the following events in chronological order: 1. Objectives Resolution 2. Adoption of National Flag 3. Setting up of Drafting Committee 4. Submission of draft constitution

    1. 1, 2, 3, 4
    2. 2, 1, 3, 4
    3. 1, 3, 2, 4
    4. 3, 1, 2, 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1, 2, 3, 4

    Explanation: Objectives Resolution (13 Dec 1946) → National Flag (22 Jul 1947) → Drafting Committee (29 Aug 1947) → Draft submitted (21 Feb 1948).

  10. Consider the following statements: 1. The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949. 2. The National Anthem was adopted on 22 July 1947. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1 only

    Explanation: Statement 1 is correct. The National Flag (not Anthem) was adopted on 22 July 1947; the Anthem was adopted on 24 January 1950.

  11. Who was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee?

    1. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    2. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
    3. Jawaharlal Nehru
    4. K.M. Munshi
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

    Explanation: The Drafting Committee was headed by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar.

  12. When was the Drafting Committee set up?

    1. 15 August 1947
    2. 21 February 1948
    3. 29 August 1947
    4. 13 December 1946
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 29 August 1947

    Explanation: The Drafting Committee was set up on 29 August 1947.

  13. How many members did the Drafting Committee have?

    1. Five
    2. Seven
    3. Eleven
    4. Nine
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Seven

    Explanation: The Drafting Committee had seven members, all of whom were members of the Constituent Assembly.

  14. When did the Drafting Committee submit the draft constitution to the Assembly?

    1. 21 February 1948
    2. 26 November 1949
    3. 4 November 1948
    4. 29 August 1947
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 21 February 1948

    Explanation: On 21 February 1948, the Drafting Committee submitted the draft constitution to the Assembly.

  15. Who among the following was NOT a member of the Drafting Committee?

    1. N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar
    2. Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar
    3. K.M. Munshi
    4. Jawaharlal Nehru
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Jawaharlal Nehru

    Explanation: Drafting Committee members: Ambedkar, Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar, N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar, K.M. Munshi, Mohammad Saadulla, B.L. Mitter, D.P. Khaitan. Nehru was not a member.

  16. Who among the following was a member of the Drafting Committee?

    1. Sardar Patel
    2. Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha
    3. Mohammad Saadulla
    4. Sir B.N. Rau
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Mohammad Saadulla

    Explanation: Mohammad Saadulla was one of the seven members of the Drafting Committee. B.N. Rau was the constitutional advisor, not a member.

  17. Who is called the 'Father of the Indian Constitution'?

    1. Jawaharlal Nehru
    2. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    3. Mahatma Gandhi
    4. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

    Explanation: Because he was Chairman of the Drafting Committee, Dr. B.R. Ambedkar is called the Father of the Indian Constitution.

  18. Consider the following statements about the Drafting Committee: 1. All its members were members of the Constituent Assembly. 2. Sir B.N. Rau was its Chairman. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1 only

    Explanation: All seven members were members of the Constituent Assembly. Its Chairman was Dr. B.R. Ambedkar; B.N. Rau was the constitutional advisor.

  19. Who was the Chairman of the Union Powers Committee of the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Jawaharlal Nehru
    2. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
    3. Sardar Patel
    4. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Jawaharlal Nehru

    Explanation: The Union Powers Committee was chaired by Jawaharlal Nehru.

  20. Who chaired the Steering Committee of the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    2. Sardar Patel
    3. Jawaharlal Nehru
    4. K.M. Munshi
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Dr. Rajendra Prasad

    Explanation: The Steering Committee and the Rules of Procedure Committee were chaired by Dr. Rajendra Prasad.

  21. Who was the Chairman of the Provincial Constitution Committee?

    1. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    2. Sardar Patel
    3. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
    4. Jawaharlal Nehru
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Sardar Patel

    Explanation: The Provincial Constitution Committee was chaired by Sardar Patel.

  22. The Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights, Minorities and Tribal and Excluded Areas was headed by:

    1. J.B. Kripalani
    2. Jawaharlal Nehru
    3. Sardar Patel
    4. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Sardar Patel

    Explanation: The Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights, Minorities and Tribal and Excluded Areas was chaired by Sardar Patel.

  23. Which of the following committees was NOT chaired by Jawaharlal Nehru?

    1. States Committee
    2. Rules of Procedure Committee
    3. Union Constitution Committee
    4. Union Powers Committee
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Rules of Procedure Committee

    Explanation: Nehru chaired the Union Powers, Union Constitution and States Committees. The Rules of Procedure Committee was chaired by Dr. Rajendra Prasad.

  24. Match the committees with their chairmen: A. States Committee – 1. Sardar Patel B. Steering Committee – 2. Jawaharlal Nehru C. Provincial Constitution Committee – 3. Dr. Rajendra Prasad

    1. A-2, B-1, C-3
    2. A-2, B-3, C-1
    3. A-3, B-1, C-2
    4. A-1, B-2, C-3
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) A-2, B-3, C-1

    Explanation: States Committee – Nehru; Steering Committee – Rajendra Prasad; Provincial Constitution Committee – Sardar Patel.

  25. Dr. Rajendra Prasad chaired which pair of committees of the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Provincial Constitution Committee and Advisory Committee
    2. Union Powers Committee and States Committee
    3. Steering Committee and Rules of Procedure Committee
    4. Drafting Committee and Steering Committee
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Steering Committee and Rules of Procedure Committee

    Explanation: Dr. Rajendra Prasad chaired the Steering Committee and the Rules of Procedure Committee.
