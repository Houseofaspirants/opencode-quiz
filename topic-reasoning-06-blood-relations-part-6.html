Blood Relations MCQ – Part 6: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Seven people A–G have exactly two married couples. A, a man, is B’s father; B is C’s brother. C is married to D and is mother of son E. F is A’s wife and mother of B and C. G is B’s daughter. Which named person is E’s mother’s brother’s daughter?

    1. C
    2. G
    3. B
    4. F
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) G

    Explanation: E’s mother is C, her brother is B, and B’s daughter is G.

  2. X @ Y denotes X is Y’s father; X # Y denotes X is Y’s mother; X $ Y denotes X is Y’s sister. In M @ N # P $ Q, which person’s gender is NOT fixed?

    1. M
    2. N
    3. P
    4. Q
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Q

    Explanation: M is male, N female and P female; Q is only described as P’s sibling.

  3. At a Jalandhar reunion, a man, Tarun, introduces a boy: “His father’s mother is my mother’s sister.” Which woman connects the boy’s father to Tarun as cousins?

    1. The boy’s sister and Tarun’s mother
    2. The boy’s maternal grandmother and Tarun’s mother
    3. The boy’s paternal grandmother and Tarun’s mother
    4. The boy’s mother and Tarun’s sister
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) The boy’s paternal grandmother and Tarun’s mother

    Explanation: The boy’s father and Tarun have mothers who are sisters: the boy’s paternal grandmother and Tarun’s mother.

  4. A is B’s brother. B is C’s daughter. C’s gender is unstated. Which conclusion cannot be fixed without C’s gender?

    1. Whether B is female
    2. Whether C is A’s father or mother
    3. Whether A is male
    4. Whether A and B are siblings
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Whether C is A’s father or mother

    Explanation: A and B share parent C, but no word fixes C as male or female.

  5. Code: X + Y means X is Y’s father; X - Y means X is Y’s sister; X * Y means X is Y’s son. In A - B + C * D, replace only the first symbol so that A becomes C’s paternal uncle. Which replacement works?

    1. Replace - with +
    2. Keep - unchanged
    3. Replace - with *
    4. No listed symbol can do this
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) No listed symbol can do this

    Explanation: A must become B’s brother, but the code has father, sister and son only; none supplies brother.

  6. A woman, Manpreet, is the sister of a man, Jaswant. Jaswant’s son Kuldeep is married to Reet. Reet’s mother is Savita. Which person is Reet’s husband’s paternal aunt?

    1. Savita
    2. Jaswant
    3. Reet
    4. Manpreet
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Manpreet

    Explanation: Reet’s husband is Kuldeep; his father Jaswant’s sister is Manpreet.

  7. A man, Harpal, has a sister Inderjit and a brother Jagdeep. Inderjit’s son Kamal is father of a daughter, Lavi. What is Jagdeep to Kamal?

    1. Maternal grandfather
    2. Paternal uncle
    3. Maternal uncle
    4. Father
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Maternal uncle

    Explanation: Jagdeep and Inderjit are siblings; Inderjit is Kamal’s mother, so Jagdeep is his maternal uncle.

  8. A verbal tree gives three generations: couple R (male)–S (female); their son T and daughter U; T’s son V; U’s daughter W. Who belongs to the youngest generation but is not R’s grandson?

    1. V
    2. T
    3. U
    4. W
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) W

    Explanation: V and W are the youngest generation; W is a granddaughter, whereas V is a grandson.

  9. Which statement alone answers “How is A related to B?” I. A is the only daughter of B’s mother. II. A and B are distinct people, and A is B’s sister. Treat I and II independently.

    1. I alone, since II gives no gender
    2. Both are insufficient even together
    3. II alone, since I does not exclude A being B
    4. I alone proves A is B’s mother
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) II alone, since I does not exclude A being B

    Explanation: I permits B to be that only daughter A; II explicitly distinguishes them and states sister.

  10. In a code, X + Y is X is Y’s wife; X # Y is X is Y’s brother; X @ Y is X is Y’s father. Decode L + M # N @ O. Who is O’s father’s brother’s wife?

    1. L
    2. O
    3. N
    4. M
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) L

    Explanation: O’s father is N, N’s brother is M, and M’s wife is L.

  11. A woman, N, is mother of P. P is parent of Q; Q is father of R. Which description of N relative to R is certain even though P’s gender is unknown?

    1. Paternal aunt
    2. Great-grandmother
    3. Mother-in-law
    4. Maternal grandmother
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Great-grandmother

    Explanation: R → father Q → parent P → mother N gives three upward links to a female ancestor.

  12. A boy, Samar, points to a woman: “My father’s wife is her only daughter.” Samar’s mother and father are married to each other. The woman is Samar’s _____.

    1. Mother
    2. Maternal grandmother
    3. Paternal aunt
    4. Paternal grandmother
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Maternal grandmother

    Explanation: His father’s wife is his mother; her mother is Samar’s maternal grandmother.

  13. X / Y means X is Y’s father, X * Y means X is Y’s brother, X - Y means X is Y’s daughter. Which expression establishes both that A is D’s paternal uncle and D is female?

    1. D - A * B / C
    2. A * B / D * C
    3. A * B / D - C
    4. A / B * D - C
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) A * B / D - C

    Explanation: A is brother of D’s father B; D - C fixes D as female.

  14. Six people A–F form three generations with two couples. A is B’s wife. C is their only daughter. E is C’s husband. D is C’s son and F is D’s sister. Every child has both parents in the family. Which pair consists of one male and one female grandchild of A?

    1. D and F
    2. D and E
    3. B and C
    4. C and E
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) D and F

    Explanation: D and F are C’s son and daughter; C is A’s daughter, so both are A’s grandchildren through her.

  15. A woman, Bharti, has daughter Charanpreet. Charanpreet is married to Daler. Daler’s elder sister is Ekam. Which statement about Bharti is established?

    1. She is Ekam’s mother
    2. She is Daler’s paternal aunt
    3. She is Daler’s mother-in-law
    4. She is Ekam’s sister
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) She is Daler’s mother-in-law

    Explanation: Bharti is the mother of Daler’s wife Charanpreet, hence his mother-in-law.

  16. A man, Ashwin, has a younger sister Bhavana. Bhavana’s daughter Chahat has a son Darsh. Identify Ashwin’s relationship to Chahat’s mother.

    1. Grandfather
    2. Maternal uncle
    3. Father
    4. Elder brother
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Elder brother

    Explanation: Chahat’s mother is Bhavana, who is Ashwin’s younger sister; therefore Ashwin is her elder brother.

  17. In one family, each son has 2 brothers and each daughter has 3 sisters. All children share the same two parents. Including the parents, how many members are there?

    1. 7
    2. 10
    3. 9
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9

    Explanation: There are 3 sons and 4 daughters; adding 2 parents gives 9 members.

  18. In a verbal tree, A (male) and B (female) have son C and daughter D. C and wife E have daughter F. D and husband G have son H. Which person is both H’s maternal uncle and F’s father?

    1. C
    2. G
    3. A
    4. H
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) C

    Explanation: C is the brother of H’s mother D and is explicitly F’s father.

  19. A is B’s sister, B is C’s brother, and C is D’s mother. The ages are not given. Which person is certainly female and in D’s mother’s generation, but is not D’s mother?

    1. B
    2. A
    3. C
    4. D
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) A

    Explanation: A, B and C are siblings; A is female and is the sister of D’s mother C.

  20. X + Y means X is Y’s father; X @ Y means X is Y’s wife; X # Y means X is Y’s sister. All letters denote distinct people. Which expression can represent a valid family without conflicting genders? Assume husbands are male and wives female.

    1. A @ B + C # D
    2. A @ B # C + D
    3. A # B @ C # D
    4. A + B @ C # D
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A @ B + C # D

    Explanation: In the valid expression A is female, B male and C female. The alternatives force C or B to be both male and female.

  21. A woman, Garima, introduces a man: “My daughter is his son’s wife.” Garima’s daughter is Hina and the man’s son is Irfan. Which pair is necessarily married?

    1. Hina and Irfan
    2. Garima and Irfan
    3. Hina and the introduced man
    4. Garima and the introduced man
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Hina and Irfan

    Explanation: The stated daughter Hina is the stated son Irfan’s wife.

  22. Code: X % Y means X is Y’s daughter; X + Y means X is Y’s father; X @ Y means X is Y’s husband. In P % Q @ R, which additional coded fact proves that R is P’s mother without relying on a spouse-parent convention?

    1. Q + P
    2. P % R
    3. R % S
    4. S + Q
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) P % R

    Explanation: Q @ R fixes R as female; P % R then explicitly makes P R’s daughter, proving maternity.

  23. To decide whether P is Q’s maternal grandfather: I. P is father of R and R is Q’s parent. II. R is female. Which sufficiency decision is correct?

    1. Both are required and together sufficient
    2. I alone is sufficient
    3. II alone is sufficient
    4. Even together insufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Both are required and together sufficient

    Explanation: I gives a grandfather but leaves the side unknown; II makes parent R the mother, establishing the maternal side.

  24. A woman, Kiran, is wife of Lalit. Lalit’s mother Mansi has a brother, Naresh. Naresh’s daughter is Oorja. What is Mansi to Kiran’s husband?

    1. Mother-in-law
    2. Mother
    3. Maternal aunt
    4. Sister
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Mother

    Explanation: Kiran’s husband is Lalit, whose mother is explicitly Mansi; the Naresh–Oorja branch is irrelevant.

  25. R is father of S. S is sister of T, and T is father of U. V is U’s mother’s sister. What relationship between R and V is established by these facts?

    1. V is R’s daughter
    2. R is V’s father
    3. R is V’s maternal uncle
    4. R’s son is the father of V’s sister’s child
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) R’s son is the father of V’s sister’s child

    Explanation: R’s son T is U’s father; U’s mother is V’s sister, so the first description preserves every stated link.
