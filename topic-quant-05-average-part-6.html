Question bank

Average MCQ – Part 6: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. After the smallest of 11 consecutive integers is removed, the remaining 10 average 43.5. Find the average of the original 11 integers.

    1. 42.5
    2. 44
    3. 44.5
    4. 43
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 43

    Explanation: Removing the smallest shifts the midpoint up by 0.5. Original average = 43.5 − 0.5 = 43.

  2. Groups A and B contain 16 and 24 candidates and together average 72 marks. A averages 78. 6 candidates averaging 83 marks move from A to B. Find B’s new average.

    1. 70
    2. 72
    3. 73
    4. 71
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 71

    Explanation: B’s original total = 40 × 72 − 16 × 78 = 1632. New average = (1632 + 6 × 83) ÷ 30 = 71.

  3. The average of 25 readings is 56. A reading of 40 is replaced by x, and then every reading is increased by 10%. The final average is 63.8. Find x.

    1. 85
    2. 90
    3. 95
    4. 80
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 90

    Explanation: Average before the percentage increase = 63.8 ÷ 1.1 = 58. Thus x = 40 + 25 × (58 − 56) = 90.

  4. 2 numbers average 37. A third number is 10 more than the larger of them. If all 3 average 42, find the smallest number.

    1. 32
    2. 37
    3. 42
    4. 52
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 32

    Explanation: Third number = 3 × 42 − 2 × 37 = 52. Larger original number = 52 − 10 = 42, so smallest = 74 − 42 = 32.

  5. A set contains both positive and negative values and has average −7. If the sum is −126, how many observations are there?

    1. 19
    2. 17
    3. 21
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 18

    Explanation: Count = total ÷ average = (−126) ÷ (−7) = 18.

  6. In a list, 45 was entered as 54 and 92 as 29. These errors lowered the average by 1.8. How many entries are in the list?

    1. 35
    2. 30
    3. 25
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 30

    Explanation: Net total shortfall = 45 + 92 − 54 − 29 = 54. Count = 54 ÷ 1.8 = 30.

  7. Petrol is bought for ₹1,800 at ₹90 per litre and for ₹2,200 at ₹110 per litre. Find the average price per litre in ₹.

    1. 100
    2. 99
    3. 101
    4. 102
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 100

    Explanation: Quantities are 1800 ÷ 90 = 20 and 2200 ÷ 110 = 20 litres. Average price = 4000 ÷ 40 = ₹100.

  8. A group of 12 has average age 24 years. 2 people aged 30 and 36 join, followed by 1 person aged 42. Find the final average age in years.

    1. 27
    2. 26
    3. 26.4
    4. 28.8
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 26.4

    Explanation: Total added = 108 years. Final average = (12 × 24 + 108) ÷ 15 = 26.4 years.

  9. 20 numbers average 28. The first 12 average 26 and the last 12 average 31. Find the average of the 16 numbers outside the overlap.

    1. 28
    2. 27.25
    3. 29
    4. 26.75
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 27.25

    Explanation: Overlap total = 12 × 26 + 12 × 31 − 20 × 28 = 124. Outside average = (560 − 124) ÷ 16 = 27.25.

  10. A set has average 96. Every value is divided by 4 and then increased by 7. Find the resulting average.

    1. 31
    2. 24
    3. 25.75
    4. 38
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 31

    Explanation: New average = 96 ÷ 4 + 7 = 31.

  11. 24 sacks average 46 kg. After some sacks averaging 40 kg are removed, the remaining sacks average 48 kg. How many sacks were removed?

    1. 8
    2. 4
    3. 6
    4. 5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 6

    Explanation: Removed count = 24 × (48 − 46) ÷ (48 − 40) = 6.

  12. A bowler’s average is 28 runs per wicket. He takes 5 wickets while conceding 36 runs, and his average falls to 24. How many wickets had he taken earlier?

    1. 21
    2. 24
    3. 18
    4. 26
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 21

    Explanation: If earlier wickets are w, 28w + 36 = 24(w + 5). Thus 4w = 84 and w = 21.

  13. 8 years ago, a couple averaged 26 years of age. Their child is now 7 years old. Find the present average age of the 3, in years.

    1. 25
    2. 24
    3. 26
    4. 27
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 25

    Explanation: Couple’s present total = 2 × (26 + 8) = 68. Family average = (68 + 7) ÷ 3 = 25 years.

  14. Find the average of the prime numbers between 50 and 70.

    1. 59
    2. 61
    3. 60
    4. 62
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 60

    Explanation: The primes are 53, 59, 61 and 67. Their symmetric pairs total 120, so the average is 60.

  15. A batter averages 52 runs per innings over 10 innings. What is the greatest possible average after the next innings if that innings cannot exceed 85 runs?

    1. 56
    2. 54
    3. 55
    4. 57
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 55

    Explanation: Use the maximum score 85. Greatest average = (10 × 52 + 85) ÷ 11 = 55.

  16. In a 32-member group, replacing 2 members of weights 44 kg and 52 kg raises the average by 0.75 kg. 1 newcomer weighs 58 kg. Find the other newcomer’s weight in kg.

    1. 60
    2. 62
    3. 64
    4. 66
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 62

    Explanation: New pair total = 44 + 52 + 32 × 0.75 = 120 kg. Other weight = 120 − 58 = 62 kg.

  17. 2 batches average 82 and 62 marks. Together they average 70. If the higher-scoring batch has 24 candidates, find the total number of candidates.

    1. 48
    2. 64
    3. 72
    4. 60
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 60

    Explanation: Higher : lower = (70 − 62) : (82 − 70) = 2 : 3. Total = 24 × 5 ÷ 2 = 60.

  18. A candidate scores 65% in an examination with maximum 400 marks and 80% in another with maximum 600 marks. Find the combined percentage.

    1. 72.5
    2. 75
    3. 74
    4. 76
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 74

    Explanation: Combined percentage = (400 × 65 + 600 × 80) ÷ 1000 = 74%.

  19. The daily numbers of defects 0, 1, 2 and 3 occur on 2, 4, 3 and 1 days respectively. Find the mean defects per day.

    1. 1.3
    2. 1.5
    3. 1.2
    4. 1.4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1.3

    Explanation: Mean = (0 × 2 + 1 × 4 + 2 × 3 + 3 × 1) ÷ 10 = 1.3.

  20. Among 18 numbers, the first 10 average 44 and the last 10 average 50. Their common pair averages 47. Find the average of all 18 numbers.

    1. 46
    2. 48
    3. 47
    4. 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 47

    Explanation: Total = 10 × 44 + 10 × 50 − 2 × 47 = 846. Average = 846 ÷ 18 = 47.

  21. The average of A and B is 31, and the average of B and C is 37. If A is 24, find the average of A and C.

    1. 32
    2. 30
    3. 34
    4. 36
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 30

    Explanation: B = 62 − 24 = 38 and C = 74 − 38 = 36. Average of A and C = (24 + 36) ÷ 2 = 30.

  22. A worker’s daily income averages ₹480 over a week and daily expenditure averages ₹350 over the same week. Find the total savings for the week in ₹.

    1. 130
    2. 910
    3. 830
    4. 960
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 910

    Explanation: Daily saving = 480 − 350 = ₹130. Weekly saving = 7 × 130 = ₹910.

  23. A bus covers 210 km at an average speed of 42 km/h, including a halt of 48 minutes. Find its average running speed in km/h.

    1. 48
    2. 52
    3. 54
    4. 50
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 50

    Explanation: Total time = 210 ÷ 42 = 5 hours. Running time = 5 − 48 ÷ 60 = 4.2 hours, so running speed = 210 ÷ 4.2 = 50 km/h.

  24. Can the average of 5 real numbers be determined? I: Their largest value is 60 and smallest is 20. II: Their middle value when sorted is 40. Choose the code: 1 = I alone is sufficient; 2 = II alone is sufficient; 3 = both together are needed and sufficient; 4 = both together are insufficient.

    1. 1
    2. 4
    3. 2
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 4

    Explanation: Both 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 and 20, 25, 40, 50, 60 satisfy both statements. Their averages are 40 and 39, so even together the statements are insufficient.

  25. 20 observations average 36. It is found that every observation was recorded 2 units too high. Find the corrected total.

    1. 718
    2. 720
    3. 760
    4. 680
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 680

    Explanation: Correct average = 36 − 2 = 34. Correct total = 20 × 34 = 680.
