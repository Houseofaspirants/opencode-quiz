About this cluster

What Punjab Art covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Punjab Art covers the craft and visual-heritage side of the Punjab GK section: embroidery traditions, fresco and wall painting, handicrafts and the architecture associated with the region. Questions here are recognition-based, asking which art form belongs to which practice or region, so the material is best learned visually and by pairing rather than by reading descriptions at length. The lane overlaps slightly with Punjab Culture, and the two are often revised together because examiners draw from both. The important habit is to attach each art form to the technique that defines it, since that is what separates the options in a question. Work through the lanes below one form at a time and test each before moving on.
