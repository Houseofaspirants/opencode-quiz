Question bank

Blood Relations MCQ – Part 5: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Code X + Y: X is Y’s father; X - Y: X is Y’s mother; X @ Y: X is Y’s brother. Which expression puts A two generations above D but does NOT prove that A is D’s grandfather?

    1. A + B + D
    2. A @ B + C - D
    3. A + D @ B
    4. A + B - D
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) A @ B + C - D

    Explanation: A is the brother of D’s mother’s father B, so he is two generations above D without being D’s grandfather.

  2. An elder woman, Bimla, is the mother of a man, Chandan. Chandan is the father of Deepa; Deepa is the mother of Eklavya. What is Bimla’s relationship to Deepa?

    1. Mother
    2. Great-grandmother
    3. Paternal grandmother
    4. Maternal grandmother
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Paternal grandmother

    Explanation: Use Deepa → father Chandan → mother Bimla; Eklavya lies beyond the requested path.

  3. A is mother of B; B is brother of C; C is father of D. Starting at D’s generation, which pair is definitely at levels +1 and +2 respectively?

    1. A and B
    2. B and A
    3. C and B
    4. D and C
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) B and A

    Explanation: B shares C’s parental generation at +1; their mother A is at +2.

  4. Seven people H–N form one family. H is a man married to I. J is their only son. K is J’s sister. L, a woman, is a daughter-in-law of H. M and N are the son and daughter of J. Exactly two couples exist, and every child’s parents are married. Who is M’s mother?

    1. L
    2. N
    3. K
    4. I
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) L

    Explanation: H’s only son is J, so daughter-in-law L is J’s wife and the stated married-parent rule makes her M’s mother.

  5. A man, Omkar, points to an elderly man: “My mother is his only child.” Which relationship is certain?

    1. The man is Omkar’s paternal grandfather
    2. Omkar is the man’s son
    3. The man is Omkar’s father-in-law
    4. The man is Omkar’s maternal grandfather
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) The man is Omkar’s maternal grandfather

    Explanation: The pointed man is the father of Omkar’s mother.

  6. X @ Y means X is Y’s son; X # Y means X is Y’s sister; X $ Y means X is Y’s father. For A @ B # C $ D, choose the shortest correct path from D to A.

    1. Father → brother → daughter
    2. Mother → brother → son
    3. Mother → sister → daughter
    4. Father → sister → son
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Father → sister → son

    Explanation: D’s father is C, C’s sister is B, and B’s son is A.

  7. A man, Farhan, is married to Zoya. Zoya’s mother is Sameera and her father is Khalid. Which pair gives Farhan’s mother-in-law and father-in-law in that order?

    1. Khalid, Sameera
    2. Sameera, Khalid
    3. Zoya, Khalid
    4. Sameera, Farhan
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Sameera, Khalid

    Explanation: His wife Zoya’s mother is Sameera and her father is Khalid.

  8. A verbal tree has A (female) married to B (male). Their son C marries D (female), and daughter E marries F (male). C–D have son G; E–F have daughter H. Which pair is connected only through marriage in the stated tree, with neither a parent–child nor a sibling link?

    1. C and G
    2. C and E
    3. A and E
    4. E and F
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) E and F

    Explanation: E and F are spouses; the other pairs are parent–child or siblings.

  9. Three sisters each have two daughters. Their mother has no other children. Counting only these named generations, how many females are simultaneously a daughter and a mother of someone in the group?

    1. 3
    2. 7
    3. 6
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 3

    Explanation: Each of the three sisters is a daughter of the eldest woman and a mother of two girls; no other overlap is given.

  10. Question: Who is T’s father? I. R is T’s father. II. R is a man, S is T’s mother, and R and S are T’s only parents. Choose the sufficiency assessment.

    1. Either statement alone is sufficient
    2. Only I is sufficient
    3. Both are required
    4. Only II is sufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Either statement alone is sufficient

    Explanation: I names the father directly; II independently makes R the male parent of T.

  11. X + Y means X is Y’s mother; X - Y means X is Y’s son; X * Y means X is Y’s sister. Which expression establishes that P is Q’s granddaughter through a daughter?

    1. Q + R - P * S
    2. Q + R + S - P
    3. Q + R + P * S
    4. P + R + Q * S
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Q + R + P * S

    Explanation: Q is R’s mother, R is P’s mother, and P * S proves P is female.

  12. A man has a daughter and a son. His daughter’s child X and his son’s child Y have unspecified genders. Which statement is certain?

    1. He is maternal grandfather to both
    2. He is X’s maternal and Y’s paternal grandfather
    3. X is his grandson and Y his granddaughter
    4. X and Y are siblings
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) He is X’s maternal and Y’s paternal grandfather

    Explanation: The parent’s gender fixes the maternal or paternal branch even when the grandchildren’s genders are unknown.

  13. A man, Suraj, has a daughter Mita. Mita’s brother Nikhil has a son, Ojas. Which person lies between Suraj and Ojas on their direct descent line?

    1. Nikhil
    2. Mita
    3. Ojas
    4. No middle person
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Nikhil

    Explanation: Nikhil shares father Suraj with Mita; the direct line is Suraj → Nikhil → Ojas.

  14. Use X @ Y for X is Y’s mother, X # Y for X is Y’s daughter, X $ Y for X is Y’s brother. In A # B $ C @ D, which relation between A and D is proved?

    1. Mother’s brother’s daughter
    2. Mother’s sister’s daughter
    3. Father’s sister’s daughter
    4. Sister
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Mother’s brother’s daughter

    Explanation: D’s mother is C, whose brother is B; B’s daughter A is D’s maternal-uncle’s daughter.

  15. A woman, Nalini, introduces a man: “His daughter is my grandson’s mother.” Nalini’s son and his wife are that grandson’s parents. What is the introduced man to Nalini’s son?

    1. Maternal uncle
    2. Son-in-law
    3. Father-in-law
    4. Father
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Father-in-law

    Explanation: The daughter is Nalini’s son’s wife; her father is his father-in-law.

  16. Eight relatives: A and B are husband and wife; C is their son and D their daughter. E is C’s wife and F is D’s husband. G is C–E’s daughter; H is D–F’s son. Considering only these eight, who has both a daughter and a granddaughter?

    1. A and B
    2. E and F
    3. D and F
    4. C and E
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A and B

    Explanation: A and B have daughter D and granddaughter G through son C.

  17. A written tree shows P (female)–Q (male) as married parents of son R and daughter S. R and wife T have sons U and V. S has daughter W. Which pair has the same paternal grandparents?

    1. R and W
    2. U and W
    3. U and V
    4. S and U
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) U and V

    Explanation: U and V share father R, whose parents are P and Q.

  18. Priya is wife of Rohan, who is younger than his brother Sagar. Sagar’s wife Tina is younger than Priya. What is Tina to Priya?

    1. Husband’s sister
    2. Husband’s younger brother’s wife
    3. Brother’s wife
    4. Husband’s elder brother’s wife
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Husband’s elder brother’s wife

    Explanation: The husbands’ relative ages control this term: Sagar is Rohan’s elder brother, so Tina is Priya’s elder co-sister-in-law.

  19. X : Y means X is Y’s father; X ; Y means X is Y’s sister; X / Y means X is Y’s wife. Which expression makes C the mother of A while also showing A has a brother B? B is explicitly male. For every married couple, the children shown are children of both spouses.

    1. C / D : A ; B
    2. A ; B : C / D
    3. C / D : B ; A
    4. C ; D : B / A
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) C / D : A ; B

    Explanation: C is D’s wife and D is A’s father; A is B’s sister and B is explicitly male, so B is her brother.

  20. Seven relatives include 2 proved males, 3 proved females and 2 people whose genders are unrestricted. What is the largest possible excess of females over males?

    1. 5
    2. 3
    3. 2
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 3

    Explanation: Make both unknowns female: 5 females minus 2 males gives an excess of 3.

  21. A is B’s daughter; B is C’s son. Which extra fact is enough to decide whether C is A’s paternal grandfather or paternal grandmother?

    1. D is C’s child
    2. B has a brother
    3. C is D’s wife
    4. A is female
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) C is D’s wife

    Explanation: C is already on A’s paternal side; wife fixes C as female, hence paternal grandmother.

  22. A is the mother of B. C is B’s father. D is C’s brother. Which statement is NOT implied, without any marriage information?

    1. C is male
    2. D is B’s paternal uncle
    3. A is B’s female parent
    4. A is C’s wife
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) A is C’s wife

    Explanation: Parenthood does not itself state a marriage; D’s paternal-uncle link and both parental genders are established.

  23. A woman, Tanvi, points to a girl: “Her father’s only sister is my mother.” Which statement locates Tanvi relative to the girl?

    1. Tanvi is the girl’s sister
    2. Tanvi is the girl’s mother
    3. Tanvi is the girl’s maternal-aunt’s daughter
    4. Tanvi is the girl’s paternal-aunt’s daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Tanvi is the girl’s paternal-aunt’s daughter

    Explanation: Tanvi’s mother is the girl’s father’s sister, so Tanvi is the paternal-aunt’s daughter.

  24. Let X % Y mean X is Y’s mother, X @ Y mean X is Y’s husband, and X # Y mean X is Y’s brother. Assume husband and wife are male and female respectively. Assess A % B @ C # D.

    1. A is female
    2. B is male
    3. The expression is already inconsistent
    4. D’s gender is unspecified
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) The expression is already inconsistent

    Explanation: B @ C makes C a wife, hence female, but C # D makes C a brother, hence male; the expression conflicts internally.

  25. A woman, Roshni, has a brother Vivek. Vivek’s daughter is Yamini. Yamini’s brother Zubin is father of a girl Aanya. What is Roshni to Zubin?

    1. Maternal aunt
    2. Paternal aunt
    3. Paternal grandmother
    4. Mother-in-law
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Paternal aunt

    Explanation: Zubin and Yamini share father Vivek; Vivek’s sister Roshni is Zubin’s paternal aunt.
