Why it matters

Why Guru Har Rai Ji Punjabi Set is worth the hours

Where the marks actually come from.

Guru Har Rai Ji Punjabi Set is factual rather than analytical: you either recall it in seconds or you lose the mark to somebody who did. That makes it one of the cheapest sections to convert - a handful of short sittings usually settles the whole cluster.
