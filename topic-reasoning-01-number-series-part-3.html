Question bank

Number Series MCQ – Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Choose the missing value in 35, 50, 63, 82, 99, ?.

    1. 120
    2. 122
    3. 121
    4. 123
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 122

    Explanation: The terms are 6² − 1, 7² + 1, 8² − 1, 9² + 1, 10² − 1. Next is 11² + 1 = 122.

  2. An alternating rise-and-fall pattern gives 28, 30, 27, 31, 26, 32, ? . What follows?

    1. 25
    2. 27
    3. 38
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 25

    Explanation: The changes are +2, −3, +4, −5, +6, then −7. Hence 32 − 7 = 25.

  3. The sequence 3, 5, 9, 16, 28, 48, ? adds a small increasing adjustment to a two-term sum. Find its next term.

    1. 76
    2. 80
    3. 82
    4. 81
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 81

    Explanation: Add the previous two terms and then 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 successively. The next term is 28 + 48 + 5 = 81.

  4. Fill the blank in 2, 8, 72, ?, 28800, using the pattern of multipliers.

    1. 1296
    2. 1440
    3. 864
    4. 1152
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1152

    Explanation: Multiply by 2², 3², 4², 5². The blank is 72 × 16 = 1152; 1152 × 25 = 28800.

  5. For the interwoven list 11, 150, 13, 143, 17, 136, 19, 129, ... , which number occupies position 11?

    1. 122
    2. 29
    3. 23
    4. 31
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 29

    Explanation: Odd positions contain consecutive primes 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29. Position 11 is the sixth odd-position term, so it is 29.

  6. Kirandeep obtains 4, 18, 8, 30, 14, 48, 23, ? by alternating two rules. Determine the missing value.

    1. 45
    2. 75
    3. 72
    4. 69
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 75

    Explanation: Alternate ×3 + 6 and ÷2 − 1. After 48 ÷ 2 − 1 = 23, apply 23 × 3 + 6 = 75.

  7. A token series starts 38, 49, 60, 71 and ends at 170 with the same gap. How many tokens are in the series, including both ends?

    1. 14
    2. 12
    3. 13
    4. 11
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 13

    Explanation: There are (170 − 38) ÷ 11 = 12 gaps. Including the first token gives 12 + 1 = 13 tokens.

  8. If the bases are consecutive primes starting at 3, which number fits 12, 30, 56, 132, 182, ?

    1. 272
    2. 306
    3. 289
    4. 342
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 306

    Explanation: Each term is p(p + 1) for p = 3, 5, 7, 11, 13. Next p = 17, giving 17 × 18 = 306.

  9. Find the faulty entry in 8, 10, 27, 14, 64, 22, 126, 38, 216, 70.

    1. 216
    2. 38
    3. 22
    4. 126
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 126

    Explanation: Odd positions should be 2³, 3³, 4³, 5³, 6³, so 126 should be 125. Even-position gaps are 4, 8, 16, 32, confirming that strand.

  10. Recognise the bases in 4, 9, 25, 64, 144, ? and supply the next number.

    1. 225
    2. 324
    3. 289
    4. 256
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 289

    Explanation: The square bases are 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, with gaps 1, 2, 3, 4. The next base is 17, giving 17² = 289.

  11. The successive multipliers are −2, +3, −4, +5, −6. Complete 3, −6, −18, 72, 360, ?.

    1. 1800
    2. -2160
    3. -1800
    4. 2160
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) -2160

    Explanation: The final multiplier is −6. Therefore 360 × (−6) = −2160.

  12. Find the next output of the rule hidden in 4, 10, 23, 51, 109, ?.

    1. 225
    2. 231
    3. 229
    4. 227
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 229

    Explanation: Double and add successive primes 2, 3, 5, 7, 11. Thus 109 × 2 + 11 = 229.

  13. The jumps grow rapidly in 11, 13, 19, 43, 163, ? . Which term follows?

    1. 489
    2. 763
    3. 979
    4. 883
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 883

    Explanation: The gaps are 2!, 3!, 4!, 5!, then 6!. Thus 163 + 720 = 883.

  14. A puzzle combines 6, 8, 12, 16, 20, 32, 30, 64, ?, ? . Select the ordered pair that continues it.

    1. (42, 128)
    2. (36, 128)
    3. (40, 128)
    4. (42, 96)
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) (42, 128)

    Explanation: Odd positions are 2×3, 3×4, 4×5, 5×6, then 6×7 = 42. Even positions double, so 64 × 2 = 128.

  15. From the third entry onward, add the previous two entries and then add 3. What fills the blank in 4, 9, 16, 28, 47, ?

    1. 78
    2. 79
    3. 75
    4. 94
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 78

    Explanation: Use 28 + 47 + 3 = 78.

  16. Which value continues 17, 43, 89, 161, 265, ? when each term combines a square and the preceding integer’s cube?

    1. 399
    2. 448
    3. 392
    4. 407
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 407

    Explanation: The rule is n² + (n − 1)³ for n = 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. Next is 8² + 7³ = 64 + 343 = 407.

  17. Complete the multiplication chain 2, 18, ?, 630, 1890, 1890.

    1. 108
    2. 126
    3. 90
    4. 144
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 126

    Explanation: The multipliers descend through odd numbers 9, 7, 5, 3, 1. Hence 18 × 7 = 126, followed by 126 × 5 = 630.

  18. Amandeep repeats the cycle +7, ×2, −5. Starting with 9, he gets 9, 16, 32, 27, 34, 68, ? . Find the next entry.

    1. 136
    2. 61
    3. 63
    4. 75
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 63

    Explanation: After +7 and ×2, the third operation is −5. Thus 68 − 5 = 63.

  19. One quotient has been misprinted in 46080, 23040, 5760, 960, 125, 12. Which quotient is wrong?

    1. 12
    2. 5760
    3. 125
    4. 960
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 125

    Explanation: Divide by 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 in order. The fifth term must be 960 ÷ 8 = 120 and then 120 ÷ 10 = 12, so 125 is wrong.

  20. After 24, 29, 38, 52, 57, 66, 80, what are the next two entries when the gap cycle repeats?

    1. (85, 94)
    2. (85, 99)
    3. (89, 103)
    4. (94, 99)
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) (85, 94)

    Explanation: The gap cycle is +5, +9, +14. After 80, add 5 and then 9 to get 85 and 94.

  21. The descending consecutive primes are 73, 71, ?, 61, ?, 53. What is the sum of the two missing primes?

    1. 128
    2. 124
    3. 126
    4. 120
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 126

    Explanation: The missing primes are 67 and 59. Their sum is 67 + 59 = 126.

  22. The terms 36, 80, 150, 252, 392, ? follow n³ + n². Determine the missing term.

    1. 576
    2. 512
    3. 648
    4. 567
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 576

    Explanation: The bases are 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, then 8. Therefore 8³ + 8² = 512 + 64 = 576.

  23. Supply the final value in 4, 22, 91, 278, 563, ? by tracking multipliers and additions separately.

    1. 1129
    2. 564
    3. 574
    4. 576
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 574

    Explanation: The rules are ×5 + 2, ×4 + 3, ×3 + 5, ×2 + 7. Next is ×1 + 11, so 563 + 11 = 574.

  24. Compare successive ratios in 96, 144, 288, 720, 2160, ? . Which value continues the pattern?

    1. 8640
    2. 5400
    3. 6480
    4. 7560
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 7560

    Explanation: The multipliers are 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, then 3.5. Thus 2160 × 3.5 = 7560.

  25. Find the missing pair in 2, 200, 3, 199, 8, 191, 19, 164, 46, 100, ?, ?.

    1. (111, -25)
    2. (111, 75)
    3. (65, -25)
    4. (92, -25)
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) (111, -25)

    Explanation: Odd positions use next = 2 × latest + older, giving 2 × 46 + 19 = 111. Even positions subtract 1³, 2³, 3³, 4³, then 5³, giving 100 − 125 = −25.
