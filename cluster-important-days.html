About this cluster

What Important Days and Dates covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Important Days is one of the most scoring lanes in general awareness, because the material is fixed in advance and can be fully covered before the exam. Questions ask which observance falls on which date, what purpose it serves and occasionally what theme is associated with it. Since the list changes very little from year to year, the lane rewards a single careful compilation followed by repeated testing rather than continuous revision. The common error is confusing observances with similar purposes, so the lanes below group them by theme - health, environment, commemorations, international days - which keeps adjacent items distinguishable. Always confirm the date of a lesser-known observance against an official source, since secondary notes are the usual source of small but examinable errors.
