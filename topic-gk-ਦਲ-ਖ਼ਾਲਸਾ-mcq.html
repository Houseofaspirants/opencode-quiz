Why it matters

Why ਦਲ ਖ਼ਾਲਸਾ Mcq is worth the hours

Where the marks actually come from.

ਦਲ ਖ਼ਾਲਸਾ Mcq is factual rather than analytical: you either recall it in seconds or you lose the mark to somebody who did. That makes it one of the cheapest sections to convert - a handful of short sittings usually settles the whole cluster.
