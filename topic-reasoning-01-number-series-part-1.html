Question bank

Number Series MCQ – Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. At a Ludhiana test centre, seat numbers follow 143, 156, 169, 182, ... . What is the 9th seat number in this sequence?

    1. 234
    2. 260
    3. 239
    4. 247
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 247

    Explanation: The common difference is 13. The 9th term is 143 + 8 × 13 = 247.

  2. Which value replaces the blank in 8, 22, 64, ?, 568, 1702?

    1. 194
    2. 192
    3. 188
    4. 190
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 190

    Explanation: Each term is the previous term × 3 − 2. Thus 64 × 3 − 2 = 190, and 190 × 3 − 2 = 568.

  3. Complete the list: 122, 170, 226, 290, ?.

    1. 400
    2. 362
    3. 324
    4. 360
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 362

    Explanation: These are 11² + 1, 13² + 1, 15² + 1 and 17² + 1. Next is 19² + 1 = 362.

  4. Gurleen separates the odd and even positions of 12, 320, 17, 160, 26, 80, 39, 40, ?, ? . Which pair fills the blanks in order?

    1. (52, 20)
    2. (56, 10)
    3. (56, 20)
    4. (54, 40)
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) (56, 20)

    Explanation: Odd-position terms have differences 5, 9, 13, 17, giving 39 + 17 = 56. Even-position terms halve, giving 40 ÷ 2 = 20.

  5. A machine displays 32, 48, 72, 108, ? in successive cycles. Select its next display.

    1. 156
    2. 216
    3. 144
    4. 162
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 162

    Explanation: Multiply by 3/2 each time. Hence 108 × 3 ÷ 2 = 162.

  6. One entry breaks the pattern in 18, 24, 34, 48, 68, 88. Identify that entry.

    1. 68
    2. 48
    3. 88
    4. 34
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 68

    Explanation: The differences should be 6, 10, 14, 18, 22. Therefore 48 + 18 = 66 should replace 68, and 66 + 22 = 88.

  7. Among consecutive prime numbers, Aman has written 41, 43, ?, 53, 59. Which number has he omitted?

    1. 49
    2. 47
    3. 51
    4. 45
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 47

    Explanation: The consecutive primes are 41, 43, 47, 53, 59. The omitted number is 47.

  8. Find the missing internal term: 21, 25, ?, 49, 81, 145.

    1. 33
    2. 37
    3. 41
    4. 29
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 33

    Explanation: The differences double: 4, 8, 16, 32, 64. Thus the missing term is 25 + 8 = 33.

  9. Harpreet uses the sum of the preceding two entries to continue 9, 14, 23, 37, ?, 97. What belongs in the gap?

    1. 62
    2. 74
    3. 60
    4. 51
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 60

    Explanation: Add the preceding two terms: 23 + 37 = 60. The check is 37 + 60 = 97.

  10. The same step pattern connects 7, 8, 18, 57, 232, ? . Determine the final term.

    1. 1160
    2. 1398
    3. 1164
    4. 1165
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1165

    Explanation: The operations are ×1 + 1, ×2 + 2, ×3 + 3 and ×4 + 4. Next, 232 × 5 + 5 = 1165.

  11. Choose the continuation of 120, 336, 720, 1320, ?.

    1. 2178
    2. 1728
    3. 2184
    4. 2197
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 2184

    Explanation: The terms are n³ − n for n = 5, 7, 9, 11. The next base is 13, so 13³ − 13 = 2184.

  12. An instrument records −1458, −486, −162, −54, ? . What is its next reading?

    1. -27
    2. -16
    3. 18
    4. -18
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) -18

    Explanation: Divide each term by 3. Therefore −54 ÷ 3 = −18.

  13. In 31, 36, 45, 60, 83, ?, the differences themselves follow a pattern. Supply the missing value.

    1. 118
    2. 114
    3. 116
    4. 112
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 116

    Explanation: The first differences are 5, 9, 15, 23; their increases are 4, 6, 8. The next increase is 10, so 83 + 33 = 116.

  14. Two familiar power sequences are interwoven: 16, 27, 25, 64, 36, 125, 49, ? . Which value comes next?

    1. 343
    2. 169
    3. 216
    4. 64
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 216

    Explanation: Odd positions contain 4², 5², 6², 7²; even positions contain 3³, 4³, 5³, 6³. The required value is 6³ = 216.

  15. Riya copied a ×4 series as 7, 28, 112, 446, 1792. Which copied number needs correction?

    1. 112
    2. 446
    3. 28
    4. 1792
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 446

    Explanation: 112 × 4 = 448 and 448 × 4 = 1792. Hence 446 is the incorrect entry.

  16. A display alternates two operations: 6, 18, 14, 42, 38, 114, ? . Select the missing reading.

    1. 110
    2. 118
    3. 342
    4. 108
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 110

    Explanation: The operations alternate ×3 and −4. After 38 × 3 = 114, subtract 4 to get 110.

  17. Determine the missing pair in 30, 24, 130, 120, 350, 336, ?, ? by considering paired cube adjustments.

    1. (720, 738)
    2. (738, 720)
    3. (738, 728)
    4. (730, 728)
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) (738, 720)

    Explanation: Each odd base supplies n³ + n followed by n³ − n, using bases 3, 5, 7, then 9. The pair is (9³ + 9, 9³ − 9) = (738, 720).

  18. What replaces ? in 180, 161, 144, 131, 120, ? if successive prime numbers are subtracted in descending order?

    1. 107
    2. 111
    3. 113
    4. 109
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 113

    Explanation: The subtractions are 19, 17, 13, 11, then 7. Hence 120 − 7 = 113.

  19. A decreasing sequence reads 286, 269, ?, 235, 218. Fill its vacant position.

    1. 252
    2. 251
    3. 253
    4. 246
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 252

    Explanation: Subtract 17 at each step. The missing term is 269 − 17 = 252.

  20. Meera divides by a different prime at each step: 30030, 15015, 5005, 1001, 143, ? . Find the next quotient.

    1. 17
    2. 13
    3. 19
    4. 11
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 13

    Explanation: The divisors are consecutive primes 2, 3, 5, 7, 11. The next quotient is 143 ÷ 11 = 13.

  21. Identify the two numbers that follow 4, 90, 7, 82, 11, 72, 18, 60, 29, 46 in the same order.

    1. (47, 30)
    2. (47, 32)
    3. (46, 30)
    4. (58, 32)
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) (47, 30)

    Explanation: Odd positions follow 4, 7, 11, 18, 29, so the next is 18 + 29 = 47. Even positions decrease by 8, 10, 12, 14, then 16, giving 46 − 16 = 30.

  22. The terms 310, 261, 225, 200, 184, ? decrease by a recognisable family of numbers. Choose the next term.

    1. 176
    2. 175
    3. 168
    4. 180
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 175

    Explanation: Subtract 7², 6², 5², 4², then 3². Therefore 184 − 9 = 175.

  23. For the progression 630, 310, 150, 70, 30, ?, which result fits every transition?

    1. 10
    2. 15
    3. 5
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 10

    Explanation: Each step is ÷2 − 5. Thus 30 ÷ 2 − 5 = 10.

  24. Exactly one term is incorrect in 6, 11, 32, 121, 601, 3601. Which term is it?

    1. 11
    2. 601
    3. 121
    4. 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 32

    Explanation: Use ×2 − 1, ×3 − 2, ×4 − 3, ×5 − 4, ×6 − 5. The corrected third term is 11 × 3 − 2 = 31, so 32 is wrong; 31 × 4 − 3 = 121.

  25. Continue the zigzag sequence 50, 53, 47, 59, 35, ?.

    1. 83
    2. 59
    3. 71
    4. 11
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 83

    Explanation: The signed differences are +3, −6, +12, −24, then +48. Hence 35 + 48 = 83.
