What belongs in Study and what does not?

Study holds learning material only — notes, PDFs, revision sheets and the chapters they belong to. Questions live in Practice, papers live in Previous Year Papers, and books live in Books. Nothing in Study will interrupt you with a test.

How do I move through a subject?

Open the subject and you land on its Study Material: every file filed under it under one heading, newest and featured first. Use the search box to narrow it by title or keyword, or choose Browse chapters to walk the category → chapter → part tree when you would rather follow the syllabus than skim a shelf.

Is the material available in Punjabi?

Yes. Each card states the language the file itself is written in, so a Punjabi file reads ਪੰਜਾਬੀ on the card before you open it. Where a note exists in both, neither language is treated as the translation of the other.

Do I have to pay for anything?

No. Every note, PDF and revision sheet here is free, with no login and no paid tier.
