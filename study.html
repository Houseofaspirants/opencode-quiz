What belongs in Study and what does not?

Study holds learning material only — notes, PDFs, revision sheets and the chapters they belong to. Questions live in Practice, papers live in Previous Year Papers, and books live in Books. Nothing in Study will interrupt you with a test.

How do I move through a subject?

Open a subject and choose a language first — the page shows only its language cards until you do. Then you get that language's chapters, then the parts of a chapter in reading order. Each part opens with Read Online, Download, Related Chapters and the part before and after it, so you can read straight through without going back to the shelf.

Is the material available in Punjabi?

Yes, and choosing a language comes before anything else, so a Punjabi reader never passes an English page to reach Punjabi material. Every part states the language it is written in, and where a chapter exists in both, neither language is treated as the translation of the other.

Do I have to pay for anything?

No. Every note, PDF and revision sheet here is free, with no login and no paid tier.
