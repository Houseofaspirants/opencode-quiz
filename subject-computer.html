About this subject

All about Computer

What this page holds and how to work through it.

Computer awareness is where effort converts into marks faster than anywhere else on the paper. The questions are short and factual - what a key does, how a network addresses a device, what a protocol is called, which layer a packet belongs to - and the syllabus, while wide, repeats itself heavily from paper to paper.

Because the questions are factual, the method that works is exposure: attempt, read the explanation, repeat. Fifteen minutes a day across MS Office, internet and networking, operating systems, AI basics and cyber security will outperform a weekend of reading, and every set you finish here saves a page of notes you would otherwise have to write.

Computer on House of Aspirants publishes as separate, self-contained sets rather than one endless paper. Each set runs 30 seconds per question with four options and a written explanation, so a wrong answer teaches the next attempt instead of only scoring it.

Work it in three moves: open the daily quiz (20 questions) to find the lane that is costing you marks, run a topic set when you want depth, and keep a full mock test for the weekend. A set is cleared at 40% accuracy, and your subject-wise accuracy is tracked on the progress page.

A wrong answer lands in your bookmarks together with its explanation, so revision becomes a second pass over the same set instead of a separate chapter of notes.
