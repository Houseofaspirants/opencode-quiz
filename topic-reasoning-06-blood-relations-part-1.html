Question bank

Blood Relations MCQ – Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Ravinder is the father of a girl, Mahi. Mahi has a younger brother, Tej. Identify Ravinder’s relationship to Tej.

    1. Maternal uncle
    2. Father
    3. Brother
    4. Grandfather
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Father

    Explanation: Mahi and Tej are siblings, so their father is Ravinder.

  2. At a Ludhiana exhibition, a woman, Harleen, points to a boy: “His mother is my father’s younger sister.” Which description fits the boy?

    1. Son of her paternal aunt
    2. Her nephew through her brother
    3. Her own son
    4. Son of her maternal uncle
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Son of her paternal aunt

    Explanation: The boy’s mother is Harleen’s paternal aunt; he is her paternal-aunt’s son.

  3. In a code, X @ Y means X is Y’s mother, and X # Y means X is Y’s brother. In L @ M # N, what is L to N?

    1. Daughter
    2. Mother
    3. Sister
    4. Maternal aunt
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Mother

    Explanation: L is M’s mother, and M and N are siblings; therefore L is N’s mother.

  4. Gurpreet is a man whose wife is Jasleen. Jasleen’s younger brother is Arjun. What is Arjun’s relationship to Gurpreet?

    1. Son-in-law
    2. Younger brother
    3. Wife’s brother
    4. Sister’s husband
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Wife’s brother

    Explanation: Arjun is the brother of Gurpreet’s wife, so he is Gurpreet’s brother-in-law through his wife.

  5. Seven relatives are Baldev, Rani, Karan, Neha, Pooja, Aman and Tara. Baldev is Rani’s husband. Neha is their daughter and Aman’s paternal aunt. Pooja is Baldev’s daughter-in-law and Tara is Neha’s daughter. Karan is Aman’s father. There are exactly two married couples, and all named parents of a child are married. Which pair consists of first cousins?

    1. Pooja and Neha
    2. Baldev and Aman
    3. Karan and Neha
    4. Aman and Tara
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Aman and Tara

    Explanation: Neha is the sister of Aman’s father Karan; her daughter Tara and Aman are first cousins.

  6. P is the parent of Q. Q is the mother of a girl, R. Which fact about P follows with certainty?

    1. P is R’s mother
    2. P is R’s paternal grandmother
    3. P is R’s grandfather
    4. P is R’s maternal grandparent
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) P is R’s maternal grandparent

    Explanation: The route from R is mother Q, then Q’s parent P; P’s gender is unspecified.

  7. Code: X + Y means X is Y’s father; X - Y means X is Y’s sister; X / Y means X is Y’s mother. Which expression makes A the maternal aunt of D’s father?

    1. D + C / B - A
    2. A - B / C + D
    3. A / B - C + D
    4. A - B + C + D
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) A - B / C + D

    Explanation: D’s father is C; C’s mother is B, whose sister is A.

  8. A girl, Diya, is the daughter of Sonal’s brother. Choose the precise relationship of Diya to Sonal, a woman.

    1. Sister’s daughter
    2. Daughter
    3. Younger sister
    4. Brother’s daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Brother’s daughter

    Explanation: The middle link is Sonal’s brother; his daughter is Sonal’s niece through her brother.

  9. A family tree is described in words: Mohan (male) and Usha (female) are married. Their children are Dev (male) and Leena (female). Dev and Asha (female) have a daughter, Pari. Who is Pari’s paternal grandmother?

    1. Leena
    2. Usha
    3. Pari
    4. Asha
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Usha

    Explanation: Pari’s father is Dev, and Dev’s mother is Usha.

  10. Nitin, a man, introduces an elderly woman: “She is the mother of my mother’s only daughter.” What should Nitin call her?

    1. Maternal grandmother
    2. Sister
    3. Mother-in-law
    4. Mother
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Mother

    Explanation: Nitin’s mother’s only daughter is his sister; that sister’s mother is Nitin’s own mother.

  11. Question: Is A the maternal aunt of boy D? I. A is sister of B; B is a parent of C; C is D’s father. II. B is female. Do these statements settle the question, whether the answer is yes or no?

    1. I alone settles it
    2. Even together they do not settle it
    3. II alone settles it
    4. Only both together settle it
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) I alone settles it

    Explanation: I places A two generations above D on his father’s side, so she is not his maternal aunt; B’s gender is unnecessary.

  12. Meera’s husband is the only son of Kamla, a woman. Kamla’s daughter Kavita has a son, Rohit. Select Rohit’s relationship to Meera’s husband.

    1. Wife’s brother
    2. Brother’s son
    3. Sister’s son
    4. Son
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Sister’s son

    Explanation: Kavita is Meera’s husband’s sister, so Kavita’s son is his nephew through his sister.

  13. The code X * Y means X is Y’s wife, X % Y means X is Y’s father, and X & Y means X is Y’s daughter. Decode P * Q % R & P. Which person is certainly R’s mother?

    1. R
    2. Cannot be determined
    3. P
    4. Q
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) P

    Explanation: R is P’s daughter, and P is female because P is Q’s wife; P is R’s mother.

  14. At a family meal, a woman attends with her daughter and that daughter’s daughter. Counting only these three, how many are daughters of another person present?

    1. 3
    2. 2
    3. 0
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2

    Explanation: The middle woman is the eldest woman’s daughter, and the youngest is the middle woman’s daughter.

  15. Aman’s father has a younger brother, Hardeep. Hardeep’s wife is Rupinder. Which relation connects Rupinder to Aman?

    1. Mother’s sister
    2. Father’s sister
    3. Father’s younger brother’s wife
    4. Mother’s brother’s wife
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Father’s younger brother’s wife

    Explanation: Hardeep is Aman’s paternal uncle, younger than his father; Hardeep’s wife is his paternal aunt by marriage.

  16. A man, Vikram, points to a woman’s photograph: “Her only daughter’s husband is the father of my wife’s only brother.” Vikram’s wife and her brother have the same parents, who are married. The photographed woman is Vikram’s wife’s _____.

    1. Paternal grandmother
    2. Mother
    3. Paternal aunt
    4. Maternal grandmother
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Maternal grandmother

    Explanation: The daughter’s husband is the wife’s father, so the daughter is the wife’s mother; the photographed woman is her maternal grandmother.

  17. X > Y means X is Y’s son; X = Y means X is Y’s wife; X < Y means X is Y’s brother. Which expression proves M is the mother of N’s father?

    1. M = N > P > Q
    2. N > P = M < Q
    3. N > P < M = Q
    4. N > P > M = Q
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) N > P > M = Q

    Explanation: P is N’s male parent because P is M’s son; M is P’s female parent because M is Q’s wife.

  18. Eight relatives are Iqbal, Farida, Salim, Imran, Nisha, Reena, Zoya and Kabir. Iqbal and Farida are married parents of brothers Salim and Imran. Nisha and Reena are their daughters-in-law. Zoya’s father is Imran; Kabir’s mother is Nisha. Zoya and Kabir are cousins. All children’s parents are named and married. Who is Zoya’s mother?

    1. Reena
    2. Cannot be determined
    3. Nisha
    4. Farida
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Reena

    Explanation: Cousins have different fathers here: Kabir’s father is Salim, married to Nisha; Imran’s wife and Zoya’s mother is Reena.

  19. A is B’s mother, B is C’s father, and C is D’s mother. Relative to D’s generation, where is A placed?

    1. One generation above
    2. The same generation
    3. Three generations above
    4. Two generations above
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Three generations above

    Explanation: From D, move up to C, then B, then A: three parent links.

  20. A tree described in words has married parents Ajay (male) and Seema (female), their son Raghav and daughter Anu, Anu’s husband Vikas, and their son Yuvraj. A student writes four labels. Which label reverses the relationship incorrectly?

    1. Yuvraj is Raghav’s maternal uncle
    2. Anu is Yuvraj’s mother
    3. Ajay is Anu’s father
    4. Seema is Yuvraj’s maternal grandmother
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Yuvraj is Raghav’s maternal uncle

    Explanation: Raghav is the brother of Yuvraj’s mother; Yuvraj is Raghav’s sister’s son, not his maternal uncle.

  21. A woman, Pritam, has an elder son Jatin and a younger son Naman. Jatin is married to Ritu, and Naman to Simmi. What is Ritu to Simmi?

    1. Husband’s elder brother’s wife
    2. Brother’s wife
    3. Husband’s sister
    4. Husband’s younger brother’s wife
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Husband’s elder brother’s wife

    Explanation: Simmi’s husband is Naman; his elder brother Jatin’s wife Ritu is her elder co-sister-in-law.

  22. A is B’s brother; B is C’s mother; C is a girl. Conclusion I: A is C’s maternal uncle. Conclusion II: C is A’s sister’s daughter. Choose the valid assessment.

    1. Only I follows
    2. Both I and II follow
    3. Only II follows
    4. Neither I nor II follows
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Both I and II follow

    Explanation: A is the brother of C’s mother B; conversely C is the daughter of A’s sister.

  23. Use X + Y for X is Y’s daughter, X $ Y for X is Y’s husband, and X # Y for X is Y’s sister. In T + U $ V # W, identify T’s father.

    1. U
    2. W
    3. V
    4. T
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) U

    Explanation: T is U’s daughter, and U is male because U is V’s husband; U is T’s father.

  24. The father of a boy, Laksh, is the brother of Rhea’s mother. Rhea is a girl. Which description correctly gives Rhea’s link to Laksh?

    1. Father’s sister’s daughter
    2. Mother’s brother’s daughter
    3. Sister’s daughter
    4. Father’s brother’s daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Father’s sister’s daughter

    Explanation: Rhea’s mother is Laksh’s father’s sister, so Rhea is Laksh’s paternal-aunt’s daughter.

  25. At an Amritsar wedding, woman Navjot introduces a girl: “Her father is the son of the only daughter of my mother. I am that only daughter, and I have only one son.” Navjot’s son’s wife is the girl’s mother. Which relationship does Navjot have to the girl?

    1. Mother-in-law
    2. Paternal aunt
    3. Paternal grandmother
    4. Maternal grandmother
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Paternal grandmother

    Explanation: The only daughter is Navjot, so the girl’s father is Navjot’s son; Navjot is her paternal grandmother.
