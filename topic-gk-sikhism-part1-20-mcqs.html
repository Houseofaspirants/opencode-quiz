  1. Guru Nanak Dev Ji was born on which date?

    1. 15 April 1469
    2. 15 May 1469
    3. 7 September 1539
    4. 31 March 1504
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 15 April 1469

    Explanation: The notes state that Guru Nanak Dev Ji was born on 15 April 1469.

  2. Guru Nanak Dev Ji was born at:

    1. Kiratpur Sahib
    2. Talwandi Rai Bhoi Ki
    3. Goindwal Sahib
    4. Khadur Sahib
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Talwandi Rai Bhoi Ki

    Explanation: The notes say he was born at Talwandi Rai Bhoi Ki, now Nankana Sahib.

  3. Guru Nanak Dev Ji's parents were:

    1. Hari Das and Daya Kaur
    2. Mehta Kalu and Mata Tripta
    3. Guru Ram Das and Bhani Ji
    4. Baba Gurditta and Ananti
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Mehta Kalu and Mata Tripta

    Explanation: The notes list Mehta Kalu and Mata Tripta as his parents.

  4. Guru Nanak Dev Ji's wife was:

    1. Khivi Ji
    2. Sulakhani Ji
    3. Mata Gujri Ji
    4. Bhani Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Sulakhani Ji

    Explanation: Sulakhani Ji is mentioned as Guru Nanak Dev Ji's wife.

  5. Guru Nanak Dev Ji founded which city?

    1. Amritsar
    2. Kartarpur
    3. Anandpur Sahib
    4. Goindwal Sahib
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Kartarpur

    Explanation: Kartarpur is listed as the city founded by Guru Nanak Dev Ji.

  6. Guru Nanak Dev Ji attained Jyoti Jot on:

    1. 16 August 1604
    2. 7 September 1539
    3. 11 November 1675
    4. 30 March 1699
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 7 September 1539

    Explanation: The notes mention Jyoti Jot on 7 September 1539 at Kartarpur Sahib.

  7. Which companion of Guru Nanak Dev Ji is identified as Muslim in the notes?

    1. Bhai Bala Ji
    2. Bhai Mardana Ji
    3. Bhai Gurdas Ji
    4. Bhai Jaita Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Bhai Mardana Ji

    Explanation: Bhai Mardana Ji is noted as Muslim.

  8. Guru Nanak Dev Ji spent how many years in missionary tours?

    1. 10 years
    2. 15 years
    3. 20 years
    4. 25 years
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 20 years

    Explanation: The notes say he spent 20 years in four lengthy journeys (Udasis).

  9. Which of the following was visited by Guru Nanak Dev Ji according to the notes?

    1. Rameshwaram
    2. Kedarnath
    3. Sarnath
    4. Puri only
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Rameshwaram

    Explanation: Rameshwaram is one of the places listed as visited by him.

  10. Langar Pratha was introduced by Guru Nanak Dev Ji at:

    1. Goindwal Sahib
    2. Khadur Sahib
    3. Kartarpur
    4. Amritsar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Kartarpur

    Explanation: The notes say the community kitchen system was introduced at Kartarpur.

  11. The marriage anniversary tradition of Guru Nanak Dev Ji and Sulakhni Mata is popularly called:

    1. Hola Mohalla
    2. Babe Da Viah
    3. Bandichhor Diwas
    4. Akhand Path
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Babe Da Viah

    Explanation: The notes refer to the wedding anniversary tradition as 'Babe Da Viah'.

  12. According to the notes, Guru Nanak Dev Ji married Sulakhni by taking how many rounds around the sacred fire?

    1. Two
    2. Four
    3. Seven
    4. Nine
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Four

    Explanation: The PDF says he took four rounds instead of seven.

  13. Guru Nanak Dev Ji's compositions in the notes total:

    1. 638 hymns
    2. 974 hymns
    3. 1,430 hymns
    4. 52 hymns
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 974 hymns

    Explanation: The notes list a total of 974 hymns (Shabad).

  14. Which of these is listed as a composition of Guru Nanak Dev Ji?

    1. Sukhmani Sahib
    2. Japji Sahib
    3. Zafarnamah
    4. Anand Sahib
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Japji Sahib

    Explanation: Japji Sahib is listed under Guru Nanak Dev Ji's compositions.

  15. Sidh Gosht in the notes refers to:

    1. Marriage hymns
    2. Teachings and conversations with Siddhas
    3. Martyrdom of the Gurus
    4. A battlefield hymn
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Teachings and conversations with Siddhas

    Explanation: The notes explain that Sidh Gosht contains teachings and conversations with Siddhas.

  16. Guru Angad Dev Ji's original name was:

    1. Bhai Lehna Ji
    2. Bhai Jetha Ji
    3. Bhai Mardana Ji
    4. Bhai Bala Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Bhai Lehna Ji

    Explanation: The notes identify Bhai Lehna Ji as Guru Angad Dev Ji's original name.

  17. Guru Angad Dev Ji was born on:

    1. 31 March 1504
    2. 5 May 1479
    3. 24 September 1534
    4. 19 June 1595
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 31 March 1504

    Explanation: The notes give 31 March 1504 as his birth date.

  18. Guru Angad Dev Ji standardized which script?

    1. Persian
    2. Gurmukhi
    3. Devanagari
    4. Shahmukhi
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Gurmukhi

    Explanation: He is credited with standardizing the Punjabi script, Gurmukhi.

  19. Which institution did Guru Angad Dev Ji establish for physical and spiritual exercise?

    1. Akal Takht
    2. Mal Akhara

    4. Aurangzeb

  22. Guru Amardas Ji was born in which family?

    1. Sodhi family

    4. Khatri family
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Bhalla family

  23. Guru Amardas Ji was born at:

    1. Basarke (Amritsar)

  24. Guru Amardas Ji's wife was:

    1. Mansa Devi
    2. Khivi Ji
    3. Mata Bhani
    4. Mata Gujri Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Mansa Devi

    Explanation: Mansa Devi is listed as Guru Amardas Ji's wife.

  25. Which reform is associated with Guru Amardas Ji?

    1. Abolished Sati and Purdah
    2. Founded Kartarpur
    3. Created Panj Pyare
    4. Wrote Zafarnamah
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Abolished Sati and Purdah

    Explanation: The notes say he abolished Sati and the Purdah system.

  26. Guru Amardas Ji made which system integral with the principle 'Pangat must precede Sangat'?

    1. Langar System
    2. Masand System
    3. Miri and Piri
    4. Gatka
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Langar System

    Explanation: The notes say he made the Langar system integral.

  27. How many Manjis were established by Guru Amardas Ji?

    3. 22
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 22

    Explanation: The notes mention the 22 Manji system.

  28. Guru Amardas Ji founded:

    1. Goindwal Sahib
    2. Amritsar
    3. Kiratpur Sahib
    4. Tarn Taran
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Goindwal Sahib

    Explanation: Goindwal Sahib (Bauli Sahib) is listed as his founded city.

  29. Which Mughal emperor visited Goindwal Sahib and gifted land to Bibi Bhani Ji?

    1. Babur
    2. Humayun
    3. Akbar
    4. Jahangir
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Akbar

    Explanation: The notes state that Emperor Akbar visited Goindwal Sahib and gifted land to Bibi Bhani Ji.

    1. Anand Sahib
    2. Japji Sahib
