06 Subject Verb Agreement Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Choose the correct simple present form: One of the hand pumps near the bus stand _____ contaminated water.

    1. supply
    2. have supplied
    3. supplies
    4. are supplying
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) supplies

    Explanation: 'One' is the subject and takes the singular verb 'supplies'. The plural noun 'pumps' after 'of' does not control agreement.

  2. Choose the pair that correctly completes the sentence in the simple present: Neither of the two passwords _____ access to the server, but either of the backup codes _____.

    1. allow; work
    2. allows; work
    3. allow; works
    4. allows; works
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) allows; works

    Explanation: In formal examination usage, 'neither of' and 'either of' take singular verbs, giving 'allows' and 'works'. The plural nouns following 'of' do not control agreement.

  3. Choose the correct simple present form: A pair of binoculars _____ available at the birdwatching centre.

    1. are
    2. is
    3. were
    4. have been
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) is

    Explanation: The singular head noun 'pair' requires 'is'. The plural form 'binoculars' does not control the verb in this phrase.

  4. Choose the pair that correctly completes the sentence in the simple present: I am the person who _____ the hostel accounts, and you are the person who _____ the monthly statement.

    1. maintain; approve
    2. maintains; approve
    3. maintains; approves
    4. maintain; approves
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) maintains; approves

    Explanation: In both relative clauses, 'who' has the singular antecedent 'the person', requiring 'maintains' and 'approves'. The earlier pronouns 'I' and 'you' do not control these relative-clause verbs.

  5. Choose the correct simple present form: Each of the pedestrian bridges _____ a separate safety inspection before the fair opens.

    1. undergo
    2. have undergone
    3. are undergoing
    4. undergoes
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) undergoes

    Explanation: 'Each' is the singular subject and requires 'undergoes'. The plural 'bridges' in the following phrase does not determine agreement.

  6. Choose the correct simple present form: The news about the scholarship results _____ encouraging.

    1. are
    2. were
    3. is
    4. have been
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) is

    Explanation: News is singular in meaning and takes 'is' despite ending in 's'. 'Are' incorrectly treats its spelling as evidence of plurality.

  7. Choose the correct simple present form: These scissors _____ sharpening before the tailoring workshop begins.

    1. needs
    2. has needed
    3. need
    4. is needing
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) need

    Explanation: Scissors takes a plural verb when used without a singular expression such as 'a pair of'. Therefore, 'need' is correct.

  8. Choose the pair that correctly completes the sentence in the simple present: Two thousand rupees _____ the fixed fee for the course, and the fees collected from all batches _____ the cost of materials.

    1. are; covers
    2. is; cover
    3. is; covers
    4. are; cover
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) is; cover

    Explanation: A sum of money treated as one amount takes singular 'is'. The separate plural subject 'fees' requires 'cover', even though both clauses concern money.

  9. Choose the correct simple present form: Many a farmer _____ on this cooperative for affordable seeds.

    1. depend
    2. depends
    3. are depending
    4. have depended
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) depends

    Explanation: 'Many a' takes a singular noun and a singular verb, so 'depends' is required. Its plural sense does not permit 'depend' in this construction.

  10. Choose the pair that correctly completes the sentence in the simple present: Not only the dentist but also the nurses _____ protective masks, and not only the nurses but also the dentist _____ attendance electronically.

    1. wear; records
    2. wears; record
    3. wear; record
    4. wears; records
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) wear; records

    Explanation: With 'not only...but also', the verb agrees with the nearer subject. Plural 'nurses' requires 'wear', while singular 'dentist' requires 'records'.

  11. Choose the correct simple present form: Neither the laboratory assistants nor the technician _____ permission to handle the chemical without supervision.

    1. has
    2. have
    3. are having
    4. have had
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) has

    Explanation: With 'neither...nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject, the singular 'technician'. Therefore, 'has' is required despite the plural first subject.

  12. Choose the pair that correctly completes the sentence in the simple present: Mehak is one of the architects who _____ accessible buildings, and the ramp in her latest project _____ wheelchair users to enter independently.

    1. design; enables
    2. designs; enable
    3. designs; enables
    4. design; enable
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) design; enables

    Explanation: 'Who' refers to plural 'architects', so 'design' is required. The subject of the second clause is singular 'ramp', which takes 'enables'.

  13. Choose the correct simple present form: The unemployed _____ access to suitable vocational training.

    1. needs
    2. has needed
    3. is needing
    4. need
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) need

    Explanation: 'The unemployed' means unemployed people and takes a plural verb. Hence 'need' is correct, whereas 'needs' treats the expression as singular.

  14. Choose the correct simple present form: The advice offered by the career counsellor _____ practical.

    1. is
    2. are
    3. were
    4. have been
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) is

    Explanation: Advice is an uncountable noun and takes the singular verb 'is'. Its reference to potentially several suggestions does not justify 'are'.

  15. Choose the correct simple present form: There _____ little drinking water left in the tank.

    1. are
    2. is
    3. were
    4. have been
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) is

    Explanation: The verb agrees with 'water', an uncountable singular noun following 'there'. Thus 'is' is required even though the sentence concerns a quantity.

  16. Choose the correct simple present form: The librarian, together with the student representatives, _____ the reading-room timetable.

    1. prepare
    2. have prepared
    3. are preparing
    4. prepares
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) prepares

    Explanation: 'Together with' introduces an additional phrase without making the subject plural. The singular 'librarian' therefore takes 'prepares'.

  17. Choose the correct simple present form: The district magistrate, in addition to the municipal officers, _____ the sanitation drive every fortnight.

    1. review
    2. reviews
    3. are reviewing
    4. have reviewed
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) reviews

    Explanation: 'In addition to' does not join subjects in the way 'and' does. The singular subject 'district magistrate' requires 'reviews'.

  18. Choose the correct simple present form: All of the flour stored in these sacks _____ to the community kitchen.

    1. belongs
    2. belong
    3. are belonging
    4. have belonged
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) belongs

    Explanation: With 'all of', agreement depends on the following noun. Uncountable 'flour' takes 'belongs'; the intervening plural 'sacks' does not change that.

  19. Choose the correct simple present form: Either the village head or the residents _____ the irrigation department whenever the canal overflows.

    1. informs
    2. has informed
    3. inform
    4. is informing
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) inform

    Explanation: The nearer subject in the 'either...or' construction is plural 'residents', so 'inform' is required. The singular first subject does not control the verb.

  20. Using British English collective-noun agreement, choose the pair that matches the stated meanings in the simple present: The team, functioning as one unit, _____ a common strategy; the same team, considered as individual players, _____ their own travel arrangements.

    1. follow; makes
    2. follows; makes
    3. follows; make
    4. follow; make
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) follows; make

    Explanation: A collective noun viewed as a unit takes singular 'follows'. When its members are explicitly considered individually, British English uses plural agreement, giving 'make'.

  21. Choose the correct simple present form: 'The Arabian Nights' _____ several stories familiar to young readers.

    1. contains
    2. contain
    3. are containing
    4. have contained
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) contains

    Explanation: The title names a single work and therefore takes 'contains'. The plural word 'Nights' within the title does not require a plural verb.

  22. Choose the pair that correctly completes the sentence in the simple present: The number of online transactions _____ steadily, while a number of customers _____ cash payments.

    1. increase; prefers
    2. increase; prefer
    3. increases; prefers
    4. increases; prefer
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) increases; prefer

    Explanation: 'The number of' takes singular 'increases', whereas 'a number of' means several and takes plural 'prefer'. Treating both expressions alike produces an agreement error.

  23. Choose the pair that correctly completes the sentence in the simple present: More than one mural _____ the station walls, but more murals than one _____ signs of water damage.

    1. decorate; shows
    2. decorates; shows
    3. decorates; show
    4. decorate; show
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) decorates; show

    Explanation: 'More than one' followed by a singular noun takes singular 'decorates'. In the differently structured second clause, the subject is plural 'murals', requiring 'show'.

  24. Choose the correct simple present form: Five years _____ the minimum service required for this promotion.

    1. are
    2. is
    3. were
    4. have been
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) is

    Explanation: A period considered as one total duration takes a singular verb. Here, five years represents one service requirement, so 'is' is correct.

  25. Choose the correct simple present form: Physics _____ part of the entrance-test syllabus.

    1. are
    2. were
    3. have been
    4. is
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) is

    Explanation: Physics names an academic subject and takes a singular verb. Its final 's' does not make 'are' appropriate.
