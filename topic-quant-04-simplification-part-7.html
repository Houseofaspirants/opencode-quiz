Question bank

Simplification MCQ – Part 7: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. The block 27 repeats endlessly in 0.272727… . What is 11 times this number plus 0.75?

    1. 3.75
    2. 3.25
    3. 30.75
    4. 2.75
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 3.75

    Explanation: The recurring value is 27/99 = 3/11; hence 11 × (3/11) + 0.75 = 3.75.

  2. Evaluate: 98 × 75.

    1. 7250
    2. 7500
    3. 7450
    4. 7350
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 7350

    Explanation: Use 75 = 3 × 100/4: 98 × 3 × 25 = 7350.

  3. Find the sum of 1/(√k + √(k + 1)) for every integer k from 8 to 48 inclusive.

    1. 7 − √2
    2. 5
    3. 7 − 2√2
    4. 7 + 2√2
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 7 − 2√2

    Explanation: Rationalise each term to √(k + 1) − √k; cancellation leaves √49 − √8 = 7 − 2√2.

  4. ₹7200 is shared equally among some volunteers. With 6 more volunteers, each would receive ₹60 less. How many volunteers were there originally?

    1. 18
    2. 24
    3. 36
    4. 30
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 24

    Explanation: Let the original count be n: 7200/n − 7200/(n + 6) = 60 gives n(n + 6) = 720 = 24 × 30. Thus n = 24.

  5. Find the value of ? in 17 × 36 − ? × 12 = 228.

    1. 19
    2. 32
    3. 30
    4. 51
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 32

    Explanation: Subtract 228 from 612 and divide by 12: ? = 384 ÷ 12 = 32.

  6. If 3^(x + 2) − 3^x = 648, find 2^x.

    1. 16
    2. 4
    3. 32
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 16

    Explanation: Factor 3^x: 8 × 3^x = 648, so 3^x = 81 and x = 4; hence 2^x = 16.

  7. Evaluate: 998 × 997.

    1. 996006
    2. 995000
    3. 995600
    4. 995006
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 995006

    Explanation: Relative to 1000, subtract 2 + 3 and add 2 × 3: 995000 + 6 = 995006.

  8. Find the value of ? in 315 + ? ÷ 14 = 402.

    1. 1134
    2. 1302
    3. 1218
    4. 87
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1218

    Explanation: The quotient is 402 − 315 = 87; multiply by 14 to obtain 1218.

  9. If u − v = 8 and uv = 48, find (u² + v²)/(u + v)².

    1. 5/3
    2. 2/5
    3. 3/8
    4. 5/8
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 5/8

    Explanation: The numerator is 8² + 2 × 48 = 160, while the denominator is 8² + 4 × 48 = 256; reduce to 5/8.

  10. Evaluate: 28 × 6 − 180 ÷ 9 + 42 ÷ 7 × 3.

    1. 166
    2. 150
    3. 130
    4. 178
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 166

    Explanation: Group the calculated terms: 168 − 20 + 18 = 166.

  11. Find the value of 240% of 35.

    1. 840
    2. 84
    3. 74
    4. 8.4
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 84

    Explanation: 240% = 2.4; multiply 35 × 24 and divide by 10 to get 84.

  12. Evaluate: 7/9 − [(5/6 − 1/3) × 4/9].

    1. 7/18
    2. 1/3
    3. 5/9
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5/9

    Explanation: The inner difference is 1/2; hence 7/9 − 2/9 = 5/9.

  13. A fruit seller sells 1/4 of his oranges, discards 1/9 of the remainder, then sells half of what is left. If 84 remain, how many oranges were discarded?

    1. 21
    2. 42
    3. 63
    4. 28
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 21

    Explanation: Before the final sale, 168 remained; this is 8/9 of the quantity before discarding. The discarded 1/9 equals 168 ÷ 8 = 21.

  14. Evaluate: 720 ÷ [(16 − 7) × {12 − (3 + 5)}] − 9.

    1. 71
    2. 9
    3. 11
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 11

    Explanation: The divisor is 9 × 4 = 36; hence 720 ÷ 36 − 9 = 11.

  15. Find the value of 33⅓% of 81 + 66⅔% of 54.

    1. 45
    2. 63
    3. 72
    4. 54
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 63

    Explanation: Use 1/3 and 2/3: 27 + 36 = 63.

  16. Find [1/(3 − √7) − 1/(3 + √7)] × √7.

    1. 21
    2. 3
    3. 14
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 7

    Explanation: The bracket becomes 2√7/(9 − 7) = √7; multiplying by √7 gives 7.

  17. Of 100 trainees, 58 passed arithmetic and 63 passed reasoning. If 14 failed both, how many passed both?

    1. 35
    2. 37
    3. 21
    4. 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 35

    Explanation: At least one pass = 100 − 14 = 86; both = 58 + 63 − 86 = 35.

  18. Jaspreet buys 30 kg of two rice varieties costing ₹42 and ₹48 per kg. The total bill is ₹1350. How many kg of the ₹42 variety did he buy?

    1. 10
    2. 25
    3. 15
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 15

    Explanation: If all cost ₹48, the bill would be ₹1440. Each kg of cheaper rice saves ₹6; hence the quantity is (1440 − 1350) ÷ 6 = 15 kg.

  19. Evaluate: |−32| ÷ (−4) − (−7).

    1. 15
    2. 1
    3. -15
    4. -1
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) -1

    Explanation: The absolute value is 32; thus −8 + 7 = −1.

  20. Evaluate: (2 + 1/(3 + 1/(4 + 1/2))) − 2.

    1. 2/7
    2. 9/29
    3. 7/23
    4. 29/9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 9/29

    Explanation: Subtracting 2 leaves the continued fraction: 1/(3 + 2/9) = 9/29.

  21. Which option is closest to 14.98% of 798?

    1. 130
    2. 100
    3. 110
    4. 120
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 120

    Explanation: Estimate 15% of 800 = 120; the actual value is 119.5404, nearest to 120.

  22. Evaluate: ∛0.000216 × 500.

    1. 60
    2. 30
    3. 108
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 30

    Explanation: ∛0.000216 = 0.06; hence 0.06 × 500 = 30.

  23. Define a ◇ b = ab + a − b. Find (6 ◇ 4) − (4 ◇ 6).

    1. 4
    2. 8
    3. 2
    4. 0
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 4

    Explanation: The common product cancels: (24 + 2) − (24 − 2) = 4.

  24. Find (28³ − 13³)/(28 − 13) − 28 × 13.

    1. 589
    2. 1317
    3. 953
    4. 225
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 953

    Explanation: The quotient is 28² + 28 × 13 + 13²; subtract the middle term to get 784 + 169 = 953.

  25. Evaluate: (1 + 5/7) × (2 + 1/3) ÷ (4 + 4/5).

    1. 5/3
    2. 7/6
    3. 5/6
    4. 6/5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5/6

    Explanation: The first product is (12/7) × (7/3) = 4; then 4 × (5/24) = 5/6.
