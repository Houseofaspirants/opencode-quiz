Question bank

Decimal Fractions MCQ – Part 4: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Write 4 + 7/100 + 9/10000 as a decimal.

    1. 4.079
    2. 4.0709
    3. 4.0079
    4. 4.709
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 4.0709

    Explanation: Align the place values: 4 + 0.07 + 0.0009 = 4.0709.

  2. What is the difference between 0.(7), where 7 repeats, and 0.7?

    1. 1/90
    2. 7/9
    3. 7/100
    4. 7/90
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 7/90

    Explanation: The difference is 7/9 − 7/10 = 7/90.

  3. If a = 0.85 and b = 0.15, evaluate (a² + b²)² − (a² − b²)².

    1. 0.2601
    2. 0.0325125
    3. 0.065025
    4. 0.65025
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 0.065025

    Explanation: Use (u + v)² − (u − v)² = 4uv with u = a² and v = b²: 4 × (0.85 × 0.15)² = 0.065025.

  4. Which statement is true?

    1. 0.375 < 5/13 < 0.39
    2. 0.39 < 5/13 < 0.375
    3. 0.375 < 0.39 < 5/13
    4. 5/13 < 0.375 < 0.39
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 0.375 < 5/13 < 0.39

    Explanation: 5/13 is about 0.384615, so it lies between 0.375 and 0.39.

  5. By what number must 7.35 be divided to obtain 0.0735?

    1. 0.01
    2. 10
    3. 1000
    4. 100
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 100

    Explanation: The decimal point moves 2 places left, so the divisor is 100.

  6. A parcel weighs 3.65 kg including packaging of 0.275 kg. What is the total mass of the contents of 8 identical parcels, in kg?

    1. 29.2
    2. 27
    3. 26.8
    4. 31.4
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 27

    Explanation: Contents per parcel = 3.65 − 0.275 = 3.375 kg; total = 3.375 × 8 = 27 kg.

  7. Data sufficiency: How many litres of petrol did Aman buy? I. His bill was ₹971.25 after a reduction of ₹4.50 per litre from the displayed rate of ₹97 per litre. II. After he used 0.4 of the petrol bought, 6.3 litres remained.

    1. Each statement alone is sufficient.
    2. Statement II alone is sufficient; I alone is not.
    3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
    4. Statement I alone is sufficient; II alone is not.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Each statement alone is sufficient.

    Explanation: I gives 971.25/(97 − 4.50) = 10.5 litres. II gives 6.3/(1 − 0.4) = 10.5 litres, so each statement alone is sufficient.

  8. Evaluate 0.32 ÷ 0.008 × 0.15.

    1. 60
    2. 800/3
    3. 6
    4. 0.6
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 6

    Explanation: Perform division and multiplication from left to right: 0.32 ÷ 0.008 = 40, then 40 × 0.15 = 6.

  9. A number becomes 0.6 times its original value. What is its percentage decrease?

    1. 60
    2. 6
    3. 40
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 40

    Explanation: Decrease = (1 − 0.6) × 100 = 40%.

  10. If 0.001875 = p/q in lowest terms, find q − p.

    1. 1597
    2. 797
    3. 1599
    4. 1603
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1597

    Explanation: 0.001875 = 1875/1000000 = 3/1600, so q − p = 1600 − 3 = 1597.

  11. If x = 0.12(3), where only 3 repeats, find the smallest positive integer k such that kx is an integer.

    1. 30
    2. 300
    3. 900
    4. 1000
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 300

    Explanation: x = (123 − 12)/900 = 37/300. Since 37 and 300 are coprime, the smallest k is 300.

  12. Find x: (7.84 − x) ÷ 0.16 = 28.5.

    1. 4.56
    2. 12.4
    3. 3.38
    4. 3.28
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 3.28

    Explanation: 7.84 − x = 28.5 × 0.16 = 4.56, so x = 7.84 − 4.56 = 3.28.

  13. Which number is greater than 0.4 and less than 0.41?

    1. 81/200
    2. 41/100
    3. 9/20
    4. 2/5
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 81/200

    Explanation: 81/200 = 0.405, and 0.4 < 0.405 < 0.41.

  14. Evaluate 4.8 × 1.25 − 2.4 × 0.75.

    1. 0.42
    2. 7.8
    3. 4.2
    4. 3.6
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 4.2

    Explanation: 4.8 × 1.25 = 6 and 2.4 × 0.75 = 1.8, so the difference is 4.2.

  15. Rounded to the nearest tenth, what is (39.8 × 0.602) ÷ 1.21?

    1. 20.0
    2. 1.98
    3. 198.0
    4. 19.8
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 19.8

    Explanation: The quotient is about 19.8013, so rounding to the nearest tenth gives 19.8.

  16. Two couriers charge ₹42.75 and ₹51.15 for the first kg. Their charges rise by ₹3.60 and ₹2.40 respectively for every additional kg. At what total mass, in kg, are their charges equal?

    1. 6
    2. 8
    3. 9
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 8

    Explanation: The initial gap is 51.15 − 42.75 = 8.4 and closes by 3.60 − 2.40 = 1.2 per extra kg. Extra mass = 8.4/1.2 = 7 kg, so total mass = 1 + 7 = 8 kg.

  17. How many digits are in the shortest repeating block of the decimal expansion of 1/11?

    1. 4
    2. 2
    3. 1
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2

    Explanation: 1/11 = 0.(09), so the shortest repeating block is 09 and contains 2 digits.

  18. A fraction has value 0.48. Its numerator is increased by 25% and its denominator by 20%. What is the new value?

    1. 0.576
    2. 0.6
    3. 0.46
    4. 0.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 0.5

    Explanation: New value = 0.48 × 1.25/1.20 = 0.48 × 25/24 = 0.5.

  19. If a/b = 0.6 and b is nonzero, find (5a − 2b) ÷ (3a + b).

    1. 7/5
    2. 5/4
    3. 5/14
    4. 1/4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5/14

    Explanation: Divide numerator and denominator by b: (5 × 0.6 − 2)/(3 × 0.6 + 1) = 1/2.8 = 5/14.

  20. Which fraction has the greatest shortfall from 1?

    1. 17/18
    2. 39/40
    3. 23/24
    4. 31/32
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 17/18

    Explanation: The shortfalls from 1 are 1/18, 1/24, 1/32 and 1/40 for denominators 18, 24, 32 and 40. The greatest is 1/18, so the fraction is 17/18.

  21. What is 5 − 0.0007?

    1. 4.993
    2. 4.9997
    3. 4.99993
    4. 4.9993
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 4.9993

    Explanation: Write 5 as 5.0000, then subtract: 5.0000 − 0.0007 = 4.9993.

  22. A student multiplies a positive number by 0.04 instead of dividing it by 0.04. The incorrect result is what fraction of the correct result?

    1. 1/250
    2. 1/625
    3. 1/2500
    4. 1/25
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1/625

    Explanation: Incorrect/correct = (0.04x)/(x/0.04) = 0.04² = 0.0016 = 1/625.

  23. Data sufficiency: What is the value of a positive decimal x? I. x has exactly two digits after the decimal point, both nonzero and 0.4 < x < 0.5. II. In lowest terms, x = p/20 for a positive integer p coprime to 20, and 0.4 < x < 0.5.

    1. Statement II alone is sufficient; I alone is not.
    2. Each statement alone is sufficient.
    3. Statement I alone is sufficient; II alone is not.
    4. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Statement II alone is sufficient; I alone is not.

    Explanation: Statement I permits values such as 0.41 and 0.42. Statement II gives 8 < p < 10, so p = 9 and x = 9/20 = 0.45; II alone is sufficient.

  24. Evaluate (2.4² + 0.7² − 2 × 2.4 × 0.7) ÷ 0.17.

    1. 1.7
    2. 10
    3. 17
    4. 170
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 17

    Explanation: The numerator is (2.4 − 0.7)² = 1.7² = 2.89; then 2.89 ÷ 0.17 = 17.

  25. Evaluate 1/(0.2 × 0.3) + 1/(0.3 × 0.4) + 1/(0.4 × 0.5).

    1. 300
    2. 20
    3. 3
    4. 30
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 30

    Explanation: Use 1/[a(a + 0.1)] = 10[1/a − 1/(a + 0.1)]. The sum telescopes to 10(1/0.2 − 1/0.5) = 10(5 − 2) = 30.
