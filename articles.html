Before You Read

Study guide questions

How these guides are written, checked and kept useful.

All questions →

Are the study guides free?

Yes. Every guide on this hub is free to read in full, with no sign-up and no paywall. The practice they link to is free as well.

How long does one guide take to read?

Ten to fifteen minutes. Each one ends with links straight into the matching quizzes, so you can turn what you just read into practice in the same sitting.

Should I read a guide first or start practising?

Practise first if your exam is close — the mock test will tell you which guide matters. Read first only if you are starting a subject from zero.

Who writes and reviews the guides?

The House of Aspirants editorial team writes and reviews every guide, keeping them evergreen: strategy and method instead of dates, vacancies or rumours that expire.
