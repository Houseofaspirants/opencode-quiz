Question bank

Physical Features and Rivers of Punjab - Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. In the Rigvedic period, Punjab was known as:

    1. Pentapotamia
    2. Land of Five Rivers
    3. Sapta Sindhu
    4. Doab
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Sapta Sindhu

    Explanation: In the Rigvedic period Punjab was Sapta Sindhu, the Land of Seven Rivers. It became the Land of Five Rivers in the Mahabharata period.

  2. Which two rivers of the Rigvedic Sapta Sindhu are NOT among the five rivers of the Mahabharata period?

    1. Indus and Saraswati
    2. Sutlej and Beas
    3. Ravi and Chenab
    4. Jhelum and Ravi
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Indus and Saraswati

    Explanation: Sapta Sindhu: Indus, Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Chenab, Jhelum and Saraswati. The five rivers of the Mahabharata period are Sutlej, Beas, Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum, so the Indus and Saraswati drop out.

  3. The Greek name for Punjab was:

    1. Sapta Sindhu
    2. Pentapotamia
    3. Mesopotamia
    4. Aryavarta
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Pentapotamia

    Explanation: The Greeks called Punjab Pentapotamia, meaning the land of five rivers.

  4. The word 'Punjab' comes from which language?

    1. Sanskrit
    2. Greek
    3. Arabic
    4. Persian
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Persian

    Explanation: 'Punjab' is Persian: Punj means five and Aab means water.

  5. Which is the longest river in Punjab?

    1. Sutlej
    2. Beas
    3. Ravi
    4. Ghaggar
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Sutlej

    Explanation: The Sutlej is the longest river in Punjab. The Ravi is the shortest.

  6. Which is the shortest river in Punjab?

    1. Sutlej
    2. Beas
    3. Ravi
    4. Chenab
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Ravi

    Explanation: The Ravi is the shortest river in Punjab and the Sutlej the longest.

  7. The Sutlej originates from:

    1. Beas Kund near Rohtang Pass
    2. Rakas Tal near Mansarovar
    3. Bara Bhangal in the Dhauladhar range
    4. Shiwalik hills in Solan district
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Rakas Tal near Mansarovar

    Explanation: The Sutlej rises at Rakas Tal near Mansarovar in Tibet. Beas Kund is the source of the Beas, Bara Bhangal of the Ravi and the Solan Shiwaliks of the Ghaggar.

  8. In Tibet, the Sutlej is known as:

    1. Tsangpo
    2. Shutudri
    3. Arjikiya
    4. Langchen Khambab
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Langchen Khambab

    Explanation: The Tibetan name of the Sutlej is Langchen Khambab. Shutudri is its Vedic name and Arjikiya is the Vedic name of the Beas.

  9. The Beas joins the Sutlej at:

    1. Harike
    2. Ropar
    3. Panjnad
    4. Pathankot
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Harike

    Explanation: The Beas meets the Sutlej at Harike in Tarn Taran district.

  10. The Ranjit Sagar Dam is built on which river?

    1. Sutlej
    2. Beas
    3. Ravi
    4. Ghaggar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Ravi

    Explanation: The Ranjit Sagar Dam is on the Ravi, which enters Punjab at Pathankot. Bhakra Nangal is on the Sutlej and Pong Dam on the Beas.

  11. At which place does the Beas enter Punjab?

    1. Ropar
    2. Hoshiarpur
    3. Pathankot
    4. Patiala
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Hoshiarpur

    Explanation: The Beas enters Punjab at Hoshiarpur. The Sutlej enters at Ropar, the Ravi at Pathankot and the Ghaggar at Patiala.

  12. What was the Vedic name of the Ravi?

    1. Shutudri
    2. Arjikiya
    3. Vipasha
    4. Parushni
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Parushni

    Explanation: The Vedic name of the Ravi is Parushni and its Sanskrit name is Iravati. Shutudri is the Sutlej, Arjikiya is the Beas, and Vipasha is the Sanskrit name of the Beas.

  13. Which set of rivers and Sanskrit names is correctly matched?

    1. Sutlej-Shatadru; Beas-Vipasha; Ravi-Iravati
    2. Sutlej-Vipasha; Beas-Iravati; Ravi-Shatadru
    3. Sutlej-Iravati; Beas-Shatadru; Ravi-Vipasha
    4. Sutlej-Shatadru; Beas-Iravati; Ravi-Vipasha
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Sutlej-Shatadru; Beas-Vipasha; Ravi-Iravati

    Explanation: In river order Sutlej–Beas–Ravi the Sanskrit names are Shatadru, Vipasha and Iravati (memory trick: S-V-I).

  14. Which river of Punjab is seasonal and is identified with the Saraswati?

    1. Sutlej
    2. Beas
    3. Ravi
    4. Ghaggar
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Ghaggar

    Explanation: The Ghaggar flows only in the rainy season and is identified with the Saraswati. The Sutlej, Beas and Ravi are perennial.

  15. Trimab is the meeting point of which rivers?

    1. Sutlej, Beas and Ravi
    2. Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum
    3. Beas, Sutlej and Ghaggar
    4. Indus, Jhelum and Sutlej
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum

    Explanation: Trimab is where the Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum meet. The Trimab then joins the Sutlej at Panjnad in Pakistan.

  16. Panjnad is the place where:

    1. The Beas joins the Sutlej in Tarn Taran
    2. The Ravi, Chenab and Jhelum first meet
    3. The Trimab meets the Sutlej, in Pakistan
    4. The Sutlej enters Punjab at Ropar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) The Trimab meets the Sutlej, in Pakistan

    Explanation: Panjnad, in Pakistan, is where the combined Trimab (Ravi, Chenab, Jhelum) meets the Sutlej. The Beas–Sutlej confluence is Harike.

  17. The Bhakra Nangal Dam is built on which river?

    1. Ravi
    2. Beas
    3. Sutlej
    4. Ghaggar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Sutlej

    Explanation: The Bhakra Nangal Dam is on the Sutlej. Pong Dam is on the Beas and Ranjit Sagar Dam on the Ravi.

  18. At which place does the Sutlej enter Punjab?

    1. Ropar
    2. Hoshiarpur
    3. Pathankot
    4. Harike
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Ropar

    Explanation: The Sutlej enters Punjab at Ropar. The Beas enters at Hoshiarpur and the Ravi at Pathankot.

  19. The Beas river originates from:

    1. Rakas Tal near Mansarovar
    2. Bara Bhangal in the Dhauladhar range
    3. Shiwalik hills in Solan district
    4. Beas Kund near Rohtang Pass
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Beas Kund near Rohtang Pass

    Explanation: The Beas rises at Beas Kund in the Kullu hills near Rohtang Pass. Rakas Tal is the source of the Sutlej and Bara Bhangal of the Ravi.

  20. The Ravi originates at Bara Bhangal in which mountain range?

    1. Pir Panjal
    2. Dhauladhar
    3. Zanskar
    4. Shiwalik
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Dhauladhar

    Explanation: The Ravi rises at Bara Bhangal in the Dhauladhar range of Himachal Pradesh.

  21. The Ghaggar originates in the Shiwalik hills of which district?

    1. Kullu
    2. Kangra
    3. Solan
    4. Chamba
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Solan

    Explanation: The Ghaggar rises in the Shiwalik hills of Solan district, Himachal Pradesh, and enters Punjab at Patiala.

  22. 'Shutudri' was the Vedic name of which river?

    1. Sutlej
    2. Beas
    3. Ravi
    4. Jhelum
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Sutlej

    Explanation: Shutudri is the Vedic name of the Sutlej (Sanskrit: Shatadru). Arjikiya is the Beas and Parushni the Ravi.

  23. 'Iravati' is the Sanskrit name of which river?

    1. Sutlej
    2. Beas
    3. Ghaggar
    4. Ravi
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Ravi

    Explanation: Iravati is the Sanskrit name of the Ravi. Shatadru is the Sutlej and Vipasha the Beas.

  24. Harike, where the Beas meets the Sutlej, is in which district?

    1. Ferozepur
    2. Tarn Taran
    3. Amritsar
    4. Kapurthala
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Tarn Taran

    Explanation: Harike, the confluence of the Beas and the Sutlej, is in Tarn Taran district.

  25. The Kandi Canal and the Mukerian Hydel Channel are associated with which river?

    1. Sutlej
    2. Ravi
    3. Beas
    4. Ghaggar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Beas

    Explanation: Pong Dam, the Kandi Canal and the Mukerian Hydel Channel are Beas projects. The Upper Bari Doab Canal and Ranjit Sagar Dam are on the Ravi.
