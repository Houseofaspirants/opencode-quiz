Financial Position and Agriculture of Punjab - Part 2: all 25 questions

  1. Which group contains the major crops of Punjab listed in the chapter?

    1. Wheat, rice, cotton and sugarcane
    2. Wheat, tea, coffee and rubber
    3. Rice, jute, tea and tobacco
    4. Cotton, coffee, rubber and sugarcane
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Wheat, rice, cotton and sugarcane

    Explanation: The major crops listed are wheat, rice, cotton and sugarcane. The other groups include crops not listed as Punjab's major crops in the chapter. These crops span both Rabi and Kharif cultivation.

  2. Match the product with the top producer mentioned for it: A. Wheat B. Rice C. Cotton D. Milk 1. Uttar Pradesh 2. West Bengal 3. Gujarat

    1. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-1
    2. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-2
    3. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-1
    4. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-3
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A-1, B-2, C-3, D-1

    Explanation: Uttar Pradesh is listed as the top producer of wheat and milk, West Bengal of rice, and Gujarat of cotton. Therefore A-1, B-2, C-3, D-1 is correct. The repeated Uttar Pradesh entry is required because it leads in two listed products.

  3. Punjab's milk production was about how much, according to the chapter figures?

    1. About 10 million tonnes
    2. About 12 million tonnes
    3. About 14 million tonnes
    4. About 16 million tonnes
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) About 14 million tonnes

    Explanation: Punjab's milk production is given as about 14 million tonnes. The other quantities are not the stated production figure. Punjab's national rank in milk is 7th and its share is about 6%.

  4. Which of the following is NOT correctly matched with Punjab's crop specialisation?

    1. Kinnow — Fazilka/Abohar belt
    2. Grapes — Bathinda
    3. Litchi — Pathankot-Gurdaspur
    4. Kinnow — Bathinda
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Kinnow — Bathinda

    Explanation: Kinnow is associated with the Fazilka/Abohar belt, while Bathinda is associated with grapes. Litchi is associated with Pathankot-Gurdaspur. Thus, Kinnow—Bathinda is the incorrect match.

  5. Consider the following statements about kinnow cultivation in Punjab: 1. Fazilka is an important kinnow district. 2. The Abohar belt is described as leading. 3. Muktsar and Hoshiarpur are also listed for citrus fruits. Which of the above is/are correct?

    1. 1 and 2 only
    2. 2 and 3 only
    3. 1 and 3 only
    4. 1, 2 and 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation: All three statements are correct. Fazilka, especially the leading Abohar belt, along with Muktsar and Hoshiarpur, is listed for citrus fruits such as kinnow. The other options omit one correct location.

  6. Which district is associated with grape cultivation in Punjab?

    1. Bathinda
    2. Fazilka
    3. Pathankot
    4. Hoshiarpur
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Bathinda

    Explanation: Bathinda is associated with grapes. Fazilka is important for kinnow, while Pathankot-Gurdaspur is associated with litchi. Hoshiarpur is listed among citrus-fruit areas.

  7. The Pathankot-Gurdaspur area is associated with which fruit?

    1. Kinnow
    2. Grapes
    3. Litchi
    4. Potato
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Litchi

    Explanation: Litchi is associated with the Pathankot-Gurdaspur area. Kinnow is associated with the Fazilka/Abohar belt and other citrus areas. Grapes are associated with Bathinda.

  8. Where is Kheti Bhawan located?

    1. Mohali
    2. Ludhiana
    3. Bathinda
    4. Fazilka
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Mohali

    Explanation: Kheti Bhawan is located in Mohali. Ludhiana is the location of Punjab Agricultural University. Bathinda and Fazilka are identified with agricultural specialisations or projects, not Kheti Bhawan.

  9. Punjab Agricultural University is located in:

    1. Mohali
    2. Ludhiana
    3. Muktsar
    4. Pathankot
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Ludhiana

    Explanation: Punjab Agricultural University is located in Ludhiana. Mohali is associated with Kheti Bhawan. Muktsar and Pathankot are mentioned in relation to crop specialisation.

  10. Punjab Agricultural University was established in which year?

    1. 1952
    2. 1962
    3. 1972
    4. 1982
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1962

    Explanation: Punjab Agricultural University was established in 1962. The other years are not the year given. Its location is Ludhiana.

  11. Consider the following statements: 1. Kheti Bhawan is located in Mohali. 2. Punjab Agricultural University is located in Ludhiana. 3. Punjab Agricultural University was established in 1962. Which of the above is/are correct?

    1. 1 and 2 only
    2. 2 and 3 only
    3. 1 and 3 only
    4. 1, 2 and 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation: All three statements are correct. Kheti Bhawan is in Mohali, while Punjab Agricultural University is in Ludhiana and was established in 1962. The other options leave out one correct institutional fact.

  12. About what percentage of farm households in Punjab were indebted?

    1. About 44%
    2. About 54%
    3. About 64%
    4. About 74%
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) About 54%

    Explanation: About 54% of farm households in Punjab were indebted. The other percentages are not the stated estimate. The figure is attributed to NSSO SAS 2019.

  13. According to NSSO SAS 2019, which state had the highest percentage of indebted farm households, as compared with Punjab's about 54%?

    1. Andhra Pradesh, about 93%
    2. Uttar Pradesh, about 83%
    3. West Bengal, about 73%
    4. Gujarat, about 63%
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Andhra Pradesh, about 93%

    Explanation: Andhra Pradesh had the highest stated share, at more than 93%, while Punjab's figure was about 54%. The other state-percentage pairs are not the comparison given. This is a farm-household indebtedness comparison, not an agricultural-production ranking.

  14. Assertion (A): Punjab did not have the highest percentage of indebted farm households in the comparison cited. Reason (R): Andhra Pradesh was reported at more than 93%, whereas Punjab was at about 54%. Choose the correct option.

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

    Explanation: Both A and R are true, and R explains A because Andhra Pradesh's reported share exceeds Punjab's. The other options either reject the comparison or deny the figures. The comparison is linked to NSSO SAS 2019.

  15. The Malwa Canal Project is intended to irrigate about how many acres?

    1. 1.28 lakh acres
    2. 1.58 lakh acres
    3. 1.78 lakh acres
    4. 1.98 lakh acres
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1.78 lakh acres

    Explanation: The Malwa Canal Project is intended to irrigate about 1.78 lakh acres. The other figures are not the stated target. The project concerns parts of southern Punjab listed in the chapter.

  16. Which option correctly matches the Malwa Canal Project with its stated districts?

    1. Bathinda, Faridkot, Ferozepur and Muktsar
    2. Bathinda, Ludhiana, Patiala and Mohali
    3. Fazilka, Hoshiarpur, Pathankot and Gurdaspur
    4. Amritsar, Jalandhar, Kapurthala and Moga
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Bathinda, Faridkot, Ferozepur and Muktsar

    Explanation: The project is to irrigate areas in Bathinda, Faridkot, Ferozepur and Muktsar. The other groups are not the district combination given. Its stated irrigation target is about 1.78 lakh acres.

  17. Which season extends from November to March and includes wheat and mustard?

    1. Rabi (Haarhi)
    2. Kharif (Saauni)
    3. Zaid
    4. Monsoon season
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Rabi (Haarhi)

    Explanation: Rabi, called Haarhi, extends from November to March and includes wheat and mustard. Kharif runs from July to October, while Zaid lies between Rabi and Kharif. Monsoon season is not the listed Punjabi agricultural-season label here.

  18. Match the agricultural season with its duration: A. Rabi (Haarhi) B. Kharif (Saauni) C. Zaid 1. November-March 2. July-October 3. March-July

    1. A-1, B-2, C-3
    2. A-2, B-1, C-3
    3. A-3, B-2, C-1
    4. A-1, B-3, C-2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A-1, B-2, C-3

    Explanation: Rabi runs from November to March, Kharif from July to October, and Zaid from March to July. Thus A-1, B-2, C-3 is correct. The durations show that Zaid falls between Rabi and Kharif.

  19. Consider the following statements about Rabi crops: 1. They need a cool climate for growth. 2. They need long day length for flowering. 3. Wheat, mustard, cumin and coriander are examples. Which of the above is/are correct?

    1. 1 and 2 only
    2. 2 and 3 only
    3. 1 and 3 only
    4. 1, 2 and 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation: All three statements correctly describe Rabi crops. The listed examples are wheat, mustard, cumin and coriander, and the season's characteristics include cool conditions and long day length for flowering. The other options omit one correct feature.

  20. Which of the following is a Kharif crop listed for Punjab?

    1. Cumin
    2. Coriander
    3. Rice
    4. Mustard
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Rice

    Explanation: Rice is listed as a Kharif crop. Cumin, coriander and mustard are listed among Rabi examples. Kharif crops require hot weather, rainwater and short day length.

  21. Consider the following statements about Kharif crops: 1. The season extends from July to October. 2. Kharif crops need hot weather and rainwater. 3. Rice, maize, bajra, cotton, sugarcane and soybean are examples. Which of the above is/are correct?

    1. 1 and 2 only
    2. 2 and 3 only
    3. 1 and 3 only
    4. 1, 2 and 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation: All three statements are correct. Kharif lasts from July to October, requires hot weather and rainwater, and includes the listed crops. The other options omit one of these defining points.

  22. Zaid crops are grown mainly during which period?

    1. November-March
    2. July-October
    3. March-July
    4. October-December
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) March-July

    Explanation: Zaid crops are grown from March to July, between Rabi and Kharif. November-March is Rabi, and July-October is Kharif. The remaining period is not the stated Zaid duration.

  23. Which option correctly matches Zaid crops with examples?

    1. Pumpkin, cucumber, bitter gourd and watermelon
    2. Wheat, mustard, cumin and coriander
    3. Rice, maize, cotton and soybean
    4. Wheat, rice, cotton and sugarcane
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Pumpkin, cucumber, bitter gourd and watermelon

    Explanation: The Zaid examples are pumpkin, cucumber, bitter gourd and watermelon. The second group consists of Rabi examples, while the third group consists of Kharif examples. The fourth group contains major crops, not the specific Zaid list.

  24. Which of the following is NOT correctly matched with an agricultural season?

    1. Rabi — cool climate and long day length for flowering
    2. Kharif — hot weather, rainwater and short day length
    3. Zaid — summer crops between Rabi and Kharif
    4. Zaid — winter crops needing cool climate for growth
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Zaid — winter crops needing cool climate for growth

    Explanation: The fourth match is incorrect because winter crops needing cool climate describe Rabi, not Zaid. Rabi, Kharif and Zaid are correctly described by the first three options. Zaid consists of summer crops grown between the other two seasons.

  25. Arrange the three agricultural seasons in chronological order according to their stated starting months.

    1. Kharif → Zaid → Rabi
    2. Rabi → Kharif → Zaid
    3. Zaid → Rabi → Kharif
    4. Rabi → Zaid → Kharif
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Rabi → Zaid → Kharif

    Explanation: By starting month, Rabi begins in November, Zaid in March, and Kharif in July. Therefore the chronological order is Rabi → Zaid → Kharif. The other sequences disrupt the stated seasonal calendar.
