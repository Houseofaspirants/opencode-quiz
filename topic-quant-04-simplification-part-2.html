Question bank

Simplification MCQ – Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A number was multiplied by 46 instead of 64, making the result 1458 too small. Find the number.

    1. 18
    2. 90
    3. 72
    4. 81
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 81

    Explanation: The error is (64 − 46) times the number; hence the number is 1458 ÷ 18 = 81.

  2. Evaluate: (3^5 × 3²) ÷ 3^4.

    1. 243
    2. 27
    3. 81
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 27

    Explanation: Combine exponents: 3^(5 + 2 − 4) = 3³ = 27.

  3. For a positive integer n, 4^n × 8^(n − 1) = 2^22. Find n.

    1. 4
    2. 11
    3. 5
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5

    Explanation: Write both bases as powers of 2: 2n + 3(n − 1) = 22, so 5n = 25 and n = 5.

  4. Data sufficiency: What is the initial quantity of grain in a store? I: After selling 3/8 of it, 150 kg remained. II: The quantity sold was 90 kg.

    1. Statement I alone is sufficient
    2. Even both statements together are insufficient
    3. Statement II alone is sufficient
    4. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Statement I alone is sufficient

    Explanation: Statement I gives the initial quantity as 150 × 8/5 = 240 kg. Statement II alone gives no fraction or initial total.

  5. What is 398.7 ÷ 19.9, rounded to the nearest integer?

    1. 19
    2. 18
    3. 20
    4. 21
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 20

    Explanation: Use 400 ÷ 20 = 20; the actual quotient is just above 20 and below 20.5, so it rounds to 20.

  6. Simplify [√72 + √32 − √8] ÷ √2.

    1. 8
    2. 12
    3. 10
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 8

    Explanation: Extract √2: (6√2 + 4√2 − 2√2) ÷ √2 = 8.

  7. Evaluate: −[−26 + (−17)] + (−19).

    1. -24
    2. 62
    3. -62
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 24

    Explanation: The bracket is −43; reversing its sign gives 43 − 19 = 24.

  8. Evaluate: 0.48 × 2.5 ÷ 0.06 − 7.5.

    1. 27.5
    2. 12.5
    3. 20
    4. 2.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 12.5

    Explanation: Pair 0.48 ÷ 0.06 = 8; then 8 × 2.5 − 7.5 = 12.5.

  9. If x − y = 5 and xy = 24, find x³ − y³.

    1. 365
    2. 125
    3. 485
    4. 235
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 485

    Explanation: Use (x − y)³ + 3xy(x − y) = 125 + 360 = 485.

  10. Evaluate: 840 ÷ {35 − [24 − (18 − 7)]} × 11.

    1. 462
    2. 385
    3. 210
    4. 420
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 420

    Explanation: The divisor is 35 − (24 − 11) = 22, giving 840 ÷ 22 × 11 = 420.

  11. Find the value of 62.5% of 256.

    1. 160
    2. 96
    3. 192
    4. 128
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 160

    Explanation: Since 62.5% = 5/8, the value is 256 × 5/8 = 160.

  12. Evaluate: (2 + 3/5) × (1 + 7/13) − 3/4.

    1. 17/4
    2. 13/4
    3. 5/4
    4. 11/4
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 13/4

    Explanation: Convert to improper fractions: (13/5) × (20/13) − 3/4 = 4 − 3/4 = 13/4.

  13. Find (1 − 1/5)(1 − 1/6)(1 − 1/7), continuing through (1 − 1/24), with every integer denominator included.

    1. 1/6
    2. 5/24
    3. 4/23
    4. 1/24
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1/6

    Explanation: Rewrite as (4/5)(5/6) through (23/24); cancellation leaves 4/24 = 1/6.

  14. Find the value of ? in 18 × ? − 756 ÷ 9 = 510.

    1. 23
    2. 33
    3. 28
    4. 35
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 33

    Explanation: Move 84 to the other side: ? = (510 + 84) ÷ 18 = 33.

  15. Evaluate: √(1296 ÷ 16).

    1. 6
    2. 81
    3. 9
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9

    Explanation: Divide inside the root: √81 = 9.

  16. Evaluate: 3 − 2/(1 + 1/(2 + 1/4)).

    1. 13/21
    2. 17/13
    3. 19/13
    4. 21/13
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 21/13

    Explanation: Work upwards: 2 + 1/4 = 9/4 and 1 + 4/9 = 13/9; hence 3 − 18/13 = 21/13.

  17. Find the value of (18% of 750 + 32% of 625) ÷ 5.

    1. 67
    2. 335
    3. 71
    4. 65
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 67

    Explanation: The two percentages are 135 and 200; (135 + 200) ÷ 5 = 67.

  18. In a group of 90 people, 52 like tea and 46 like coffee. Everyone likes at least one. How many like exactly one?

    1. 8
    2. 82
    3. 98
    4. 90
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 82

    Explanation: Both = 52 + 46 − 90 = 8; exactly one = 90 − 8 = 82.

  19. Evaluate: 8640 ÷ 45.

    1. 172
    2. 216
    3. 96
    4. 192
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 192

    Explanation: Divide by 5 and then by 9: 1728 ÷ 9 = 192.

  20. For x ≠ 6, simplify (x² − 36)/(x − 6) − (x² − 12x + 36)/(x − 6).

    1. 36
    2. 6
    3. 12
    4. 0
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 12

    Explanation: Factor the numerators: (x + 6) − (x − 6) = 12.

  21. Without carrying out full multiplication, identify the value of 247 × 36 + 153 × 36.

    1. 14400
    2. 14040
    3. 14436
    4. 14364
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 14400

    Explanation: Take 36 common: (247 + 153) × 36 = 400 × 36 = 14400.

  22. Evaluate: 85².

    1. 7250
    2. 7025
    3. 7225
    4. 7125
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 7225

    Explanation: For a number ending in 5, multiply 8 × 9 and append 25: 7225.

  23. Evaluate: 168 ÷ 7 × 3 + 96 ÷ 8 − 17.

    1. 55
    2. 3
    3. 79
    4. 67
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 67

    Explanation: Keep equal-priority operations left to right: 24 × 3 + 12 − 17 = 67.

  24. If x = √6 + √5, find x² + 1/x².

    1. 11
    2. 22
    3. 24
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 22

    Explanation: Since 1/x = √6 − √5, the sum is 2(6 + 5) = 22.

  25. If a ★ b = a² − 2b, find (5 ★ 4) ★ 3.

    1. 17
    2. 283
    3. 295
    4. 277
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 283

    Explanation: First 5 ★ 4 = 25 − 8 = 17; then 17 ★ 3 = 289 − 6 = 283.
