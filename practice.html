Which practice type should I use first?

A topic quiz on the chapter you just read. It is short, it is scoped to one chapter, and it tells you within ten minutes whether the reading landed. Mock tests are for after the syllabus, not before.

How are questions mixed in a mock test?

Like the real paper. Every subject gets its share of the questions in the exam's weightage, the questions come from as many different chapters as possible, and the paper is laid out subject by subject. Choose 100 questions in 100 or 120 minutes, or set your own.

Do I need an account to attempt a test?

No. Every test runs in your browser. Scores, streaks and bookmarks stay on your own device unless you choose to sign in.

Where did the questions come from?

They follow the pattern of previous year papers for Punjab Police, PSSSB and PPSC exams, written to the same syllabus. The papers themselves are published separately in Previous Year Papers.
