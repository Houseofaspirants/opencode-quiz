Question bank

Number System MCQ – Part 4: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Which expression is always odd for every integer n?

    1. n² + n
    2. 2n²
    3. n² − n
    4. n² + n + 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) n² + n + 1

    Explanation: n(n + 1) is even because one of two consecutive integers is even. Thus n² + n + 1 is always odd.

  2. How many positive integers at most 600 are divisible by 12 or 18, but not by both?

    1. 67
    2. 51
    3. 83
    4. 17
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 51

    Explanation: There are 50 multiples of 12, 33 of 18 and 16 of their LCM 36. Excluding the common multiples from both counts gives 50 + 33 − 2 × 16 = 51.

  3. What is the sum of the place values of the two occurrences of 8 in 8,03,280?

    1. 800080
    2. 800008
    3. 800800
    4. 880000
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 800080

    Explanation: The two place values are 800000 and 80. Their sum is 800000 + 80 = 800080.

  4. Find the greatest perfect cube that divides 2^8 × 3^5 × 5^4 exactly.

    1. 108000
    2. 36000
    3. 216000
    4. 432000
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 216000

    Explanation: For a cube divisor, take the greatest multiple of 3 not exceeding each exponent. Thus the divisor is 2^6 × 3³ × 5³ = (4 × 3 × 5)³ = 216000.

  5. What is the unit digit of 7^24 − 3^17?

    1. 2
    2. 4
    3. 6
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 8

    Explanation: 7^24 ends in 1 and 3^17 ends in 3. The difference has unit digit 1 − 3 + 10 = 8.

  6. Find the least positive integer k such that 90k is a perfect square and 60k is a perfect cube.

    1. 450
    2. 56250
    3. 11250
    4. 33750
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 56250

    Explanation: For 90k to be a square and 60k a cube, the least exponents of 2, 3 and 5 in k are 1, 2 and 5 respectively. Thus k = 2 × 3² × 5^5 = 56250; indeed, 90k = 2250² and 60k = 150³.

  7. Find the least positive integer n such that n! is divisible by 10^29.

    1. 125
    2. 120
    3. 124
    4. 130
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 125

    Explanation: 124! has 24 + 4 = 28 trailing zeros, while 125! has 25 + 5 + 1 = 31. The count jumps past 29 at 125, so the least n is 125.

  8. Find the remainder when 16^51 is divided by 17.

    1. 0
    2. 1
    3. 16
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 16

    Explanation: 16 is equivalent to −1 modulo 17. An odd power therefore leaves remainder −1 + 17 = 16.

  9. The sum of 8 consecutive even positive integers is 344. Find the smallest integer.

    1. 34
    2. 38
    3. 43
    4. 36
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 36

    Explanation: Their average is 344 ÷ 8 = 43. The smallest is 7 below the average, so it is 43 − 7 = 36.

  10. A positive integer is divided by 15 and leaves remainder 11. What remainder does its square leave when divided by 5?

    1. 1
    2. 0
    3. 2
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1

    Explanation: The integer leaves remainder 1 on division by 5 because 11 leaves remainder 1. Its square therefore leaves remainder 1² = 1.

  11. How many two-digit numbers have digit product 18?

    1. 2
    2. 4
    3. 3
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 4

    Explanation: The possible ordered digit pairs are (2, 9), (3, 6), (6, 3) and (9, 2). Hence there are 4 such numbers.

  12. How many positive factors of 1,080 are divisible by 6 but not by 12?

    1. 12
    2. 8
    3. 6
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 6

    Explanation: 1080 = 2³ × 3³ × 5. Such a factor must have exponent exactly 1 for 2, exponent 1, 2 or 3 for 3, and exponent 0 or 1 for 5, giving 1 × 3 × 2 = 6.

  13. What is the least non-negative integer that must be subtracted from 7,000 to make the result divisible by both 24 and 35?

    1. 8
    2. 560
    3. 40
    4. 280
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 280

    Explanation: LCM of 24 and 35 is 840. Since 7000 = 840 × 8 + 280, subtract the remainder 280.

  14. What is the value of √3249?

    1. 57
    2. 53
    3. 67
    4. 59
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 57

    Explanation: 57² = (50 + 7)² = 2500 + 700 + 49 = 3249. Hence √3249 = 57.

  15. Find the remainder when 4^31 + 4^32 + 4^33 is divided by 7.

    1. 1
    2. 0
    3. 3
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 0

    Explanation: Factor out 4^31: the expression becomes 4^31 × (1 + 4 + 16) = 4^31 × 21. It is divisible by 7, so the remainder is 0.

  16. A sum of ₹1,080 is shared equally among n students. If 3 more students join, each share decreases by ₹30. Find n.

    1. 6
    2. 12
    3. 9
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9

    Explanation: The decrease gives 1080 × 3 ÷ [n(n + 3)] = 30, so n(n + 3) = 108 = 9 × 12. Hence n = 9.

  17. How many trailing zeros are there in 75! ÷ 25!?

    1. 18
    2. 6
    3. 15
    4. 12
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 12

    Explanation: 75! contains 15 + 3 = 18 factors of 5, while 25! contains 5 + 1 = 6. The quotient has 18 − 6 = 12 factors of 5 and enough factors of 2, so it has 12 trailing zeros.

  18. Find the last two digits of 99^51 + 1.

    1. 00
    2. 01
    3. 98
    4. 99
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 00

    Explanation: For the last two digits, 99 acts as −1. Thus 99^51 + 1 has remainder (−1)^51 + 1 = 0 on division by 100, so it ends in 00.

  19. Find the sum of all three-digit numbers formed using each of the digits 3, 5 and 8 exactly once.

    1. 1776
    2. 3552
    3. 5328
    4. 3330
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 3552

    Explanation: Each digit appears twice in each place. The sum is 2 × (3 + 5 + 8) × (100 + 10 + 1) = 3552.

  20. Find the sum of all positive factors of 98 that are perfect squares.

    1. 49
    2. 57
    3. 50
    4. 98
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 50

    Explanation: 98 = 2 × 7². Its square factors are 1 and 49, whose sum is 1 + 49 = 50.

  21. The sum of the first 15 terms of an arithmetic progression is 600. Find its 8th term.

    1. 35
    2. 45
    3. 80
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 40

    Explanation: For an odd number of AP terms, the average is the middle term. Thus the 8th term is 600 ÷ 15 = 40.

  22. If a + b = 13 and ab = 36, find a² + b².

    1. 97
    2. 169
    3. 133
    4. 241
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 97

    Explanation: a² + b² = (a + b)² − 2ab = 13² − 2 × 36 = 97.

  23. What is the greatest four-digit number that is a multiple of 45 and has digit sum 18?

    1. 9990
    2. 9900
    3. 9855
    4. 9810
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 9900

    Explanation: A digit sum of 18 ensures divisibility by 9; the number must also end in 0 or 5. Taking the first two digits as 99 leaves digit sum 0 for the last two, giving the greatest number 9900.

  24. What is the unit digit of the product 1³ × 2³ × 3³ × 4³ × 5³ × 6³?

    1. 2
    2. 5
    3. 6
    4. 0
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 0

    Explanation: The product is (1 × 2 × 3 × 4 × 5 × 6)³ = 720³. Since the base ends in 0, its cube also has unit digit 0.

  25. Find the remainder when (23² − 1) ÷ 8 is divided by 5.

    1. 0
    2. 2
    3. 1
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1

    Explanation: Use the difference of squares: (23² − 1) ÷ 8 = 22 × 24 ÷ 8 = 66. Dividing 66 by 5 leaves remainder 1.
