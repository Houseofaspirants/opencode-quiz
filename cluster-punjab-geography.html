About this cluster

What Punjab Geography covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Punjab Geography is a compact, high-return lane because the state has a small number of features that examiners return to repeatedly: its rivers, its physiographic divisions, its soils, its crops and its climate. Questions are usually direct and rarely require interpretation, which makes the area ideal for daily short sets rather than long study sessions. Because district names and administrative boundaries change over time, always confirm the current configuration in an official notification instead of relying on an old note. The lanes below start with the rivers, since the drainage pattern explains both the physiographic regions and the agriculture that follows from them. Revise in that order and the rest of the subject tends to fall into place.
