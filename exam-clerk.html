Where should I start for Clerk?

Start with English, Computer. Each subject page above lists its categories, its quizzes and the exams that rely on it, so you never have to guess what to open next.

Is the material on this portal free?

Yes. Every quiz, mock test and study guide on House of Aspirants is free, and no sign-in is needed to open a set - Google sign-in is optional and only saves scores, bookmarks and progress.

Do you publish dates, marks or admit cards?

No, and that is deliberate. Vacancies, dates, marks and admit cards change and this portal does not track them - always read the official notification on the recruiting body's own website before acting on a date.
