Question bank

Number System MCQ – Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Which of the following numbers is prime?

    1. 119
    2. 121
    3. 127
    4. 133
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 127

    Explanation: For 127, test only the primes 2, 3, 5, 7 and 11, since √127 is less than 12. None divides it, so 127 is prime.

  2. The five-digit number 4a7b2 is divisible by 36. What is the greatest possible value of a + b?

    1. 14
    2. 5
    3. 17
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 14

    Explanation: Divisibility by 4 requires b to be odd. The digit sum is 13 + a + b, so a + b must be 5 or 14 for divisibility by 9; 14 is possible with a = 9 and b = 5.

  3. What is the least perfect square divisible by both 24 and 40?

    1. 120
    2. 900
    3. 14400
    4. 3600
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 3600

    Explanation: LCM of 24 and 40 is 120 = 2³ × 3 × 5. Make each exponent even: 2^4 × 3² × 5² = 3600.

  4. Find the sum of the cubes of the first 9 natural numbers.

    1. 729
    2. 2025
    3. 4050
    4. 285
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2025

    Explanation: The sum of cubes is the square of the sum: (9 × 10 ÷ 2)² = 45² = 2025.

  5. How many positive factors of 2^5 × 3^4 × 5² are odd multiples of 9?

    1. 15
    2. 18
    3. 9
    4. 45
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9

    Explanation: An odd factor has exponent 0 for 2. The exponent of 3 can be 2, 3 or 4, and that of 5 can be 0, 1 or 2, giving 3 × 3 = 9 factors.

  6. What is the unit digit of 6^125 + 8^42?

    1. 0
    2. 2
    3. 4
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 0

    Explanation: Every positive power of 6 ends in 6. Since 42 leaves remainder 2 on division by 4, 8^42 ends in 4; 6 + 4 = 10 gives unit digit 0.

  7. What is the smallest five-digit number exactly divisible by 27?

    1. 10008
    2. 10026
    3. 9990
    4. 10017
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 10017

    Explanation: 10000 = 27 × 370 + 10. Add 27 − 10 = 17 to obtain 10017.

  8. A number leaves remainder 17 when divided by 28. What remainder does it leave when divided by 7?

    1. 1
    2. 3
    3. 4
    4. 5
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 3

    Explanation: Write the number as 28q + 17. Since 28q is divisible by 7, the remainder is that of 17 ÷ 7, namely 3.

  9. Find the remainder when 3^100 is divided by 17.

    1. 4
    2. 9
    3. 13
    4. 16
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 13

    Explanation: 3^4 = 81 leaves remainder 13, and 3^8 leaves remainder −1 modulo 17, so 3^16 leaves remainder 1. Since 100 = 16 × 6 + 4, the required remainder is 13.

  10. Evaluate 998 × 1002.

    1. 1000004
    2. 999004
    3. 996004
    4. 999996
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 999996

    Explanation: Use (1000 − 2)(1000 + 2) = 1000² − 2² = 1000000 − 4 = 999996.

  11. What is the greatest digit x for which 6x34 is divisible by 6?

    1. 9
    2. 8
    3. 7
    4. 5
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 8

    Explanation: The last digit is even, so only divisibility by 3 remains. The digit sum 13 + x is a multiple of 3 for x = 2, 5 or 8; the greatest is 8.

  12. How many integers n satisfy 20 < √n < 21?

    1. 40
    2. 39
    3. 41
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 40

    Explanation: Squaring gives 400 < n < 441. The integers from 401 to 440 total 440 − 401 + 1 = 40.

  13. A positive integer has exactly 24 positive factors. Its prime factorisation is 2^a × 3², where a is a non-negative integer. Find a.

    1. 6
    2. 8
    3. 7
    4. 11
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 7

    Explanation: The factor count is (a + 1)(2 + 1) = 24. Hence a + 1 = 8 and a = 7.

  14. In an arithmetic progression, the first term is 8 and the common difference is 6. Which term is 152?

    1. 24
    2. 26
    3. 27
    4. 25
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 25

    Explanation: Solve 8 + (n − 1) × 6 = 152. Thus n − 1 = 24 and n = 25.

  15. What are the last two digits of 3^40?

    1. 21
    2. 01
    3. 41
    4. 81
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 01

    Explanation: 3^10 ends in 49, so 3^20 ends in 49² = 2401, hence in 01. Squaring again shows that 3^40 also ends in 01.

  16. The sum of the first n positive even integers is 870. Find n.

    1. 29
    2. 28
    3. 30
    4. 31
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 29

    Explanation: The sum is n(n + 1), so n(n + 1) = 870 = 29 × 30. Therefore n = 29.

  17. How many consecutive positive integers n satisfy that n! has exactly 24 trailing zeros?

    1. 0
    2. 4
    3. 5
    4. 10
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5

    Explanation: 100! has 20 + 4 = 24 trailing zeros, and the count stays 24 through 104!. At 105! it becomes 21 + 4 = 25, so there are 5 values.

  18. What is the least positive integer that leaves remainder 2 when divided by 5 and remainder 3 when divided by 7?

    1. 12
    2. 22
    3. 32
    4. 17
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 17

    Explanation: Numbers leaving remainder 2 on division by 5 begin 2, 7, 12, 17. The first of these leaving remainder 3 on division by 7 is 17.

  19. If x + 1/x = 5 and x ≠ 0, find x³ + 1/x³.

    1. 125
    2. 110
    3. 115
    4. 140
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 110

    Explanation: Use x³ + 1/x³ = (x + 1/x)³ − 3(x + 1/x). The value is 5³ − 3 × 5 = 110.

  20. A number is divided by 9, giving quotient q and remainder 7. If the same number is divided by 7, the quotient is q + 4 and the remainder is 1. Find the number.

    1. 106
    2. 97
    3. 115
    4. 99
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 106

    Explanation: Equate the two forms: 9q + 7 = 7(q + 4) + 1. Thus 2q = 22, q = 11 and the number is 9 × 11 + 7 = 106.

  21. Using each of the digits 0, 4, 7 and 8 exactly once, what is the greatest four-digit number divisible by 4?

    1. 8704
    2. 8470
    3. 8740
    4. 7840
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 8740

    Explanation: The first two digits can be 87, leaving 4 and 0. Both 40 and 04 are divisible by 4, and 8740 is the greater arrangement.

  22. A three-digit number has hundreds digit 3 more than its units digit. Its tens digit is twice its units digit, and its digit sum is 19. Find the number.

    1. 748
    2. 874
    3. 682
    4. 784
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 784

    Explanation: Let the units digit be u. Then (u + 3) + 2u + u = 19, giving u = 4; the digits are 7, 8 and 4.

  23. Find the remainder when 12^35 − 1 is divided by 11.

    1. 1
    2. 0
    3. 10
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 0

    Explanation: 12 leaves remainder 1 on division by 11. Therefore 12^35 − 1 leaves remainder 1^35 − 1 = 0.

  24. What is the least positive integer that must be added to 6,700 to obtain a perfect cube?

    1. 441
    2. 59
    3. 159
    4. 1300
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 159

    Explanation: 18³ = 5832 and 19³ = 6859. The next perfect cube is 6859, so add 6859 − 6700 = 159.

  25. How many ordered digit pairs (a, b) make the four-digit number 5a2b divisible by 15?

    1. 7
    2. 6
    3. 8
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 7

    Explanation: For divisibility by 5, b = 0 or 5. If b = 0, a can be 2, 5 or 8; if b = 5, a can be 0, 3, 6 or 9, giving 3 + 4 = 7 pairs.
