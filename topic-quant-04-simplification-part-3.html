Question bank

Simplification MCQ – Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Find the value of ? in (5/8) × ? + 42 = 217.

    1. 175
    2. 350
    3. 280
    4. 224
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 280

    Explanation: The fractional part is 175; multiply by 8/5 to get ? = 280.

  2. Evaluate: 1006 × 994.

    1. 999994
    2. 999964
    3. 999936
    4. 1000036
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 999964

    Explanation: These are 1000 ± 6, so the product is 1000² − 6² = 999964.

  3. Find (47³ + 23³)/(47² − 47 × 23 + 23²) − 23.

    1. 93
    2. 70
    3. 24
    4. 47
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 47

    Explanation: Factor the sum of cubes; the quotient is 47 + 23, so subtracting 23 gives 47.

  4. Evaluate: [{72 ÷ (3 + 5)} + 11] × 6 − 48 ÷ 4.

    1. 108
    2. 120
    3. 96
    4. 132
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 108

    Explanation: The square bracket equals 9 + 11 = 20; then 120 − 12 = 108.

  5. Evaluate: (7.25 + 2.75) × (0.625 ÷ 0.125).

    1. 8
    2. 50
    3. 5
    4. 80
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 50

    Explanation: The sum is 10 and the quotient is 5; their product is 50.

  6. Find 1/{2 + [(√48 + √75)/(√27 − √12)]}.

    1. 1/5
    2. 1/9
    3. 1/11
    4. 11
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1/11

    Explanation: Express the surds in multiples of √3: the inner fraction is (4 + 5)/(3 − 2) = 9. The full value is 1/(2 + 9) = 1/11.

  7. Evaluate: ∛(64 × 125).

    1. 20
    2. 10
    3. 40
    4. 25
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 20

    Explanation: Cube roots distribute over this product: ∛64 × ∛125 = 4 × 5 = 20.

  8. Evaluate: 144 − 96 ÷ (8 × 3) + 5 × 7.

    1. 151
    2. 179
    3. 43
    4. 175
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 175

    Explanation: Evaluate the grouped product first: 144 − 4 + 35 = 175.

  9. A tank is 7/8 full. After 36 litres are removed and 12 litres are added, it is 5/8 full. What is 3/4 of its capacity?

    1. 72 litres
    2. 108 litres
    3. 48 litres
    4. 96 litres
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 72 litres

    Explanation: The net loss of 24 litres equals 2/8 of capacity, so capacity = 96 litres. Its 3/4 is 72 litres.

  10. Which listed number can equal 683 × 47, using its last digit and an estimate between 32000 and 32200?

    1. 32100
    2. 32107
    3. 31101
    4. 32101
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 32101

    Explanation: The last digit is 3 × 7 → 1; the range eliminates 31101. The remaining value is 32101.

  11. Evaluate: (5³ − 5²) ÷ 5.

    1. 4
    2. 100
    3. 20
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 20

    Explanation: Factor out 5²: 25 × (5 − 1) ÷ 5 = 20.

  12. Which option is closest to √624 × 15.9?

    1. 360
    2. 400
    3. 440
    4. 480
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 400

    Explanation: Use √625 = 25 and 15.9 ≈ 16; 25 × 16 = 400, which is the nearest listed value.

  13. Find 4 × [(√11 + √3)/(√11 − √3) + (√11 − √3)/(√11 + √3)] − 6.

    1. 22
    2. 8
    3. 14
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 8

    Explanation: Combine the conjugate fractions: their sum is 2(11 + 3)/(11 − 3) = 7/2. Thus 4 × (7/2) − 6 = 8.

  14. Evaluate: 5/6 + 7/15 − 3/10.

    1. 1
    2. 2/3
    3. 6/5
    4. 4/5
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1

    Explanation: With denominator 30, the numerator is 25 + 14 − 9 = 30; the value is 1.

  15. Find the value of 16⅔% of 516.

    1. 85
    2. 172
    3. 43
    4. 86
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 86

    Explanation: 16⅔% is exactly 1/6, so 516 ÷ 6 = 86.

  16. If √(x + 24) = √x + 2 and x ≥ 0, find x.

    1. 5
    2. 100
    3. 25
    4. 36
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 25

    Explanation: Square once: 24 = 4√x + 4, so √x = 5 and x = 25.

  17. If a ⊙ b = (a + b)/(a − b), find (9 ⊙ 3) + (10 ⊙ 6).

    1. 2
    2. 4
    3. 8
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 6

    Explanation: The two results are 12/6 = 2 and 16/4 = 4; their sum is 6.

  18. Of 150 students, 84 play cricket and 72 play football; 30 play neither. How many play cricket only?

    1. 48
    2. 66
    3. 54
    4. 36
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 48

    Explanation: At least one = 120; both = 84 + 72 − 120 = 36. Cricket only = 84 − 36 = 48.

  19. Evaluate: |−12 + 5| + |−18 ÷ 3|.

    1. 25
    2. -13
    3. 13
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 13

    Explanation: Take magnitudes after simplifying: 7 + 6 = 13.

  20. If p − q = 9 and p² + q² = 369, find (p + q)².

    1. 576
    2. 657
    3. 738
    4. 450
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 657

    Explanation: Use (p + q)² = 2(p² + q²) − (p − q)² = 738 − 81 = 657.

  21. A quiz has 40 questions, with +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong answer. Simran attempts all and scores 125. How many answers are wrong?

    1. 5
    2. 7
    3. 9
    4. 35
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 7

    Explanation: A perfect score is 160; each wrong answer lowers it by 5. Thus wrong answers = (160 − 125) ÷ 5 = 7.

  22. Evaluate: 48 × 25.

    1. 2400
    2. 960
    3. 1250
    4. 1200
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1200

    Explanation: 25 is one quarter of 100: 48 ÷ 4 × 100 = 1200.

  23. Evaluate: [(3 + 1/4) − (1 + 5/6)] × 12.

    1. 19
    2. 29
    3. 17
    4. 13
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 17

    Explanation: Use twelfths: (39/12 − 22/12) × 12 = 17.

  24. If x > 0 and x + √x = 72, find x − √x.

    1. 56
    2. 80
    3. 64
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 56

    Explanation: Put √x = t; t(t + 1) = 72 = 8 × 9, so t = 8 and x = 64. The required value is 64 − 8 = 56.

  25. Find the value of [(45% of 360) − (15% of 240)] ÷ (7% of 200).

    1. 7
    2. 11
    3. 9
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9

    Explanation: The numerator is 162 − 36 = 126 and the denominator is 14; hence 126 ÷ 14 = 9.
