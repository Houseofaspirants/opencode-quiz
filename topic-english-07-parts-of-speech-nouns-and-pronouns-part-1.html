Question bank

07 Parts Of Speech Nouns And Pronouns Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Choose the correct completion: Aman and Ravi jointly own one workshop. ___ workshop is near the bus stand.

    1. Aman's and Ravi's
    2. Aman and Ravi's
    3. Aman's and Ravi
    4. Aman and Ravis'
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Aman and Ravi's

    Explanation: For joint possession, the possessive ending is normally attached only to the final name. Aman's and Ravi's would ordinarily indicate separate possession.

  2. Choose the correct replacement for the bracketed words: The revised policy introduces [a stricter criteria] for promotion.

    1. a stricter criterias
    2. a stricter criterion
    3. a stricter criterions
    4. a stricter criterium
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) a stricter criterion

    Explanation: Criterion is singular and criteria is plural. The article a therefore requires criterion.

  3. Complete the sentence: Please keep this information between you and ___.

    1. me
    2. I
    3. myself
    4. mine
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) me

    Explanation: A preposition takes an objective pronoun, so between requires me. I is a subject form.

  4. In 'The principal herself checked the sealed packets', how is 'herself' used?

    1. As a reflexive object
    2. As a possessive pronoun
    3. As an emphatic pronoun
    4. As a relative pronoun
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) As an emphatic pronoun

    Explanation: Herself emphasises that the principal personally performed the action. It is not a reflexive object because checked already has the object packets.

  5. Complete the sentence in formal written English: The consultant to ___ the department referred the dispute has replied.

    1. whom
    2. who
    3. whose
    4. that
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) whom

    Explanation: Whom is the objective relative pronoun required after the preposition to. Who is not used directly after a preposition in this formal construction.

  6. Complete the sentence: If one accepts a public responsibility, one must fulfil ___ obligations.

    1. one's
    2. ones
    3. ones'
    4. one
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) one's

    Explanation: The possessive form of the indefinite pronoun one is one's. Ones' is not the possessive form required here.

  7. Choose the correct replacement for 'many useful advices': The mentor gave us many useful advices.

    1. much useful advices
    2. several useful pieces of advice
    3. several useful piece of advice
    4. many useful advice
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) several useful pieces of advice

    Explanation: Advice is uncountable; individual recommendations are pieces of advice. Many cannot directly modify advice.

  8. Under the traditional formal rule requiring a subject pronoun after the linking verb 'be', complete: It was ___ who first reported the discrepancy.

    1. me
    2. myself
    3. mine
    4. I
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) I

    Explanation: Traditional formal grammar uses the subjective form I as the complement of be. Me is common in everyday usage but does not satisfy the expressly stated rule.

  9. Complete the sentence: The farmer counted twelve ___ near the canal.

    1. sheeps
    2. sheepes
    3. sheep's
    4. sheep
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) sheep

    Explanation: Sheep has the same singular and plural form. Sheeps is not its standard plural.

  10. Complete the sentence: Each of the two machines has ___ own serial number.

    1. its
    2. their
    3. it's
    4. theirs
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) its

    Explanation: Each treats every machine individually, so the singular inanimate possessive its is required. It's means it is or it has and cannot express possession.

  11. The noun 'jury' in 'The jury delivered its verdict' is a:

    1. material noun
    2. abstract noun
    3. proper noun
    4. collective noun
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) collective noun

    Explanation: Jury names a group of people considered as a unit and is therefore collective. An abstract noun names a quality, state or idea.

  12. Complete the sentence: Let ___ present the joint proposal.

    1. he and I
    2. him and I
    3. him and me
    4. he and me
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) him and me

    Explanation: Let takes objective pronouns, so both coordinated pronouns must be him and me. Him and I mixes objective and subjective case.

  13. Choose the sentence that correctly means 'She trusts me more than she trusts him.'

    1. She trusts me more than he does.
    2. She trusts I more than him.
    3. She trusts me more than him.
    4. She trusts myself more than he.
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) She trusts me more than him.

    Explanation: Him is the object of the understood verb trusts in the comparison. He does makes the comparison between two people doing the trusting and changes the meaning.

  14. Complete the sentence: I interviewed two applicants, but neither of ___ had the required certificate.

    1. they
    2. them
    3. their
    4. theirs
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) them

    Explanation: Of is a preposition and requires the objective form them. They cannot serve as the object of of.

  15. Complete the sentence: The officer ___ everyone believed would lead the inquiry declined the assignment.

    1. whom
    2. whose
    3. who
    4. which
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) who

    Explanation: Who is the subject of would lead within the embedded clause. The intervening words everyone believed do not make it an object requiring whom.

  16. Choose the correct completion: The hostel has a separate reading room for women; it is the ___ reading room.

    1. womens
    2. womens'
    3. women
    4. women's
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) women's

    Explanation: An irregular plural that does not end in s forms its possessive with apostrophe-s. Womens' incorrectly treats women as a plural ending in s.

  17. Complete the sentence: The two wrestlers congratulated ___ after their bout.

    1. each other's
    2. themselves each
    3. one's another
    4. each other
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) each other

    Explanation: Each other expresses a reciprocal action between the two wrestlers. Each other's is possessive and requires a following noun.

  18. Complete the sentence: The candidate ___ admit card was damaged received a duplicate.

    1. who
    2. whose
    3. whom
    4. which
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) whose

    Explanation: Whose expresses possession before a noun. Whom cannot show that the admit card belongs to the candidate.

  19. Choose the correct plural: Both of their ___ attended the ceremony.

    1. mothers-in-law
    2. mother-in-laws
    3. mothers-in-laws
    4. mother's-in-law
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) mothers-in-law

    Explanation: In mother-in-law, the principal noun mother takes the plural ending. Adding s only to law pluralises the wrong element.

  20. Choose the correct completion using the double possessive: This portrait belongs to a friend ___.

    1. of my father's
    2. of my father
    3. of my fathers'
    4. of my father's his
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) of my father's

    Explanation: The double possessive combines of with a possessive form: a friend of my father's. Of my father is a different construction and does not meet the stated requirement.

  21. Complete the sentence: The charity supplied winter clothing to several ___.

    1. passer-bys
    2. passers-bys
    3. passers-by
    4. passer's-by
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) passers-by

    Explanation: The plural of passer-by is passers-by because passer is the principal noun. Passer-bys incorrectly adds the plural ending to the adverb.

  22. Choose the feminine counterpart of 'nephew' to complete the sentence: Harjit introduced his brother's daughter as his ___.

    1. aunt
    2. sister
    3. niece
    4. cousin
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) niece

    Explanation: A brother's or sister's daughter is a niece, the feminine counterpart of nephew. A cousin belongs to a different family relationship.

  23. Complete the sentence: The athlete blamed ___ for missing the final practice session.

    1. him
    2. his
    3. himself
    4. he
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) himself

    Explanation: A reflexive pronoun is required when the subject and object refer to the same person. Him normally indicates another male person here.

  24. Choose the grammatically correct sentence.

    1. The office needs several new furnitures.
    2. The office needs several new piece of furniture.
    3. The office needs several new furniture.
    4. The office needs several new pieces of furniture.
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) The office needs several new pieces of furniture.

    Explanation: Furniture is uncountable and is counted through expressions such as pieces of furniture. Furnitures is not the standard plural for ordinary office items.

  25. Complete the sentence: The research team recorded three unusual atmospheric ___.

    1. phenomenon
    2. phenomena
    3. phenomenas
    4. phenomenons
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) phenomena

    Explanation: The standard plural of phenomenon is phenomena. Phenomenon is singular and cannot follow three.
