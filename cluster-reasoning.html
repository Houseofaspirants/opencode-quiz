About this cluster

What Reasoning covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Reasoning is the section where preparation converts into marks most directly, because the question types are fixed and improvement comes from practice rather than from reading. Exams draw from a stable set of patterns - series, coding and decoding, direction, relationships, syllogisms, ranking and analogy - and the same patterns recur with only the numbers and letters changed. The correct approach is therefore type-by-type: master one pattern completely, then move to the next, rather than mixing them and never reaching fluency. Speed matters more here than in any other section, so timed practice should follow untimed accuracy rather than replace it. The lanes below are ordered from the simplest recognition tasks to the multi-step problems, so your accuracy builds before the clock is introduced.

What does Reasoning cover?

Reasoning covers Number and Letter Series, Coding and Decoding, Direction Sense, Blood Relations and the rest. The lane sits under Reasoning. Every area listed above links back to the page that hosts it.

How should I study Reasoning?

Revise one area at a time and test it before moving on - the topics above are ordered so each rests on the one before it. Once they are familiar, switch to timed practice: sets run at 30 seconds a question, 60 for Reasoning and Quantitative Aptitude and clear at 40% accuracy.
