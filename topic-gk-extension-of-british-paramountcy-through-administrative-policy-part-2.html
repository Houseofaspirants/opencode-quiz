  1. Which statement about Awadh is NOT correct?

    1. It was annexed in 1856
    2. It was annexed under the Doctrine of Lapse
    3. Wajid Ali Shah was removed

    Show answer

    Answer: (B) It was annexed under the Doctrine of Lapse

    Explanation: The correct answer is It was annexed under the Doctrine of Lapse. Awadh was annexed in 1856 after Wajid Ali Shah was removed on the ground of misgovernment, not under the Doctrine of Lapse. It therefore satisfies the association or chronology asked in the question. The other alternatives are incorrect here: The remaining three statements state the chapter's account of the annexation.
