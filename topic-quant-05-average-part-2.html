Question bank

Average MCQ – Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. 6 consecutive even integers average 29. Find the average of the smallest 2 integers.

    1. 24
    2. 26
    3. 25
    4. 27
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 25

    Explanation: The middle pair is 28 and 30, so the smallest pair is 24 and 26. Its average = (24 + 26) ÷ 2 = 25.

  2. A library club has 16 members with average age 25 years. 4 members join and the average becomes 27 years. Find the average age of the newcomers in years.

    1. 33
    2. 32
    3. 37
    4. 35
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 35

    Explanation: Newcomers average = 27 + 16 × (27 − 25) ÷ 4 = 35 years.

  3. 4 years ago, 20 pupils averaged 12 years. Today, 5 of them averaging 18 years leave and 5 pupils averaging 13 years join. Find the present average age of the class in years.

    1. 15
    2. 14.75
    3. 15.75
    4. 16.25
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 14.75

    Explanation: Before the changes, today’s average is 12 + 4 = 16. The replacement lowers it by 5 × (18 − 13) ÷ 20 = 1.25, giving 14.75 years.

  4. 25 scooters average 48 km per litre in a test, and 15 scooters average 56 km per litre. Find the combined average of these 40 recorded mileage values.

    1. 52
    2. 50
    3. 53
    4. 51
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 51

    Explanation: Combined average = 48 + 15 × 8 ÷ 40 = 51.

  5. 7 daily temperatures have deviations −4, 3, 2, −1, 5, −3 and −2 from 30°C. Find their average temperature in °C.

    1. 31
    2. 29
    3. 30
    4. 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 30

    Explanation: The deviations sum to 0, so the average remains 30°C.

  6. The average load of 36 bags rises by 1.5 kg when 4 bags averaging 21 kg are replaced by 4 bags averaging x kg. Find x.

    1. 27
    2. 33
    3. 36
    4. 34.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 34.5

    Explanation: Total increase = 36 × 1.5 = 54 kg. New bags average = 21 + 54 ÷ 4 = 34.5 kg.

  7. 2 sections average 58 and 73 marks. Their combined average is 64. Find the ratio of their numbers of students, in the same order. Give the first term when the second term is 2.

    1. 2
    2. 4
    3. 5
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 3

    Explanation: Required ratio = (73 − 64) : (64 − 58) = 9 : 6 = 3 : 2. The first term is 3.

  8. The average of 8 numbers is 46. 7 of them total 301. Find the remaining number.

    1. 67
    2. 65
    3. 69
    4. 61
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 67

    Explanation: Missing number = 8 × 46 − 301 = 67.

  9. The average sales over Monday to Friday is ₹8,400 per day. The average over Tuesday to Saturday is ₹9,100 per day. If Saturday’s sales are ₹12,500, find Monday’s sales in ₹.

    1. 11800
    2. 5500
    3. 9000
    4. 9500
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9000

    Explanation: Saturday exceeds Monday by 5 × (9100 − 8400) = 3500. Monday = 12500 − 3500 = ₹9000.

  10. The average of 18 values is 45. If 8 is subtracted from each value and the result is divided by 2, find the new average.

    1. 14.5
    2. 22.5
    3. 26.5
    4. 18.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 18.5

    Explanation: New average = (45 − 8) ÷ 2 = 18.5.

  11. After 3 workers averaging 42 years leave a team of 18, the remaining workers average 30 years. Find the original average age in years.

    1. 34
    2. 32
    3. 36
    4. 31
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 32

    Explanation: Original average = 30 + 3 × (42 − 30) ÷ 18 = 32 years.

  12. A car covers the first 1/3 of a 180 km journey at 30 km/h and the remainder at 60 km/h. Find its average speed in km/h.

    1. 40
    2. 50
    3. 48
    4. 45
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 45

    Explanation: Time = 60 ÷ 30 + 120 ÷ 60 = 4 hours. Average speed = 180 ÷ 4 = 45 km/h.

  13. Jaspreet scores 72, 81, 69 and 78 in 4 papers. What minimum mark is needed in the 5th paper for an average of at least 76?

    1. 76
    2. 80
    3. 78
    4. 82
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 80

    Explanation: Required mark = 5 × 76 − (72 + 81 + 69 + 78) = 80.

  14. Find the average of 1³, 2³, 3³, 4³ and 5³.

    1. 45
    2. 25
    3. 55
    4. 75
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 45

    Explanation: Sum of cubes = (5 × 6 ÷ 2)² = 225. Average = 225 ÷ 5 = 45.

  15. A clerk totals 24 payments correctly but divides the total by 20 and reports an average of ₹720. Find the correct average payment in ₹.

    1. 600
    2. 864
    3. 680
    4. 620
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 600

    Explanation: Total = 720 × 20. Correct average = 720 × 20 ÷ 24 = ₹600.

  16. The present average age of a couple is 33 years. Their child is 6 years old. What was the average age of all 3 exactly 4 years ago, in years?

    1. 22
    2. 24
    3. 20
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 20

    Explanation: Present total = 2 × 33 + 6 = 72. 4 years ago, average = 72 ÷ 3 − 4 = 20 years.

  17. A batter averages 46 runs per innings over 12 innings. How many runs must he score in the next innings to raise this average to 49?

    1. 82
    2. 85
    3. 88
    4. 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 85

    Explanation: Required score = 49 + 12 × (49 − 46) = 85.

  18. A factory’s 60 staff average ₹24,000 monthly salary. 20 supervisors average ₹36,000. What percentage of the total monthly salary bill is paid to supervisors?

    1. 33.333
    2. 50
    3. 60
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 50

    Explanation: Supervisors’ share = (20 × 36000) ÷ (60 × 24000) × 100 = 50%.

  19. The values 10, 20 and 30 have frequencies 3, x and 2 respectively. Their mean is 19. Find x.

    1. 4
    2. 6
    3. 5
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5

    Explanation: Deviations from 19 give 3 × (−9) + x + 2 × 11 = 0. Hence x = 5.

  20. In an examination, paper X has maximum 150 marks and paper Y has maximum 250 marks. Aman scores 72% in X. What percentage must he score in Y to obtain 80% overall?

    1. 84.8
    2. 88
    3. 80
    4. 86.4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 84.8

    Explanation: Required marks in Y = 0.8 × 400 − 0.72 × 150 = 212. Percentage = 212 × 100 ÷ 250 = 84.8%.

  21. 3 numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5 : 8 and average 48. Find the difference between the largest and the smallest.

    1. 45
    2. 40
    3. 48
    4. 72
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 45

    Explanation: 1 ratio unit = 3 × 48 ÷ 16 = 9. Difference = (8 − 3) × 9 = 45.

  22. A team scores 168 runs in 20 completed overs. Find its average run rate per over.

    1. 8
    2. 8.4
    3. 8.2
    4. 8.6
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 8.4

    Explanation: Run rate = 168 ÷ 20 = 8.4 runs per over.

  23. 10 nurses average 28 years. 2 new nurses join; their ages differ by 4 years. The new group averages 29 years. Find the older newcomer’s age in years.

    1. 36
    2. 34
    3. 32
    4. 38
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 36

    Explanation: Newcomers total = 12 × 29 − 10 × 28 = 68. Older age = (68 + 4) ÷ 2 = 36 years.

  24. What is the average of A, B and C? I: The averages of A and B, and B and C, are 25 and 31 respectively. II: The average of A and C is 34. Choose the code: 1 = I alone is sufficient; 2 = II alone is sufficient; 3 = both together are needed and sufficient; 4 = both together are insufficient.

    1. 1
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 3

    Explanation: I leaves 1 variable free, and II gives only 1 pair total. Together, average = (25 + 31 + 34) ÷ 3 = 30, so both are needed and sufficient.

  25. A hostel’s food bill is ₹18,600 for 30 residents. After 10 more residents join, the bill becomes ₹22,800. By how much does the average bill per resident fall, in ₹?

    1. 40
    2. 50
    3. 60
    4. 70
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 50

    Explanation: Old average = 18600 ÷ 30 = 620; new average = 22800 ÷ 40 = 570. Fall = ₹50.
