Question bank

Fundamental Rights and Writs - Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Fundamental Rights are called 'fundamental' mainly because they are:

    1. Granted by Parliament through ordinary law
    2. Applicable only during emergency
    3. Borrowed from the British Constitution
    4. Enshrined in the Constitution and justiciable
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Enshrined in the Constitution and justiciable

    Explanation: They are enshrined in the Constitution and are justiciable — a person can directly approach the High Court or Supreme Court if they are violated.

  2. How many Fundamental Rights are there in the Indian Constitution at present?

    1. Eight
    2. Seven
    3. Five
    4. Six
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Six

    Explanation: Originally there were seven Fundamental Rights; currently there are six.

  3. Which Fundamental Right was deleted by the 44th Amendment Act, 1978?

    1. Right to Property
    2. Right to Freedom of Religion
    3. Right to Constitutional Remedies
    4. Right against Exploitation
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Right to Property

    Explanation: The Right to Property (Article 31) was deleted by the 44th Amendment, 1978 and made a legal right under Article 300-A.

  4. At present, the Right to Property is a:

    1. Fundamental Duty under Article 51A
    2. Fundamental Right under Article 31
    3. Directive Principle under Article 39
    4. Legal right under Article 300-A
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Legal right under Article 300-A

    Explanation: The Right to Property is now a legal (constitutional) right under Article 300-A in Part XII.

  5. In which Part of the Constitution does Article 300-A (Right to Property) lie?

    1. Part III
    2. Part IV
    3. Part XII
    4. Part XI
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Part XII

    Explanation: Article 300-A is in Part XII (Finance, Property, Contracts and Suits).

  6. The Right to Equality is contained in which Articles?

    1. 23–24
    2. 25–28
    3. 14–18
    4. 19–22
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 14–18

    Explanation: Right to Equality: Articles 14–18.

  7. Match the Fundamental Rights with Articles:A.Right to Freedom – 1. 23–24B.Right against Exploitation – 2. 29–30C.Freedom of Religion – 3. 19–22D.Cultural and Educational Rights – 4. 25–28

    1. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
    2. A-3, B-4, C-1, D-2
    3. A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2
    4. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2

    Explanation: Freedom 19–22; Exploitation 23–24; Religion 25–28; Cultural and Educational 29–30.

  8. Fundamental Rights in the Indian Constitution are derived from the Constitution of:

    1. UK
    2. Ireland
    3. Canada
    4. USA
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) USA

    Explanation: Fundamental Rights are derived from the Constitution of the USA.

  9. Which part of the Indian Constitution is described as its 'Magna Carta'?

    1. Preamble
    2. Fundamental Rights
    3. Directive Principles
    4. Fundamental Duties
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Fundamental Rights

    Explanation: Fundamental Rights are described as the Magna Carta of the Indian Constitution.

  10. Which of the following Articles is available to foreigners also?

    1. Article 29
    2. Article 15
    3. Article 14
    4. Article 19
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 14

    Explanation: Articles 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30 are not available to foreigners; Article 14 (equality before law) applies to all persons.

  11. Which set of Articles is NOT available to foreigners?

    1. 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30
    2. 14, 20, 21 and 22
    3. 20, 21, 23 and 24
    4. 14, 15, 25 and 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30

    Explanation: Rights under Articles 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30 are available only to citizens.

  12. During a national emergency, which Fundamental Rights cannot be suspended?

    1. Articles 21 and 32
    2. Articles 20 and 21
    3. Articles 19 and 21
    4. Articles 14 and 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Articles 20 and 21

    Explanation: Fundamental Rights can be suspended during a national emergency, except those under Articles 20 and 21.

  13. Consider the following statements:1.Parliament can amend Fundamental Rights through a constitutional amendment.2.Such an amendment can alter the basic structure of the Constitution.Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1 only

    Explanation: Parliament can amend Fundamental Rights, but only if the amendment does not alter the basic structure of the Constitution.

  14. The application of Fundamental Rights can be restricted in an area placed under:

    1. Financial Emergency only
    2. Fifth Schedule
    3. President's Rule
    4. Martial law
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Martial law

    Explanation: The application of Fundamental Rights can be restricted in an area under martial law (military rule).

  15. Which Article of the Constitution defines the term 'State' for the purpose of Fundamental Rights?

    1. Article 36
    2. Article 1
    3. Article 13
    4. Article 12
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 12

    Explanation: Article 12 defines 'State'.

  16. Which of the following is included in the definition of 'State' under Article 12?

    1. A private company with no state control
    2. A registered trade union
    3. LIC, ONGC and SAIL
    4. A political party
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) LIC, ONGC and SAIL

    Explanation: 'State' includes all other authorities — statutory or non-statutory — like LIC, ONGC and SAIL.

  17. Under Article 12, a private body is included in the definition of 'State' when:

    1. It is registered under the Companies Act
    2. It employs more than 100 people
    3. It works as an instrument of the State
    4. It earns profit
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) It works as an instrument of the State

    Explanation: A private body or agency working as an instrument of the State is included in 'State'.

  18. Local authorities such as municipalities and panchayats are included in the definition of 'State' under:

    1. Article 40
    2. Article 12
    3. Article 243
    4. Article 13
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 12

    Explanation: Article 12 includes all local authorities — municipalities, panchayats, district boards, improvement trusts, etc.

  19. Which Article provides for the doctrine of judicial review?

    1. Article 12
    2. Article 13
    3. Article 14
    4. Article 368
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 13

    Explanation: Article 13 provides for the doctrine of judicial review.

  20. Under Article 13, which courts can declare invalid any law that affects Fundamental Rights?

    1. Only the Supreme Court
    2. The Supreme Court and the High Courts
    3. District Courts
    4. Only the High Courts
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) The Supreme Court and the High Courts

    Explanation: Article 13 gives power to the Supreme Court (Art. 32) and High Courts (Art. 226) to declare such laws invalid.

  21. 'Equality before law and equal protection of laws' is guaranteed by:

    1. Article 14
    2. Article 21
    3. Article 15
    4. Article 16
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 14

    Explanation: Article 14 guarantees equality before law and equal protection of laws.

  22. Article 15 prohibits discrimination on grounds of:

    1. Religion, race, caste, sex or place of birth
    2. Religion, language, wealth or residence
    3. Caste, income, education or sex
    4. Race, property, profession or language
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Religion, race, caste, sex or place of birth

    Explanation: Article 15 prohibits discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex or place of birth.

  23. Equality of opportunity in matters of public employment is provided under:

    1. Article 15
    2. Article 16
    3. Article 14
    4. Article 19
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 16

    Explanation: Article 16 provides equality of opportunity in matters of public employment.

  24. Which Article abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form?

    1. Article 18
    2. Article 23
    3. Article 17
    4. Article 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 17

    Explanation: Article 17 abolishes untouchability and prohibits its practice.

  25. Article 18 abolishes all titles EXCEPT:

    1. Titles conferred by foreign states
    2. Royal and religious titles
    3. Military and academic titles
    4. Hereditary titles
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Military and academic titles

    Explanation: Article 18 abolishes titles except military and academic ones.
