    Explanation: Composer Shantanu Moitra was selected; the award carries a Rs 5 lakh tax-free cash prize. Lata Mangeshkar received the Bharat Ratna in 2001.

    Explanation: NCB was established in 1986 under the Ministry of Home Affairs; its key law is the NDPS Act, 1985.
