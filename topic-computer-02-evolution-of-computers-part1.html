Question bank

Evolution of Computers - Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Which is considered the earliest known calculating device?

    1. Pascaline
    2. Abacus
    3. Analytical Engine
    4. ENIAC
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Abacus

    Explanation: Abacus = First known calculating device.

  2. In which country was the Abacus developed?

    1. China
    2. Japan
    3. Egypt
    4. Greece
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) China

    Explanation: The Abacus was developed in China.

  3. The Abacus was developed approximately how many years ago?

    1. 2000 years
    2. 3000 years
    3. 5000 years
    4. 1000 years
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5000 years

    Explanation: The Abacus was developed in China around 5000 years ago.

  4. Who invented the Pascaline (Adding Machine)?

    1. Blaise Pascal
    2. Charles Babbage
    3. Herman Hollerith
    4. Alan Turing
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Blaise Pascal

    Explanation: Exam Point: Blaise Pascal invented the Pascaline (Adding Machine).

  5. The first successful mechanical calculator (Pascaline) was invented around:

    1. 1830–1835
    2. 1890–1895
    3. 1944–1946
    4. 1642–1645
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1642–1645

    Explanation: Blaise Pascal invented the first successful mechanical calculator around 1642–1645.

  6. Which operations could the Pascaline perform?

    1. Multiplication and Division only
    2. All four arithmetic operations
    3. Addition and Subtraction only
    4. Logical operations only
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Addition and Subtraction only

    Explanation: The Pascaline performed addition and subtraction.

  7. Who designed the Analytical Engine?

    1. Blaise Pascal
    2. Charles Babbage
    3. John W. Mauchly
    4. Ada Lovelace
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Charles Babbage

    Explanation: The Analytical Engine was designed by Charles Babbage.

  8. The Analytical Engine was designed during:

    1. The early 1830s
    2. The early 1640s
    3. The 1890s
    4. The 1940s
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) The early 1830s

    Explanation: Charles Babbage designed the Analytical Engine during the early 1830s.

  9. The Analytical Engine is considered the first design of a:

    1. General-purpose electronic digital computer
    2. Commercial computer
    3. General-purpose mechanical computer
    4. Stored-program computer
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) General-purpose mechanical computer

    Explanation: The Analytical Engine is considered the first design of a general-purpose mechanical computer.

  10. Who is known as the "Father of the Computer"?

    1. Alan Turing
    2. Blaise Pascal
    3. J. Presper Eckert
    4. Charles Babbage
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Charles Babbage

    Explanation: Because of his contribution, Charles Babbage is known as the Father of the Computer.

  11. What is the full form of ENIAC?

    1. Electronic Numeric Information and Calculator
    2. Electrical Numerical Integrator and Calculator
    3. Electronic Network Integrator and Computer
    4. Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer

    Explanation: ENIAC stands for Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer.

  12. ENIAC was developed by:

    1. John W. Mauchly and J. Presper Eckert
    2. Charles Babbage and Ada Lovelace
    3. Alan Turing and Herman Hollerith
    4. Blaise Pascal and Charles Babbage
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) John W. Mauchly and J. Presper Eckert

    Explanation: ENIAC was developed by John W. Mauchly and J. Presper Eckert.

  13. In which year was ENIAC proposed?

    1. 1946
    2. 1951
    3. 1944
    4. 1942
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1944

    Explanation: ENIAC was proposed in 1944 and completed in 1946.

  14. In which year was ENIAC completed?

    1. 1944
    2. 1946
    3. 1951
    4. 1964
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1946

    Explanation: ENIAC was completed in 1946.

  15. ENIAC used thousands of:

    1. Transistors
    2. Vacuum tubes
    3. Integrated circuits
    4. Microprocessors
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Vacuum tubes

    Explanation: ENIAC was fully electronic, very large and used thousands of vacuum tubes.

  16. Which is regarded as the first general-purpose electronic digital computer?

    1. ENIAC
    2. EDSAC
    3. UNIVAC-I
    4. Analytical Engine
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) ENIAC

    Explanation: Exam Point: ENIAC is regarded as the first general-purpose electronic digital computer.

  17. What is the full form of EDSAC?

    1. Electronic Digital Storage Automatic Computer
    2. Electronic Data Storage Automatic Calculator
    3. Electronic Delay System Automatic Computer
    4. Electronic Delay Storage Automatic Calculator
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Electronic Delay Storage Automatic Calculator

    Explanation: EDSAC stands for Electronic Delay Storage Automatic Calculator.

  18. EDSAC was developed at:

    1. University of Pennsylvania (USA)
    2. Bell Laboratories (USA)
    3. University of Cambridge (England)
    4. MIT (USA)
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) University of Cambridge (England)

    Explanation: EDSAC was developed at the University of Cambridge, England.

  19. EDSAC was among the first ______ computers.

    1. Stored-program
    2. Commercial
    3. Mechanical
    4. Analog
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Stored-program

    Explanation: EDSAC could store programs and instructions in memory; it was among the first stored-program computers.

  20. Which was the first commercially available computer?

    1. ENIAC
    2. UNIVAC-I
    3. EDSAC
    4. Pascaline
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) UNIVAC-I

    Explanation: Exam Point: UNIVAC-I = First Commercial Computer.

  21. In which year was UNIVAC-I introduced?

    1. 1946
    2. 1944
    3. 1951
    4. 1964
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1951

    Explanation: UNIVAC-I was introduced in 1951.

  22. Who is known as the first computer programmer?

    1. Ada Lovelace
    2. Alan Turing
    3. Charles Babbage
    4. Herman Hollerith
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Ada Lovelace

    Explanation: Ada Lovelace wrote the first algorithm for Babbage's Analytical Engine and is the first programmer.

  23. Alan Turing, a British mathematician and logician, is known as the:

    1. Father of the Computer
    2. Father of Computer Science
    3. First Computer Programmer
    4. Inventor of the Punch Card Machine
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Father of Computer Science

    Explanation: Alan Turing is known as the Father of Computer Science (and often of Artificial Intelligence).

  24. Herman Hollerith invented the Punch Card Machine used in the U.S. Census of:

    1. 1946
    2. 1951
    3. 1830
    4. 1890
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1890

    Explanation: Herman Hollerith invented the Punch Card Machine used in the 1890 U.S. Census.

  25. Herman Hollerith's work led to the formation of a company that later became:

    1. C-DAC
    2. Cray Research
    3. Apple
    4. IBM
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) IBM

    Explanation: Hollerith's work led to a company that later became IBM (International Business Machines).
