Question bank

Ranking and Time Sequence Test MCQ – Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. In a merit list, Navjot is 18th from the top and 24th from the bottom. How many candidates are listed?

    1. 42
    2. 43
    3. 41
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 41

    Explanation: Total = 18 + 24 - 1 = 41.

  2. A row has 53 people. Isha is 17th from the left and Kabir is 14th from the right. How many people stand between them?

    1. 22
    2. 24
    3. 23
    4. 21
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 22

    Explanation: Kabir is 53 - 14 + 1 = 40th from the left; the gap is 40 - 17 - 1 = 22.

  3. Preet is 12th from the left and Danish is 15th from the right. Exactly 6 people separate them. What is the minimum possible row strength?

    1. 33
    2. 20
    3. 19
    4. 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 19

    Explanation: Danish can be at left position 5 or 19. The totals are 5 + 15 - 1 = 19 and 19 + 15 - 1 = 33; choose 19.

  4. Ritu, 9th from the left, swaps with Farhan, 13th from the right. Ritu becomes 26th from the left. What is Farhan’s new rank from the right?

    1. 31
    2. 29
    3. 28
    4. 30
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 30

    Explanation: Total = 26 + 13 - 1 = 38. Farhan occupies left position 9, so his right rank is 38 - 9 + 1 = 30.

  5. Tara is heavier than Mehak but lighter than Zoya. Zoya is lighter than Bani. Who is the heaviest?

    1. Zoya
    2. Bani
    3. Mehak
    4. Tara
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Bani

    Explanation: Descending order is Bani > Zoya > Tara > Mehak.

  6. Sahil stands 21st from the front. He moves forward past 8 people and then back past 3 people. What is his final front rank?

    1. 18
    2. 16
    3. 15
    4. 10
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 16

    Explanation: Final rank = 21 - 8 + 3 = 16.

  7. Jasleen ranks 14th among 48 candidates. 5 candidates above her and 7 below her leave. Then 4 new candidates are placed above her. Find her rank from the bottom.

    1. 28
    2. 24
    3. 29
    4. 27
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 28

    Explanation: New total = 48 - 12 + 4 = 40; new top rank = 14 - 5 + 4 = 13. Bottom rank = 40 - 13 + 1 = 28.

  8. A night bus leaves Patiala at 22:48 and reaches its destination at 03:17 the next day. It stops for 34 minutes en route. How many minutes is it moving?

    1. 269
    2. 245
    3. 235
    4. 225
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 235

    Explanation: Elapsed time is 72 + 197 = 269 minutes. Moving time = 269 - 34 = 235 minutes.

  9. A recruitment camp starts on Thursday. What weekday is its 19th day, counting the starting day as Day 1?

    1. Tuesday
    2. Monday
    3. Wednesday
    4. Sunday
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Monday

    Explanation: Day 19 is 18 days later; 18 mod 7 = 4. Thursday + 4 days is Monday.

  10. In a mixed row, Simran is 23rd from the left and the 9th girl from that end. How many boys stand to her left?

    1. 15
    2. 14
    3. 16
    4. 13
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 14

    Explanation: There are 22 people before her, including 8 girls. Boys = 22 - 8 = 14.

  11. Two rows of 18 seats face each other. Row I faces north and Row II faces south. A is 6th from his own left in Row I. B sits opposite A. C is 4 seats to B’s own left in Row II. What is C’s rank from his own right?

    1. 17
    2. 2
    3. 9
    4. 10
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 10

    Explanation: B is 6th from his own right. Moving 4 seats to B’s left increases the right rank to 6 + 4 = 10.

  12. For an interview, Karan arrives 16 minutes early. Noor arrives 27 minutes after Karan, at 10:41. At what time was the interview scheduled?

    1. 10:36
    2. 10:25
    3. 10:14
    4. 10:30
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 10:30

    Explanation: Karan arrives at 10:41 - 27 minutes = 10:14. Scheduled time = 10:14 + 16 minutes = 10:30.

  13. A station clock is always 11 minutes slow. It displays 17:46. What is the actual time?

    1. 17:57
    2. 18:03
    3. 18:46
    4. 17:35
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 17:57

    Explanation: Add the slow error: 17:46 + 11 minutes = 17:57.

  14. In a queue, twice as many people stand behind Aman as ahead of him. If he is 13th from the front, what is the queue strength?

    1. 39
    2. 36
    3. 38
    4. 37
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 37

    Explanation: Ahead = 12 and behind = 24. Total = 12 + 1 + 24 = 37.

  15. At actual 07:00, a clock is 9 minutes slow. It then gains 3 minutes per true hour. Later it shows 11:03. What is the actual time then?

    1. 11:06
    2. 11:00
    3. 11:12
    4. 10:54
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 11:00

    Explanation: Initial display is 06:51; displayed advance is 252 minutes. True elapsed = 252 × 60/63 = 240 minutes, giving 11:00.

  16. Leena is 8th from the left and Om is 28th from the left. A person stands exactly midway between them. What is that person’s left rank?

    1. 18
    2. 20
    3. 17
    4. 19
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 18

    Explanation: The midpoint rank is (8 + 28)/2 = 18.

  17. An online test starts at 14:35 and lasts 1 hour 48 minutes without a break. When does it finish?

    1. 16:23
    2. 15:23
    3. 16:33
    4. 16:13
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 16:23

    Explanation: 14:35 + 1 hour = 15:35; adding 48 minutes gives 16:23.

  18. 4 runners finish the same distance. P takes 48 seconds; Q takes 3 seconds longer than P; R takes 2 seconds less than Q; S takes 1 second less than P. Who is second fastest?

    1. Q
    2. P
    3. S
    4. R
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) P

    Explanation: Times are S = 47, P = 48, R = 49 and Q = 51 seconds. Lower time means faster, so P is second.

  19. A, B and C initially occupy left positions 7, 19 and 32 in a row of 44. First A swaps with B, then B swaps with C, and finally A swaps with C. What is A’s final rank from the right?

    1. 38
    2. 26
    3. 37
    4. 13
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 38

    Explanation: After the first two swaps, A is at 19 and C at 7. The last swap puts A at 7, so its right rank is 44 - 7 + 1 = 38.

  20. 6 workshops P, Q, R, S, T and U occupy consecutive days, one daily. P is immediately before S; R is immediately after S; Q is before P; U is last; T is after R. Which workshop is on Day 4?

    1. T
    2. S
    3. P
    4. R
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) R

    Explanation: The block P-S-R needs Q before it and T after it, with U last. The only order is Q-P-S-R-T-U.

  21. A file is 16th from the top in a stack of 61 files. How many files lie below it?

    1. 46
    2. 47
    3. 45
    4. 44
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 45

    Explanation: Files below = 61 - 16 = 45.

  22. In a row, Deep is 10th and Harnoor is 16th from the left. Deep leaves his place and inserts himself immediately after the person originally 20th. What is Harnoor’s new left rank?

    1. 14
    2. 16
    3. 15
    4. 17
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 15

    Explanation: Removing Deep shifts original positions 11 through 20 one place left. Harnoor moves from 16 to 15.

  23. Distinct persons X and Y are 17th from the left and 11th from the right. Exactly 3 people lie between them. What is the sum of all possible row strengths?

    1. 54
    2. 56
    3. 50
    4. 62
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 54

    Explanation: Y can be at left position 13 or 21, giving totals 23 or 31. Their sum is 23 + 31 = 54.

  24. A tower makes 9 equally spaced strikes in 24 seconds, measured from the first to the ninth. How many seconds will 14 strikes take at the same spacing?

    1. 45
    2. 36
    3. 42
    4. 39
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 39

    Explanation: 8 gaps take 24 seconds, so each gap takes 3 seconds. 14 strikes have 13 gaps: 13 × 3 = 39 seconds.

  25. In a merit list of 72, Dev is 21st. The top 12 and bottom 18 candidates are removed. Of those remaining, only candidates at odd-numbered original ranks are selected. What is Dev’s rank from the bottom in this selected group?

    1. 19
    2. 18
    3. 17
    4. 16
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 17

    Explanation: Selected original ranks are 13 through 53 in steps of 2. From 21 through 53 there are (53 - 21)/2 + 1 = 17 selected candidates.
