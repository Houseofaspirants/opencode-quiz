About this cluster

What Modern History covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Modern History carries the densest question density in Punjab papers because it links events, organisations and personalities in a single chain. Exams tend to ask about the founding of bodies, the sequence of movements, key acts and agreements, and the leaders associated with each phase. Because the period is short compared with the ancient and medieval eras, it rewards careful sequencing: if you can place an event on the timeline, you can usually eliminate two of the four options immediately. Punjab papers also lean on the freedom movement as it played out in the province, so regional participation belongs in the same revision pass as the national story. Work through the lanes below in order to keep the sequence intact.
