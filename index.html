Frequently asked questions

The questions new aspirants ask us most often.

Is House of Aspirants really free?

Yes. Every quiz, mock test, study guide, bookmark and leaderboard feature is free. There is no paid tier, no trial and no card required, and the portal carries no intrusive advertising.

Which exams does this portal cover?

Punjab Police Constable, ASI, SI, Intelligence Assistant and Jail Warder, plus PSSSB, PPSC and PSPCL posts such as Patwari, Forest Guard, Clerk, Senior Assistant and Lab Attendant.

Do I need to sign in before taking a quiz?

No. Open any subject, pick a topic and start answering. Google sign-in is optional and is only used to save your scores, bookmarks and streaks across devices.

What is the Daily Quiz?

A fresh mixed challenge published every day from all seven subjects. It takes a few minutes, resets daily and counts towards your streak and your leaderboard position.

Can I practise on my phone without internet?

Yes. After the first visit the app shell is cached, so opened pages, saved quizzes and bookmarks keep working offline. The interface is mobile-first and installs to your home screen.
