Question bank

Decimal Fractions MCQ – Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Which decimal is less than 9/16?

    1. 0.56
    2. 0.5625
    3. 0.565
    4. 0.57
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 0.56

    Explanation: 9/16 = 0.5625; only 0.56 is smaller than this value.

  2. Evaluate 9.36 ÷ 0.12 + 1.8 ÷ 0.03.

    1. 84
    2. 780
    3. 138
    4. 13.8
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 138

    Explanation: Clear the decimals in each quotient: 936/12 + 180/3 = 78 + 60 = 138.

  3. Let x = 0.3(18) and y = 0.(27), where the bracketed digits repeat. Find 22(x − y).

    1. 10
    2. 1
    3. 2
    4. 0.1
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1

    Explanation: x = (318 − 3)/990 = 7/22 and y = 27/99 = 3/11, so 22(x − y) = 22 × 1/22 = 1.

  4. Gurleen walks 1.35 km in the morning and 0.85 km in the evening each day. How many km does she walk in 15 days?

    1. 30
    2. 20.25
    3. 3.3
    4. 33
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 33

    Explanation: Daily distance = 1.35 + 0.85 = 2.2 km; total = 2.2 × 15 = 33 km.

  5. In 52.076, which digit has a place value of 0.07?

    1. 6
    2. 5
    3. 7
    4. 0
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 7

    Explanation: The hundredths digit is 7, and its place value is 7/100 = 0.07.

  6. What is the difference between the largest and smallest of 0.68, 2/3, 0.675 and 27/40?

    1. 1/75
    2. 1/150
    3. 1/200
    4. 1/120
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1/75

    Explanation: The largest is 0.68 = 17/25 and the smallest is 2/3; the difference is 17/25 − 2/3 = 1/75.

  7. If 0.2x + 0.3y = 1.8 and 0.3x + 0.2y = 1.7, find x² − y².

    1. 7
    2. −1
    3. 49
    4. −7
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) −7

    Explanation: Adding gives 0.5(x + y) = 3.5, so x + y = 7; subtracting gives 0.1(y − x) = 0.1, so x − y = −1. Thus x² − y² = (x + y)(x − y) = −7.

  8. If 48.6 ÷ 0.18 = 270, find 0.486 ÷ 1.8.

    1. 27
    2. 0.27
    3. 0.027
    4. 2.7
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 0.27

    Explanation: The dividend becomes 1/100 as large and the divisor becomes 10 times as large; hence the quotient is 270 ÷ 1000 = 0.27.

  9. Which recurring decimal equals 8/11? In each option, the digits in brackets repeat.

    1. 0.(81)
    2. 0.(72)
    3. 0.(8)
    4. 0.(18)
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 0.(72)

    Explanation: 8/11 = 72/99, so its repeating block is 72 and the decimal is 0.(72).

  10. Find x: 3.6x + 0.84 = 5.4.

    1. 4/3
    2. 17/10
    3. 19/150
    4. 19/15
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 19/15

    Explanation: 3.6x = 5.4 − 0.84 = 4.56, so x = 4.56/3.6 = 19/15.

  11. Data sufficiency: What is the marked price per kg of a batch of wheat? I. The batch costs ₹1134 after a discount of 0.1 of its marked price. II. Its mass is the amount left after 0.1 of a 35 kg stock is removed.

    1. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.
    2. Statement II alone is sufficient; I alone is not.
    3. Each statement alone is sufficient.
    4. Statement I alone is sufficient; II alone is not.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone is sufficient.

    Explanation: I gives marked total = 1134/0.9 = ₹1260, while II gives mass = 35 × 0.9 = 31.5 kg. Together the marked price per kg is 1260/31.5 = ₹40; neither statement alone gives it.

  12. A quantity is first increased by 20% and then multiplied by 0.75. What percentage of the original quantity remains?

    1. 120
    2. 95
    3. 90
    4. 80
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 90

    Explanation: Combined multiplier = 1.2 × 0.75 = 0.9, which is 90% of the original.

  13. Evaluate 0.0072 ÷ 0.9.

    1. 0.08
    2. 0.0008
    3. 0.8
    4. 0.008
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 0.008

    Explanation: Multiply both numbers by 10: 0.072 ÷ 9 = 0.008.

  14. Find the sum of the place values of the two occurrences of 6 in 6.406.

    1. 6.6
    2. 6.006
    3. 0.606
    4. 6.06
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 6.006

    Explanation: The two place values are 6 and 0.006, so their sum is 6.006.

  15. Which is the third number when 0.71, 12/17, 5/7, 0.708 and 0.715 are arranged in ascending order?

    1. 12/17
    2. 0.708
    3. 0.71
    4. 5/7
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 0.71

    Explanation: The order is 12/17 < 0.708 < 0.71 < 5/7 < 0.715, so the third number is 0.71.

  16. Evaluate 0.04(7) + 0.00(2), where the bracketed digits repeat.

    1. 0.05
    2. 0.049
    3. 0.045
    4. 0.005
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 0.05

    Explanation: Convert to fractions: (47 − 4)/900 + 2/900 = 45/900 = 0.05.

  17. What is the smallest positive integer n for which 0.00056 × 10^n is an integer?

    1. 5
    2. 3
    3. 6
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 5

    Explanation: There are 5 decimal places and the last digit is nonzero; 0.00056 × 10^5 = 56.

  18. Evaluate 2.75 × 6.4 − 1.25 × 6.4.

    1. 0.96
    2. 25.6
    3. 9.6
    4. 1.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9.6

    Explanation: Take 6.4 common: (2.75 − 1.25) × 6.4 = 1.5 × 6.4 = 9.6.

  19. Two positive decimals have product 0.0675, and one is three times the other. Find their sum.

    1. 0.3
    2. 0.6
    3. 0.45
    4. 0.9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 0.6

    Explanation: Let the numbers be x and 3x; then x² = 0.0675/3 = 0.0225, so x = 0.15. Their sum is 4 × 0.15 = 0.6.

  20. A water tank contains 48.75 litres. After 6.25 litres are used, the rest is shared equally among 5 containers. How many litres does each container receive?

    1. 11
    2. 7.5
    3. 9.75
    4. 8.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 8.5

    Explanation: Water left = 48.75 − 6.25 = 42.5 litres; each container gets 42.5 ÷ 5 = 8.5 litres.

  21. Which percentage is equal to 7/20?

    1. 70
    2. 3.5
    3. 35
    4. 28
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 35

    Explanation: 7/20 × 100 = 35%.

  22. How many of these equalities are correct: 0.7 = 7/10; 0.07 = 7/1000; 0.007 = 7/10000; 0.0007 = 7/1000?

    1. 1
    2. 0
    3. 2
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1

    Explanation: Only 0.7 = 7/10 is correct. The correct fractions for 0.07, 0.007 and 0.0007 are 7/100, 7/1000 and 7/10000.

  23. If N = 0.006 × 0.0008 ÷ 0.00012, how many places must its decimal point move right to obtain 400?

    1. 5
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 4

    Explanation: N = 0.04, and 0.04 × 10000 = 400; the required shift is 4 places right.

  24. Evaluate 14.625 − 3.875 − 2.125 − 0.625.

    1. 7.375
    2. 8
    3. 8.625
    4. 9.25
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 8

    Explanation: Group the subtractions: 3.875 + 2.125 = 6 and 14.625 − 0.625 = 14; hence 14 − 6 = 8.

  25. If x − 1/x = 0.5, find x² + 1/x².

    1. 0.25
    2. 1.25
    3. 2.25
    4. 2.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 2.25

    Explanation: Squaring gives x² + 1/x² − 2 = 0.25, so the required value is 2.25.
