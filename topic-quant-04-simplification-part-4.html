Question bank

Simplification MCQ – Part 4: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Find the value of ? in 84 ÷ [? − (19 − 8)] = 7.

    1. 12
    2. 1
    3. 23
    4. 30
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 23

    Explanation: The divisor must be 12; therefore ? − 11 = 12 and ? = 23.

  2. Evaluate: ∛(1728 ÷ 27).

    1. 8
    2. 4
    3. 3
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 4

    Explanation: The radicand is 64; its cube root is 4.

  3. For real x, (x − 2)³ + (x − 11)³ + (x − 14)³ = 3(x − 2)(x − 11)(x − 14). Find x.

    1. 9
    2. 7
    3. 11
    4. 27
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 9

    Explanation: The three terms cannot all be equal, so the cubic identity forces their sum to be zero: 3x − 27 = 0, giving x = 9.

  4. Evaluate: (4 + 1/2) ÷ (1 + 4/5) − (2 + 1/6).

    1. 2/3
    2. 13/3
    3. 1/6
    4. 1/3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1/3

    Explanation: The quotient is (9/2) × (5/9) = 5/2; subtract 13/6 to get 1/3.

  5. Amandeep reads 3/10 of a book on Monday and 2/7 of the remaining pages on Tuesday. If 150 pages remain, how many pages did the book have?

    1. 210
    2. 300
    3. 420
    4. 350
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 300

    Explanation: The unread fraction is (7/10)(5/7) = 1/2; hence total pages = 150 × 2 = 300.

  6. Evaluate: [(3/4 + 1/6) ÷ (5/8 − 1/3)] ÷ (1 + 1/7).

    1. 11/2
    2. 22/7
    3. 11/4
    4. 7/22
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 11/4

    Explanation: The first quotient is (11/12) ÷ (7/24) = 22/7; dividing by 8/7 gives 11/4.

  7. Evaluate: √0.0081 × 100.

    1. 9
    2. 81
    3. 0.9
    4. 90
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 9

    Explanation: √0.0081 = 0.09; multiplying by 100 gives 9.

  8. In a survey, 35 people use only app X, 28 use only app Y, 17 use both, and 12 use neither. How many people were surveyed?

    1. 75
    2. 109
    3. 80
    4. 92
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 92

    Explanation: These four groups do not overlap; add 35 + 28 + 17 + 12 = 92.

  9. Find the positive value of x if x² − 14x + 49 = 4x² − 28x + 49.

    1. 14/3
    2. 7/3
    3. 7
    4. 14
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 14/3

    Explanation: Cancel 49 and rearrange: 3x² − 14x = x(3x − 14) = 0. The positive solution is 14/3.

  10. Round 83.746 to two decimal places and 6.284 to two decimal places. What is their sum?

    1. 89.93
    2. 90.04
    3. 90.02
    4. 90.03
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 90.03

    Explanation: The rounded numbers are 83.75 and 6.28; their sum is 90.03.

  11. Evaluate: 49².

    1. 2499
    2. 2401
    3. 2501
    4. 2399
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2401

    Explanation: Use (50 − 1)² = 2500 − 100 + 1 = 2401.

  12. Evaluate: 4.08 ÷ 0.12 + 0.09 ÷ 0.003.

    1. 37
    2. 6.4
    3. 64
    4. 340
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 64

    Explanation: Scale each quotient separately: 408 ÷ 12 + 90 ÷ 3 = 34 + 30 = 64.

  13. Find (684² − 316²)/(684 − 316) − (684 − 316).

    1. 1368
    2. 632
    3. 368
    4. 1000
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 632

    Explanation: Cancel the difference factor to get 684 + 316; subtracting 684 − 316 leaves 2 × 316 = 632.

  14. Evaluate: (4/9 ÷ 2/3) + (5/8 ÷ 15/16).

    1. 5/3
    2. 8/9
    3. 2/3
    4. 4/3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 4/3

    Explanation: Multiply by reciprocals: 2/3 + 2/3 = 4/3.

  15. Evaluate: 625 × 32.

    1. 20000
    2. 18000
    3. 10000
    4. 2000
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 20000

    Explanation: Pair 625 × 16 = 10000; doubling gives 20000.

  16. A positive integer x satisfies ∛(8x) = √(4x). Find x.

    1. 16
    2. 4
    3. 1
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1

    Explanation: Raise both sides to power 6: (8x)² = (4x)³, giving 64x² = 64x³. Since x > 0, x = 1.

  17. Evaluate: 320 − [144 ÷ {6 + (15 − 9)}] × 7.

    1. 248
    2. 224
    3. 236
    4. 308
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 236

    Explanation: The divisor is 12, so subtract 12 × 7 = 84 from 320 to get 236.

  18. For 5/12 + 7/18, which option passes both the common-denominator calculation and the check that the result is below 1?

    1. 12/30
    2. 43/36
    3. 29/18
    4. 29/36
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 29/36

    Explanation: Use denominator 36: 15/36 + 14/36 = 29/36, which is below 1.

  19. Find the value of 87.5% of 192.

    1. 168
    2. 176
    3. 144
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 168

    Explanation: 87.5% = 7/8, giving 192 × 7/8 = 168.

  20. Find (5 + 2√6)/(5 − 2√6) + (5 − 2√6)/(5 + 2√6).

    1. 50
    2. 98
    3. 100
    4. 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 98

    Explanation: The product of the two denominators is 25 − 24 = 1; the combined numerator is 2(25 + 24) = 98.

  21. Evaluate: 630 ÷ 9 ÷ 5 × 4 + 26.

    1. 40
    2. 70
    3. 82
    4. 306
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 82

    Explanation: Successive division gives 630 ÷ 45 = 14; then 14 × 4 + 26 = 82.

  22. Evaluate: −8 × (−7) − (−36) ÷ 6.

    1. 50
    2. 62
    3. -62
    4. -50
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 62

    Explanation: Two negative factors give 56, and subtracting −6 gives 62.

  23. Data sufficiency: What is the price of one notebook? I: 4 notebooks and 3 pens cost ₹190. II: 8 notebooks and 6 pens cost ₹380.

    1. Even both statements together are insufficient
    2. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone
    3. Statement I alone is sufficient
    4. Statement II alone is sufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Even both statements together are insufficient

    Explanation: Statement II is exactly twice statement I, so there is only one independent equation for two prices. Even together they do not determine the notebook price.

  24. If x + 1/x = 7, find x^4 + 1/x^4.

    1. 2401
    2. 2209
    3. 2303
    4. 2207
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 2207

    Explanation: First x² + 1/x² = 49 − 2 = 47; squaring once more gives 47² − 2 = 2207.

  25. In 48 + 6 × 5 − 9, replace + by ÷, × by +, and − by × simultaneously once. Evaluate using normal precedence.

    1. 85
    2. 13
    3. 65
    4. 53
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 53

    Explanation: The new expression is 48 ÷ 6 + 5 × 9 = 8 + 45 = 53.
