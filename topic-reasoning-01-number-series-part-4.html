Question bank

Number Series MCQ – Part 4: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A square-number list was entered as 144, 169, 196, 226, 256, 289. Identify the typing error.

    1. 256
    2. 196
    3. 226
    4. 289
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 226

    Explanation: The terms are 12² through 17². Since 15² = 225, the printed term 226 is wrong.

  2. A sequence begins 20, 33, 42, 47. Its successive gaps decrease by 4 throughout. What is the largest term among its first 10 terms?

    1. 47
    2. 48
    3. 52
    4. 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 48

    Explanation: The gaps are 13, 9, 5, 1, −3, −7, ... . The sequence peaks at 47 + 1 = 48 and then decreases.

  3. Which value extends 720, 360, 1080, 270, 1350, 225, ? using the continuing operation pattern?

    1. 1350
    2. 1125
    3. 1925
    4. 1575
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1575

    Explanation: The operations are ÷2, ×3, ÷4, ×5, ÷6, then ×7. Thus 225 × 7 = 1575.

  4. The bases of 27, 125, 343, 1331, 2197, ? form a special number sequence. Find the next cube.

    1. 4913
    2. 4096
    3. 6859
    4. 3375
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 4913

    Explanation: The cube bases are consecutive primes 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, then 17. Hence 17³ = 4913.

  5. Each term after the first two is their running sum. Recover the first two terms, in order, of ?, ?, 17, 28, 45, 73.

    1. (11, 6)
    2. (6, 11)
    3. (5, 12)
    4. (7, 10)
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) (6, 11)

    Explanation: The second term is 28 − 17 = 11. The first term is 17 − 11 = 6, giving the pair (6, 11).

  6. Complete the alternating calculation chain 4, 11, 35, 73, 221, ?.

    1. 444
    2. 445
    3. 443
    4. 665
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 445

    Explanation: Alternate ×2 + 3 and ×3 + 2. The required step is 221 × 2 + 3 = 445.

  7. In 2, 121, 6, 100, 24, 81, 120, 64, 720, ?, which number completes the even-position strand?

    1. 36
    2. 5040
    3. 49
    4. 25
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 49

    Explanation: Odd positions contain 2!, 3!, 4!, 5!, 6!. Even positions are 11², 10², 9², 8², then 7² = 49.

  8. Examine 12, 18, 33, 68, 145, 288, ? . Its gaps are products of adjacent primes. Determine the next term.

    1. 457
    2. 431
    3. 535
    4. 509
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 509

    Explanation: The gaps are 2×3, 3×5, 5×7, 7×11, 11×13. Next add 13 × 17 = 221, giving 288 + 221 = 509.

  9. An 8-term arithmetic series starts at 17 and ends at 80. What is the sum of its 4th and 5th terms?

    1. 97
    2. 80
    3. 63
    4. 194
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 97

    Explanation: In an arithmetic series, equally distant pairs from the ends have the same sum. Thus the 4th and 5th terms add to 17 + 80 = 97.

  10. Only one entry is wrong in 4, 7, 9, 20, 36, 66, 121, 222. Locate it using a three-term recurrence.

    1. 222
    2. 121
    3. 36
    4. 66
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 66

    Explanation: Each term should equal the sum of the previous three. The sixth term is 9 + 20 + 36 = 65, and then 20 + 36 + 65 = 121 and 36 + 65 + 121 = 222; hence 66 is wrong.

  11. In a geometric series with a positive common ratio, the 2nd term is 54 and the 5th is 1458. What is the 3rd term?

    1. 216
    2. 486
    3. 108
    4. 162
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 162

    Explanation: Three ratio steps give 1458 ÷ 54 = 27 = 3³, so the common ratio is 3. The 3rd term is 54 × 3 = 162.

  12. What replaces the final blank in 12, 17, 45, 220, 1536, ? when prime multipliers and descending subtractions operate together?

    1. 16896
    2. 10749
    3. 16893
    4. 16889
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 16893

    Explanation: The operations are ×2 − 7, ×3 − 6, ×5 − 5, ×7 − 4. Next is ×11 − 3, giving 1536 × 11 − 3 = 16893.

  13. The recurrence in 2, 6, 9, 14, 24, 37, ? includes alternating adjustments. Select the next term.

    1. 62
    2. 61
    3. 60
    4. 63
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 62

    Explanation: Add the previous two terms, then alternate +1 and −1. The next adjustment is +1, so 24 + 37 + 1 = 62.

  14. The entries 19, 37, 61, 91, 127, ? equal differences of consecutive cubes. Find the missing entry.

    1. 169
    2. 168
    3. 171
    4. 153
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 169

    Explanation: The terms run from 3³ − 2³ through 7³ − 6³. Next is 8³ − 7³ = 512 − 343 = 169.

  15. In 47, 51, 42, 67, 18, 139, ?, the jumps combine primes, powers and alternating signs. Which number is next?

    1. -4
    2. -30
    3. -42
    4. 308
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) -30

    Explanation: The differences are +2², −3², +5², −7², +11². The next is −13² = −169, so 139 − 169 = −30.

  16. Two strands share the same changing factors in opposite ways: 3, 5040, 6, 2520, 18, 840, 72, 210, 360, 42, ?, ? . Choose the next pair.

    1. (2160, 7)
    2. (1800, 7)
    3. (2160, 6)
    4. (2520, 7)
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) (2160, 7)

    Explanation: Odd positions multiply by 2, 3, 4, 5, then 6; even positions divide by the same factors. Thus the next pair is (360 × 6, 42 ÷ 6) = (2160, 7).

  17. A processor repeatedly squares its current value and subtracts 2. Its outputs are 3, 7, ?, 2207. Recover the missing output.

    1. 51
    2. 49
    3. 47
    4. 45
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 47

    Explanation: The missing value is 7² − 2 = 47. It checks because 47² − 2 = 2207.

  18. Adding each term’s digit sum should generate the next term in 26, 34, 41, 46, 57, 67. Which entry violates this rule?

    1. 67
    2. 57
    3. 41
    4. 46
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 57

    Explanation: 46 + 4 + 6 = 56, not 57. With that correction, 56 + 5 + 6 = 67, so only 57 is wrong.

  19. Pair primes with exactly one prime between them: 3 + 7, 5 + 11, 7 + 13, and so on. Their sums form 10, 16, 20, 28, 32, 40, ? . Find the next sum.

    1. 44
    2. 52
    3. 46
    4. 48
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 48

    Explanation: The last given sum is 17 + 23 = 40. The next pair is 19 and 29, with only 23 between them, so 19 + 29 = 48.

  20. Which continuation fits 100, 102, 105, 109, 118, 126, 153, ? after splitting the gaps into two alternating lists?

    1. 177
    2. 185
    3. 180
    4. 169
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 169

    Explanation: The gaps are 2, 3, 4, 9, 8, 27. Alternate gap lists are 2, 4, 8, 16 and 3, 9, 27, so add 16: 153 + 16 = 169.

  21. A four-term moving window drives 1, 4, 6, 9, 18, 29, 50, 88, ? . Determine the next term.

    1. 167
    2. 159
    3. 149
    4. 138
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 149

    Explanation: Add the latest three terms and subtract the term immediately before them: 29 + 50 + 88 − 18 = 149. This also gives 18 + 29 + 50 − 9 = 88.

  22. A multiplication pattern repeats each multiplier twice: 5, 10, 20, 60, 180, 720, ? . Select the next number.

    1. 1440
    2. 3600
    3. 2160
    4. 2880
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 2880

    Explanation: The multipliers are 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4. Hence 720 × 4 = 2880.

  23. Puneet adds each number to the number formed by reversing its digits: 24, 66, 132, 363, 726, 1353, ? . What is next?

    1. 4888
    2. 3531
    3. 4884
    4. 2706
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 4884

    Explanation: Reversing 1353 gives 3531. Therefore 1353 + 3531 = 4884.

  24. Complete the second strand in 22, 6, 26, 10, 34, 15, 38, 21, 46, ?.

    1. 28
    2. 27
    3. 25
    4. 29
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 28

    Explanation: Odd positions are twice the consecutive primes 11, 13, 17, 19, 23. Even positions are triangular numbers 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, so the answer is 28.

  25. The series 50, 54, 62, 71, 98, 114, 178, ?, ? uses square and cube jumps in pairs. Which ordered pair completes it?

    1. (242, 367)
    2. (203, 328)
    3. (203, 303)
    4. (194, 319)
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) (203, 328)

    Explanation: The gaps are 2², 2³, 3², 3³, 4², 4³, then 5², 5³. Thus 178 + 25 = 203 and 203 + 125 = 328.
