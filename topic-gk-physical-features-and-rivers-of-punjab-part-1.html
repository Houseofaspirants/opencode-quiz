Question bank

Physical Features and Rivers of Punjab - Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. What is the average altitude of Punjab above sea level?

    1. 400–700 m
    2. 180–300 m
    3. 300–400 m
    4. Below 100 m
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 180–300 m

    Explanation: Punjab lies mostly 180–300 m above sea level. 400–700 m is the height of the Shiwalik Hills and 300–400 m is the Kandi belt.

  2. The height of the Shiwalik Hills in Punjab is:

    1. 180–300 m
    2. 300–400 m
    3. 400–700 m
    4. Below 180 m
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 400–700 m

    Explanation: The Shiwalik Hills rise to 400–700 m and are the highest landform of Punjab. The Kandi is 300–400 m and the plains 180–300 m.

  3. What is the width of the Shiwalik Hills belt in Punjab?

    1. 5–12 km
    2. 10–15 km
    3. 20–30 km
    4. 1–3 km
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 5–12 km

    Explanation: The Shiwalik belt is 5–12 km wide. The 10–15 km width belongs to the Kandi area at the foot of the hills.

  4. The foothill plain at the base of the Shiwalik Hills is called:

    1. Bet
    2. Tibba
    3. Doab
    4. Kandi
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Kandi

    Explanation: The Kandi is the foothill belt at the base of the Shiwalik Hills, 300–400 m high. Tibbas are sand dunes and a Doab is land between two rivers.

  5. What is the width of the Kandi belt?

    1. 5–12 km
    2. 10–15 km
    3. 25–40 km
    4. 50–60 km
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 10–15 km

    Explanation: The Kandi belt is 10–15 km wide and lies at a height of 300–400 m. The 5–12 km width is that of the Shiwalik Hills.

  6. About what share of Punjab's area is covered by the alluvial plains?

    1. 30%
    2. 50%
    3. 70%
    4. 90%
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 70%

    Explanation: The alluvial plains cover about 70% of the state and form its agricultural heartland.

  7. The Majha region lies between which two rivers?

    1. Ravi and Beas
    2. Beas and Sutlej
    3. Sutlej and Ghaggar
    4. Ravi and Chenab
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Ravi and Beas

    Explanation: Majha lies between the Ravi and the Beas. Doaba is between the Beas and Sutlej, and the Malwa plains are between the Sutlej and Ghaggar.

  8. Majha is also known as:

    1. Bist–Jalandhar Doab
    2. Rachna Doab
    3. Sindh Sagar Doab
    4. Upper Bari Doab
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Upper Bari Doab

    Explanation: Majha is the Upper Bari Doab, between the Ravi and Beas. The Bist–Jalandhar Doab is Doaba.

  9. The Bist–Jalandhar Doab is another name for:

    1. Majha
    2. Doaba
    3. Malwa
    4. Kandi
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Doaba

    Explanation: Doaba, the land between the Beas and Sutlej, is called the Bist–Jalandhar Doab. Majha is the Upper Bari Doab.

  10. The Malwa plains lie between:

    1. Ravi and Beas
    2. Beas and Sutlej
    3. Sutlej and Ghaggar
    4. Ravi and Ghaggar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Sutlej and Ghaggar

    Explanation: The Malwa plains stretch between the Sutlej and the Ghaggar, south of the Sutlej.

  11. What are the sand dunes of south-west Punjab locally called?

    1. Tibbas
    2. Choes
    3. Bets
    4. Duns
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Tibbas

    Explanation: The semi-arid sand dunes of south-west Punjab are locally called tibbas.

  12. The sand dune (tibba) area of Punjab lies at a height of:

    1. 400–700 m
    2. 300–400 m
    3. 180–300 m
    4. Below 180 m
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Below 180 m

    Explanation: The sand dunes are the lowest landform of Punjab, below 180 m. The land slopes down from the Shiwalik Hills in the north-east to these dunes in the south-west.

  13. Which of these districts is NOT part of the Shiwalik Hills region of Punjab?

    1. Gurdaspur
    2. Hoshiarpur
    3. Bathinda
    4. Rupnagar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Bathinda

    Explanation: Gurdaspur, Pathankot, Hoshiarpur, Nawanshahr and Rupnagar have the Shiwalik Hills. Bathinda lies in the sand-dune area of the south-west.

  14. In which of these districts are sand dunes (tibbas) found?

    1. Pathankot
    2. Fazilka
    3. Gurdaspur
    4. Rupnagar
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Fazilka

    Explanation: Tibbas are found in Fazilka, Ferozepur, Muktsar, Patiala, Sangrur, Bathinda and Mansa. Pathankot, Gurdaspur and Rupnagar are in the Shiwalik region.

  15. Going from north-east to south-west, which is the correct order of Punjab's landforms?

    1. Shiwalik Hills → Kandi → Alluvial Plains → Sand Dunes
    2. Kandi → Shiwalik Hills → Sand Dunes → Alluvial Plains
    3. Shiwalik Hills → Alluvial Plains → Kandi → Sand Dunes
    4. Alluvial Plains → Kandi → Shiwalik Hills → Sand Dunes
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Shiwalik Hills → Kandi → Alluvial Plains → Sand Dunes

    Explanation: The land slopes down from the Shiwalik Hills (400–700 m) to the Kandi (300–400 m), then the alluvial plains (180–300 m) and finally the sand dunes (below 180 m).

  16. Which set of landforms and heights is correctly matched?

    1. Shiwalik-300–400 m; Kandi-400–700 m; Alluvial Plains-below 180 m; Sand Dunes-180–300 m
    2. Shiwalik-180–300 m; Kandi-below 180 m; Alluvial Plains-400–700 m; Sand Dunes-300–400 m
    3. Shiwalik-400–700 m; Kandi-180–300 m; Alluvial Plains-300–400 m; Sand Dunes-below 180 m
    4. Shiwalik-400–700 m; Kandi-300–400 m; Alluvial Plains-180–300 m; Sand Dunes-below 180 m
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Shiwalik-400–700 m; Kandi-300–400 m; Alluvial Plains-180–300 m; Sand Dunes-below 180 m

    Explanation: Shiwalik Hills are 400–700 m, the Kandi 300–400 m, the alluvial plains 180–300 m and the sand dunes below 180 m. The other sets interchange the heights.

  17. Which landform of Punjab separates the Himalayas from the plains?

    1. Kandi
    2. Shiwalik Hills
    3. Sand dunes
    4. Malwa plains
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Shiwalik Hills

    Explanation: The forested Shiwalik Hills separate the Himalayas from the plains. The Kandi lies at their base.

  18. The Kandi belt is found in which of these districts?

    1. Hoshiarpur
    2. Bathinda
    3. Mansa
    4. Fazilka
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Hoshiarpur

    Explanation: The Kandi area covers the eastern parts of Pathankot, Hoshiarpur, Ropar, Gurdaspur and Nawanshahr. Bathinda, Mansa and Fazilka have sand dunes.

  19. Which landform is called the agricultural heartland of Punjab?

    1. Shiwalik Hills
    2. Kandi
    3. Alluvial plains
    4. Sand dunes
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Alluvial plains

    Explanation: The alluvial plains, about 70% of the state's area, are the agricultural heartland of Punjab.

  20. The sand dune area of south-west Punjab is best described as:

    1. Forested hills
    2. A foothill plain
    3. A fertile river doab
    4. Semi-arid and desert-like
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Semi-arid and desert-like

    Explanation: The tibba region of south-west Punjab is semi-arid and desert-like. Forested hills describe the Shiwaliks and the foothill plain is the Kandi.

  21. In which direction does the land of Punjab slope?

    1. From south-west to north-east
    2. From north-east to south-west
    3. From west to east
    4. From south to north
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) From north-east to south-west

    Explanation: The land slopes down from the Shiwalik Hills in the north-east to the sandy plains in the south-west.

  22. Which region of Punjab lies between the Beas and the Sutlej?

    1. Majha
    2. Malwa
    3. Doaba
    4. Kandi
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Doaba

    Explanation: Doaba lies between the Beas and the Sutlej. Majha is between the Ravi and Beas, and Malwa between the Sutlej and Ghaggar.

  23. The alluvial plains of Punjab lie at a height of:

    1. 180–300 m
    2. 300–400 m
    3. 400–700 m
    4. Below 180 m
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 180–300 m

    Explanation: The alluvial plains are 180–300 m high, the same as the state's average altitude.

  24. Which of these districts is NOT part of the sand dune (tibba) region?

    1. Mansa
    2. Sangrur
    3. Muktsar
    4. Hoshiarpur
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Hoshiarpur

    Explanation: Tibbas are found in Fazilka, Ferozepur, Muktsar, Patiala, Sangrur, Bathinda and Mansa. Hoshiarpur lies in the Shiwalik and Kandi area.

  25. Which statement about the physical features of Punjab is correct?

    1. The Kandi is wider than 20 km and higher than the Shiwaliks
    2. The Shiwalik Hills are forested and 5–12 km wide
    3. The alluvial plains cover about 30% of the state
    4. The tibbas are found mainly in Gurdaspur and Pathankot
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) The Shiwalik Hills are forested and 5–12 km wide

    Explanation: The Shiwalik Hills are forested and 5–12 km wide. The Kandi is 10–15 km wide and lower (300–400 m), the plains cover about 70%, and tibbas are in the south-west.
