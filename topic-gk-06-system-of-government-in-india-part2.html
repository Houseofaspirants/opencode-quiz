Question bank

System of Government in India - Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. In the parliamentary system, when can the lower house be dissolved?

    1. Only after a fixed term of 6 years
    2. Never
    3. Only by the Supreme Court
    4. When the majority party has no confidence in the House
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) When the majority party has no confidence in the House

    Explanation: In the parliamentary system, the lower house can be dissolved (e.g., when the majority party has no confidence in the house).

  2. In a unitary system of government:

    1. All powers are vested in the national government
    2. Powers are divided between centre and states
    3. States are sovereign
    4. There is no national government
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) All powers are vested in the national government

    Explanation: Unitary system: all powers are vested in the national government.

  3. In a federal system:

    1. All powers lie with the national government
    2. Powers are divided between national and state governments by the Constitution itself
    3. All powers lie with the states
    4. The judiciary appoints the government
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Powers are divided between national and state governments by the Constitution itself

    Explanation: Federal system: powers are divided by the Constitution itself, and both governments operate independently in their own jurisdictions.

  4. India's system of government is described as:

    1. Purely federal
    2. Purely unitary
    3. Confederal
    4. Quasi-federal
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Quasi-federal

    Explanation: India is a blend of federal and unitary systems, called quasi-federal.

  5. India adopted its federal (quasi-federal) system from the Constitution of:

    1. USA
    2. Canada
    3. Australia
    4. Switzerland
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Canada

    Explanation: India adopted the quasi-federal system from the Canadian Constitution.

  6. Which of the following is a federal feature of the Indian Constitution?

    1. Written constitution
    2. Single citizenship
    3. Integrated judiciary
    4. All-India Services
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Written constitution

    Explanation: Written constitution is a federal feature; single citizenship, integrated judiciary and All-India Services are unitary features.

  7. Which of the following is a unitary feature of the Indian Constitution?

    1. Division of powers
    2. Bicameralism
    3. Single citizenship
    4. Dual polity
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Single citizenship

    Explanation: Single citizenship is a unitary feature.

  8. Which of the following is NOT a federal feature?

    1. Independent judiciary
    2. Emergency provisions
    3. Rigid constitution
    4. Supremacy of the Constitution
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Emergency provisions

    Explanation: Emergency provisions are a unitary feature.

  9. Which of the following is NOT a unitary feature?

    1. Bicameralism
    2. Appointment of Governor by the Centre
    3. Strong centre
    4. Integrated election machinery
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Bicameralism

    Explanation: Bicameralism (lower house for people, upper house for states) is a federal feature.

  10. In bicameralism, the upper house represents:

    1. The President
    2. The states
    3. The judiciary
    4. The people directly
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) The states

    Explanation: Bicameralism: the lower house represents the people, the upper house represents the states.

  11. 'States not indestructible' as a unitary feature means:

    1. States can abolish the Centre
    2. States can secede from India
    3. States have their own armies
    4. States cannot change their area by themselves
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) States cannot change their area by themselves

    Explanation: States not indestructible: states cannot change their area by themselves; Parliament can reorganise them.

  12. 'No equality of state representation' means:

    1. States are not represented in Parliament
    2. All states have equal seats in Rajya Sabha
    3. Each state has a different number of seats in Parliament
    4. Only big states are represented
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Each state has a different number of seats in Parliament

    Explanation: No equality of state representation: each state has a different number of seats in Parliament (unitary feature).

  13. All-India Services (IAS, IPS, IFS) are considered a unitary feature because:

    1. Officers are appointed by the Centre but work in the states
    2. They are elected by people
    3. They are controlled by state governments
    4. They work only in Delhi
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Officers are appointed by the Centre but work in the states

    Explanation: All-India Services officers are appointed by the Centre but work in the states.

  14. Consider the following statements:1.Independent judiciary is a federal feature.2.Integrated judiciary is a unitary feature.Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Both 1 and 2

    Explanation: Both are correct: an independent judiciary that can declare laws invalid is federal; a single integrated judicial system is unitary.

  15. 'Integrated election machinery' means:

    1. Elections are conducted by courts
    2. Parliament conducts state elections
    3. A single body conducts elections for both the Centre and the states
    4. Each state has its own national election body
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) A single body conducts elections for both the Centre and the states

    Explanation: Integrated election machinery: a single body conducts elections for the Centre and the states (unitary feature).

  16. Match the features:A.Dual polity – 1. UnitaryB.Flexibility of the Constitution – 2. FederalC.Supremacy of the Constitution – 3. FederalD.Single constitution for all states – 4. Unitary

    1. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
    2. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
    3. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
    4. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4

    Explanation: Dual polity & supremacy of Constitution – federal; flexibility & single constitution – unitary.

  17. Which organ of government makes laws?

    1. Executive
    2. Legislature
    3. Election Commission
    4. Judiciary
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Legislature

    Explanation: The Legislature makes rules and regulations in the form of laws.

  18. Which organ of government implements (executes) laws?

    1. Legislature
    2. Judiciary
    3. Executive
    4. Media
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Executive

    Explanation: The Executive executes/implements the laws made by the legislature.

  19. Which organ of government interprets the law?

    1. Legislature
    2. Executive
    3. Cabinet
    4. Judiciary
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Judiciary

    Explanation: The Judiciary interprets the law and checks whether laws and actions are according to the Constitution.

  20. Which organ can declare any action against the Constitution invalid?

    1. President alone
    2. Legislature
    3. Executive
    4. Judiciary
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Judiciary

    Explanation: The Judiciary declares any action against the Constitution invalid.

  21. Match the organs with their functions:A.Legislature – 1. Interprets lawsB.Executive – 2. Makes lawsC.Judiciary – 3. Implements laws

    1. A-2, B-3, C-1
    2. A-2, B-1, C-3
    3. A-1, B-3, C-2
    4. A-3, B-2, C-1
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A-2, B-3, C-1

    Explanation: Legislature – makes; Executive – implements; Judiciary – interprets.

  22. The legislative branch of government is responsible for:

    1. Making laws
    2. Conducting elections
    3. Interpreting laws
    4. Executing laws
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Making laws

    Explanation: The legislative branch is responsible for making laws.

  23. In which system are the President and legislators elected separately for a fixed term?

    1. Quasi-federal system
    2. Parliamentary system
    3. Presidential system
    4. Unitary system
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Presidential system

    Explanation: In the presidential system, the President and legislators are elected separately for a fixed term.

  24. Urban areas in a district are administered by:

    1. Zila Parishad
    2. Village panchayats
    3. Gram Sabha
    4. Municipal bodies (municipalities/corporations)
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Municipal bodies (municipalities/corporations)

    Explanation: Urban areas are administered by municipal corporations/municipalities.

  25. 'Strong centre' as a unitary feature implies that:

    1. States have more powers than the Centre
    2. The central government has more powers than the states
    3. Centre and states have exactly equal powers
    4. The Centre has no power over states
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) The central government has more powers than the states

    Explanation: Strong centre: the central government has more powers.
