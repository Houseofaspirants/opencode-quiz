About this cluster

What Panchayati Raj covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Panchayati Raj is a well-scoring lane because the structure is fixed, clearly named and rarely ambiguous. The subject covers the three tiers of rural local government, the constitutional amendment that gave panchayats their status, how members are elected, how reservations apply and what finances each tier handles. Questions usually ask which body sits at which level or which function belongs to which tier, so learning the hierarchy as a single picture is more effective than studying the three tiers separately. Because the lane also appears under local government, both are best revised together. The lanes below work upward from the village level to the district level, then cover the constitutional framework and the elections that fill the seats.
