Question bank

DPSP and Fundamental Duties - Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. The Gandhian principles of DPSP represent:

    1. The programme of reconstruction enunciated by Gandhi during the national movement
    2. Gandhi's views on foreign policy
    3. Rules for the Congress party
    4. Gandhi's economic theory of socialism
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) The programme of reconstruction enunciated by Gandhi during the national movement

    Explanation: Gandhian principles represent the programme of reconstruction enunciated by Gandhi during the national movement.

  2. Which Article directs the State to secure a Uniform Civil Code for all citizens?

    1. Article 45
    2. Article 44
    3. Article 50
    4. Article 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 44

    Explanation: Article 44: secure for all citizens a Uniform Civil Code throughout the country.

  3. After the 86th Amendment, Article 45 directs the State to provide:

    1. Free and compulsory education for children of 6–14 years
    2. Early childhood care and education for all children until they complete six years
    3. Free higher education for all
    4. Mid-day meals in schools
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Early childhood care and education for all children until they complete six years

    Explanation: Amended Article 45: early childhood care and education for all children until they complete the age of six years.

  4. Organising agriculture and animal husbandry on modern and scientific lines is a directive under:

    1. Article 47
    2. Article 48
    3. Article 43
    4. Article 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 48

    Explanation: Article 48 (Liberal-Intellectual): organise agriculture and animal husbandry on modern and scientific lines.

  5. Protection of monuments and places of national importance is a directive under:

    1. Article 29
    2. Article 48A
    3. Article 51
    4. Article 49
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 49

    Explanation: Article 49: protect monuments, places and objects of artistic or historic interest declared to be of national importance.

  6. Separation of the judiciary from the executive is a directive under:

    1. Article 124
    2. Article 50
    3. Article 49
    4. Article 44
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 50

    Explanation: Article 50: separate the judiciary from the executive in the public services of the State.

  7. Promotion of international peace and security is a directive under:

    1. Article 51A
    2. Article 253
    3. Article 50
    4. Article 51
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 51

    Explanation: Article 51: promote international peace and security and maintain just and honourable relations between nations.

  8. Match the Articles with directives:A.Article 40 – 1. Uniform Civil CodeB.Article 44 – 2. Village panchayatsC.Article 50 – 3. International peaceD.Article 51 – 4. Separation of judiciary from executive

    1. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
    2. A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
    3. A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
    4. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3

    Explanation: Art 40 – panchayats; Art 44 – UCC; Art 50 – separation of judiciary; Art 51 – international peace.

  9. The Liberal-Intellectual principles of DPSP reflect the ideology of:

    1. Communism
    2. Socialism
    3. Liberalism
    4. Gandhism
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Liberalism

    Explanation: Liberal-Intellectual principles reflect the ideology of liberalism.

  10. Which of the following is a Liberal-Intellectual principle of DPSP?

    1. Free legal aid
    2. Participation of workers in management
    3. Promotion of cottage industries
    4. Separation of judiciary from executive
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Separation of judiciary from executive

    Explanation: Article 50 (separation of judiciary) is Liberal-Intellectual; 43A and 39A are Socialistic; cottage industries is Gandhian.

  11. Which Amendment added Articles 39A, 43A and 48A to the DPSPs?

    1. 44th Amendment, 1978
    2. 86th Amendment, 2002
    3. 42nd Amendment, 1976
    4. 97th Amendment, 2011
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 42nd Amendment, 1976

    Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) added 39(f), 39A, 43A and 48A.

  12. Protection and improvement of the environment and safeguarding of forests and wildlife is a directive under:

    1. Article 49
    2. Article 51A(g)
    3. Article 48A
    4. Article 48
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 48A

    Explanation: Article 48A (added by the 42nd Amendment): protect and improve the environment and safeguard forests and wildlife.

  13. Which Amendment added a new directive under Article 38 requiring the State to minimise inequalities in income, status, facilities and opportunities?

    1. 44th Amendment, 1978
    2. 42nd Amendment, 1976
    3. 86th Amendment, 2002
    4. 73rd Amendment, 1992
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 44th Amendment, 1978

    Explanation: The 44th Amendment (1978) added this new DPSP under Article 38.

  14. Which Amendment changed the subject matter of Article 45 and made elementary education a Fundamental Right?

    1. 42nd Amendment, 1976
    2. 44th Amendment, 1978
    3. 97th Amendment, 2011
    4. 86th Amendment, 2002
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 86th Amendment, 2002

    Explanation: The 86th Amendment (2002) changed Article 45 and inserted Article 21A.

  15. Article 43B relating to co-operative societies was added by:

    1. 73rd Amendment, 1992
    2. 97th Amendment, 2011
    3. 42nd Amendment, 1976
    4. 86th Amendment, 2002
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 97th Amendment, 2011

    Explanation: The 97th Amendment (2011) added Article 43B relating to co-operative societies.

  16. The directive 'to secure opportunities for the healthy development of children' [Article 39(f)] was added by:

    1. 86th Amendment, 2002
    2. Original Constitution
    3. 42nd Amendment, 1976
    4. 44th Amendment, 1978
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 42nd Amendment, 1976

    Explanation: The 42nd Amendment added Article 39(f) — opportunities for healthy development of children.

  17. Arrange the following amendments related to DPSP in chronological order:1.Article 43B added2.Article 48A added3.New directive under Article 384.Article 45 changed

    1. 2, 3, 4, 1
    2. 3, 2, 4, 1
    3. 2, 3, 1, 4
    4. 2, 4, 3, 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2, 3, 4, 1

    Explanation: 48A (42nd, 1976) → Art 38 directive (44th, 1978) → Art 45 change (86th, 2002) → 43B (97th, 2011).

  18. Fundamental Duties were added to the Constitution by:

    1. 86th Amendment Act, 2002
    2. 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
    3. 1st Amendment Act, 1951
    4. 44th Amendment Act, 1978
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 42nd Amendment Act, 1976

    Explanation: Fundamental Duties were added by the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976.

  19. On whose recommendation were Fundamental Duties added to the Constitution?

    1. Sarkaria Commission
    2. Balwant Rai Mehta Committee
    3. Verma Committee
    4. Swaran Singh Committee
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Swaran Singh Committee

    Explanation: Fundamental Duties were added on the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee.

  20. Fundamental Duties are contained in which Part and Article of the Constitution?

    1. Part IV, Article 51
    2. Part III, Article 32
    3. Part IV A, Article 51A
    4. Part V, Article 51
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Part IV A, Article 51A

    Explanation: Fundamental Duties: Part IV A, Article 51A.

  21. How many Fundamental Duties are there at present?

    1. 10
    2. 9
    3. 11
    4. 12
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 11

    Explanation: Originally there were 10 duties; one more was added by the 86th Amendment, 2002 — now 11.

  22. How many Fundamental Duties were originally added in 1976?

    1. 11
    2. 10
    3. 8
    4. 12
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 10

    Explanation: Originally there were 10 Fundamental Duties.

  23. The 11th Fundamental Duty was added by which Amendment?

    1. 86th Amendment, 2002
    2. 42nd Amendment, 1976
    3. 44th Amendment, 1978
    4. 97th Amendment, 2011
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 86th Amendment, 2002

    Explanation: The 11th Fundamental Duty was added by the 86th Amendment Act, 2002.

  24. The 11th Fundamental Duty requires parents/guardians to:

    1. Plant trees
    2. Vote in every election
    3. Pay taxes honestly
    4. Provide opportunities for education to their child or ward between 6 and 14 years
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Provide opportunities for education to their child or ward between 6 and 14 years

    Explanation: 11th duty: provide opportunities for education to one's child or ward between the age of six and fourteen years.

  25. Fundamental Duties apply to:

    1. Indian citizens only
    2. Only members of armed forces
    3. Only government employees
    4. All persons in India including foreigners
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Indian citizens only

    Explanation: Fundamental Duties apply to Indian citizens only and do not extend to foreigners.
