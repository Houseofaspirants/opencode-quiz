Question bank

Average MCQ – Part 5: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Find the average of all 3-digit integers that leave remainder 2 when divided by 40.

    1. 542
    2. 522
    3. 562
    4. 550
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 542

    Explanation: First term = 122 and last term = 962. Average = (122 + 962) ÷ 2 = 542.

  2. Daily sales average ₹3,500 over days 1–8 and ₹3,800 over days 3–10. Days 1 and 2 average ₹2,900. Find the average sales over days 9 and 10, in ₹.

    1. 3800
    2. 4100
    3. 5300
    4. 3500
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 4100

    Explanation: The entering pair’s total exceeds the leaving pair by 8 × 300 = ₹2400. Its average = 2900 + 2400 ÷ 2 = ₹4100.

  3. For 30 entries, a clerk omits 60, records 48 as 84, and divides the resulting total by 29 to get 54. Find the correct average.

    1. 53
    2. 55
    3. 52
    4. 54.2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 53

    Explanation: Correct total = 29 × 54 + 60 − 84 + 48 = 1590. Correct average = 1590 ÷ 30 = 53.

  4. In a company, 40% of staff average 45 years and the rest average 35 years. Find the average staff age in years.

    1. 40
    2. 39
    3. 41
    4. 38
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 39

    Explanation: Average = 35 + 0.4 × (45 − 35) = 39 years.

  5. By how much does the average of 14² and 16² exceed 15²?

    1. 2
    2. 4
    3. 1
    4. 0
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1

    Explanation: For numbers equally spaced around 15, the square-average exceeds 15² by the square of the gap. Difference = (196 + 256) ÷ 2 − 225 = 1.

  6. A 20-member squad averages 58 kg. Replacing its lightest member by an 82 kg member raises the average by 1.5 kg. The new member is then replaced by a 62 kg member. Find the net increase in average weight from the original squad, in kg.

    1. 1
    2. 2.5
    3. 0.5
    4. 0.75
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 0.5

    Explanation: First member weighed 82 − 20 × 1.5 = 52 kg. Net increase = (62 − 52) ÷ 20 = 0.5 kg.

  7. A group of 18 has average score 55. 2 new members join with equal scores and the average becomes 57. Find each newcomer’s score.

    1. 73
    2. 75
    3. 77
    4. 65
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 75

    Explanation: Each newcomer’s score = (20 × 57 − 18 × 55) ÷ 2 = 75.

  8. The average of 4 numbers is 29. 3 numbers are 17, 26 and 39. Find twice the fourth number.

    1. 34
    2. 58
    3. 68
    4. 72
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 68

    Explanation: Fourth number = 4 × 29 − 17 − 26 − 39 = 34. Twice it is 68.

  9. A batch averages 56 marks. 10 candidates average 68, 2 score exactly 56 each, and every other candidate scores 50. Find the total number of candidates.

    1. 30
    2. 28
    3. 34
    4. 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 32

    Explanation: The top 10 create 10 × 12 = 120 excess marks. Each 50-mark candidate offsets 6, so there are 20 of them; total = 10 + 2 + 20 = 32.

  10. 5 tins weigh 97, 103, 99, 105 and 101 kg. By how much does their average exceed 100 kg?

    1. 2
    2. 5
    3. 1
    4. 0.5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1

    Explanation: Deviations total −3 + 3 − 1 + 5 + 1 = 5. Average excess = 5 ÷ 5 = 1 kg.

  11. A group averages 44 years. When a 64-year-old leaves, the average falls to 43 years. Find the original group size.

    1. 20
    2. 22
    3. 21
    4. 19
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 21

    Explanation: The remaining count = (64 − 44) ÷ (44 − 43) = 20. Original count = 21.

  12. A vehicle covers half its journey at 36 km/h, 1/4 at 54 km/h and the last 1/4 at 108 km/h. Find its average speed in km/h.

    1. 48
    2. 66
    3. 54
    4. 60
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 48

    Explanation: Take 216 km: time = 108 ÷ 36 + 54 ÷ 54 + 54 ÷ 108 = 4.5 hours. Average speed = 216 ÷ 4.5 = 48 km/h.

  13. The average of 16 bills is ₹250. It is discovered that a ₹320 bill was counted twice and a ₹160 bill was omitted, while 16 was still used as divisor. Find the correct average bill in ₹.

    1. 260
    2. 230
    3. 245
    4. 240
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 240

    Explanation: Correct total = 16 × 250 − 320 + 160. Correct average = 250 − 160 ÷ 16 = ₹240.

  14. 5 consecutive multiples of 6 average 90. Find the second-largest of them.

    1. 102
    2. 84
    3. 90
    4. 96
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 96

    Explanation: The middle term is 90; the second-largest is 90 + 6 = 96.

  15. Simran’s average in 4 papers is 73. Her 5th-paper mark is 8 more than her final 5-paper average. Find her 5th-paper mark.

    1. 81
    2. 83
    3. 85
    4. 79
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 83

    Explanation: If final average is x, 4 × 73 + x + 8 = 5x gives x = 75. 5th mark = 75 + 8 = 83.

  16. 12 numbers average 50. The first 7 average 48 and the last 7 average 53. Their 2 common numbers differ by 9. Find the larger common number.

    1. 58
    2. 49
    3. 54
    4. 62
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 58

    Explanation: Common total = 7 × 48 + 7 × 53 − 12 × 50 = 107. Larger = (107 + 9) ÷ 2 = 58.

  17. The average age of 3 siblings is 18 years. Their mother is included and the average becomes 25 years. Find the mother’s age in years.

    1. 43
    2. 49
    3. 50
    4. 46
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 46

    Explanation: Mother’s age = 4 × 25 − 3 × 18 = 46 years.

  18. A class has 45 pupils averaging 62 marks. Boys average 58 and girls average 68. How many more boys than girls are there?

    1. 6
    2. 12
    3. 15
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 9

    Explanation: Boys : girls = (68 − 62) : (62 − 58) = 3 : 2. Difference = 45 × 1/5 = 9.

  19. The values 10, 20 and 30 occur 2, 5 and 3 times respectively. Find their mean.

    1. 20
    2. 21
    3. 22
    4. 23
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 21

    Explanation: Mean = (10 × 2 + 20 × 5 + 30 × 3) ÷ 10 = 21.

  20. 10 numbers average 36. 1 number is replaced by the average of the other 9, and the new average of all 10 becomes 35. Find the number that was replaced.

    1. 45
    2. 44
    3. 46
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 45

    Explanation: Replacing a value by the other 9’s mean makes all 10’s mean equal to that same mean, 35. Original value = 10 × 36 − 9 × 35 = 45.

  21. A batter has scored 720 runs in 18 innings. What average runs per innings must he achieve in the next 6 innings to finish with an average of 45?

    1. 55
    2. 60
    3. 50
    4. 65
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 60

    Explanation: Required average = (24 × 45 − 720) ÷ 6 = 60.

  22. A bowler’s average is 24 runs per wicket after taking 18 wickets. How many runs has he conceded?

    1. 432
    2. 402
    3. 448
    4. 424
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 432

    Explanation: Runs conceded = 24 × 18 = 432.

  23. A trader buys 20 notebooks at ₹35 each and 30 at ₹45 each. He sells all at ₹46 each. Find his average profit per notebook in ₹.

    1. 5
    2. 6
    3. 4
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 5

    Explanation: Average cost = (20 × 35 + 30 × 45) ÷ 50 = ₹41. Average profit = 46 − 41 = ₹5.

  24. 6 positive integers average 15. Exactly 2 are greater than 15. What is the greatest possible value of the largest integer?

    1. 74
    2. 69
    3. 70
    4. 75
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 70

    Explanation: Minimise 4 integers at 1 each and the other integer above 15 at 16. Largest = 6 × 15 − 4 − 16 = 70.

  25. In a 31-day month beginning on Monday, a shop earns ₹2,000 on each Sunday and ₹1,380 on every other day. Find its average daily earnings in ₹.

    1. 1480
    2. 1500
    3. 1440
    4. 1460
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1460

    Explanation: There are 4 Sundays and 27 other days. Average = (4 × 2000 + 27 × 1380) ÷ 31 = ₹1460.
