Question bank

Simplification MCQ – Part 6: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A machine fills 750 mL bottles from 18 litres of juice. How many completely filled bottles are obtained if 1.5 litres are spilled first?

    1. 20
    2. 25
    3. 22
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 22

    Explanation: Juice available is 16.5 litres = 16500 mL; 16500 ÷ 750 = 22 bottles.

  2. Evaluate: 112².

    1. 14400
    2. 12144
    3. 12444
    4. 12544
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 12544

    Explanation: Use (110 + 2)² = 12100 + 440 + 4 = 12544.

  3. Gurleen owns 3/5 of a business and sells 2/3 of her holding for ₹96000. At the same valuation, she then buys 1/8 of the whole business using part of that money. How much of the sale money remains?

    1. ₹66000
    2. ₹72000
    3. ₹48000
    4. ₹84000
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) ₹66000

    Explanation: The sold fraction is (3/5)(2/3) = 2/5, so the whole business is worth ₹240000. Buying 1/8 costs ₹30000, leaving 96000 − 30000 = ₹66000.

  4. A full reserved ticket costs ₹680. The basic fare of a half ticket is half the full basic fare, but the reservation charge is the same. One full and one half reserved ticket together cost ₹1090. Find the reservation charge per ticket.

    1. ₹130
    2. ₹140
    3. ₹410
    4. ₹270
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) ₹140

    Explanation: The half ticket costs 1090 − 680 = 410. Twice the half ticket minus one full ticket leaves one reservation charge: 820 − 680 = ₹140.

  5. Evaluate: 96 ÷ 4 × (7 − 2) − 54 ÷ 6.

    1. 102
    2. 129
    3. 15
    4. 111
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 111

    Explanation: Divide 96 by 4 before multiplying by 5: 24 × 5 − 9 = 111.

  6. Find √(29 + 12√5) − √(29 − 12√5).

    1. 12
    2. 4√5
    3. 6
    4. 2√5
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 6

    Explanation: The radicands are (3 + 2√5)² and (3 − 2√5)²; their positive roots are 3 + 2√5 and 2√5 − 3, giving 6.

  7. Evaluate: (7^0 + 2^3)².

    1. 64
    2. 81
    3. 100
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 81

    Explanation: A non-zero number to power 0 is 1; thus (1 + 8)² = 81.

  8. Evaluate: [250 − {90 − (48 ÷ 6)}] ÷ 7 + 13.

    1. 37
    2. 21
    3. 35
    4. 39
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 37

    Explanation: The numerator is 250 − 82 = 168; thus 168 ÷ 7 + 13 = 37.

  9. Find the product (1 − 1/4²)(1 − 1/5²)(1 − 1/6²), continuing through (1 − 1/12²).

    1. 13/16
    2. 3/4
    3. 13/12
    4. 11/16
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 13/16

    Explanation: Split each factor into [(k − 1)/k][(k + 1)/k]; the product becomes (3/12)(13/4) = 13/16.

  10. Find the value of ? in 960 ÷ 16 × ? − 75 = 345.

    1. 6
    2. 4.5
    3. 8
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 7

    Explanation: The coefficient is 60; solve 60 × ? = 420 to get 7.

  11. Find the value of 6.25% of 736.

    1. 44
    2. 23
    3. 46
    4. 92
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 46

    Explanation: 6.25% = 1/16; hence 736 ÷ 16 = 46.

  12. Data sufficiency: What is the value of 3x + 2y? I: x + y = 18. II: x − y = 4.

    1. Even both statements together are insufficient
    2. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone
    3. Statement I alone is sufficient
    4. Statement II alone is sufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone

    Explanation: Neither statement alone fixes 3x + 2y. Together they give x = 11 and y = 7, so the value is 47.

  13. Find the positive integer x satisfying √(x² + 15x) = x + 6.

    1. 18
    2. 12
    3. 6
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 12

    Explanation: Square and cancel x²: 15x = 12x + 36, so 3x = 36 and x = 12.

  14. Evaluate: [(5 + 5/8) − (2 + 7/12)] ÷ (1 + 11/24).

    1. 73/24
    2. 35/73
    3. 73/35
    4. 71/35
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 73/35

    Explanation: The difference is 73/24 and the divisor is 35/24; cancel 24 to get 73/35.

  15. Find the value of 75% of 28% of 600.

    1. 168
    2. 450
    3. 42
    4. 126
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 126

    Explanation: Multiply 3/4 by 28% to get 21%; 21% of 600 = 126.

  16. Evaluate: (1/(1 + 1/2) + 1/(1 + 1/3)) ÷ (1 − 1/6).

    1. 17/10
    2. 7/5
    3. 10/17
    4. 17/12
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 17/10

    Explanation: The sum is 2/3 + 3/4 = 17/12; multiply by 6/5 to obtain 17/10.

  17. If r = 0.363636… and s = 0.166666…, find (r − s) × 66.

    1. 35
    2. 11
    3. 22
    4. 13
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 13

    Explanation: Use r = 4/11 and s = 1/6; (24/66 − 11/66) × 66 = 13.

  18. What is ∛2190, rounded to the nearest integer?

    1. 12
    2. 15
    3. 13
    4. 14
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 13

    Explanation: Since 13³ = 2197, the cube root of 2190 is just below 13 and rounds to 13.

  19. Evaluate: −{15 − [−6 − (−11)]}.

    1. -10
    2. -20
    3. 10
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) -10

    Explanation: The square bracket is 5, so the expression is −(15 − 5) = −10.

  20. If m² + n² = 130 and mn = 33, find (m + n)/(m − n), given m > n > 0.

    1. 4/7
    2. 7/4
    3. 7/8
    4. 49/16
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 7/4

    Explanation: The positive sums and differences are √196 = 14 and √64 = 8; their ratio is 7/4.

  21. Find the value of ? in (? + 3/4) ÷ (5/6) = 21/10.

    1. 5/2
    2. 1/2
    3. 1
    4. 7/4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1

    Explanation: Multiply by 5/6: ? + 3/4 = 7/4, giving ? = 1.

  22. Evaluate: 7560 ÷ 35.

    1. 108
    2. 216
    3. 206
    4. 252
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 216

    Explanation: Divide by 7 and then by 5: 1080 ÷ 5 = 216.

  23. Interchange ÷ and − in 63 ÷ 18 − 3 + 4 × 2. What is the resulting value?

    1. 65
    2. 49
    3. 73
    4. 23
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 65

    Explanation: After the swap, 63 − 18 ÷ 3 + 4 × 2 = 63 − 6 + 8 = 65.

  24. If a + b + c = 0 and abc = −42, find (a + b)(b + c)(c + a) − (a³ + b³ + c³)/2.

    1. 168
    2. 21
    3. 63
    4. 105
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 105

    Explanation: Since the sum is zero, (a + b)(b + c)(c + a) = −abc = 42 and a³ + b³ + c³ = 3abc = −126. Hence 42 − (−126)/2 = 105.

  25. Evaluate: 2/3 × 9/14 + 5/7 × 7/10.

    1. 13/21
    2. 13/14
    3. 3/7
    4. 11/14
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 13/14

    Explanation: Cancel before multiplying: 3/7 + 1/2 = 13/14.
