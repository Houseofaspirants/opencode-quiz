Quick answer

House of Aspirants questions are written and reviewed by our editorial team, checked against the exam syllabus and official sources, and revised whenever a guide changes. Corrections are applied on request through the contact page, and every guide shows the date it was last reviewed.

1. Who writes this content

Everything on this portal — questions, explanations and study guides — is produced by the House of Aspirants editorial team. We do not publish guest posts, sponsored questions or AI-generated answers without review, and no external body reviews or endorses what we publish.

2. How a question is written

  • • Each question maps to one syllabus topic, so revision stays targeted.
  • • Exactly one option is correct and the distractors are plausible but clearly wrong.
  • • Every question carries an explanation, so the reasoning is visible rather than memorised.
  • • We avoid questions that depend on a date, a vacancy or an eligibility rule, because those change between notifications.

3. Where the facts come from

Before publishing or updating a guide we check the topic against the issuing body’s own syllabus and notification. For general awareness and current affairs we rely on official sites and established reporting. Always confirm dates, eligibility and vacancies in the official notification from PPSC, PSSSB, Punjab Police or PSPCL — never on this portal alone.

4. Corrections

Found something wrong? Use the contact page and quote the question text. Corrections are made in the source question file, so the fix reaches every future attempt rather than a single page.

5. Content update dates

Every study guide carries a visible review date next to the byline, and this page carries the date its own policy was last reviewed. A guide is revised whenever a source changes or a reader reports a problem — the date moves only when the content actually changes.

6. Educational purpose

All content is provided for study and practice only. Practice is free — start from any subject or a full mock test. Scores and analytics do not guarantee success in any examination, and the full terms of use and privacy policy apply.

7. Independence & disclaimer

House of Aspirants is an independent, unofficial study portal. Nothing here is connected to, sponsored by or endorsed by Punjab Police, PSSSB, PPSC, PSPCL or any government department. Exam names are used only to describe the syllabus a set practises.

Editorial policy — common questions

Who writes the questions and study guides on House of Aspirants?

The House of Aspirants editorial team writes and reviews them, checking each explanation against the exam syllabus and keeping answers self-contained so the reasoning stays visible.

How do you check whether an answer is correct?

Every question is checked against the issuing body’s own syllabus and notification, and factual claims are verified against official sources before publishing.

How often is the content updated?

Each guide shows the date it was last reviewed, and the content is revised whenever a source changes or a reader reports a problem.

How do I report a wrong answer?

Use the contact page and quote the question text. Corrections are applied to the source question file so the fix reaches every future attempt.

Is House of Aspirants affiliated with any recruiting body?

No. This is an independent, unofficial study portal and nothing here is connected to or endorsed by Punjab Police, PSSSB, PPSC, PSPCL or any government department.
