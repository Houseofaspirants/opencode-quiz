Question bank

Problems on Numbers MCQ – Part 6: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. At a fair in Ludhiana, 3 identical entry tickets and a parking charge of ₹18 cost ₹159 altogether. Find the price of one ticket.

    1. 47
    2. 59
    3. 53
    4. 41
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 47

    Explanation: Ticket price = (159 − 18) ÷ 3 = ₹47.

  2. 1/6 of a number equals 1/4 of the number obtained by subtracting 40 from it. Find the number.

    1. 60
    2. 80
    3. 120
    4. 160
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 120

    Explanation: x/6 = (x − 40)/4 gives 2x = 3x − 120; hence x = 120.

  3. Positive numbers a and b satisfy a + b = 17 and a² + b² = 169. Find a^4 + b^4.

    1. 14161
    2. 21361
    3. 24961
    4. 28561
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 21361

    Explanation: ab = (17² − 169)/2 = 60; a^4 + b^4 = 169² − 2 × 60² = 21361.

  4. A positive number is reduced by 5, and the result is cubed. Adding 12 gives 1012. Find the original number.

    1. 5
    2. 10
    3. 20
    4. 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 15

    Explanation: Cube = 1012 − 12 = 1000, so the intermediate value is 10; original = 10 + 5 = 15.

  5. Two numbers have sum 208. Their difference is half their sum. Find the larger number.

    1. 156
    2. 104
    3. 130
    4. 52
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 156

    Explanation: Difference = 208/2 = 104; larger = (208 + 104)/2 = 156.

  6. The ratio of two positive numbers is 5 : 6. Adding 14 to the smaller and subtracting 10 from the larger makes the new numbers equal. Find the product of the original numbers.

    1. 20736
    2. 14400
    3. 17280
    4. 18000
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 17280

    Explanation: 6k − 5k = 14 + 10 gives k = 24; product = 30k² = 30 × 576 = 17280.

  7. Three numbers A, B and C satisfy A + B = 89, 2B + C = 138 and C − A = 7. Find B.

    1. 42
    2. 54
    3. 40
    4. 47
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 42

    Explanation: Subtracting A + B = 89 from 2B + C = 138 gives B + C − A = 49; hence B + 7 = 49 and B = 42.

  8. A positive integer leaves remainder 17 when divided by 24. Its quotient is 3 times the remainder. What remainder does the integer leave when divided by 9?

    1. 0
    2. 5
    3. 8
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 8

    Explanation: The integer = 24 × 51 + 17 = 1241; 1241 = 9 × 137 + 8, so the remainder is 8.

  9. Eleven consecutive integers have sum 22. How many of these integers are negative?

    1. 5
    2. 2
    3. 4
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 3

    Explanation: Middle integer = 22 ÷ 11 = 2; the integers run from −3 to 7, giving 3 negative integers.

  10. The product of the smallest and largest of three consecutive positive odd integers is 957. Find the square of the middle integer.

    1. 953
    2. 965
    3. 958
    4. 961
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 961

    Explanation: For middle integer m, (m − 2)(m + 2) = m² − 4 = 957; hence m² = 961.

  11. A three-digit number has hundreds digit 2. Moving that digit to the units place, without changing the order of the other digits, increases the number by 468. Find the original number.

    1. 247
    2. 742
    3. 274
    4. 724
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 274

    Explanation: Write the number as 200 + r; the new number is 10r + 2, so 9r − 198 = 468 gives r = 74 and original = 274.

  12. Two positive fractions have product 8/75. Dividing the larger by the smaller gives 3/2. Find their sum.

    1. 2/3
    2. 4/5
    3. 1/3
    4. 14/15
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2/3

    Explanation: Let the fractions be 3k and 2k: 6k² = 8/75 gives k = 2/15; sum = 5k = 2/3.

  13. A two-digit number has digit sum 12. Its units digit is 4 less than its tens digit. What is the difference between the place values of its digits?

    1. 84
    2. 4
    3. 76
    4. 44
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 76

    Explanation: Digits are 8 and 4, so the difference of place values = 80 − 4 = 76.

  14. A two-digit number plus twice its reverse equals 180. Its tens digit is twice its units digit. Find the original number.

    1. 63
    2. 84
    3. 42
    4. 48
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 84

    Explanation: Let units = u and tens = 2u; original = 21u and reverse = 12u, so 45u = 180 gives u = 4 and original = 84.

  15. A fraction has numerator and denominator in the ratio 3 : 8. Subtracting 6 from the denominator increases its value to 1/2. Find the original denominator.

    1. 24
    2. 48
    3. 18
    4. 30
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 24

    Explanation: Let numerator = 3k and denominator = 8k; 3k/(8k − 6) = 1/2 gives k = 3 and denominator = 24.

  16. A calculator user intends to multiply a positive number by 1.25 but multiplies it by 0.125 instead. The result is 81 less than intended. What result should have been obtained?

    1. 9
    2. 72
    3. 100
    4. 90
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 90

    Explanation: The wrong multiplier is 1/10 of the intended multiplier; hence 90% of the correct result = 81 and correct result = 90.

  17. What is the sum of positive integers A, B and C? Statement I: A + B = 70. Statement II: B + C = 90. Which assessment is correct?

    1. Statement II alone is sufficient
    2. Statement I alone is sufficient
    3. Even both statements together are insufficient
    4. Both statements together are sufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Even both statements together are insufficient

    Explanation: Together they give A + B + C = 160 − B; B is not fixed. For example, (30, 40, 50) totals 120, while (20, 50, 40) totals 110.

  18. Find the missing value: 8 × ? − 17 = 3 × ? + 108, where both question marks represent the same number.

    1. 31
    2. 25
    3. 18
    4. 21
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 25

    Explanation: (8 − 3)x = 108 + 17 = 125, so x = 25.

  19. Arjun puts 2/7 of his counters in box A and 3/14 of the original total in box B. The remaining 63 counters go into box C. How many more counters are in A than in B?

    1. 9
    2. 18
    3. 27
    4. 36
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 9

    Explanation: Remaining fraction = 1 − 2/7 − 3/14 = 1/2, so total = 126; A − B = (2/7 − 3/14) × 126 = 9.

  20. When an integer is divided by 17, its remainder exceeds its quotient by 2. When divided by 13, its quotient is 3 more than the first quotient and its remainder is 3. Find the integer.

    1. 136
    2. 146
    3. 156
    4. 143
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 146

    Explanation: Let the first quotient be q: n = 17q + q + 2 = 13(q + 3) + 3. Thus 5q = 40, so q = 8 and n = 146.

  21. Two numbers have sum 159. 1/3 of the larger exceeds 1/3 of the smaller by 9. Find their product.

    1. 5940
    2. 6138
    3. 6030
    4. 6318
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 6138

    Explanation: Difference = 3 × 9 = 27; numbers = (159 + 27)/2 = 93 and 66, so product = 6138.

  22. Three positive numbers A, B and C satisfy A : B = 4 : 7 and B : C = 14 : 9. If C exceeds A by 12, find B.

    1. 144
    2. 108
    3. 84
    4. 168
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 168

    Explanation: A : B : C = 8 : 14 : 9; one ratio part = 12, so B = 14 × 12 = 168.

  23. A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20% of the increased value. If the final value is 276, find the original number.

    1. 264
    2. 345
    3. 220.8
    4. 276
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 276

    Explanation: The net multiplier = 1.25 × 0.80 = 1, so the original number is also 276.

  24. Five consecutive integers have sum 305. What is the difference between twice the largest and the smallest?

    1. 59
    2. 67
    3. 63
    4. 65
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 67

    Explanation: Middle = 305 ÷ 5 = 61; smallest = 59 and largest = 63, so 2 × 63 − 59 = 67.

  25. Real numbers a and b satisfy a² + b² = 50 and ab = 7. Find |a + b| + |a − b|.

    1. 16
    2. 14
    3. 2
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 14

    Explanation: |a + b| = √(50 + 14) = 8 and |a − b| = √(50 − 14) = 6; their sum is 14.
