All about Geography & Environment

Where this lane sits inside General Knowledge.

Geography and environment reward map memory. Rivers and their tributaries, drainage patterns, climate and soil belts, national parks and the standard environmental vocabulary - these are one-line questions with no partial credit, so accuracy is the whole game.

Practise them as short sets twice a week and keep a single revision sheet of what you miss. The candidates who improve here rarely study more; they simply revisit the same sheet more often.

This lane sits inside General Knowledge (6 lanes in total), so it stays narrow on purpose: 0 topic sets and 0 questions currently publish under Geography & Environment. Every question is timed at 30 seconds, carries four options and explains why the right answer is right.

Run a set untimed first to find the gaps, then repeat it against the clock. The questions you miss twice are your revision list - keep them to one line each and revise the file the night before the paper.

Papers that ask for it: Patwari, Forest Guard.
