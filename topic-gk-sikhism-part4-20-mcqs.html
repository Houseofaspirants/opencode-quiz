Sikhism (Sikh Dharam) - Part 4: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji is given which title in the notes?

    1. Bala Pir
    2. Hind Di Chadar
    3. Sachha Padshah
    4. First Saint Soldier
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Hind Di Chadar

    Explanation: The notes identify him as Hind Di Chadar.

  2. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji was born on:

    1. 1 April 1621
    2. 7 July 1656
    3. 22 December 1666
    4. 19 June 1595
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1 April 1621

    Explanation: The notes give 1 April 1621 as his birth date.

  3. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji's son was:

    1. Sahibzada Ajit Singh
    2. Guru Gobind Singh Ji
    3. Sahibzada Fateh Singh
    4. Baba Buddha Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Guru Gobind Singh Ji

    Explanation: The notes state that Guru Gobind Singh Ji was his son.

  4. Which person persuaded Guru Teg Bahadur Ji to accept Sikh leadership at Baba Bakala?

    1. Bhai Mardana Ji
    2. Makhan Shah Lubana
    3. Bhai Jaita Ji
    4. Bhai Gurdas Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Makhan Shah Lubana

    Explanation: The notes mention Makhan Shah Lubana.

  5. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji was executed on the order of:

    1. Akbar
    2. Aurangzeb
    3. Jahangir
    4. Shah Jahan
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Aurangzeb

    Explanation: The notes state he was executed on the order of Aurangzeb.

  6. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji was executed at:

    1. Amritsar
    2. Chandni Chowk, Delhi
    3. Lahore
    4. Anandpur Sahib
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Chandni Chowk, Delhi

    Explanation: The notes mention Chandni Chowk, Delhi.

  7. Which two followers were executed along with Guru Teg Bahadur Ji?

    1. Bhai Bala Ji and Bhai Mardana Ji
    2. Bhai Mati Das Ji and Bhai Dayala Ji
    3. Bhai Gurdas Ji and Bhai Jaita Ji
    4. Bhai Lehna Ji and Bhai Daya Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Bhai Mati Das Ji and Bhai Dayala Ji

    Explanation: The notes list Bhai Mati Das Ji and Bhai Dayala Ji.

  8. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji's body was cremated by:

    1. Lakhi Shah Lubana
    2. Makhan Shah Lubana
    3. Bhai Jaita
    4. Baba Buddha Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Lakhi Shah Lubana

    Explanation: The notes say Lakhi Shah Lubana took the body and cremated it.

  9. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji's head was taken back to Anandpur Sahib by:

    1. Bhai Jaita
    2. Bhai Gurdas
    3. Bhai Mardana
    4. Bhai Bala
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Bhai Jaita

    Explanation: Bhai Jaita is mentioned as taking back the head.

  10. Guru Teg Bahadur Ji founded:

    1. Amritsar
    2. Anandpur Sahib
    3. Kiratpur Sahib
    4. Goindwal Sahib
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Anandpur Sahib

    Explanation: The notes say he founded Anandpur Sahib (also known as Chakk Nanaki).

  11. Guru Gobind Singh Ji was born on:

    1. 22 December 1666
    2. 1 April 1621
    3. 7 July 1656
    4. 19 June 1595
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 22 December 1666

    Explanation: The notes state 22 December 1666 at Patna, Bihar.

  12. Guru Gobind Singh Ji established the Khalsa Panth on:

    1. 30 March 1699
    2. 11 November 1675
    3. 7 September 1539
    4. 16 August 1604
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 30 March 1699

    Explanation: The notes say the Khalsa Panth was established on 30 March 1699 at Anandpur Sahib.

  13. How many Ks are part of the Khalsa identity in the notes?

    1. Three
    2. Five
    3. Seven
    4. Nine
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Five

    Explanation: The notes list the Five Ks.

  14. Which slogan is linked with Guru Gobind Singh Ji in the notes?

    1. Bole So Nihal
    2. Sat Sri Akal
    3. Jo Bole So Nihal
    4. Har Har Mahadev
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Sat Sri Akal

    Explanation: The notes mention the jaikara 'Bole So Nihal.'

  15. Which of these is listed as a composition of Guru Gobind Singh Ji?

    1. Zafarnamah
    2. Anand Sahib
    3. Japji Sahib
    4. Lavan
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Zafarnamah

    Explanation: Zafarnamah is listed under his key compositions.

  16. Dasam Granth was written at:

    1. Kiratpur Sahib
    2. Talwandi Sabo / Damdama Sahib
    3. Goindwal Sahib
    4. Khadur Sahib
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Talwandi Sabo / Damdama Sahib

    Explanation: The notes say Guru Ji wrote the Dasam Granth at Talwandi Sabo, now Damdama Sahib.

  17. Guru Gobind Singh Ji wrote Zafarnamah at:

    1. Dina village
    2. Amritsar
    3. Patna
    4. Nanded
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Dina village

    Explanation: The notes say Zafarnamah was written at Dina village in 1706.

  18. Which battle is connected with the deaths of Ajit Singh and Jujhar Singh?

    1. Bhangani
    2. Chamkaur
    3. Nadaun
    4. Basoli
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Chamkaur

    Explanation: The notes say in the Battle of Chamkaur (1704), Ajit and Jujhar Singh were killed.

  19. Guru Gobind Singh Ji attained Jyoti Jot in:

    1. Nanded Sahib
    2. Kiratpur Sahib
    3. Delhi
    4. Amritsar
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Nanded Sahib

    Explanation: The notes say Jyoti Jot was in 1708 at Nanded Sahib.

  20. Guru Granth Sahib Ji has how many pages according to the notes?

    1. 1,040
    2. 1,230
    3. 1,430
    4. 1,530
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1,430

    Explanation: The notes state 1,430 pages.

  21. Who compiled the written form of Guru Granth Sahib Ji under the guidance of Guru Arjan Dev Ji?

    1. Bhai Mardana Ji
    2. Bhai Gurdas Ji
    3. Baba Buddha Ji
    4. Bhai Jaita Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Bhai Gurdas Ji

    Explanation: The notes say it was written down by Bhai Gurdas Ji under Guru Arjan Dev Ji's guidance.

  22. When was Guru Granth Sahib Ji first placed for worship at Harmandir Sahib?

    1. 16 August 1604
    2. 30 March 1699
    3. 7 September 1539
    4. 11 November 1675
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 16 August 1604

    Explanation: The notes say it was first placed for worship on 16 August 1604.

  23. Who was the first Granthi of Guru Granth Sahib Ji?

    1. Baba Buddha Ji
    2. Bhai Gurdas Ji
    3. Bhai Jaita Ji
    4. Bhai Bala Ji
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Baba Buddha Ji

    Explanation: The notes identify Baba Buddha Ji as the first Granthi.

  24. Which of the following works are mentioned as included in Guru Granth Sahib Ji?

    1. Kabir Ji, Ravidas Ji, Sheikh Farid
    2. Only Guru Gobind Singh Ji
    3. Only the first five Gurus
    4. Only Bhatt poets
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Kabir Ji, Ravidas Ji, Sheikh Farid

    Explanation: The notes say Guru Granth Sahib Ji contains works of Kabir Ji, Ravidas Ji and Sheikh Farid.

  25. Which of these is NOT included in Guru Granth Sahib Ji according to the notes?

    1. Works of Guru Gobind Singh Ji
    2. Works of Kabir Ji
    3. Works of Ravidas Ji
    4. Works of Sheikh Farid
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Works of Guru Gobind Singh Ji

    Explanation: The notes clearly state that Guru Gobind Singh Ji's compositions are not included.
