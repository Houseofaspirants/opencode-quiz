Question bank

DPSP and Fundamental Duties - Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Consider the following statements about Fundamental Duties:1.They were part of the original Constitution.2.They are non-justiciable.Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2 only

    Explanation: Fundamental Duties were NOT in the original Constitution (added in 1976). They are non-justiciable.

  2. Which Fundamental Duty requires citizens to abide by the Constitution and respect the National Flag and National Anthem?

    1. Ninth duty
    2. Eleventh duty
    3. First duty
    4. Fifth duty
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) First duty

    Explanation: The first duty: abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals and institutions, the National Flag and the National Anthem.

  3. Which of the following is NOT a Fundamental Duty?

    1. To develop scientific temper and humanism
    2. To defend the country when called upon
    3. To vote in elections
    4. To safeguard public property and abjure violence
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) To vote in elections

    Explanation: Voting is not listed among the 11 Fundamental Duties.

  4. 'To develop scientific temper, humanism and the spirit of inquiry and reform' is a:

    1. Directive Principle
    2. Preamble objective
    3. Fundamental Right
    4. Fundamental Duty
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Fundamental Duty

    Explanation: It is the 8th Fundamental Duty under Article 51A.

  5. 'To protect and improve the natural environment including forests, lakes, rivers and wildlife' is found in:

    1. Article 51A
    2. Article 47
    3. Article 21
    4. Article 39
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 51A

    Explanation: It is a Fundamental Duty under Article 51A (7th duty). (Article 48A is the corresponding DPSP for the State.)

  6. Which Fundamental Duty includes renouncing practices derogatory to the dignity of women?

    1. Value and preserve composite culture
    2. Promote harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood
    3. Strive towards excellence
    4. Cherish the ideals of the freedom struggle
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Promote harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood

    Explanation: The 5th duty: promote harmony and common brotherhood ... and renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women.

  7. 'To uphold and protect the sovereignty, unity and integrity of India' is a:

    1. Fundamental Duty
    2. Directive Principle
    3. Emergency provision
    4. Fundamental Right
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Fundamental Duty

    Explanation: It is the 3rd Fundamental Duty under Article 51A.

  8. Which statement about Fundamental Duties is correct?

    1. There is no legal sanction against their violation
    2. They can be enforced through writs
    3. They were borrowed from the USA
    4. They apply to foreigners too
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) There is no legal sanction against their violation

    Explanation: Fundamental Duties are non-justiciable; there is no legal sanction against their violation.

  9. 'To strive towards excellence in all spheres of individual and collective activity' is which Fundamental Duty?

    1. 11th
    2. 10th
    3. 8th
    4. 6th
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 10th

    Explanation: The 10th duty: strive towards excellence in all spheres of individual and collective activity.

  10. Which pair is correctly matched?

    1. DPSP – UK; Fundamental Duties – Fazl Ali Commission
    2. DPSP – Ireland; Fundamental Duties – Swaran Singh Committee
    3. DPSP – USA; Fundamental Duties – Sarkaria Commission
    4. DPSP – Canada; Fundamental Duties – Kothari Commission
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) DPSP – Ireland; Fundamental Duties – Swaran Singh Committee

    Explanation: DPSP was borrowed from Ireland; Fundamental Duties were added on the Swaran Singh Committee's recommendation.

  11. 'To value and preserve the rich heritage of the country's composite culture' is a duty under:

    1. Article 51
    2. Article 29
    3. Article 49
    4. Article 51A
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 51A

    Explanation: This is the 6th Fundamental Duty under Article 51A.

  12. Which of the following is a difference between Fundamental Rights and DPSPs?

    1. Fundamental Rights are justiciable; DPSPs are not
    2. DPSPs are in Part III; Fundamental Rights in Part IV
    3. Both are non-justiciable
    4. DPSPs can be enforced by the Supreme Court under Article 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Fundamental Rights are justiciable; DPSPs are not

    Explanation: Fundamental Rights (Part III) are justiciable; DPSPs (Part IV) are non-justiciable.

  13. Which Article contains the directive regarding 'equitable distribution of material resources of the community for the common good'?

    1. Article 39
    2. Article 41
    3. Article 43
    4. Article 38
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 39

    Explanation: Article 39 directs equitable distribution of material resources for the common good.

  14. Which of the following is a Socialistic principle of DPSP?

    1. Protection of monuments (Article 49)
    2. Village panchayats (Article 40)
    3. Uniform Civil Code (Article 44)
    4. Free legal aid to the poor (Article 39A)
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Free legal aid to the poor (Article 39A)

    Explanation: Article 39A is a Socialistic principle.

  15. DPSPs are contained in which Articles of the Constitution?

    1. 52–151
    2. 12–35
    3. 36–51
    4. 51A
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 36–51

    Explanation: Part IV (Articles 36–51) contains the DPSPs.

  16. Which of the following Fundamental Duties was added by the 86th Amendment?

    1. Developing scientific temper
    2. Protecting the natural environment
    3. Safeguarding public property
    4. Providing opportunities for education to children aged 6–14
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Providing opportunities for education to children aged 6–14

    Explanation: The 86th Amendment (2002) added the 11th duty — education opportunities for children aged 6–14.

  17. Cherishing and following the noble ideals that inspired the national struggle for freedom is the ______ Fundamental Duty.

    1. 4th
    2. 1st
    3. 2nd
    4. 7th
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 2nd

    Explanation: The 2nd duty: cherish and follow the noble ideals that inspired the national struggle for freedom.

  18. 'To safeguard public property and to abjure violence' is which Fundamental Duty?

    1. 10th
    2. 8th
    3. 9th
    4. 5th
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 9th

    Explanation: The 9th duty: safeguard public property and abjure violence.

  19. Article 48 of the Constitution figures in which DPSP categories as per the classification in the notes?

    1. Only Socialistic
    2. Only Liberal-Intellectual
    3. Only Gandhian
    4. Both Gandhian (cow slaughter) and Liberal-Intellectual (scientific agriculture)
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Both Gandhian (cow slaughter) and Liberal-Intellectual (scientific agriculture)

    Explanation: Article 48 has a Gandhian part (prohibit cow slaughter) and a Liberal-Intellectual part (organise agriculture and animal husbandry on scientific lines).

  20. 'To defend the country and render national service when called upon to do so' is which Fundamental Duty?

    1. 2nd
    2. 3rd
    3. 4th
    4. 9th
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 4th

    Explanation: The 4th duty: defend the country and render national service when called upon.

  21. Consider the following statements:1.Fundamental Duties are contained in Part IV A.2.Like Fundamental Rights, Fundamental Duties are justiciable.Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1 only

    Explanation: Part IV A is correct. Fundamental Duties, like DPSPs, are non-justiciable.

  22. Article 43 appears in which DPSP category with respect to promotion of cottage industries on individual or co-operative basis in rural areas?

    1. Liberal-Intellectual
    2. Gandhian
    3. None
    4. Socialistic
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Gandhian

    Explanation: Promotion of cottage industries in rural areas (Article 43) is a Gandhian principle.

  23. The Indian Constitution defines two types of rights. Which are they?

    1. Natural and legal rights
    2. Individual and collective rights
    3. Civil and criminal rights
    4. Justiciable (Fundamental Rights) and non-justiciable (DPSPs)
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Justiciable (Fundamental Rights) and non-justiciable (DPSPs)

    Explanation: Justiciable rights — Fundamental Rights; non-justiciable rights — DPSPs.

  24. Which of the following was NOT added by the 42nd Amendment to the DPSPs?

    1. Article 39A
    2. Article 43A
    3. Article 48A
    4. Article 43B
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 43B

    Explanation: Article 43B was added by the 97th Amendment (2011); 39A, 43A and 48A were added by the 42nd Amendment.

  25. 'Promote harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood transcending religious, linguistic and regional diversities' is a:

    1. Fundamental Right
    2. Fundamental Duty
    3. Directive Principle
    4. Preamble ideal only
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Fundamental Duty

    Explanation: This is the 5th Fundamental Duty under Article 51A.
