About this cluster

What Punjab History covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Punjab History is a core part of the Punjab GK section and appears in some form in nearly every state-level paper. The questions concentrate on the succession of rulers, the major battles and treaties, the Sikh movement and its leadership, and the province's role in the freedom struggle. Because the period is politically dense, sequencing matters more than detail: knowing what came immediately before and after an event is usually enough to eliminate two options. Regional participation in the national movement is a recurring theme, so it should be revised alongside the broader freedom story rather than separately. The lanes below run in roughly chronological order, which keeps the cause-and-effect chain visible and makes revision far faster than studying isolated facts.
