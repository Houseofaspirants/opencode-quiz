Question bank

Simplification MCQ – Part 8: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Let t = 0.0585858… , where only 58 repeats after the first decimal digit 0. Find 990t.

    1. 5.8
    2. 580
    3. 58
    4. 52
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 58

    Explanation: Since 10t = 58/99, t = 58/990; therefore 990t = 58.

  2. Evaluate: (√256 + √625) ÷ √(81/9).

    1. 41
    2. 41/9
    3. 13
    4. 41/3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 41/3

    Explanation: The three roots are 16, 25 and 3; therefore (16 + 25) ÷ 3 = 41/3.

  3. If a + b = 9 and a³ + b³ = 189, find (a − b)².

    1. 41
    2. 1
    3. 20
    4. 9
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 1

    Explanation: From 189 = 729 − 27ab, get ab = 20; then (a − b)² = 81 − 80 = 1.

  4. Find the value of ? in (? ÷ 24) + (? ÷ 36) = 25.

    1. 360
    2. 450
    3. 900
    4. 300
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 360

    Explanation: Combine coefficients: ? × (5/72) = 25; hence ? = 360.

  5. Evaluate: 132 ÷ 11 × 5 − 84 ÷ 7 × 2 + 19.

    1. 79
    2. 55
    3. 43
    4. 31
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 55

    Explanation: Apply division and multiplication left to right: 60 − 24 + 19 = 55.

  6. Let x = √10 − 3. Find (x + 1/x)² − (x − 1/x)².

    1. 4
    2. 36
    3. 40
    4. 1
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 4

    Explanation: Use the difference of the two squares directly: 4 × x × (1/x) = 4.

  7. Evaluate: 65 × 75.

    1. 4900
    2. 4225
    3. 4875
    4. 4925
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 4875

    Explanation: The factors are 70 ± 5; hence 70² − 5² = 4900 − 25 = 4875.

  8. Convert (2 hours 45 minutes + 1 hour 35 minutes) ÷ 4 into minutes.

    1. 70
    2. 60
    3. 75
    4. 65
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 65

    Explanation: The total is 165 + 95 = 260 minutes; 260 ÷ 4 = 65 minutes.

  9. Evaluate: [(2 + 2/3) ÷ (1 + 1/7) − (3/4 ÷ 9/14)] ÷ (7/12).

    1. 2
    2. 1
    3. 4
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2

    Explanation: The two quotients are 7/3 and 7/6; their difference is 7/6, and (7/6) ÷ (7/12) = 2.

  10. Which option is closest to 998.5 ÷ 0.501?

    1. 1800
    2. 2100
    3. 2000
    4. 1900
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 2000

    Explanation: Use 1000 ÷ 0.5 = 2000; the actual quotient is about 1993.01, closest to 2000.

  11. Evaluate: |−6 × 5 + 8| − |−4 × 3|.

    1. -10
    2. -34
    3. 34
    4. 10
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 10

    Explanation: The two absolute values are 22 and 12; their difference is 10.

  12. Evaluate: 560 ÷ {8 + [30 − (18 + 6)]} + 3 × 9.

    1. 49
    2. 67
    3. 83
    4. 40
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 67

    Explanation: The divisor is 8 + 6 = 14; then 40 + 27 = 67.

  13. A bus leaves Ludhiana with some passengers. At the next stop 2/5 get off and 18 board; at the following stop 1/3 get off and 9 board. It then has 57 passengers. How many started?

    1. 72
    2. 90
    3. 120
    4. 60
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 90

    Explanation: Reverse the changes: before the second stop there were (57 − 9) × 3/2 = 72. Initially there were (72 − 18) × 5/3 = 90.

  14. Evaluate: (13/18 + 5/12 − 7/24) × 72.

    1. 79
    2. 65
    3. 61
    4. 47
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 61

    Explanation: With denominator 72, the numerator is 52 + 30 − 21 = 61.

  15. Evaluate: (10^4 × 10²) ÷ 10^5.

    1. 10
    2. 100
    3. 1
    4. 1000
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 10

    Explanation: Add numerator exponents and subtract the denominator exponent: 10^(4 + 2 − 5) = 10.

  16. Find [(51 + 29)² + (51 − 29)²] ÷ 2 − 51².

    1. 484
    2. 1480
    3. 2601
    4. 841
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 841

    Explanation: The averaged squares equal 51² + 29²; subtracting 51² leaves 29² = 841.

  17. In a farm enclosure there are only ducks and goats, with 54 heads and 152 legs in total. How many ducks are there?

    1. 32
    2. 27
    3. 44
    4. 22
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 32

    Explanation: If all were ducks there would be 108 legs; the extra 44 legs represent 22 goats. Hence ducks = 54 − 22 = 32.

  18. Data sufficiency: In a club, how many members speak both Punjabi and Hindi? I: 42 speak Punjabi and 38 speak Hindi. II: 65 speak at least one of these languages.

    1. Statement II alone is sufficient
    2. Statement I alone is sufficient
    3. Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone
    4. Even both statements together are insufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Both statements together are sufficient, but neither alone

    Explanation: Neither statement alone gives the overlap. Together, both languages = 42 + 38 − 65 = 15.

  19. Find the value of 112.5% of 144.

    1. 126
    2. 162
    3. 160
    4. 180
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 162

    Explanation: 112.5% = 9/8; hence 144 ÷ 8 × 9 = 162.

  20. For x > 1, √(x + 7) ÷ √(x − 1) = 3. Find x².

    1. 2
    2. 16
    3. 9
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 4

    Explanation: Square: x + 7 = 9(x − 1), giving 8x = 16 and x = 2; therefore x² = 4.

  21. Find the value of ? in (875 − ?) × 8 ÷ 25 = 168.

    1. 350
    2. 455
    3. 707
    4. 525
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 350

    Explanation: The bracket is 168 × 25 ÷ 8 = 525; therefore ? = 875 − 525 = 350.

  22. Evaluate: 408 × 15.

    1. 6020
    2. 4080
    3. 8160
    4. 6120
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 6120

    Explanation: Multiply by 10 and add half of that product: 4080 + 2040 = 6120.

  23. Round each of 1204, 589 and 347 to the nearest ten, then evaluate the first minus the second plus the third.

    1. 950
    2. 960
    3. 962
    4. 970
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 960

    Explanation: The rounded values are 1200, 590 and 350; 1200 − 590 + 350 = 960.

  24. Find (3/5) × (4/6) × (5/7), continuing with both numerator and denominator increasing by 1, through (18/20).

    1. 3/20
    2. 6/95
    3. 3/95
    4. 1/95
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 3/95

    Explanation: Cancel the common factors from 5 through 18; the product is (3 × 4)/(19 × 20) = 3/95.

  25. Evaluate: (12.5 × 0.16 + 0.875) ÷ 0.125.

    1. 21
    2. 230
    3. 16
    4. 23
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 23

    Explanation: The numerator is 2 + 0.875 = 2.875; dividing by 1/8 gives 23.
