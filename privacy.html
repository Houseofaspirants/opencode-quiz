Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Overview

House of Aspirants is a static website. Starting an MCQ set asks you to sign in with Google; when you do, your Google name, e-mail, profile photo and set statistics are stored in Google Firebase so your progress and rankings follow your account. Everything else stays in your browser, and there are no passwords. The site shows ads from Google AdSense and measures visits with Google Analytics; both use cookies, as explained in sections 5 and 6 below.

2. Data stored on your device

  • • MCQ answers in progress — so you can resume after a refresh.
  • • Results, progress & streaks — for your dashboard and badges.
  • • Bookmarks — questions you save for revision.
  • • Theme preference — dark or light mode.
  • • Leaderboard attempts — kept in this browser, and mirrored to your Google account when signed in.

All of this lives in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser data removes it, and the “Reset progress” button deletes it instantly. Signed-in students can ask us to delete their Firebase account data at any time via the contact page.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • • No phone numbers, addresses or payment details
  • • No passwords (sign-in uses your Google account)
  • • No selling or sharing of data (we have none to sell)

4. Third-party links

The site links to Telegram, Instagram and YouTube. Their own privacy policies apply once you click through.

5. Advertising

House of Aspirants uses Google AdSense to show ads. No popups or popunders are used.

  • • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • • Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's advertising cookie policy.

House of Aspirants is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.in. Book links on the Books page are affiliate links: we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you.

6. Cookies and analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It sets cookies that record anonymous usage such as pages viewed and device type, and it is reported to us only in aggregate. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Blocking cookies does not stop the MCQ sets from working.

7. Offline storage (PWA)

A service worker caches MCQ files so the site works offline. This cache is local to your browser and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.
