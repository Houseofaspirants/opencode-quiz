Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. Overview

House of Aspirants Quiz Portal is a static website. It has no user accounts, no login and no server-side database of personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

  • • Quiz answers in progress — so you can resume after a refresh.
  • • Results, progress & streaks — for your dashboard and badges.
  • • Bookmarks — questions you save for revision.
  • • Theme preference — dark or light mode.
  • • Leaderboard attempts — stored only in this browser.

All of this lives in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser data removes it, and the “Reset progress” button deletes it instantly.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • • No names, emails or phone numbers
  • • No account registration
  • • No tracking cookies
  • • No selling or sharing of data (we have none to sell)

4. Third-party links

The site links to Telegram, Instagram and YouTube. Their own privacy policies apply once you click through.

5. Advertising

Advertisement areas are plain embedded banners. If a third-party ad network is added later, its own policy will be disclosed in this section. No popups or popunders are used.

6. Offline storage (PWA)

A service worker caches quiz files so the site works offline. This cache is local to your browser and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us here or message us on Telegram.
