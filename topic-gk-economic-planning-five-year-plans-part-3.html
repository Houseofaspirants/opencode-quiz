Question bank

Economic Planning & Five Year Plans - Part 3: all 18 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Which measure was introduced in the Sixth Plan to check overpopulation?

    1. Green Revolution
    2. LPG reforms
    3. PURA
    4. Family planning
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Family planning

    Explanation: Family planning was introduced in the Sixth Plan to check overpopulation. The other options are incorrect because the Green Revolution began in the Third Plan, LPG reforms in 1991, and PURA was recommended in the Tenth Plan. NABARD was established in 1982 during the Sixth Plan.

  2. NABARD was established in 1982 on the recommendation of the:

    1. D.P. Dhar Committee
    2. C. Rangarajan Committee
    3. Gunnar Myrdal Committee
    4. Sivaraman Committee
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Sivaraman Committee

    Explanation: NABARD was established in 1982 on the recommendation of the Sivaraman Committee. The other options are incorrect because D.P. Dhar prepared MNP, Rangarajan prepared the Eleventh Plan, and Myrdal suggested the Rolling Plan. NABARD was established during the Sixth Plan.

  3. Assertion (A): The Seventh Plan was successful. Reason (R): It achieved about 6% growth despite severe drought in its first three years.

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

    Explanation: Both A and R are true, and the approximately 6% growth despite severe drought supports the assessment of success. The other options are incorrect because the other choices deny either the stated success, the growth fact, or their stated explanatory connection. The Seventh Plan period was 1985–1990.

  4. The third Plan Holiday, due to economic instability and a foreign exchange crisis, covered:

    1. 1985–1987
    2. 1988–1990
    3. 1990–1992
    4. 1992–1994
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1990–1992

    Explanation: The third Plan Holiday covered 1990–1992 and had two annual plans. The other options are incorrect because the other periods do not match the third plan holiday. Economic instability and a foreign exchange crisis were the reasons.

  5. The LPG reforms were introduced in 1991 under Prime Minister:

    1. Jawaharlal Nehru
    2. Atal Bihari Vajpayee
    3. P.V. Narasimha Rao
    4. Morarji Desai
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) P.V. Narasimha Rao

    Explanation: LPG reforms were introduced in 1991 under PM P.V. Narasimha Rao. The other options are incorrect because Nehru is linked to socialist influence on planning, while the other named leaders were not Prime Minister in 1991. LPG stands for Liberalisation, Privatisation and Globalisation.

  6. In LPG reforms, the letter “G” stands for:

    1. Growth
    2. Globalisation
    3. Governance
    4. Green Revolution
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Globalisation

    Explanation: In LPG reforms, “G” stands for Globalisation. The other options are incorrect because Growth and Governance are not expansions of LPG here, and Green Revolution is a Third Plan development. The other two letters stand for Liberalisation and Privatisation.

  7. The Eighth Five Year Plan promoted:

    1. Heavy industries only
    2. MSMEs and modernisation of industry
    3. Nationalisation of major banks
    4. Family planning
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) MSMEs and modernisation of industry

    Explanation: The Eighth Plan promoted MSMEs and modernisation of industry. The other options are incorrect because heavy-industry priority was in the Second Plan, bank nationalisation in the Fourth, and family planning in the Sixth. The Eighth Plan covered 1992–1997.

  8. PURA was recommended in the Tenth Plan by:

    1. C. Rangarajan
    2. D.P. Dhar
    3. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
    4. Gunnar Myrdal
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

    Explanation: PURA was recommended by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam in the Tenth Plan. The other options are incorrect because Rangarajan prepared the Eleventh Plan, Dhar prepared MNP, and Myrdal suggested the Rolling Plan. PURA means Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas.

  9. PURA stands for:

    1. Public Utilities for Rural Agriculture
    2. Planning for Urban and Rural Areas
    3. Programme for Universal Rural Access
    4. Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas

    Explanation: PURA expands to Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas. The other options are incorrect because the other expansions are not the official wording. It was recommended in the Tenth Plan.

  10. The Eleventh Five Year Plan was prepared under:

    1. D.P. Dhar
    2. C. Rangarajan
    3. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
    4. P.V. Narasimha Rao
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) C. Rangarajan

    Explanation: The Eleventh Plan was prepared under C. Rangarajan. The other options are incorrect because Dhar prepared MNP, Kalam recommended PURA, and Rao was PM for the 1991 LPG reforms. The Eleventh Plan theme was “Rapid and More Inclusive Growth”.

  11. Which theme is correctly associated with the Eleventh Plan?

    1. Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth
    2. Rapid and More Inclusive Growth
    3. Socialistic Pattern of Society
    4. Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Rapid and More Inclusive Growth

    Explanation: The Eleventh Plan theme was “Rapid and More Inclusive Growth”. The other options are incorrect because the first theme belongs to the Twelfth Plan, socialistic pattern to Industrial Policy 1956, and PURA to the Tenth Plan. The Eleventh Plan achieved about 8% average growth.

  12. Which Five Year Plan is regarded as the most successful, with average growth of about 8%?

    1. Ninth Plan
    2. Tenth Plan
    3. Eleventh Plan
    4. Twelfth Plan
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Eleventh Plan

    Explanation: The Eleventh Plan was the most successful plan, with average growth of about 8%. The other options are incorrect because the Ninth Plan marked a role shift, the Tenth featured PURA, and the Twelfth was the last plan. The Seventh Plan’s growth rate was about 6%, not about 8%.

  13. Which was the last Five Year Plan in India?

    1. Ninth Plan
    2. Tenth Plan
    3. Eleventh Plan
    4. Twelfth Plan
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Twelfth Plan

    Explanation: The Twelfth Plan was the last Five Year Plan. The other options are incorrect because the Ninth, Tenth and Eleventh Plans all preceded the Twelfth Plan. The Twelfth Plan covered 2012–2017.

  14. The theme of the Twelfth Plan was:

    1. Rapid and More Inclusive Growth
    2. Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth
    3. Socialistic Pattern of Society
    4. New Agricultural Strategy
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth

    Explanation: The Twelfth Plan theme was “Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth”. The other options are incorrect because rapid and more inclusive growth was the Eleventh Plan theme, socialistic pattern was the 1956 policy goal, and new agricultural strategy belonged to the first holiday. The Twelfth Plan was the final Five Year Plan.

  15. Arrange the following plan-holiday periods in chronological order: 1. Third Plan Holiday 2. First Plan Holiday 3. Second Plan Holiday (Rolling Plan)

    1. 2–3–1
    2. 3–2–1
    3. 2–1–3
    4. 1–2–3
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2–3–1

    Explanation: The correct chronological order is first holiday (1966–1969), second holiday/Rolling Plan (1978–1980), then third holiday (1990–1992). The other options are incorrect because each other order reverses at least one of these dated periods. The first holiday had three annual plans, while the third had two.

  16. Which one of the following sets correctly matches the First to Fourth Plans with their periods?

    1. First: 1951–56; Second: 1956–61; Third: 1961–66; Fourth: 1969–74
    2. First: 1951–56; Second: 1961–66; Third: 1956–61; Fourth: 1969–74
    3. First: 1951–56; Second: 1956–61; Third: 1966–69; Fourth: 1969–74
    4. First: 1956–61; Second: 1961–66; Third: 1966–69; Fourth: 1969–74
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) First: 1951–56; Second: 1956–61; Third: 1961–66; Fourth: 1969–74

    Explanation: The periods are First 1951–56, Second 1956–61, Third 1961–66 and Fourth 1969–74. The other options are incorrect because the other sets swap plan periods or incorrectly treat the 1966–69 annual-plan interval as the Third Plan. The interval 1966–69 was the first Plan Holiday.

  17. Which one of the following sets correctly matches the Fifth to Eighth Plans with their periods?

    1. Fifth: 1974–79; Sixth: 1980–85; Seventh: 1985–90; Eighth: 1992–97
    2. Fifth: 1974–79; Sixth: 1978–80; Seventh: 1985–90; Eighth: 1992–97
    3. Fifth: 1974–79; Sixth: 1980–85; Seventh: 1990–92; Eighth: 1992–97
    4. Fifth: 1978–80; Sixth: 1980–85; Seventh: 1985–90; Eighth: 1992–97
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Fifth: 1974–79; Sixth: 1980–85; Seventh: 1985–90; Eighth: 1992–97

    Explanation: The periods are Fifth 1974–79, Sixth 1980–85, Seventh 1985–90 and Eighth 1992–97. The other options are incorrect because 1978–80 was the Rolling Plan and 1990–92 was an annual-plan holiday, not the Sixth or Seventh Plan. The Fifth Plan was terminated one year early.

  18. Which one of the following sets correctly matches the Ninth to Twelfth Plans with their periods?

    1. Ninth: 1997–2002; Tenth: 2002–07; Eleventh: 2007–12; Twelfth: 2012–17
    2. Ninth: 1997–2002; Tenth: 2007–12; Eleventh: 2002–07; Twelfth: 2012–17
    3. Ninth: 1997–2002; Tenth: 2002–07; Eleventh: 2007–12; Twelfth: 2017–22
    4. Ninth: 1992–97; Tenth: 2002–07; Eleventh: 2007–12; Twelfth: 2012–17
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Ninth: 1997–2002; Tenth: 2002–07; Eleventh: 2007–12; Twelfth: 2012–17

    Explanation: The periods are Ninth 1997–2002, Tenth 2002–07, Eleventh 2007–12 and Twelfth 2012–17. The other options are incorrect because the other sets swap plan periods, extend the Twelfth Plan beyond 2017, or assign the Eighth Plan period to the Ninth. The Twelfth Plan was the final Five Year Plan.
