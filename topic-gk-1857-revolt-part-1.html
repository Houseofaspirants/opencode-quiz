  1. Consider the following conditions before the Revolt of 1857:1.Heavy land-revenue burden on peasants2.Expansion of revenue collection to tribal lands3.Promotion of indigenous manufactured goods4.Exploitation by middlemenWhich of the statements given above are correct?

    1. 1, 2 and 4 only
    2. 1 and 3 only
    3. 2 and 3 only
    4. 1, 2, 3 and 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1, 2 and 4 only

    Explanation: The correct answer is 1, 2 and 4 only. Indigenous industry was destroyed while British manufactured goods were promoted, so statement 3 is incorrect. Foreign rule was another source of resentment. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  2. Which pair correctly identifies the leaders of the Sanyasi Revolt?

    1. Bakshi Jagabandhu Bidyadhar and Dinabandhu Santra
    2. Majnu Shah and Musa Shah
    3. Sidhu and Kanhu
    4. Gomdhar Konwar and Maharaja Purandhar Singh
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Majnu Shah and Musa Shah

    Explanation: The correct answer is Majnu Shah and Musa Shah. Sidhu and Kanhu led the Santhal Rebellion, Gomdhar Konwar and Maharaja Purandhar Singh were linked with the Ahom Revolt, and Bakshi Jagabandhu Bidyadhar with Dinabandhu Santra led the Paika Rebellion. The Sanyasi Revolt lasted from 1763 to 1800 in Bihar and Bengal. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  3. Krishnanarayan was associated with which uprising?

    1. Pahariya Rebellion
    2. Poligars' Revolt
    3. Revolt of the Moamarias
    4. Ahom Revolt
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Revolt of the Moamarias

    Explanation: The correct answer is the Revolt of the Moamarias. The Poligars’ Revolt occurred in Tinnevelly and Ramnathapuram, the Ahom Revolt was linked with Gomdhar Konwar and Maharaja Purandhar Singh, and the Pahariya Rebellion occurred in Raj Mahal Hills. The Moamaria revolt occurred during 1769-99 in Assam and parts of present-day Bangladesh. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  4. The Poligars' Revolt of 1795-1805 was centred in:

    1. Raj Mahal Hills
    2. Bihar and Bengal
    3. Assam and parts of present-day Bangladesh
    4. Tinnevelly and Ramnathapuram
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Tinnevelly and Ramnathapuram

    Explanation: The correct answer is Tinnevelly and Ramnathapuram. Bihar and Bengal were associated with the Sanyasi Revolt, Assam and parts of present-day Bangladesh with the Moamarias, and Raj Mahal Hills with the Pahariya Rebellion. The Poligars’ Revolt is recorded for 1795-1805. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  5. Match List I with List II:List I (Rebellion)A.Paika RebellionB.Ahom RevoltC.Surat Salt AgitationsD.Pagal PanthisList II (Year)1.18172.1825-353.18284.1844Choose the correct answer:

    Answer: (A) A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2

    Explanation: The correct answer is A-1, B-3, C-4, D-2. The Paika Rebellion occurred in 1817, the Ahom Revolt in 1828, and the Surat Salt Agitations in 1844. Pagal Panthis were active in Bengal during 1825-35. These dates distinguish civil and peasant uprisings before 1857. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  6. Assertion (A): The Wahabi Movement was an Islamic revivalist movement.Reason (R): It was started by Syed Ahmed of Rai Bareilly.

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A

    Explanation: The correct answer is both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A. The movement was described as an Islamic revivalist movement and was started by Syed Ahmed of Rai Bareilly. The reason identifies its initiator; it does not explain the character of revivalism. It spread across Bihar, Bengal, the North West Frontier Province, Punjab and other areas during 1830-61. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  7. The Surat Salt Agitations of 1844 were triggered by:

    1. the collection of revenue on tribal land
    2. the use of greased cartridges
    3. the increase in salt duty
    4. the reduction in field size
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) the increase in salt duty

    Explanation: The correct answer is the increase in salt duty. The locals of Surat attacked Europeans following the increase in salt duty. Reduction in field size was linked with the Moplah Uprisings, revenue collection on tribal land was a broader grievance, and greased cartridges were the immediate cause of 1857. The Surat agitation occurred in Gujarat. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  8. Karam Shah rose against the zamindars in the movement known as:

    1. Khond Uprisings
    2. Paika Rebellion
    3. Moplah Uprisings
    4. Pagal Panthis
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Pagal Panthis

    Explanation: The correct answer is Pagal Panthis. Moplah Uprisings were in the Malabar region, Khond Uprisings were led by Chakra Bisoi, and the Paika Rebellion was in Orissa. Karam Shah was associated with Pagal Panthis in Bengal during 1825-35. This is a key leader-movement pair. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  9. With reference to the Moplah Uprisings, consider the following statements:1.They occurred in the Malabar region of Kerala.2.They lasted from 1836 to 1854.3.They opposed a hike in revenue demand and reduction in field size.Which of the statements given above are correct?

    1. 1, 2 and 3
    2. 1 and 2 only
    3. 1 and 3 only
    4. 2 and 3 only
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation: The correct answer is 1, 2 and 3. All three statements describe the Moplah Uprisings. The movement occurred in Kerala’s Malabar region during 1836-1854 and opposed the stated agrarian pressures. The Pagal Panthis, by contrast, were in Bengal. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  10. The Pahariya Rebellion of 1778 occurred in:

    1. Chhotanagpur region
    2. Raj Mahal Hills

    Answer: (B) Raj Mahal Hills

Chhotanagpur was associated with Ho and Munda uprisings, Northern Circars with the revolt of the Raja of Vizianagaram, and the hilly tracts from Tamil Nadu to Bengal with Khond Uprisings. The Pahariya Rebellion is recorded in Raj Mahal Hills. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

Birsa Munda led the 1899-1900 phase in the Chhotanagpur region. The same series also records phases in 1820-22 and 1831-37.   14. Arrange the following sepoy mutinies in chronological order:1.Mutiny in Sholapur2.Vellore Mutiny3.Revolt of the Grenadier Company in Assam4.Refusal of the 19th Native Infantry at Berhampore

    Answer: (B) 2-3-1-4

The 19th Native Infantry refused the Enfield rifle at Berhampore in February 1857. This sequence shows earlier military unrest before the Revolt of 1857.   15. What was the immediate cause of the Revolt of 1857?

    1. Reduction in field size
    2. Annexation of Indian states
    3. Greased cartridges of the new rifle
    4. Increase in salt duty
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Greased cartridges of the new rifle

    Explanation: The correct answer is greased cartridges of the new rifle. The cartridges had to be bitten before loading and were reportedly greased with beef and pig fat. Cow was sacred to Hindus and pig was taboo for Muslims, creating anger in the army. The other choices refer to different grievances or events. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  16. The 19th Native Infantry at Berhampore was disbanded in:

    1. April 1857
    2. May 1857
    3. February 1857
    4. March 1857
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) March 1857

    Explanation: The correct answer is March 1857. The unit refused to use the newly introduced Enfield rifle and mutinied in February 1857. It was disbanded in March 1857. April is associated with Mangal Pandey’s execution and May with the beginning at Meerut. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

    Answer: (A) 34th Native Infantry

    Explanation: The correct answer is 34th Native Infantry. Mangal Pandey fired at the sergeant major of his unit at Barrackpore. He was a young sepoy of the 34th Native Infantry and was executed on 8 April 1857. The 19th Native Infantry was the Berhampore unit that refused the Enfield rifle. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.

  19. The Revolt of 1857 began at Meerut on:

    1. 20 September 1857
    2. 8 April 1857
    3. 10 May 1857
    4. 4 June 1857
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 10 May 1857

    Explanation: The correct answer is 10 May 1857. The revolt began at Meerut, 58 km from Delhi, on 10 May 1857. 8 April was the execution of Mangal Pandey, 4 June was the outbreak at Lucknow, and 20 September was the British recapture of Delhi. Meerut was the starting point of the revolt. This distinction is useful for matching and chronology questions on the Revolt of 1857.
