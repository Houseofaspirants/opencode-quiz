Question bank

DPSP and Fundamental Duties - Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Directive Principles of State Policy are:

    1. Enforceable only by High Courts
    2. Enforceable through writs
    3. Justiciable
    4. Non-justiciable
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Non-justiciable

    Explanation: DPSPs are non-justiciable rights — not enforceable by courts.

  2. The idea of Directive Principles of State Policy was borrowed from the Constitution of:

    1. USA
    2. Canada
    3. Ireland
    4. UK
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Ireland

    Explanation: The framers borrowed the idea of DPSPs from the Irish Constitution.

  3. DPSPs resemble the 'Instrument of Instructions' enumerated in:

    1. Indian Independence Act, 1947
    2. Government of India Act, 1919
    3. Government of India Act, 1935
    4. Indian Councils Act, 1909
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Government of India Act, 1935

    Explanation: DPSPs are known as the 'Instrument of Instructions' enumerated in the Government of India Act, 1935.

  4. Which Article states that DPSPs are 'fundamental in the governance of the country'?

    1. Article 36
    2. Article 51
    3. Article 37
    4. Article 38
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 37

    Explanation: Article 37: DPSPs are fundamental in the governance of the country and it is the duty of the State to apply them in making laws.

  5. Who described the DPSPs as a 'novel feature' of the Indian Constitution?

    1. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
    2. K.T. Shah
    3. Granville Austin
    4. Jawaharlal Nehru
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

    Explanation: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar described the DPSPs as a 'novel feature' of the Indian Constitution.

  6. DPSPs are broadly classified into which three categories?

    1. Gandhian, Marxist and Liberal
    2. Socialistic, Gandhian and Liberal-Intellectual
    3. Civil, Political and Cultural
    4. Economic, Social and Political
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Socialistic, Gandhian and Liberal-Intellectual

    Explanation: DPSPs are classified into Socialistic, Gandhian and Liberal-Intellectual principles.

  7. DPSPs are best described as:

    1. Duties of citizens
    2. Ideals the State should keep in mind while formulating policies and enacting laws
    3. Rights of citizens enforceable against the State
    4. Powers of the President
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Ideals the State should keep in mind while formulating policies and enacting laws

    Explanation: DPSPs are ideals that the State should keep in mind while formulating policies and enacting laws.

  8. Consider the following statements:1.Fundamental Rights are justiciable while DPSPs are non-justiciable.2.DPSPs are contained in Part IV of the Constitution.Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Both 1 and 2

    Explanation: Both statements are correct. DPSPs are in Part IV (Articles 36–51).

  9. The Socialistic principles of DPSP mainly aim at:

    1. Village self-government
    2. International peace
    3. Uniform Civil Code
    4. Social and economic justice and a welfare state
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Social and economic justice and a welfare state

    Explanation: Socialistic principles aim at social and economic justice and set the path towards a welfare state.

  10. Which Article directs the State to secure a social order through justice and to minimise inequalities in income?

    1. Article 39
    2. Article 46
    3. Article 38
    4. Article 41
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 38

    Explanation: Article 38: promote welfare of people by securing a social order through justice, and minimise inequalities in income, status, facilities and opportunities.

  11. 'Equal pay for equal work for men and women' is a directive under:

    1. Article 42
    2. Article 43
    3. Article 38
    4. Article 39
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 39

    Explanation: Article 39 includes equal pay for equal work for men and women.

  12. Prevention of concentration of wealth and means of production is a directive under:

    1. Article 38
    2. Article 39
    3. Article 43
    4. Article 47
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 39

    Explanation: Article 39 directs prevention of concentration of wealth and means of production, and equitable distribution of material resources.

  13. Equal justice and free legal aid to the poor is provided under:

    1. Article 41
    2. Article 38
    3. Article 39A
    4. Article 50
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 39A

    Explanation: Article 39A: promote equal justice and free legal aid to the poor.

  14. The right to work, to education and to public assistance in cases of unemployment, old age, sickness and disablement is under:

    1. Article 42
    2. Article 41
    3. Article 39
    4. Article 43
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 41

    Explanation: Article 41 secures the right to work, education and public assistance in cases of unemployment, old age, sickness and disablement.

  15. Just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief are provided under:

    1. Article 43A
    2. Article 41
    3. Article 47
    4. Article 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 42

    Explanation: Article 42: provision for just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief.

  16. A living wage and a decent standard of living for all workers is a directive under:

    1. Article 43
    2. Article 39A
    3. Article 41
    4. Article 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 43

    Explanation: Article 43: secure a living wage, decent standard of living and social and cultural opportunities for all workers.

  17. Participation of workers in the management of industries is provided under:

    1. Article 39
    2. Article 43A
    3. Article 42
    4. Article 43B
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 43A

    Explanation: Article 43A: steps to secure participation of workers in management of industries (added by 42nd Amendment).

  18. Raising the level of nutrition and standard of living and improving public health is a directive under:

    1. Article 42
    2. Article 47
    3. Article 21
    4. Article 48
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 47

    Explanation: Article 47 directs the State to raise the level of nutrition and standard of living and improve public health.

  19. Organisation of village panchayats as units of self-government is a directive under:

    1. Article 46
    2. Article 243
    3. Article 40
    4. Article 43
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 40

    Explanation: Article 40 (Gandhian): organise village panchayats as units of self-government.

  20. Promotion of cottage industries in rural areas is a part of which category of DPSP?

    1. Gandhian
    2. None of these
    3. Socialistic
    4. Liberal-Intellectual
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Gandhian

    Explanation: Article 43 (promotion of cottage industries in rural areas) is a Gandhian principle.

  21. Which Article relates to the promotion of co-operative societies?

    1. Article 43A
    2. Article 40
    3. Article 48A
    4. Article 43B
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 43B

    Explanation: Article 43B: voluntary formation, autonomous functioning, democratic control and professional management of co-operative societies.

  22. Promotion of educational and economic interests of SCs, STs and other weaker sections is a directive under:

    1. Article 46
    2. Article 41
    3. Article 45
    4. Article 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 46

    Explanation: Article 46 promotes the educational and economic interests of SCs, STs and weaker sections.

  23. Prohibition of consumption of intoxicating drinks and drugs injurious to health is a directive under:

    1. Article 48
    2. Article 39
    3. Article 43
    4. Article 47
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 47

    Explanation: Article 47 (Gandhian aspect): prohibit consumption of intoxicating drinks and drugs injurious to health.

  24. Prohibition of slaughter of cows, calves and other milch and draught cattle is a directive under:

    1. Article 48
    2. Article 48A
    3. Article 47
    4. Article 51A
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 48

    Explanation: Article 48: prohibit slaughter of cows, calves and other milch and draught cattle and improve their breeds.

  25. Which of the following is NOT a Gandhian principle of DPSP?

    1. Uniform Civil Code (Article 44)
    2. Prohibition of intoxicating drinks (Article 47)
    3. Village panchayats (Article 40)
    4. Cottage industries (Article 43)
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Uniform Civil Code (Article 44)

    Explanation: Uniform Civil Code (Article 44) is a Liberal-Intellectual principle.
