Question bank

Union and its Territory - Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Till 1947, Sikkim was ruled by:

    1. The British Resident
    2. The Chogyal
    3. The Maharaja of Nepal
    4. The Dalai Lama
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) The Chogyal

    Explanation: Sikkim was ruled by the Chogyal till 1947; then it became a protectorate of India.

  2. As a 'protectorate' of India, which subjects of Sikkim were handled by India?

    1. Police, land revenue and education
    2. Defence, external affairs and communications
    3. Finance and taxation
    4. Only defence
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Defence, external affairs and communications

    Explanation: As a protectorate, India handled Sikkim's defence, external affairs and communications.

  3. Which three states were created in 1987?

    1. Manipur, Tripura and Meghalaya
    2. Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand and Jharkhand
    3. Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh and Goa
    4. Sikkim, Goa and Mizoram
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh and Goa

    Explanation: Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh and Goa became states in 1987 (23rd, 24th, 25th).

  4. Mizoram became a state following the Mizoram Peace Accord of:

    1. 1986
    2. 1985
    3. 1987
    4. 1972
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1986

    Explanation: Mizoram became a state (1987) after the Mizoram Peace Accord (1986).

  5. Arunachal Pradesh was earlier known as:

    1. NEFA
    2. Lushai Hills
    3. Naga Hills
    4. Tuensang
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) NEFA

    Explanation: The UT of Arunachal Pradesh (NEFA) came into being in 1972 and became a state in 1987.

  6. Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand and Jharkhand were carved out in 2000 from which states respectively?

    1. Madhya Pradesh, Bihar and Uttar Pradesh
    2. Bihar, Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh
    3. Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and Bihar
    4. Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh and Bihar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh and Bihar

    Explanation: Chhattisgarh – from Madhya Pradesh; Uttarakhand – from Uttar Pradesh; Jharkhand – from Bihar.

  7. Which is the 29th state created in India (2014)?

    1. Telangana
    2. Jharkhand
    3. Chhattisgarh
    4. Uttarakhand
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Telangana

    Explanation: Telangana (2014), carved out of Andhra Pradesh, was the 29th state.

  8. Which committee was appointed for the reorganisation of Andhra Pradesh (creation of Telangana)?

    1. Justice B.N. Srikrishna Committee
    2. Shah Commission
    3. Sarkaria Commission
    4. Fazl Ali Commission
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Justice B.N. Srikrishna Committee

    Explanation: The Justice B.N. Srikrishna Committee was appointed for the reorganisation of Andhra Pradesh. It also drafted the Personal Data Protection Bill.

  9. Arrange the following states in the order of their creation: 1. Nagaland 2. Sikkim 3. Haryana 4. Gujarat

    1. 4, 1, 2, 3
    2. 1, 4, 3, 2
    3. 4, 3, 1, 2
    4. 4, 1, 3, 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 4, 1, 3, 2

    Explanation: Gujarat (1960) → Nagaland (1963) → Haryana (1966) → Sikkim (1975).

  10. Match the states with the year of their creation: A. Himachal Pradesh – 1. 1987 B. Goa – 2. 2000 C. Jharkhand – 3. 1971 D. Telangana – 4. 2014

    1. A-3, B-2, C-1, D-4
    2. A-3, B-1, C-4, D-2
    3. A-1, B-3, C-2, D-4
    4. A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) A-3, B-1, C-2, D-4

    Explanation: Himachal Pradesh – 1971; Goa – 1987; Jharkhand – 2000; Telangana – 2014.

  11. At present, how many states and union territories are there in India?

    1. 29 states and 7 union territories
    2. 28 states and 8 union territories
    3. 28 states and 9 union territories
    4. 29 states and 9 union territories
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 28 states and 8 union territories

    Explanation: India now has 28 states and 8 union territories.

  12. By the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, the State of J&K was bifurcated into:

    1. Two states — Jammu and Kashmir
    2. One state and one union territory
    3. Three union territories
    4. Two union territories — Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Two union territories — Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh

    Explanation: The J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019 bifurcated the State into two UTs — Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh.

  13. Consider the following statements: 1. The UT of Jammu and Kashmir has a legislative assembly. 2. The UT of Ladakh has a legislative assembly. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1 only

    Explanation: UT of J&K has a legislative assembly; UT of Ladakh does not and is administered by the Lieutenant Governor alone.

  14. The UT of Ladakh is administered by:

    1. An elected Chief Minister
    2. The Lieutenant Governor alone
    3. A council of ministers of J&K
    4. The Governor of Himachal Pradesh
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) The Lieutenant Governor alone

    Explanation: Ladakh does not have a legislative assembly and is administered by the Lieutenant Governor alone.

  15. Chandigarh is the capital of which state(s)?

    1. Punjab and Himachal Pradesh
    2. Haryana and Himachal Pradesh
    3. Punjab and Haryana
    4. Punjab only
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Punjab and Haryana

    Explanation: Chandigarh is the capital of both Punjab and Haryana, and is itself a Union Territory.

  16. What is the capital of Andhra Pradesh?

    1. Visakhapatnam
    2. Amaravati
    3. Hyderabad
    4. Vijayawada
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Amaravati

    Explanation: The capital of Andhra Pradesh is Amaravati.

  17. Dispur is the capital of which state?

    1. Meghalaya
    2. Arunachal Pradesh
    3. Nagaland
    4. Assam
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Assam

    Explanation: Dispur is the capital of Assam.

  18. Match the states with their capitals: A. Mizoram – 1. Kohima B. Nagaland – 2. Aizawl C. Manipur – 3. Imphal D. Tripura – 4. Agartala

    1. A-2, B-3, C-1, D-4
    2. A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
    3. A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
    4. A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4

    Explanation: Mizoram – Aizawl; Nagaland – Kohima; Manipur – Imphal; Tripura – Agartala.

  19. Gangtok is the capital of which state?

    1. Uttarakhand
    2. Arunachal Pradesh
    3. Sikkim
    4. Meghalaya
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Sikkim

    Explanation: Gangtok is the capital of Sikkim.

  20. Which Union Territory has two capitals — Srinagar (summer) and Jammu (winter)?

    1. Himachal Pradesh
    2. Jammu and Kashmir
    3. Chandigarh
    4. Ladakh
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Jammu and Kashmir

    Explanation: The UT of Jammu and Kashmir has Srinagar as summer capital and Jammu as winter capital.

  21. What is the capital of the Union Territory of Ladakh?

    1. Jammu
    2. Kargil
    3. Srinagar
    4. Leh
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Leh

    Explanation: Leh is the capital of the UT of Ladakh.

  22. Kavaratti is the capital of which Union Territory?

    1. Andaman and Nicobar Islands
    2. Puducherry
    3. Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu
    4. Lakshadweep
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Lakshadweep

    Explanation: Kavaratti is the capital of Lakshadweep.

  23. What is the capital of the Union Territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu?

    1. Panaji
    2. Silvassa
    3. Daman
    4. Diu
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Daman

    Explanation: The capital of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu is Daman.

  24. Which of the following state–capital pairs is INCORRECTLY matched?

    1. Odisha – Bhubaneswar
    2. Uttarakhand – Shimla
    3. Chhattisgarh – Raipur
    4. Jharkhand – Ranchi
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Uttarakhand – Shimla

    Explanation: Uttarakhand's capital is Dehradun; Shimla is the capital of Himachal Pradesh.

  25. Shillong is the capital of which state?

    1. Meghalaya
    2. Assam
    3. Manipur
    4. Mizoram
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Meghalaya

    Explanation: Shillong is the capital of Meghalaya.
