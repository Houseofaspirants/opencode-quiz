Question bank

Fundamental Rights and Writs - Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Match the Articles with their provisions:A.Article 14 – 1. Abolition of titlesB.Article 16 – 2. Abolition of untouchabilityC.Article 17 – 3. Equality of opportunity in public employmentD.Article 18 – 4. Equality before law

    1. A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1
    2. A-3, B-4, C-2, D-1
    3. A-4, B-3, C-1, D-2
    4. A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) A-4, B-3, C-2, D-1

    Explanation: Art 14 – equality before law; Art 16 – public employment; Art 17 – untouchability; Art 18 – titles.

  2. How many freedoms are protected under Article 19?

    1. Six
    2. Five
    3. Seven
    4. Four
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Six

    Explanation: Article 19 protects six freedoms: speech and expression, assembly, association, movement, residence and profession.

  3. Which of the following is NOT one of the six freedoms under Article 19?

    1. Freedom of profession
    2. Freedom of religion
    3. Freedom of assembly
    4. Freedom of movement
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Freedom of religion

    Explanation: Freedom of religion is under Articles 25–28, not Article 19.

  4. Protection in respect of conviction for offences is provided under:

    1. Article 19
    2. Article 22
    3. Article 21
    4. Article 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 20

    Explanation: Article 20 provides protection in respect of conviction for offences.

  5. Protection of life and personal liberty, including the right to privacy, falls under:

    1. Article 20
    2. Article 21
    3. Article 19
    4. Article 22
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 21

    Explanation: Article 21 protects life and personal liberty, including the right to privacy.

  6. The Right to Elementary Education was added as a Fundamental Right under:

    1. Article 21A
    2. Article 45
    3. Article 51A
    4. Article 29
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 21A

    Explanation: Article 21A (Right to elementary education) was added by the 86th Amendment, 2002.

  7. Which Constitutional Amendment added Article 21A to the Constitution?

    1. 42nd Amendment, 1976
    2. 86th Amendment, 2002
    3. 44th Amendment, 1978
    4. 93rd Amendment, 2005
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 86th Amendment, 2002

    Explanation: Article 21A was added by the 86th Amendment Act, 2002.

  8. Protection against arrest and detention in certain cases is provided by:

    1. Article 32
    2. Article 21
    3. Article 22
    4. Article 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 22

    Explanation: Article 22 gives protection against arrest and detention in certain cases.

  9. Consider the following statements:1.Article 21A was part of the original Constitution.2.The right to privacy is protected under Article 21.Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2 only

    Explanation: Article 21A was added by the 86th Amendment (2002). Right to privacy is under Article 21.

  10. Prohibition of traffic in human beings and forced labour is provided under:

    1. Article 23
    2. Article 17
    3. Article 24
    4. Article 21
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 23

    Explanation: Article 23 prohibits traffic in human beings and forced labour.

  11. Prohibition of employment of children in factories is provided under:

    1. Article 21A
    2. Article 23
    3. Article 45
    4. Article 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 24

    Explanation: Article 24 prohibits employment of children in factories, etc.

  12. The Right against Exploitation includes which Articles?

    1. 25 and 26
    2. 21 and 22
    3. 23 and 24
    4. 17 and 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 23 and 24

    Explanation: Right against Exploitation: Articles 23–24.

  13. Freedom of conscience and free profession, practice and propagation of religion is guaranteed by:

    1. Article 25
    2. Article 28
    3. Article 27
    4. Article 26
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 25

    Explanation: Article 25 guarantees freedom of conscience and free profession, practice and propagation of religion.

  14. Freedom to manage religious affairs is provided under:

    1. Article 26
    2. Article 27
    3. Article 30
    4. Article 25
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 26

    Explanation: Article 26 provides freedom to manage religious affairs.

  15. Freedom from payment of taxes for promotion of any particular religion is provided under:

    1. Article 28
    2. Article 25
    3. Article 26
    4. Article 27
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 27

    Explanation: Article 27: freedom from payment of taxes for promotion of any religion.

  16. Freedom from attending religious instruction or worship in certain educational institutions is provided under:

    1. Article 27
    2. Article 29
    3. Article 30
    4. Article 28
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Article 28

    Explanation: Article 28 gives freedom from attending religious instruction or worship in certain educational institutions.

  17. Protection of language, script and culture of minorities is provided under:

    1. Article 350A
    2. Article 30
    3. Article 29
    4. Article 26
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Article 29

    Explanation: Article 29 protects the language, script and culture of minorities.

  18. The right of minorities to establish and administer educational institutions is given in:

    1. Article 29
    2. Article 30
    3. Article 28
    4. Article 21A
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 30

    Explanation: Article 30 gives minorities the right to establish and administer educational institutions.

  19. Which Article is known as the 'soul of the Constitution'?

    1. Article 21
    2. Article 32
    3. Article 14
    4. Article 19
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Article 32

    Explanation: Article 32 — Right to Constitutional Remedies — is known as the soul of the Constitution.

  20. The Right to Constitutional Remedies gives the right to move which court for enforcement of Fundamental Rights?

    1. District Court
    2. Consumer Court
    3. Supreme Court
    4. Lok Adalat
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Supreme Court

    Explanation: Article 32 gives the right to move the Supreme Court for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.

  21. Under Article 32, Parliament can:

    1. Issue writs itself
    2. Abolish the writ jurisdiction of the Supreme Court
    3. Authorise any other court to issue writs
    4. Transfer writ powers to the President
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Authorise any other court to issue writs

    Explanation: Article 32 also empowers Parliament to authorise any other court to issue these writs.

  22. A writ is:

    1. An ordinance of the President
    2. A petition filed by a citizen
    3. A bill passed by Parliament
    4. A written order of the Supreme Court or a High Court for enforcement of rights
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) A written order of the Supreme Court or a High Court for enforcement of rights

    Explanation: A writ is a written order from the Supreme Court or a High Court commanding constitutional remedies against violation of Fundamental Rights.

  23. High Courts derive the power to issue writs from:

    1. Article 226
    2. Article 227
    3. Article 136
    4. Article 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Article 226

    Explanation: The Supreme Court issues writs under Article 32; High Courts under Article 226.

  24. How many kinds of writs can the Supreme Court issue?

    1. Three
    2. Six
    3. Four
    4. Five
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Five

    Explanation: Five writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari and Quo-Warranto.

  25. The literal meaning of 'Habeas Corpus' is:

    1. To forbid
    2. We command
    3. To have the body of
    4. By what authority
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) To have the body of

    Explanation: Habeas Corpus literally means 'to have the body of'.
