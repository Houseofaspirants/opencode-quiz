Question bank

Problems on Numbers MCQ – Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. When a number is divided by 8, the result is 42 less than the number. Find the number.

    1. 42
    2. 54
    3. 56
    4. 48
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 48

    Explanation: 7/8 of the number is 42, so the number = 42 × 8/7 = 48.

  2. 2/3 of a number added to 5/8 of 192 gives 248. Find 25% of the number.

    1. 64
    2. 192
    3. 48
    4. 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 48

    Explanation: 2x/3 = 248 − 120 = 128, so x = 192 and 25% of x = 48.

  3. Simran spends 1/5 of her money, then 25% of what remains. She next spends ₹90 and is left with 40% of her original money. Find her original amount.

    1. 360
    2. 300
    3. 600
    4. 450
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 450

    Explanation: After the first two expenses, 60% remains; hence 20% of the original = ₹90 and original amount = ₹450.

  4. A machine adds 12 to an input, halves the result and then subtracts 7. If the output is 31, what input was used?

    1. 64
    2. 50
    3. 76
    4. 88
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 64

    Explanation: Input = (31 + 7) × 2 − 12 = 64.

  5. Two numbers have sum 93 and difference 17. Find twice the smaller number.

    1. 38
    2. 110
    3. 55
    4. 76
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 76

    Explanation: Twice the smaller number = sum − difference = 93 − 17 = 76.

  6. The first number is 3/4 of the second, and the second is 5/6 of the third. If the first and third together total 156, find the second.

    1. 60
    2. 120
    3. 96
    4. 80
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 80

    Explanation: First : second : third = 15 : 20 : 24; 39 parts = 156, so second = 20 × 4 = 80.

  7. For three numbers, first + second = 82 and second + third = 94. The first is 2/3 of the third. Find the second.

    1. 24
    2. 36
    3. 58
    4. 70
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 58

    Explanation: Third − first = 12; with first : third = 2 : 3, first = 24 and second = 82 − 24 = 58.

  8. Two positive integers have product 2028. Dividing the larger by the smaller gives quotient 3 with no remainder. Find the sum of their squares.

    1. 6760
    2. 8112
    3. 6084
    4. 2704
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 6760

    Explanation: Let the numbers be s and 3s: 3s² = 2028 gives s² = 676; square sum = 10s² = 6760.

  9. The average of six consecutive multiples of 4 is 54. Find the smallest multiple.

    1. 44
    2. 46
    3. 48
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 44

    Explanation: Six terms spaced by 4 have average 10 above the first; smallest = 54 − 10 = 44.

  10. The squares of two consecutive positive odd integers differ by 192. Find their product.

    1. 2499
    2. 2209
    3. 2401
    4. 2303
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 2303

    Explanation: Their sum = 192 ÷ 2 = 96, so the numbers are 47 and 49; product = 48² − 1 = 2303.

  11. Positive numbers a and b satisfy a + b = 19 and ab = 84. Find a³ + b³.

    1. 2071
    2. 2359
    3. 1729
    4. 5263
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 2071

    Explanation: a³ + b³ = (a + b)³ − 3ab(a + b) = 19³ − 3 × 84 × 19 = 2071.

  12. Two positive fractions have product 27/100, and the larger is three times the smaller. Find their difference.

    1. 6/5
    2. 3/10
    3. 9/10
    4. 3/5
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 3/5

    Explanation: If the smaller is f, 3f² = 27/100 gives f = 3/10; difference = 2f = 3/5.

  13. A two-digit number exceeds its digit sum by 63. What is its tens digit?

    1. 9
    2. 7
    3. 8
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 7

    Explanation: Number − digit sum = 9 × tens digit, so tens digit = 63 ÷ 9 = 7.

  14. A two-digit number and its reverse add up to 132. The original number exceeds twice its reverse by 15. Find the original number.

    1. 39
    2. 96
    3. 93
    4. 84
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 93

    Explanation: If the reverse is r, 3r + 15 = 132 gives r = 39; original = 132 − 39 = 93.

  15. In a three-digit number, the tens digit is 4. Reversing all its digits increases it by 396, and the digit sum is 14. Find the number.

    1. 446
    2. 347
    3. 743
    4. 545
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 347

    Explanation: Units − hundreds = 396 ÷ 99 = 4 and units + hundreds = 10; digits are 3, 4, 7, giving 347.

  16. The numerator of a fraction is 4 less than its denominator. Adding 5 to both makes the fraction 7/9. Find the sum of the original numerator and denominator.

    1. 18
    2. 26
    3. 22
    4. 32
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 22

    Explanation: 9(n + 5) = 7(n + 9), so 2n = 18; numerator = 9, denominator = 13, sum = 22.

  17. What is the larger of two positive numbers? Statement I: Their sum is 64. Statement II: Their difference is 18. Which assessment is correct?

    1. Statement II alone is sufficient
    2. Both statements together are sufficient; neither alone is sufficient
    3. Statement I alone is sufficient
    4. Even both statements together are insufficient
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Both statements together are sufficient; neither alone is sufficient

    Explanation: Neither sum nor difference alone fixes the larger number; together they give (64 + 18) ÷ 2 = 41.

  18. 4 times a number plus 23 equals 6 times the number minus 15. Find the number.

    1. 8
    2. 38
    3. 19
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 19

    Explanation: 2x = 23 + 15 = 38, so x = 19.

  19. 1/3 of 4/5 of a number equals 72. By how much does the number exceed its 2/9 part?

    1. 60
    2. 270
    3. 210
    4. 198
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 210

    Explanation: The number = 72 × 15/4 = 270; excess over its 2/9 part = 7/9 × 270 = 210.

  20. A number is increased by 20%. Then 25% of the increased number is subtracted, and 36 is added. The final result equals the original number. Find the number.

    1. 240
    2. 360
    3. 400
    4. 180
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 360

    Explanation: Net multiplier = 1.20 × 0.75 = 0.90; thus 10% of the number = 36 and the number is 360.

  21. Two numbers have sum 172. If 14 is transferred from the larger to the smaller, the two become equal. Find the original smaller number.

    1. 72
    2. 79
    3. 86
    4. 58
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 72

    Explanation: Original difference = 2 × 14 = 28; smaller = (172 − 28) ÷ 2 = 72.

  22. Three numbers are in the ratio 2 : 5 : 7. The largest exceeds the smallest by 85. Find the middle number.

    1. 119
    2. 85
    3. 68
    4. 34
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 85

    Explanation: 5 ratio parts = 85, so one part = 17; middle number = 5 × 17 = 85.

  23. Nine consecutive integers have average 113. What is the sum of the smallest and largest?

    1. 226
    2. 225
    3. 217
    4. 234
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 226

    Explanation: For consecutive integers, smallest + largest = 2 × average = 226.

  24. The sum of the squares of three consecutive positive integers is 365. Find the largest integer.

    1. 11
    2. 12
    3. 10
    4. 13
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 12

    Explanation: If m is the middle integer, 3m² + 2 = 365 gives m = 11; largest = 12.

  25. A two-digit number has digit sum 14. Its tens digit is 2 more than its units digit. What is 25% of the number?

    1. 20.5
    2. 21.5
    3. 16.5
    4. 24
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 21.5

    Explanation: Digits are (14 + 2) ÷ 2 = 8 and 6; number = 86, so 25% = 21.5.
