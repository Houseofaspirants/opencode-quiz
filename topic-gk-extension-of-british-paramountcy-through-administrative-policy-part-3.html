  1. In which year was Sindh conquered, with Lord Ellenborough as Governor-General?

    1. 1845
    2. 1848
    3. 1843
    4. 1849
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1843

    Explanation: The correct answer is 1843. Sindh was conquered in 1843, when Lord Ellenborough was Governor-General. It therefore satisfies the association or chronology asked in the question. The other alternatives are incorrect here: 1845 marks the First Anglo-Sikh War, while 1848–49 marks the Second Anglo-Sikh War and Punjab's annexation. This distinction is useful for policy, treaty and state-association questions.

  2. The Treaty of Eternal Friendship was between the British and:

    1. Ranjit Singh
    2. The Nawab of Awadh
    3. Dost Mohammed
    4. The Amirs of Sindh
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) The Amirs of Sindh

    Explanation: The correct answer is The Amirs of Sindh. The Treaty of Eternal Friendship was between the Amirs of Sindh and the British. It therefore satisfies the association or chronology asked in the question. The other alternatives are incorrect here: Ranjit Singh is linked with the Treaty of Amritsar, while Awadh and Dost Mohammed belong to different contexts. This distinction is useful for policy, treaty and state-association questions.

  3. The Treaty of Amritsar mentioned in the chapter was between:

    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Ranjit Singh and the British

It therefore satisfies the association or chronology asked in the question. The other alternatives are incorrect here: The Tripartite Treaty added Shah Shuja; the other two options describe the Treaty of Sagauli and the Treaty of Lhasa. This distinction is useful for policy, treaty and state-association questions.

It therefore satisfies the association or chronology asked in the question. The other alternatives are incorrect here: Yakub Khan belongs to the Treaty of Gandamak; Nepal and Tibet belong to other treaties; Awadh and Shah Alam II belong to the Allahabad treaties. This distinction is useful for policy, treaty and state-association questions.

The other alternatives are incorrect here: The First Anglo-Sikh War was in 1845–46; the Maratha and Afghan wars concern different regions and periods. This distinction is useful for policy, treaty and state-association questions.

  7. Who defeated Siraj-ud-daula at the Battle of Plassey on 23 June 1757?

The other alternatives are incorrect here: Hastings, Wellesley and Dalhousie are associated with later policies, not this battle victory.
