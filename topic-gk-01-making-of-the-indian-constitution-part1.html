Question bank

Making of the Indian Constitution - Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Which country made the world's first written constitution?

    1. United Kingdom
    2. France
    3. Canada
    4. USA
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) USA

    Explanation: The world's first constitution was created by the people of the USA in 1789.

  2. In which year did the USA make the world's first constitution?

    1. 1787
    2. 1776
    3. 1789
    4. 1791
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1789

    Explanation: The first constitution of the world was made by the USA in 1789.

  3. A constitution is best described as:

    1. A book of religious laws only
    2. A collection of court judgements
    3. A rule book made by members of a society to run that society effectively
    4. A list of government employees
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) A rule book made by members of a society to run that society effectively

    Explanation: A constitution is a rule book created by the members of a society to run that society effectively.

  4. Who first put forward the idea of a Constituent Assembly for India?

    1. Mahatma Gandhi
    2. Jawaharlal Nehru
    3. M.N. Roy
    4. B.R. Ambedkar
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) M.N. Roy

    Explanation: The idea of a Constituent Assembly for India was first put forward in 1934 by M.N. Roy.

  5. In which year was the idea of a Constituent Assembly for India first put forward?

    1. 1940
    2. 1946
    3. 1934
    4. 1929
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 1934

    Explanation: M.N. Roy first proposed the idea of a Constituent Assembly in 1934.

  6. The Constituent Assembly of India was constituted under the scheme formulated by:

    1. Cabinet Mission
    2. Mountbatten Plan
    3. Simon Commission
    4. Cripps Mission
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Cabinet Mission

    Explanation: The Constituent Assembly was constituted in November 1946 under the scheme formulated by the Cabinet Mission.

  7. What was the total strength of the Constituent Assembly originally meant to be?

    1. 395
    2. 389
    3. 299
    4. 250
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 389

    Explanation: Its total strength was to be 389; after partition it came down to 299.

  8. After independence and partition, the strength of the Constituent Assembly came down to:

    1. 299
    2. 389
    3. 324
    4. 250
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 299

    Explanation: After partition of India, the strength of the Constituent Assembly was reduced from 389 to 299.

  9. Which of the following best describes the nature of the Constituent Assembly of India?

    1. Fully elected by universal adult franchise
    2. Fully nominated by the British Crown
    3. Partly elected and partly nominated body
    4. Fully nominated by the Indian National Congress
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Partly elected and partly nominated body

    Explanation: The Constituent Assembly was a partly elected and partly nominated body.

  10. Which important personality of that time was NOT a member of the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Sardar Patel
    2. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    3. Jawaharlal Nehru
    4. Mahatma Gandhi
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Mahatma Gandhi

    Explanation: The Constituent Assembly included all important personalities of India at that time except Mahatma Gandhi.

  11. How many Sikh seats were there in the Constituent Assembly?

    1. 4
    2. 2
    3. 6
    4. 10
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 4

    Explanation: There were only 4 Sikh seats in the Constituent Assembly.

  12. Who was appointed as the constitutional (legal) advisor to the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Sir B.N. Rau
    2. H.C. Mukherjee
    3. Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar
    4. K.M. Munshi
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Sir B.N. Rau

    Explanation: Sir B.N. Rau was appointed as the constitutional (legal) advisor to the Constituent Assembly.

  13. How many Vice-Presidents did the Constituent Assembly have?

    1. None
    2. Three
    3. One
    4. Two
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Two

    Explanation: The Constituent Assembly had two Vice-Presidents.

  14. When did the Constituent Assembly hold its first meeting?

    1. 15 August 1947
    2. 9 December 1946
    3. 13 December 1946
    4. 26 November 1949
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 9 December 1946

    Explanation: The Constituent Assembly met for the first time in New Delhi on 9 December 1946.

  15. Where was the first meeting of the Constituent Assembly held?

    1. Town Hall, Bombay
    2. Writers' Building, Calcutta
    3. Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi
    4. Constitution Hall, New Delhi
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Constitution Hall, New Delhi

    Explanation: The first meeting was held in the Constitution Hall, New Delhi on 9 December 1946.

  16. Who was elected as the temporary President of the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Jawaharlal Nehru
    2. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
    3. Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha
    4. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha

    Explanation: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha, the oldest member, was elected as the temporary President of the Assembly.

  17. Who was elected as the (permanent) President of the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha
    2. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    3. Jawaharlal Nehru
    4. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Dr. Rajendra Prasad

    Explanation: After the temporary President, Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected as the President of the Assembly by voting.

  18. On which date was Dr. Rajendra Prasad elected President of the Constituent Assembly?

    1. 11 December 1946
    2. 13 December 1946
    3. 9 December 1946
    4. 29 August 1947
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 11 December 1946

    Explanation: As per the timeline, Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected President of the Assembly on 11 December 1946.

  19. Who moved the historic 'Objectives Resolution' in the Constituent Assembly?

    1. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
    2. Jawaharlal Nehru
    3. Sardar Patel
    4. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Jawaharlal Nehru

    Explanation: On 13 December 1946, Jawaharlal Nehru moved the historic Objectives Resolution.

  20. On which date was the Objectives Resolution moved in the Constituent Assembly?

    1. 26 November 1949
    2. 9 December 1946
    3. 22 July 1947
    4. 13 December 1946
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 13 December 1946

    Explanation: Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution on 13 December 1946.

  21. What was the significance of the Objectives Resolution?

    1. It adopted the National Anthem
    2. It provided the framework of the Constitution
    3. It partitioned India
    4. It declared India a republic immediately
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) It provided the framework of the Constitution

    Explanation: The Objectives Resolution provided the framework (philosophy) of the Constitution.

  22. On which date was the Constitution of India adopted?

    1. 26 November 1949
    2. 24 January 1950
    3. 26 January 1950
    4. 15 August 1947
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 26 November 1949

    Explanation: The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949.

  23. On which date did the Constitution of India come into force?

    1. 9 December 1946
    2. 26 January 1950
    3. 15 August 1947
    4. 26 November 1949
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 26 January 1950

    Explanation: The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950, celebrated as Republic Day.

  24. When did the Constituent Assembly hold its last session?

    1. 26 November 1949
    2. 21 February 1948
    3. 24 January 1950
    4. 26 January 1950
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 24 January 1950

    Explanation: The Constituent Assembly held its last session on 24 January 1950.

  25. Which of the following events took place on 24 January 1950?

    1. Adoption of the Constitution and National Flag
    2. Constitution came into force
    3. First meeting of the Constituent Assembly
    4. Last session of the Constituent Assembly, adoption of the National Anthem and signing of the Constitution
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Last session of the Constituent Assembly, adoption of the National Anthem and signing of the Constitution

    Explanation: On 24 January 1950: last session of the Assembly, National Anthem adopted and Constitution signed.
