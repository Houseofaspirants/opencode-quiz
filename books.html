How many books do I actually need for one exam?

One reference book per subject, plus your notes and practice sets. Most candidates buy too many books and finish none of them — one book read twice beats three books read once.

Should I buy a book or use the free material here?

Start free. The notes, Expected MCQs and mock tests here cover the syllabus first; a book earns its price when you need worked examples for a method you still cannot follow.

Which book should I start with?

The subject you score worst in. Open a free practice set, notice which chapters cost you marks, and buy the book that explains exactly those chapters.

Do I need the latest edition every year?

Not for grammar, arithmetic or reasoning — those concepts do not change. For current affairs and year books, yes, because the content itself is dated.
