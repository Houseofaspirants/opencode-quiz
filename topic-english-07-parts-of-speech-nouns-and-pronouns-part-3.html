Question bank

07 Parts Of Speech Nouns And Pronouns Part 3: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Complete the sentence: The woman ___ teaches accountancy has joined the selection board.

    1. whom
    2. whose
    3. which
    4. who
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) who

    Explanation: Who refers to the woman and is the subject of teaches. Whom is an object form.

  2. Complete the sentence: When one makes a promise, one should keep ___ word.

    1. one's
    2. ones
    3. ones'
    4. one
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) one's

    Explanation: The possessive form of the pronoun one is one's. Ones without an apostrophe does not express possession.

  3. Complete the sentence: The author and the publisher signed a joint declaration, ___ has now been released publicly.

    1. which
    2. who
    3. that
    4. whose
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) which

    Explanation: Which introduces a non-defining clause referring to the declaration. That is not used after the comma to introduce this kind of relative clause.

  4. Choose the correct completion: The vehicle belongs to the person who manages the branch. It is the ___ vehicle.

    1. person's who manages the branch
    2. person who manage's the branch
    3. person who manages the branches'
    4. person who manages the branch's
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) person who manages the branch's

    Explanation: In a group genitive, the possessive ending can attach to the end of the entire phrase identifying the owner. Person's who manages the branch breaks the noun phrase by placing the possessive ending before its defining clause.

  5. Complete the sentence: The cashier handed the refund to ___.

    1. us
    2. we
    3. our
    4. ours
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) us

    Explanation: To requires an objective pronoun, so us is correct. We is used as a subject.

  6. Complete the sentence: The rescue workers congratulated ___ on completing the operation safely.

    1. themselves
    2. theirselves
    3. themselfs
    4. theirself
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) themselves

    Explanation: The plural reflexive pronoun referring to the workers is themselves. Theirselves is not the standard form.

  7. Complete the sentence: The registrar will appoint ___ the governing body recommends.

    1. whoever
    2. whomever
    3. whosever
    4. who
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) whomever

    Explanation: Whomever is the object of recommends within the clause. Whoever would be required if the pronoun were performing the recommending.

  8. Choose the correct completion: The laboratory conducted two independent ___ of the water sample.

    1. analysises
    2. analyses
    3. analysis
    4. analysises'
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) analyses

    Explanation: Analyses is the plural of analysis. Analysis is singular and cannot follow two in this context.

  9. Choose the correct replacement for 'an information': The help desk gave me an information about the venue.

    1. an informations
    2. a information
    3. a piece of information
    4. a pieces of information
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) a piece of information

    Explanation: Information is uncountable, so an individual item is a piece of information. An information incorrectly treats the noun as countable.

  10. Which pair identifies a proper noun followed by a common noun in 'Amritsar attracts tourists throughout the year'?

    1. tourists; Amritsar
    2. year; tourists
    3. Amritsar; throughout
    4. Amritsar; tourists
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Amritsar; tourists

    Explanation: Amritsar names a particular city, whereas tourists names a general class of people. Throughout is a preposition, not a common noun.

  11. Complete the sentence: The delegation members exchanged notes with one ___ before entering the chamber.

    1. another's
    2. anothers
    3. another
    4. anothers'
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) another

    Explanation: One another expresses reciprocal interaction among the members. The possessive another's cannot function as the object required here without an appropriate possessed noun.

  12. Choose the correct completion: Only one of the five pumps has lost ___ protective cover.

    1. their
    2. theirs
    3. it's
    4. its
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) its

    Explanation: One is the singular antecedent, so its refers to the single affected pump. Their would incorrectly connect the missing cover to the plural pumps.

  13. Choose the correctly punctuated expression for a hospital intended for children.

    1. childrens hospital
    2. childrens' hospital
    3. childrens's hospital
    4. children's hospital
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) children's hospital

    Explanation: Children is an irregular plural without a final s and takes apostrophe-s. Childrens' incorrectly adds an extra s before forming the possessive.

  14. Complete the sentence: The official from ___ we obtained permission has been transferred.

    1. who
    2. whose
    3. whom
    4. that
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) whom

    Explanation: Whom is required directly after the preposition from in formal written English. That cannot follow a fronted preposition.

  15. Complete the sentence: The clerk and ___ have already checked the attendance register.

    1. I
    2. me
    3. my
    4. mine
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) I

    Explanation: The coordinated phrase is the subject of have checked, so I is required. Me is an object pronoun.

  16. Complete the sentence: Neither of the two passwords works; ___ must be replaced with a valid one.

    1. they
    2. their
    3. it
    4. them
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) it

    Explanation: It serves as the singular subject referring distributively to either invalid password. Them is an object form and cannot be the subject of must be replaced.

  17. Complete the sentence: The chicks followed the ___ across the courtyard.

    1. roosteress
    2. hen
    3. henness
    4. roostress
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) hen

    Explanation: Hen is the standard female counterpart of rooster. Roosteress is not a standard English word.

  18. Choose the correct completion: The traveller bought two ___ containing detailed maps.

    1. guidesbooks
    2. guidebooks
    3. guidebook's
    4. guidesbook
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) guidebooks

    Explanation: Guidebook is a compound noun whose plural ending attaches to the final element: guidebooks. Guidesbooks incorrectly pluralises both elements.

  19. Choose the correct completion: The badge belongs to the commander-in-chief. It is the ___ badge.

    1. commander's-in-chief
    2. commanders-in-chief'
    3. commander-in-chief's
    4. commander-in-chiefs'
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) commander-in-chief's

    Explanation: The possessive ending attaches to the end of the complete singular compound: commander-in-chief's. Commander’s-in-chief incorrectly places it inside the compound.

  20. Choose the sentence that correctly means 'I submitted more applications than she submitted.'

    1. I submitted more applications than her did.
    2. I submitted more applications than she did.
    3. I submitted more applications than hers did.
    4. I submitted more applications than herself did.
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) I submitted more applications than she did.

    Explanation: She is the subject of the explicit verb did in the comparative clause. Her cannot serve as that subject.

  21. Complete the sentence: Please send the confidential attachment directly to the director or ___.

    1. myself
    2. I
    3. mine
    4. me
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) me

    Explanation: The pronoun is governed by to and must take the objective form me. Myself lacks a matching first-person subject and is not a formal substitute for me.

  22. Choose the sentence in which 'experience' is used as a countable noun.

    1. The trek gave her several unforgettable experiences.
    2. The post requires considerable administrative experience.
    3. He gained experience by working in rural branches.
    4. Practical experience improves a technician's judgement.
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) The trek gave her several unforgettable experiences.

    Explanation: Experiences means separate events that a person undergoes and is countable. Experience meaning accumulated knowledge or skill is uncountable.

  23. In 'Patience helped the trainee handle repeated complaints', 'Patience' is a:

    1. common noun naming a person
    2. collective noun naming a group
    3. material noun naming a substance
    4. abstract noun naming a quality
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) abstract noun naming a quality

    Explanation: Patience names a quality and is an abstract noun. Its capital letter at the beginning of the sentence does not make it a proper noun.

  24. Complete the sentence: The two neighbours borrowed each ___ gardening tools.

    1. others
    2. other's
    3. others'
    4. other
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) other's

    Explanation: The reciprocal expression each other forms its possessive as each other's. Each others' wrongly treats other as a plural noun here.

  25. Complete the sentence: Several ___ were grazing beyond the railway crossing.

    1. oxes
    2. ox's
    3. oxen
    4. oxens
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) oxen

    Explanation: Oxen is the irregular plural of ox. Oxens incorrectly adds another plural ending.
