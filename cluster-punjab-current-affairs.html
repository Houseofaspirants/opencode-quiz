About this cluster

What Punjab Current Affairs covers

Why the lane is split this way and the order to work through it.

Punjab Current Affairs is a focused, high-return lane because the pool of material is far smaller than national news, yet state-level papers draw on it heavily. Questions cluster around state schemes, appointments, awards, sporting achievement and governance decisions taken during the year. Because the volume is manageable, this is one of the few current-affairs areas that can realistically be covered completely rather than sampled. The main risk is staleness: state details change quickly, so confirm anything dated against an official government source before treating it as settled. The lanes below keep the material organised by theme, which makes a weekly revision pass practical - read once during the week, then test the whole set at the weekend until recall is automatic.
