Question bank

Blood Relations MCQ – Part 2: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Saira’s mother has an elder sister, Nasreen. Nasreen’s son is Faiz. Complete the chain: Faiz is Saira’s _____.

    1. Maternal-aunt’s son
    2. Paternal-aunt’s son
    3. Maternal-uncle’s son
    4. Sister’s son
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Maternal-aunt’s son

    Explanation: Faiz is the son of Saira’s mother’s sister, so the link is through her maternal aunt.

  2. For this code, X @ Y means X is Y’s father, X # Y means X is Y’s daughter, and X % Y means X is Y’s wife. In K @ L # M % K, which person is the daughter?

    1. M
    2. K
    3. L
    4. K and M
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) L

    Explanation: L is K’s child and M’s daughter; M and K are the married parents.

  3. A girl, Ishita, labels a photograph: “The woman holding me is the wife of my mother’s elder brother.” Which label is correct?

    1. Paternal aunt by marriage
    2. Maternal aunt by marriage
    3. Mother’s sister
    4. Father’s sister
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Maternal aunt by marriage

    Explanation: The mother’s brother is the maternal uncle; his wife is the maternal aunt by marriage.

  4. Six people R–W form two couples and three generations. R is S’s husband. U is their daughter; W is U’s son. T is W’s maternal uncle. V is R’s daughter-in-law. T has no brother and all spouses are among these six. Who is V’s husband?

    1. U
    2. R
    3. W
    4. T
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) T

    Explanation: T is U’s brother and thus R’s son; with no other son, daughter-in-law V must be T’s wife.

  5. Rajiv’s wife has a sister, Bina, whose husband is Suresh. Which exact marriage link joins Suresh to Rajiv?

    1. Sister’s husband
    2. Wife’s sister’s husband
    3. Husband of his daughter
    4. Wife’s brother
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Wife’s sister’s husband

    Explanation: Suresh married Rajiv’s wife’s sister; the two men are co-brothers-in-law.

  6. Can P’s gender be determined? I. P is Q’s parent. II. P is R’s sister. Select the correct sufficiency result.

    1. Even both are insufficient
    2. II alone is sufficient, I alone is not
    3. I alone is sufficient, II alone is not
    4. Both are required
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) II alone is sufficient, I alone is not

    Explanation: Parent does not specify gender; sister establishes that P is female without using I.

  7. X & Y means X is Y’s mother; X + Y means X is Y’s brother; X / Y means X is Y’s father. Fill BOTH blanks in A + B __ C __ D so A becomes D’s maternal grandfather’s brother.

    1. + followed by &
    2. / followed by /
    3. / followed by &
    4. & followed by /
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) / followed by &

    Explanation: B must be father of C, who must be mother of D; A is B’s brother.

  8. A boy has 3 brothers and 2 sisters. His sister Ginni is asked how many brothers she has. What should she answer?

    1. 4
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 5
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 4

    Explanation: The boy and his 3 brothers make 4 boys; each sister has all 4 as brothers.

  9. J is K’s younger brother but is older than L. K is L’s father. Which statement is correct despite the age information?

    1. J is two generations above L
    2. J is one generation above L
    3. J and L are siblings
    4. J is L’s maternal uncle
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) J is one generation above L

    Explanation: J is the brother of L’s father, so he occupies the parental generation.

  10. A man, Naveen, has exactly one sibling, a sister Shobha. Shobha’s mother-in-law Uma has only one son, Vikas. Vikas and Shobha have a daughter Rupal. Select the chain from Rupal to Naveen.

    1. Father → brother
    2. Mother → father
    3. Mother → brother
    4. Father → father
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Mother → brother

    Explanation: Rupal’s mother is Shobha and Shobha’s brother is Naveen; the in-law branch is extra.

  11. A tree has parents P (male) and Q (female); children R (female) and S (male); R’s husband T; and R and T’s daughter U. Which path traces U to her maternal uncle?

    1. U to T to R
    2. U to T to P
    3. U to R to Q
    4. U to R to S
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) U to R to S

    Explanation: U’s mother is R and R’s brother is S: U → R → S.

  12. Introducing two girls, a man, Deepak, says: “The elder is my sister’s daughter. The younger is the elder girl’s only sister.” What is the younger girl to Deepak?

    1. Sister’s daughter
    2. Brother’s daughter
    3. Sister
    4. Daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Sister’s daughter

    Explanation: The two girls share Deepak’s sister as their mother, so both are his nieces through his sister.

  13. X - Y denotes X is Y’s sister; X + Y denotes X is Y’s son; X * Y denotes X is Y’s father. In A - B + C * D, which claim is NOT guaranteed?

    1. C is D’s father
    2. D is male
    3. A is female
    4. B is male
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) D is male

    Explanation: Father fixes C’s gender, not D’s; the other three claims are directly established.

  14. A woman, Kusum, has a son Aditya. Aditya’s wife Malti has a father, Dinesh. Which statement correctly connects Dinesh and Kusum?

    1. Dinesh is Kusum’s husband
    2. Dinesh is Kusum’s son
    3. Kusum is Dinesh’s daughter
    4. Their children are married to each other
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Their children are married to each other

    Explanation: Kusum’s son Aditya is married to Dinesh’s daughter Malti.

  15. Eight relatives: Harish is Bela’s husband. Mini is their daughter; Yash is Neil’s maternal uncle. Mini’s husband is Om. Esha is Bela’s daughter-in-law and Ira’s mother. Neil and Ira are cousins, and every child’s parents are married and included. Which statement correctly identifies Ira’s father and the available information about Neil’s paternal aunt?

    1. Ira’s father is Om; Mini is Neil’s paternal aunt
    2. Ira’s father is Harish; Esha is Neil’s paternal aunt
    3. Ira’s father is Yash; Bela is Neil’s paternal aunt
    4. Ira’s father is Yash; Neil’s paternal aunt is not identified
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Ira’s father is Yash; Neil’s paternal aunt is not identified

    Explanation: Yash is Mini’s brother and Esha’s husband, hence Ira’s father. Neil’s father Om has no named sister.

  16. A woman, Aditi, is the mother of Bharat. Bharat is the father of Chitra, a girl. Identify Chitra’s relationship to Aditi without reversing the direction.

    1. Brother’s daughter
    2. Daughter
    3. Son’s daughter
    4. Daughter’s daughter
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Son’s daughter

    Explanation: Aditi → son Bharat → daughter Chitra gives a granddaughter through the son.

  17. Code: X @ Y means X is Y’s husband; X # Y means X is Y’s daughter; X $ Y means X is Y’s brother. Which expression proves that P is the husband of Q’s brother’s daughter?

    1. P @ R # S $ Q
    2. Q $ R @ S # P
    3. P $ R @ S # Q
    4. P @ R # Q $ S
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) P @ R # S $ Q

    Explanation: S is Q’s brother, R is S’s daughter, and P is R’s husband.

  18. A grandfather attends with his two sons. One son brings his two sons; the other brings his one son. Only these six men are counted. How many are fathers of at least one other attendee?

    1. 4
    2. 5
    3. 2
    4. 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 3

    Explanation: The grandfather and each of his two sons have children present; the three grandsons have no children stated.

  19. R’s parent S has a sister T. T’s son U is male. Is R certainly U’s male cousin?

    1. R is a cousin, but R’s gender is unknown
    2. R is U’s parent
    3. No, R must be U’s sister
    4. Yes, because U is male
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) R is a cousin, but R’s gender is unknown

    Explanation: The sibling parents establish cousinhood; U’s gender cannot establish R’s gender.

  20. In a tree described in words, Gagan (male) and Hema (female) have daughters Isha and Jaya. Isha and husband Kunal have son Lalit. Jaya and husband Manav have daughter Naina. Which two named people are spouses but not blood relatives in this tree?

    1. Isha and Jaya
    2. Lalit and Naina
    3. Hema and Isha
    4. Jaya and Manav
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Jaya and Manav

    Explanation: Jaya and Manav are explicitly a married couple; the other pairs are linked by descent.

  21. A is the father of B; B is C’s sister; C is D’s mother. Which conclusion necessarily follows?

    1. A is D’s maternal grandfather
    2. C is male
    3. D is A’s grandson
    4. B is D’s mother
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) A is D’s maternal grandfather

    Explanation: B and C share father A; since C is D’s mother, A is D’s maternal grandfather.

  22. A woman, Leela, introduces a girl: “Her father has the same biological father as me, but her father is not my brother.” Assume siblings sharing a father count as siblings. Assess the description.

    1. The girl is Leela’s daughter
    2. The girl is Leela’s sister
    3. The description is inconsistent
    4. The girl is Leela’s mother
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) The description is inconsistent

    Explanation: If both men have the same biological father, the girl’s father must be Leela’s brother; the denial conflicts with that.

  23. Suman is a woman married to Mohit. Mohit’s father has a younger son, Varun, married to Alka. Which person is Suman’s husband’s younger brother?

    1. Mohit
    2. Varun
    3. Alka
    4. Mohit’s father
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Varun

    Explanation: Varun is Mohit’s younger brother, making him Suman’s younger brother-in-law.

  24. X / Y means X is Y’s father; X + Y means X is Y’s wife; X * Y means X is Y’s sister. Decode R + S / T * U / V. What is R to V if R and S are parents of T and U?

    1. Maternal grandmother
    2. Father’s sister
    3. Paternal grandmother
    4. Mother
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Paternal grandmother

    Explanation: U is V’s father; U shares parents R and S with T, and R is female as S’s wife.

  25. Daman’s paternal grandfather has two sons: Daman’s father and his elder brother, Satpal. Satpal’s daughter is Noor. Which precise label fits Satpal’s relationship to Daman?

    1. Father’s younger brother
    2. Father’s elder brother
    3. Mother’s brother
    4. Father’s sister’s husband
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Father’s elder brother

    Explanation: Satpal is explicitly older than Daman’s father; he is the elder paternal uncle.
