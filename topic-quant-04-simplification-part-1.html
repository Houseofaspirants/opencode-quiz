Question bank

Simplification MCQ – Part 1: all 25 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. A 9 m 60 cm ribbon is cut into 8 equal pieces. Find the length of each piece in cm.

    1. 120
    2. 112.5
    3. 125
    4. 12
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 120

    Explanation: Convert first: 9 m 60 cm = 960 cm; then 960 ÷ 8 = 120 cm.

  2. Evaluate: √2025 − ∛3375.

    1. 20
    2. 40
    3. 30
    4. 60
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 30

    Explanation: Since 45² = 2025 and 15³ = 3375, the difference is 45 − 15 = 30.

  3. Harpreet spends 2/7 of her money, then 3/5 of the remainder. She receives ₹180 and now has ₹540. How much did she have initially?

    1. ₹900
    2. ₹1260
    3. ₹1890
    4. ₹1440
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) ₹1260

    Explanation: Before receiving ₹180, she had ₹360. The retained fraction is (5/7)(2/5) = 2/7, so the initial amount was 360 × 7/2 = ₹1260.

  4. Evaluate: 0.084 ÷ 0.007 + 1.25 × 2.4.

    1. 3.12
    2. 150
    3. 12.3
    4. 15
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 15

    Explanation: Scale the division by 1000: 84 ÷ 7 + 3 = 15.

  5. Find the value of ? in 2460 ÷ 15 + 3456 ÷ ? = 292.

    1. 32
    2. 24
    3. 27
    4. 36
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 27

    Explanation: The second quotient is 292 − 164 = 128; hence ? = 3456 ÷ 128 = 27.

  6. Find 1/(√7 − √5) + 1/(√7 + √5).

    1. √7/2
    2. 2√7
    3. √5
    4. √7
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) √7

    Explanation: Combine conjugate denominators: 2√7/(7 − 5) = √7.

  7. Evaluate: 2^11 ÷ 2^7.

    1. 16
    2. 32
    3. 4
    4. 8
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 16

    Explanation: Subtract exponents with the same base: 2^(11 − 7) = 2^4 = 16.

  8. Evaluate: (3.6² − 1.4²) ÷ 0.22.

    1. 5
    2. 50
    3. 22
    4. 100
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 50

    Explanation: Use the difference of squares: (3.6 − 1.4)(3.6 + 1.4) ÷ 0.22 = 2.2 × 5 ÷ 0.22 = 50.

  9. If a + b = 12 and ab = 27, find a³ + b³.

    1. 1728
    2. 972
    3. 2700
    4. 756
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 756

    Explanation: Use (a + b)³ − 3ab(a + b) = 1728 − 972 = 756.

  10. Round 15.47 and 4.52 separately to the nearest integer, then evaluate their product minus 18.

    1. 62
    2. 52
    3. 57
    4. 42
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 57

    Explanation: The rounded values are 15 and 5; 15 × 5 − 18 = 57.

  11. Evaluate: 125 × 56.

    1. 7000
    2. 700
    3. 7500
    4. 5600
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 7000

    Explanation: Use 125 = 1000/8: 56 ÷ 8 × 1000 = 7000.

  12. Find the value of ? in (? − 184) ÷ 12 × 7 = 189.

    1. 232
    2. 508
    3. 496
    4. 324
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 508

    Explanation: Reverse the operations: ? = 189 ÷ 7 × 12 + 184 = 508.

  13. Find the sum 1/(4 × 5) + 1/(5 × 6) + continuing in the same pattern up to 1/(15 × 16).

    1. 1/4
    2. 11/60
    3. 1/16
    4. 3/16
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 3/16

    Explanation: Use 1/[k(k + 1)] = 1/k − 1/(k + 1); all inner terms cancel, leaving 1/4 − 1/16 = 3/16.

  14. Which option is closest to 49.8 × 20.2?

    1. 1000
    2. 1100
    3. 1200
    4. 900
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1000

    Explanation: Use 50 × 20 = 1000; the exact product is 1005.96, closest to 1000.

  15. Evaluate: |−37| − |14 − 29|.

    1. -22
    2. 52
    3. 22
    4. -52
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 22

    Explanation: Absolute values are non-negative: 37 − 15 = 22.

  16. If x > 0 and √(3x + 9) + √(3x + 34) = 25, find x.

    1. 52
    2. 45
    3. 48
    4. 56
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 45

    Explanation: The radicands differ by 25, so the roots differ by 1. Their sum 25 gives roots 12 and 13; hence 3x + 9 = 144 and x = 45.

  17. In Patiala, 3 pens and 2 folders cost ₹154, while 2 pens and 3 folders cost ₹181. What is the cost of one folder?

    1. ₹27
    2. ₹47
    3. ₹20
    4. ₹67
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) ₹47

    Explanation: Adding gives pen + folder = 67; subtracting gives folder − pen = 27. Thus folder = (67 + 27)/2 = ₹47.

  18. Evaluate: (7/8 − 5/12) ÷ (11/18).

    1. 4/3
    2. 1/2
    3. 3/4
    4. 11/16
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 3/4

    Explanation: Use denominator 24: (11/24) × (18/11) = 3/4.

  19. Find the value of 37.5% of 640 − 12.5% of 384.

    1. 192
    2. 288
    3. 240
    4. 144
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 192

    Explanation: Use 37.5% = 3/8 and 12.5% = 1/8: 240 − 48 = 192.

  20. If a + b = 29 and ab = 198, find [(a² + b²)² − (a − b)^4]/(ab).

    1. 1976
    2. 494
    3. 890
    4. 988
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 988

    Explanation: Here a² + b² = 841 − 396 = 445 and (a − b)² = 841 − 792 = 49. Factor the numerator: (445 − 49)(445 + 49)/198 = 396 × 494/198 = 988.

  21. Among 120 candidates in Bathinda, 68 study Punjabi and 57 study English; 29 study both. How many study neither?

    1. 24
    2. 34
    3. 96
    4. 5
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 24

    Explanation: At least one subject = 68 + 57 − 29 = 96; neither = 120 − 96 = 24.

  22. Evaluate: 97 × 104.

    1. 10028
    2. 10088
    3. 10188
    4. 10012
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 10088

    Explanation: Use (100 − 3)(100 + 4) = 10000 + 100 − 12 = 10088.

  23. Evaluate: 756 ÷ 21 × 4 − 18 × 3.

    1. 162
    2. 198
    3. 72
    4. 90
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 90

    Explanation: Work left to right for division and multiplication: 36 × 4 − 54 = 90.

  24. Evaluate: (1 + 1/(2 + 1/3)) ÷ (1 − 1/(3 + 1/2)).

    1. 10/7
    2. 7/5
    3. 2
    4. 5/2
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 2

    Explanation: The numerator is 10/7 and the denominator is 5/7; their quotient is 2.

  25. Evaluate: 480 ÷ [24 − {18 − (9 − 3)}] + 7.

    1. 47
    2. 27
    3. 87
    4. 33
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 47

    Explanation: The inner values are 6 and 12; hence 480 ÷ 12 + 7 = 47.

Why it matters

Why Simplification MCQ – Part 1 is worth the hours

Where the marks actually come from.

Simplification MCQ – Part 1 is factual rather than analytical: you either recall it in seconds or you lose the mark to somebody who did. That makes it one of the cheapest sections to convert - a handful of short sittings usually settles the whole cluster.
