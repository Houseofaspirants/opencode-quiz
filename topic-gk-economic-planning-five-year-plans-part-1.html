Economic Planning & Five Year Plans - Part 1: all 18 questions

Read the question, pick an option, then open the answer.

  1. Economic planning in India primarily means:

    1. Annual budgeting by private firms
    2. Long-term government plans for coordinated development and efficient resource use
    3. Only regulation of foreign trade
    4. Only allocation of defence expenditure
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Long-term government plans for coordinated development and efficient resource use

    Explanation: Economic planning is the Government of India’s long-term approach to develop and coordinate the economy through efficient resource use. The other options are incorrect because annual budgeting, foreign-trade regulation and defence allocation are narrower activities, not the definition of economic planning. Planning is both development-oriented and coordinating in nature.

  2. Consider the following statements about the origin of India’s Five Year Plans: 1. Their idea was taken from the Soviet Union (USSR). 2. Nehru’s socialist influence shaped their adoption. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

    1. 1 only
    2. 2 only
    3. Both 1 and 2
    4. Neither 1 nor 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Both 1 and 2

    Explanation: Both statements are correct: the five-year-plan idea came from the USSR and was influenced by Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru’s socialist outlook. The other options are incorrect because the single-statement choices omit one fact, while the final choice rejects both true facts. This source is specifically linked to the early planning framework in India.

  3. The first eight Five Year Plans in India mainly emphasised:

    1. Making government only a growth facilitator
    2. Promoting MSMEs and industrial modernisation
    3. Liberalisation, Privatisation and Globalisation
    4. Expansion of the public sector and investment in heavy and basic industries
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Expansion of the public sector and investment in heavy and basic industries

    Explanation: The first eight plans focused on public-sector growth with large investment in heavy and basic industries. The other options are incorrect because growth facilitation belongs to the post-Ninth Plan shift, MSME modernisation to the Eighth Plan, and LPG reforms to 1991. The change in the government’s role is noted from the Ninth Plan onward.

  4. From the Ninth Plan (1997) onwards, the intended role of government shifted towards:

    1. Operator of all heavy industries
    2. Growth facilitator
    3. Sole agricultural producer
    4. Issuer of annual plans only
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Growth facilitator

    Explanation: From the Ninth Plan, the focus shifted to making government a growth facilitator. The other options are incorrect because operating all heavy industry is the opposite of this shift, while the other two choices are not government roles of this period. The Ninth Plan period was 1997–2002.

  5. The Constitution of India came into force on:

    1. 15 March 1950
    2. 26 January 1950
    3. 1 April 1951
    4. 15 August 1947
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 26 January 1950

    Explanation: The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950. The other options are incorrect because 15 March 1950 is the Planning Commission’s date, 1 April 1951 began the First Plan, and 15 August 1947 is not the date given for constitutional commencement. The Planning Commission was set up after the Constitution came into force.

  6. Which one of the following pairs is correctly matched?

    1. Planning Commission — 15 March 1950; Chairman: Prime Minister
    2. Planning Commission — 26 January 1950; Chairman: President
    3. Planning Commission — 1 April 1951; Chairman: Finance Minister
    4. Planning Commission — 15 August 1947; Chairman: Vice-President
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Planning Commission — 15 March 1950; Chairman: Prime Minister

    Explanation: The Planning Commission was set up on 15 March 1950 and the Prime Minister was its chairman. The other options are incorrect because the other dates and chairmanships do not match the Planning Commission. The Commission was responsible for preparing the Five Year Plans.

  7. Which body was responsible for making India’s Five Year Plans?

    1. Finance Commission
    2. NITI Aayog
    3. Planning Commission
    4. NABARD
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) Planning Commission

    Explanation: The Planning Commission was responsible for making the Five Year Plans. The other options are incorrect because the Finance Commission, NITI Aayog and NABARD did not make the Five Year Plans. NITI Aayog replaced the Planning Commission in 2015.

  8. The plan era began on 1 April 1951 with the:

    1. Second Five Year Plan
    2. Third Five Year Plan
    3. First Annual Plan
    4. First Five Year Plan
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) First Five Year Plan

    Explanation: The plan era began on 1 April 1951 with the First Five Year Plan. The other options are incorrect because the Second and Third Plans began later, while annual plans are associated with plan-holiday periods. The First Plan covered 1951–1956.

  9. In which year did NITI Aayog replace the Planning Commission?

    1. 2012
    2. 2015
    3. 2017
    4. 2020
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) 2015

    Explanation: The Planning Commission was replaced in 2015 by the think tank NITI Aayog. The other options are incorrect because 2012 is linked to the Twelfth Plan’s beginning, 2017 to its end, and 2020 is not given for this replacement. NITI Aayog issued an Action Agenda, a Medium-term Strategy Paper and a Vision Document.

  10. Match the NITI Aayog document with its duration: 1. Action Agenda 2. Medium-term Strategy Paper 3. Vision Document Which option is correct?

    1. 1–3 years; 2–7 years; 3–15 years
    2. 1–5 years; 2–10 years; 3–15 years
    3. 1–3 years; 2–5 years; 3–10 years
    4. 1–7 years; 2–10 years; 3–15 years
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 1–3 years; 2–7 years; 3–15 years

    Explanation: NITI Aayog brought out a 3-year Action Agenda, a 7-year Medium-term Strategy Paper and a 15-year Vision Document. The other options are incorrect because every other sequence changes one or more of the three durations. These documents are listed as NITI Aayog outputs after the Planning Commission’s replacement.

  11. Who first proposed economic planning in the book “Planned Economy for India” (1934)?

    1. M. Visvesvaraya
    2. M.N. Roy
    3. Shriman Narayan
    4. D.P. Dhar
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) M. Visvesvaraya

    Explanation: M. Visvesvaraya first proposed economic planning in “Planned Economy for India” in 1934. The other options are incorrect because M.N. Roy wrote the People’s Plan, Shriman Narayan the Gandhian Plan, and D.P. Dhar prepared the Minimum Needs Programme. The Bombay Plan was proposed later, in 1944.

  12. The Bombay Plan (1944) demanded planning and was associated with:

    1. 8 Indian industrialists
    2. The Planning Commission
    3. The Janata government
    4. The Sivaraman Committee
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) 8 Indian industrialists

    Explanation: The Bombay Plan of 1944 demanded planning and was associated with eight Indian industrialists. The other options are incorrect because the Planning Commission came later, the Janata government introduced the Rolling Plan, and the Sivaraman Committee is linked to NABARD. The Gandhian Plan and Bombay Plan both date to 1944.

  13. The Gandhian Plan (1944) was written by:

    1. M. Visvesvaraya
    2. Shriman Narayan
    3. M.N. Roy
    4. Gunnar Myrdal
    Show answer

    Answer: (B) Shriman Narayan

    Explanation: The Gandhian Plan of 1944 was written by Shriman Narayan. The other options are incorrect because Visvesvaraya proposed planning in a book, M.N. Roy wrote the People’s Plan, and Myrdal suggested the Rolling Plan. The People’s Plan was dated 1945.

  14. The People’s Plan (1945) was written by:

    1. Shriman Narayan
    2. M. Visvesvaraya
    3. M.N. Roy
    4. C. Rangarajan
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) M.N. Roy

    Explanation: The People’s Plan of 1945 was written by M.N. Roy. The other options are incorrect because Shriman Narayan wrote the Gandhian Plan, Visvesvaraya authored the 1934 planning proposal, and Rangarajan prepared the Eleventh Plan. The early proposals preceded the First Five Year Plan.

  15. The First Five Year Plan was based on which model?

    1. Mahalanobis model
    2. Chakravarty model
    3. Ashok Rudra–Alan Manne model
    4. Harrod–Domar model
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) Harrod–Domar model

    Explanation: The First Plan was based on the Harrod–Domar model. The other options are incorrect because Mahalanobis belongs to the Second Plan, Chakravarty to the Third, and Ashok Rudra–Alan Manne to the Fourth. Agriculture received the highest priority in the First Plan.

  16. The highest priority in the First Five Year Plan was given to:

    1. Agriculture
    2. Heavy industries
    3. Family planning
    4. MSMEs
    Show answer

    Answer: (A) Agriculture

    Explanation: Agriculture received the highest priority in the First Plan. The other options are incorrect because heavy industries were prioritised in the Second Plan, family planning in the Sixth, and MSMEs in the Eighth. The First Plan also started major river-valley and dam projects.

  17. Which of the following projects were started during the First Five Year Plan? 1. Bhakra Nangal Dam 2. Damodar Valley Project 3. Hirakud Dam Project Select the correct answer using the code below.

    1. 1 and 2 only
    2. 2 and 3 only
    3. 1 and 3 only
    4. 1, 2 and 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (D) 1, 2 and 3

    Explanation: All three listed projects—Bhakra Nangal Dam, Damodar Valley Project and Hirakud Dam Project—were started in the First Plan. The other options are incorrect because each partial combination omits one First Plan project. Five IITs were also started in the First Plan.

  18. How many Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) were started during the First Plan?

    1. 3
    2. 4
    3. 5
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (C) 5

    Explanation: Five Indian Institutes of Technology were started during the First Plan. The other options are incorrect because the values 3, 4 and 6 do not match the correct number. The First Plan ran from 1951 to 1956.
